Shipping and delivery

Version 2026-10-28.

MorphScreen is an online service, so there's nothing to ship and no shipping charge.

  • The free plan is ready when you confirm your email address or sign in with GitHub: the account comes with its first API key.
  • A paid plan starts as soon as Dodo Payments, which sells it as the merchant of record, confirms the payment, usually within seconds, and the dashboard's Billing tab shows it. A smaller plan chosen to follow the current one starts on your next billing date, which the Billing tab shows.
  • If a paid plan hasn't started within an hour of a confirmed payment, write to support@morphscreen.com with the payment's date and amount, and we'll put it right or refund it.

See also cancellation and refunds and the terms.
