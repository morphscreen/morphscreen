Sub-processors

As of 2026-10-28. When we add or drop a service, this page and its date change, and so does the privacy notice.

These are the services that handle personal data for MorphScreen. Each sees only what its job needs. Our own server runs at Hostinger in India; the API, the dashboard, this website and the database are all on it.

ServiceWhat it does for usWhat it sees
OpenRouterPasses each call's conversation to the model that answers itThe messages of each call and the answer. On our model key we use only providers that don't keep or train on them; with your own key, your OpenRouter settings apply
HostingerRents us the server and its storageEverything we keep, on the server in India
CloudflareTurnstile, the check that you're a personWhat its check needs from your browser at sign-up, sign-in and account deletion
ResendSends our mailYour email address and the mail we send you
Dodo PaymentsSells our paid plans as the merchant of record: takes the payment, charges the tax, sends the invoiceYour payment details, which we never see, your name, email address, billing country and what you pay for
GitHubSigns you in, if you choose itThat you signed in to MorphScreen; we get your account's number and verified primary address
MicrosoftClarity, recordings of visits to this website, only after a yesClicks, scrolling and the pages seen, with typed text hidden
CalendlyBooks demo callsYour name, email address and the time you pick
FathomRecords and transcribes a demo call, if you agree at its startThe call's audio, video and transcript

Two more tools handle data without sending any of it to their makers: FingerprintJS, the open-source library behind the device check at sign-up, runs in your browser, and DB-IP's country file sits on our server, which reads it to tell which country an address belongs to.
