# The Planets of Our Solar System

From the scorching surface of Mercury to the icy reaches of Neptune, our solar system holds eight fascinating worlds. Here's your tour.

These four are small, dense, and rocky. Mercury is cratered and airless, Venus is wrapped in a toxic CO₂ atmosphere that traps heat, Earth has liquid water and a protective magnetic field, and Mars shows signs of ancient rivers and lakes.

Jupiter and Saturn are mostly hydrogen and helium — true gas giants. Uranus and Neptune hold more ice (water, methane, ammonia) and are called ice giants. Saturn's rings are the brightest, but all four have rings.

<Callout tone="info" title="Pluto's demotion">
Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006 — it hasn't cleared its orbital neighbourhood. The solar system also holds five confirmed dwarf planets: Ceres, Pluto, Haumea, Makemake, and Eris.
