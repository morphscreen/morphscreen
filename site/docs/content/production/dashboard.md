The dashboard at [app.morphscreen.com](https://app.morphscreen.com) is where your account lives. Sign in with an email link or GitHub.

## Overview

This month's calls against your plan, with where the month is heading at today's pace, and a meter for each limit with what happens when it's reached. A chart shows calls a day over the last 30 days, split between those on our model and those on your own OpenRouter key.

## Keys

Make a key and copy it: it's shown once, then only its first characters. A project holds up to 20 keys. Revoking a key gives you 8 seconds to undo it, and it keeps working until then; after that it's refused everywhere.

## Calls

The newest 100 calls, each with how it ended, how long it took and how long until the first text, the model asked for and the one that answered, whether the answer was valid, which components had errors, and what repair did. Show only the failed ones to see what needs a look.

## Debug logs

Turn on debug logs for a project to keep its prompts and answers for 7 days, to see exactly what the model wrote. They're off unless you turn them on, and turning them off has an undo.

## Billing

Your plan, and every plan to move to, in dollars or rupees, monthly or yearly. Paying opens Dodo Payments' checkout over the same page. A bigger plan, or a move to yearly, starts at once; a smaller plan starts on your next billing date. Invoices and your card are on Dodo's page, a click away. Cancel whenever you like, and keep the plan with one click until the period ends ([how billing works](/pricing#billing-title)).

## Account

Your address, how you sign in, and deleting the account: everything it holds goes at once, except the payment records tax law asks us to keep.
