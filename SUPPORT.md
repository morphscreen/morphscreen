# Support

Email support@morphscreen.com for anything: a question, a bug, your account
or billing.

During the beta we reply within one business day (Monday to Friday, India
time). Security problems have their own page: SECURITY.md.

For a bug, send what you sent the API (the request without your keys), the
answer that came back or its request id (`x-request-id`), the SDK and its
version, and the browser or device. An answer that draws wrong is quickest
to fix with its text.

What we support:

- The latest release of each SDK, on what README.md lists under "Where it
  runs"
- The API's current version (`/v1`)

The beta has no uptime guarantee yet.
