## Locations in the Museum

<Card title="Mona Lisa" subtitle="Leonardo da Vinci, c. 1503–1519">
**Wing:** Denon  
**Floor:** 1st floor (Level 1)  
**Room:** 711 (Salle des États)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → Denon Wing → follow signs for "La Joconde"
</Card>

<Card title="Venus de Milo" subtitle="Alexandros of Antioch, c. 130–100 BC">
**Wing:** Sully  
**Floor:** Ground floor (Level 0)  
**Room:** 346 (Salle de la Vénus de Milo)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → Sully Wing → Greek Antiquities
</Card>

<Card title="Winged Victory of Samothrace" subtitle="Hellenistic, c. 200–190 BC">
**Wing:** Denon  
**Floor:** Ground floor (Level 0)  
**Room:** 703 (Escalier Daru, top of the grand staircase)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → Denon Wing → head straight to the grand staircase
</Card>

## Crowd-Avoidance Tips

<Card title="Timing Strategies">
<Steps>
<Step title="First hour or last hour" status="done">Doors open at 9 am (Wed–Mon); arrive by 8:45 am. Alternatively, come after 4 pm on Wed/Fri (open until 9:45 pm).</Step>
<Step title="Wednesday & Friday evenings" status="todo">Extended hours mean thinner crowds after 6 pm. The Mona Lisa room is notably calmer.</Step>
<Step title="Skip Tuesdays" status="todo">Closed day = pent-up demand Wednesday morning. If possible, avoid Wed 9–11 am.</Step>
<Step title="Buy timed tickets online" status="done">Pre-booked entry cuts the Pyramid queue from 60+ min to ~15 min.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Route Hacks">
<Columns>
<Card subtitle="Mona Lisa first? Go early.">
**Reverse the flow:** Most visitors turn right into Denon. Turn left to Sully first (Venus de Milo), loop back to Denon for Winged Victory, then hit Mona Lisa last—crowds thin as the day wears on.
</Card>
<Card subtitle="Use the Porte des Lions">
**Side entrance:** On the Seine side (Denon Wing, ground floor). Often shorter lines, drops you near Winged Victory and Italian paintings.
</Card>
<Card subtitle="Louvre app + map">
**Free official app** has real-time crowd density for major rooms. Check before you commit to a gallery.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Practical Extras">
- **Lockers:** Free, ground floor (Richelieu & Denon). Stow bags—large items banned in galleries.
- **Seating:** Foldable stools at info desks (deposit €1). Save your legs for the grand staircase.
- **Photos:** No flash, no selfie sticks. Mona Lisa is behind glass—angle from the side for less glare.
- **Restrooms:** Near each major wing entrance; lines shortest at Richelieu.
</Card>
