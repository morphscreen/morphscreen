# Louvre Quick Guide

<Badge tone="info">Top 3 must-sees</Badge>

## The Big Three

<Card title="Mona Lisa" subtitle="Leonardo da Vinci · 1503–1519">
![Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci](find)

**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 711 (Salle des États)  
**Tip:** View from the sides early morning or late afternoon; the center is always packed.
</Card>

<Card title="Venus de Milo" subtitle="Alexandros of Antioch · c. 130–100 BC">
![Venus de Milo statue](find)

**Location:** Sully Wing, Level 0, Room 346 (Gallery of Antiquities)  
**Tip:** Quieter in the first hour after opening; the room is spacious so you can circle around.
</Card>

<Card title="Winged Victory of Samothrace" subtitle="Hellenistic · c. 200–190 BC">
![Winged Victory of Samothrace statue](find)

**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 703 (atop the Daru staircase)  
**Tip:** Best seen from the bottom of the stairs; visit after 4 PM when tour groups thin out.
</Card>

## Crowd-Beating Tips

<Callout tone="success" title="Plan your visit">
<strong>Buy timed-entry tickets online</strong> — skip the pyramid queue entirely.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="info" title="Best times">
• **First hour** (9–10 AM) or **last two hours** (4–6 PM, later on Wed/Fri)  
• **Weekdays** beat weekends; Tuesday is closed so Wednesday is busier  
• **Evening openings** (Wed & Fri until 9:45 PM) are much calmer
</Callout>

<Callout tone="neutral" title="Smart routing">
• Enter via **Porte des Lions** or **Carrousel du Louvre** (underground mall) — shorter lines  
• Start with the **Denon Wing** masterpieces, then loop back to Sully/Richelieu  
• Use the **official app** for real-time room density and audio guides
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Don't forget">
• Free entry first Saturday of each month (6–9:45 PM) — extremely crowded  
• Large bags must be checked; bring only a small daypack  
• Wear comfortable shoes — the palace is 72,000 m² of marble floors
</Callout>
