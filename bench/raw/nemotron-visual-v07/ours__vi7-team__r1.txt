## Reach the Team

<Card title="Get in Touch" subtitle="How to collaborate with design">
<Columns>
<Card title="Design Requests" subtitle="For new work requests">
**Channel:** #design-requests (Slack)  
**Process:** Fill out the [design intake form](https://forms.example.com/design-intake) — includes brief, timeline, success metrics  
**SLA:** We triage within 2 business days. Urgent requests: ping @design-leads
</Card>
<Card title="Design Reviews" subtitle="For feedback on in-flight work">
**Weekly Crit:** Tuesdays 10am PT — open to all, join via calendar invite  
**Async Review:** Comment directly in Figma files using the 🔍 review flow  
**Stakeholder Sync:** First Thursday monthly — product, eng, design alignment
</Card>
<Card title="Design System" subtitle="For component questions & contributions">
**Channel:** #design-system (Slack)  
**Docs:** [ds.example.com](https://ds.example.com) — tokens, components, guidelines  
**Contribute:** Open a PR in the `design-system` repo with the "component" label
</Card>
