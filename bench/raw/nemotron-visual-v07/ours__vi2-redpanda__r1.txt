<Card title="Where It Lives" subtitle="Native range in the Eastern Himalayas and southwestern China">
![Map showing red panda range in the Himalayas](find)

Red pandas inhabit temperate forests with dense bamboo understories at elevations of 2,200–4,800 meters. Their range spans:
- Nepal
- India (Sikkim, West Bengal, Arunachal Pradesh)
- Bhutan
- Northern Myanmar
- China (Sichuan, Yunnan, Tibet)

They prefer steep slopes with plenty of tree cover and access to water.
</Card>

<Card title="What It Eats" subtitle="Primarily herbivorous with occasional omnivory">
<Columns>
<Tab title="Main Diet">
- Bamboo leaves and shoots (85–95% of diet)
- Bamboo stems (seasonally)
</Tab>
<Tab title="Supplementary Foods">
- Fruits (berries, apples)
- Acorns, roots, lichens
- Insects, bird eggs, small rodents (rare)
</Tab>
</Columns>

Red pandas have a specialized "false thumb" (modified wrist bone) for grasping bamboo. They spend up to 13 hours a day foraging and can eat up to 20,000 bamboo leaves daily.
</Card>

<Details title="Major Threats">
- Habitat loss and fragmentation from deforestation, agriculture, and infrastructure
- Poaching for fur and the illegal pet trade
- Climate change shifting bamboo distributions
- Human-wildlife conflict and free-roaming dogs
