# Houseplants for a Dark Flat

Four low‑light champions that survive (and even thrive) in dim corners. Each card shows a photo, watering schedule, and cat‑safety status.

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick tip">
Group plants with similar watering needs together to simplify your routine. Even “low light” plants appreciate a few hours of indirect light if you can spare it.
</Callout>
