# Houseplants for a Dark Flat

Four low‑light champions with care notes and cat‑safety info.

<Card title="Cast Iron Plant" subtitle="Aspidistra elatior">
![Cast Iron Plant](find)

**Water:** When top inch of soil is dry (roughly weekly)

<Badge tone="success">Safe for cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Parlor Palm" subtitle="Chamaedorea elegans">
![Parlor Palm](find)

**Water:** Keep evenly moist; water when top inch dry (roughly weekly)

<Badge tone="success">Safe for cats</Badge>
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick tip">
All four tolerate low light, but they’ll grow faster and look lusher with a few hours of indirect morning sun. Rotate pots occasionally for even growth.
</Callout>
