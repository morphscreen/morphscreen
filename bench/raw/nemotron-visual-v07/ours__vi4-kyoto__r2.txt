<Card title="Best Order for a Single Day">
<Steps>
<Step title="07:30 – Fushimi Inari Taisha" status="done" subtitle="Start early to beat crowds at the torii gates">
Arrive at opening for the most peaceful hike through thousands of vermilion gates. Allow 1.5–2 hours to reach the summit and return.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Kiyomizu-dera" status="done" subtitle="Walk the historic Higashiyama lanes en route">
Explore the famous wooden stage with city views. Visit Jishu Shrine (love deity) and drink from Otowa Waterfall's three streams.
</Step>
<Step title="12:30 – Lunch in Gion" status="current" subtitle="Traditional kaiseki or udon in the geisha district">
Walk down Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka preserved streets. Reserve ahead for top kaiseki spots.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 – Ryoan-ji" status="todo" subtitle="Contemplate Japan's most famous rock garden">
Take bus 59 or taxi northwest. Spend 45 minutes with the 15-rock zen garden and Kyoyochi Pond's walking path.
</Step>
<Step title="15:30 – Kinkaku-ji" status="todo" subtitle="End with the Golden Pavilion at golden hour">
Short bus ride or taxi. The setting sun illuminates the gold leaf beautifully. Allow 1 hour for grounds and tea house.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Transit Tip">
Buy a **Kyoto City Bus & Subway One-Day Pass (¥900)** for unlimited rides. All four sites connect via bus routes 101, 205, and 59. Taxis between northwest temples (Ryoan-ji ↔ Kinkaku-ji) cost ~¥1,000 and save 20 minutes.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Seasonal Notes">
- **Cherry blossom (late Mar–early Apr)** and **autumn foliage (mid–late Nov)**: Add 30–60 min per site for crowds.
- **Kiyomizu-dera** extends hours to 18:30–21:00 during special illuminations (spring/autumn).
- **Ryoan-ji** closes 30 min earlier Dec–Feb.
</Callout>
