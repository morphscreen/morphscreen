# Spaghetti Carbonara

![Spaghetti carbonara](find)

<Card title="Ingredients (serves 4)">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Spaghetti | 400 g |
| Guanciale (or pancetta) | 150 g |
| Egg yolks | 4 large |
| Whole egg | 1 large |
| Pecorino Romano, finely grated | 80 g |
| Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated | 40 g |
| Black pepper, freshly cracked | 2 tsp |
| Salt | for pasta water |
</Card>

<Card title="Method">
<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare the guanciale" status="done">
Cut the guanciale into ½ cm thick strips. Place in a cold large skillet and set over medium-low heat. Render slowly until the fat is translucent and the meat is crisp, 8–10 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside; keep the rendered fat in the pan.
</Step>
<Step title="Cook the pasta" status="done">
Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Salt generously (it should taste like the sea). Add spaghetti and cook 1 minute less than the package directs for al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
</Step>
<Step title="Make the carbonara sauce" status="done">
While pasta cooks, whisk egg yolks, whole egg, Pecorino, Parmigiano, and black pepper in a heat-proof bowl until a thick paste forms. Ladle 2–3 tbsp hot pasta water into the bowl, whisking constantly to temper the eggs.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine" status="current">
Return the skillet with rendered fat to the lowest heat. Add drained spaghetti and toss to coat. Remove pan from heat completely. Pour the egg-cheese mixture over the pasta, tossing vigorously and adding reserved pasta water a splash at a time until a glossy, creamy sauce clings to every strand. Fold in the crisp guanciale.
</Step>
<Step title="Serve immediately" status="todo">
Divide among warm bowls. Finish with an extra grating of Pecorino and a final crack of black pepper.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Columns>
<Stat label="Prep time" value="10 min" />

<Callout tone="info" title="Key tip">
The pan must be off the heat when you add the egg mixture, otherwise you'll get scrambled eggs. The residual heat from the pasta and pan is enough to thicken the sauce safely.
</Callout>
