<Card title="Best One-Day Order" subtitle="Optimized for travel time and crowd patterns">
<Steps>
<Step title="08:00 — Kinkaku-ji" status="current" subtitle="Golden Pavilion • Northwest Kyoto">
Arrive at opening to beat tour buses. Spend 60–75 min. Bus 101/205 from Kyoto Station (40 min).
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — Ryoan-ji" status="todo" subtitle="Zen Rock Garden • 10 min by bus from Kinkaku-ji">
Walk or bus 59. Best before midday crowds. Allow 45 min for garden and tea house.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 — Fushimi Inari Taisha" status="todo" subtitle="Torii Gate Tunnels • Southeast Kyoto">
Take JR Nara Line from Emmachi (near Ryoan-ji) to Inari Station (20 min). Hike 2–3 hrs round-trip to summit; turn back at Yotsutsuji for views if short on time.
</Step>
<Step title="15:30 — Kiyomizu-dera" status="todo" subtitle="Historic Wooden Stage • Eastern Higashiyama">
Keihan Line from Fushimi-Inari to Kiyomizu-Gojo (10 min), then 20-min uphill walk through preserved streets. Stay for sunset from the stage.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pro Tips">
• Buy an IC card (ICOCA/Suica) for seamless bus/train transfers.  
• Wear comfortable shoes — Fushimi Inari and Kiyomizu-dera involve steep walking.  
• Kinkaku-ji and Ryoan-ji share a bus route; a one-day bus pass (¥700) pays off.  
• Check seasonal hours: Kiyomizu-dera opens at 6:00 only in peak seasons.
</Callout>
