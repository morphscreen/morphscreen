<Card title="Day-by-Day Plan">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1: Belém & Riverfront">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 – Pastéis de Belém" status="done" subtitle="Try the original custard tarts">
Warm pastéis de nata with cinnamon and powdered sugar. Arrive early to avoid the queue.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Jerónimos Monastery" status="done" subtitle="Manueline masterpiece, UNESCO site">
Allow 1.5 hrs. Cloisters are the highlight. Buy tickets online.
</Step>
<Step title="12:00 – Belém Tower" status="done" subtitle="Iconic riverside fortress">
Walk the ramparts for Tagus views. Combined ticket with monastery saves €2.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 – Lunch at Ponto Final (Almada)" status="done" subtitle="Ferry across the river, seafood rice">
Take the ferry from Belém to Cacilhas (€1.50), 5-min walk to restaurant.
</Step>
<Step title="16:30 – MAAT or Coach Museum" status="todo" subtitle="Modern art or royal carriages">
MAAT for contemporary art & architecture; Coach Museum for gilded carriages.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Sunset at Miradouro de Santa Catarina" status="todo" subtitle="Drinks with a view">
Known as "Adamastor," popular sunset spot with bars and live music.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2: Alfama, Castle & Downtown">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 – Tram 28 to Alfama" status="todo" subtitle="Ride the classic yellow tram">
Board at Martim Moniz or Praça da Figueira. Hold on tight—pickpockets operate.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – São Jorge Castle" status="todo" subtitle="Moorish castle, panoramic city views">
Buy tickets online. Explore ramparts, cameras, and peacocks in the gardens.
</Step>
<Step title="12:30 – Lunch in Alfama" status="todo" subtitle="Tasca do Chico or O Trigueirinho">
Petiscos (small plates), vinho verde, fado in the evening at Tasca do Chico.
</Step>
<Step title="14:30 – Lisbon Cathedral & Miradouros" status="todo" subtitle="Sé de Lisboa, then Santa Luzia & Portas do Sol">
Walk downhill through Alfama's alleys. Two miradouros with Tagus views.
</Step>
<Step title="16:30 – Baixa & Praça do Comércio" status="todo" subtitle="Grid-plan downtown, grand riverside square">
Rua Augusta Arch elevator (€3) for rooftop view. Ginginha at a hole-in-the-wall bar.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Dinner at Time Out Market" status="todo" subtitle="Food hall with 30+ stalls">
Chef-curated stalls: seafood, pork sandwiches, Japanese, vegan. Book a table for groups.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 3: Modern Lisbon & Day Trip Option">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 – LX Factory" status="todo" subtitle="Creative hub under the 25 de Abril Bridge">
Bookshops, concept stores, street art, brunch at Wish Slow Coffee House.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Belém Cultural Center / Berardo Museum" status="todo" subtitle="Modern & contemporary art (free Sat)" >
Warhol, Picasso, Portuguese modernists. Gardens lead to the river.
</Step>
<Step title="13:30 – Lunch at Taberna da Rua das Flores" status="todo" subtitle="Modern Portuguese, no reservations">
Queue before 13:00. Tasting menu changes daily. Excellent natural wine list.
</Step>
<Step title="15:30 – Option A: Sintra day trip" status="todo" subtitle="Train 40 min, Palácio da Pena, Quinta da Regaleira">
Buy train tickets at Rossio station. Full day needed. Return by 19:00.
</Step>
<Step title="15:30 – Option B: Cascais & Estoril" status="todo" subtitle="Coastal train 35 min, beaches, Boca do Inferno">
Relaxed seaside towns. Bike the promenade. Gelato at Santini.
</Step>
<Step title="20:00 – Farewell dinner in Bairro Alto" status="todo" subtitle="Pharmácia, Taberna da Rua das Flores, or Sea Me">
End with a nightcap at Pavilhão Chinês (bar-museum) or Foxtrot (speakeasy).
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick Tips">
- **Viva Viagem card** (€0.50) + 24h passes (€6.80/day) covers metro, trams, buses, ferries, and trains to Cascais/Sintra.
- **Buy online**: Jerónimos Monastery, Belém Tower, São Jorge Castle, Rua Augusta Arch.
- **Cash needed**: Many tascas and small shops don't take cards. ATMs everywhere.
- **Pickpockets**: Tram 28, Praça do Comércio, Rossio station—wear bag in front.
- **Dinner time**: Portuguese eat late (20:00+). Kitchens close 22:00–23:00.
</Callout>
