<Card title="Where It Lives" subtitle="Habitat & Range">
**Native range:** Eastern Himalayas and southwestern China  
**Countries:** Nepal, India (Sikkim, West Bengal, Arunachal Pradesh), Bhutan, Myanmar, China (Sichuan, Yunnan, Tibet)  
**Elevation:** 2,200–4,800 meters (7,200–15,700 ft)  
**Habitat:** Temperate broadleaf and coniferous forests with dense bamboo understory  
**Key requirement:** Access to water and hollow trees or rock crevices for dens

![Map showing red panda range across the Himalayas](find)
</Card>

<Card title="What It Eats" subtitle="Diet & Feeding Habits">
**Primary food:** Bamboo (leaves, shoots, and stems) — ~85–95% of diet  
**Also eats:** Fruits, berries, acorns, roots, lichens, eggs, small birds, insects, and small rodents  
**Feeding behavior:** 
- Crepuscular (most active at dawn and dusk)
- Uses a modified wrist bone ("false thumb") to grasp bamboo stems
- Spends up to 13 hours a day foraging
- Eats ~200,000 bamboo leaves daily in summer
- Low nutritional value of bamboo requires large intake and low metabolic rate
</Card>

<Callout tone="danger" title="Major Threats">
- **Habitat loss & fragmentation** — deforestation for agriculture, logging, and development
- **Poaching** — for fur (traditional hats), pet trade, and traditional medicine
- **Climate change** — bamboo die-offs and shifting habitat zones
- **Human-wildlife conflict** — livestock competition, free-roaming dogs
- **Low reproductive rate** — 1–4 cubs per litter, high infant mortality
</Callout>

<Progress value="40" max="100" label="Estimated population remaining vs. historical baseline" tone="danger" />

**Conservation efforts:** Protected in all range countries; listed on CITES Appendix I; community-based conservation programs in Nepal and India; captive breeding programs in zoos worldwide.
</Card>
