<Card title="Day-by-Day Plan">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1: Historic Belém">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning" status="todo" subtitle="09:00–13:00">
**Belém Tower** (Torre de Belém) – UNESCO site, iconic riverside fortress
**Jerónimos Monastery** (Mosteiro dos Jerónimos) – Manueline masterpiece, Vasco da Gama's tomb
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch" status="todo" subtitle="13:00–14:30">
**Pastéis de Belém** – Original custard tarts since 1837 (expect a queue)
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon" status="todo" subtitle="14:30–18:00">
**Monument to the Discoveries** (Padrão dos Descobrimentos) – Elevator to viewpoint
**MAAT** (Museum of Art, Architecture & Technology) – Contemporary riverside museum
</Step>
<Step title="Evening" status="todo" subtitle="19:00–22:00">
Sunset drinks at **Topo Belém** rooftop bar, dinner nearby
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2: Alfama & Castle">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning" status="todo" subtitle="09:00–13:00">
**São Jorge Castle** (Castelo de São Jorge) – Moorish castle, city panoramas
**Alfama wandering** – Get lost in medieval lanes, hidden viewpoints
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch" status="todo" subtitle="13:00–14:30">
**Taberna da Rua das Flores** – Small plates, natural wine (arrive early, no reservations)
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon" status="todo" subtitle="14:30–18:00">
**Lisbon Cathedral** (Sé de Lisboa) – Romanesque survivor of 1755 quake
**National Tile Museum** (Museu Nacional do Azulejo) – Stunning azulejo panels in a convent
**Tram 28 ride** – Classic yellow tram through historic neighborhoods
</Step>
<Step title="Evening" status="todo" subtitle="19:00–23:00">
**Fado dinner** at **Clube de Fado** or **Casa de Linhares** – Book ahead
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3: Downtown & Modern">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning" status="todo" subtitle="09:00–13:00">
**Praça do Comércio** – Grand riverside square, Arco da Rua Augusta
**Elevador de Santa Justa** – Neo-Gothic lift to Carmo ruins viewpoint
**Carmo Convent** (Convento do Carmo) – Roofless Gothic church, archaeological museum
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch" status="todo" subtitle="13:00–14:30">
**Time Out Market** (Mercado da Ribeira) – Food hall with 30+ stalls from top chefs
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon" status="todo" subtitle="14:30–18:00">
**Chiado & Bairro Alto** – Shopping, cafés, street art
**LX Factory** – Creative hub in old industrial complex, bookshops, galleries
**Ler Devagar** – Stunning bookstore in a former printing press
</Step>
<Step title="Evening" status="todo" subtitle="19:00–late">
**Pink Street** (Rua Nova do Carvalho) – Bars, nightlife
**Miradouro de Santa Catarina** – Sunset drinks with bridge views
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

**€625–980 per person** (mid-range, shared double room). Budget version ~€450 (hostels, self-catering breakfast/lunch, free viewpoints). Luxury version €1,500+ (boutique hotels, fine dining, private tours).
</Callout>

<Details title="Money-saving tips" open="false">
- **Lisboa Card** (24h €21, 48h €35, 72h €44): free entry to 30+ sites + unlimited public transport. Worth it if you hit 3+ paid sights/day.
- **Free viewpoints**: Miradouro da Senhora do Monte, Miradouro de Santa Catarina, Miradouro das Portas do Sol – no ticket needed.
- **Walk**: Alfama, Baixa, Chiado, Bairro Alto are contiguous and walkable; saves tram/bus fares.
- **Lunch specials** (prato do dia): €10–15 for soup, main, drink, coffee at local tascas.
- **Water**: Tap water is safe; carry a bottle.
- **Airport metro**: Red line to city center €1.50 (with Viva Viagem card) vs €15–20 Uber.
</Details>
</Card>

<Card title="Practical Essentials">
<Columns>
<Card title="Getting Around">
- **Viva Viagem card** (€0.50): load zapping credit or 24h passes (€6.80)
- **Metro**: 4 lines, clean, fast, runs 6:30–1:00
- **Tram 15E**: Belém connection (not the touristy 28)
- **Uber/Bolt/FreeNow**: Cheap, reliable, good for late nights or hills
- **Walk**: Best way to see Alfama, Mouraria, Graça – but wear grippy shoes (cobblestones + hills)
</Card>

<Card title="When to Go">
- **Best**: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct (mild, fewer crowds)
- **Summer**: Hot, crowded, expensive, festivals (Santos Populares in June)
- **Winter**: Mild (10–15°C), rainy, cheapest, cozy Fado weather
</Card>

<Card title="Booking Ahead">
- **Must reserve**: Belcanto, Pharmácia, Clube de Fado, Time Out Market popular stalls at peak
- **Recommended**: Jerónimos Monastery (online skip-line), São Jorge Castle (summer), Tram 28 (early morning)
- **Walk-in friendly**: Most tascas, Pastéis de Belém (queue moves fast), LX Factory spots
</Card>

<Card title="Packing Essentials">
- Comfortable walking shoes with grip (cobblestones are slippery)
- Light layers + light rain jacket
- Portable charger (long days navigating)
- Euro cash (small places, tips, markets)
- Plug adapter (Type C/F, 230V)
</Card>
