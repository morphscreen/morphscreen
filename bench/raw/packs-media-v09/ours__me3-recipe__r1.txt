# Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Warm, gooey centers with crisp edges — the ultimate comfort cookie. Makes 24 cookies.

<Card title="Ingredients">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226 g) |
| Granulated sugar | 3/4 cup (150 g) |
| Light brown sugar, packed | 3/4 cup (165 g) |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| All-purpose flour | 2 1/4 cups (280 g) |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Semisweet chocolate chips | 2 cups (340 g) |
</Card>

<Card title="Directions">
<Steps>
<Step title="Preheat and prep" status="done">
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
</Step>
<Step title="Cream butter and sugars" status="done">
In a large bowl, beat softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="Add eggs and vanilla" status="done">
Beat in eggs one at a time, then mix in vanilla extract until combined.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine dry ingredients" status="done">
In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
</Step>
<Step title="Mix wet and dry" status="done">
Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture on low speed. Mix until just combined — do not overmix.
</Step>
<Step title="Fold in chocolate chips" status="done">
Stir in chocolate chips with a spatula until evenly distributed.
</Step>
<Step title="Scoop and bake" status="current">
Scoop 1.5 tbsp dough balls onto prepared sheets, spacing 2 inches apart. Bake 9–11 minutes until edges are golden but centers look soft.
</Step>
<Step title="Cool and enjoy" status="todo">
Cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. Store in an airtight container up to 5 days.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Baker's tip">
For extra chewy cookies, chill the dough for 30 minutes before baking. For crispier cookies, bake 1–2 minutes longer.
</Callout>
