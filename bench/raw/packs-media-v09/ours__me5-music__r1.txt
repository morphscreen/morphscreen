<Card title="Why These Tracks" subtitle="The science behind the selection">
<Columns>
<Details title="No lyrics = no language processing" open="false">
When words enter your ears, your brain automatically parses syntax and meaning — even if you're not "listening." Instrumental tracks sidestep that cognitive load entirely.
</Details>
<Details title="Steady tempo (60–80 BPM) mirrors resting heart rate" open="false">
A consistent, slow pulse helps regulate breathing and lowers physiological arousal, making it easier to slip into sustained attention.
</Details>
<Details title="Predictable harmonic structure" open="false">
Repetitive chord loops and minimal modulation mean your auditory cortex isn't constantly predicting what's next — freeing bandwidth for the task at hand.
</Details>
<Details title="Low information density" open="false">
Sparse arrangements (few simultaneous voices, long envelopes) reduce the "salient events" that trigger orienting responses.
</Details>
<Details title="Familiarity without nostalgia" open="false">
Curated from lesser-known artists so the music feels fresh — no personal memories hijack your focus.
</Details>
<Details title="Dynamic range compression" open="false">
Mastered for background playback: no sudden transients, no silence gaps, no volume jumps that pull attention.
</Details>
</Columns>
</Card>
