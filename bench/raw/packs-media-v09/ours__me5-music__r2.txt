## Why These Tracks

<Card title="The science behind the selection">
**Minimal lyrics** – Vocals engage language centers, pulling attention away from verbal tasks. These tracks are almost entirely instrumental.

**Steady tempo (60–80 BPM)** – Mirrors a calm resting heart rate, lowering physiological arousal without inducing drowsiness.

**Low dynamic range** – No sudden drops, builds, or surprises. Predictable audio reduces the orienting response that breaks focus.

**Repetitive harmonic structures** – Loop-based compositions (ambient, lo-fi, post-classical) create a stable auditory backdrop that the brain learns to ignore, freeing working memory.

**No emotional peaks** – Melancholy or euphoric passages trigger mind-wandering. These playlists favor neutral, slightly positive affect.

<Callout tone="info" title="Tip">Use noise-cancelling headphones at ~60 dB. Volume should feel like a quiet library, not a soundtrack.</Callout>
</Card>
