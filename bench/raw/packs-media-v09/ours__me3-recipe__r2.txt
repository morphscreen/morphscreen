<Card title="Ingredients">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226 g) |
| Granulated sugar | 3/4 cup (150 g) |
| Light brown sugar, packed | 3/4 cup (165 g) |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| All-purpose flour | 2 1/4 cups (280 g) |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Semisweet chocolate chips | 2 cups (340 g) |
</Card>

<Card title="Directions">
<Steps>
<Step title="Cream butter and sugars" status="done">
Beat softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="Add eggs and vanilla" status="done">
Mix in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla extract until combined.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine dry ingredients" status="done">
In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Gradually add to the wet mixture, mixing on low until just incorporated.
</Step>
<Step title="Fold in chocolate chips" status="done">
Gently stir in chocolate chips with a spatula until evenly distributed.
</Step>
<Step title="Chill the dough" status="done">
Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or up to 3 days) for thicker cookies.
</Step>
<Step title="Preheat and portion" status="current">
Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets with parchment. Scoop 1.5 tbsp dough balls, spacing 2 inches apart.
</Step>
<Step title="Bake" status="todo">
Bake 10–12 minutes, until edges are golden but centers still look soft. Cool on the sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
