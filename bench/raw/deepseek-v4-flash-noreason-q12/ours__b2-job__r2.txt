# Senior Product Designer

**Design Systems Team** <Badge tone="info">Full-time · Remote</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design Systems team. You'll shape how our products look and feel across web and mobile, building reusable components and patterns that empower dozens of product teams to ship polished experiences faster. You'll collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, and other designers to evolve our system from a component library into a true design language.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll bring">
- **5+ years** of product design experience, with at least 2 years working on design systems
- Strong portfolio demonstrating systematic thinking and attention to detail
- Fluency in Figma, including variables, components, and auto-layout
- Experience with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA or better)
- Ability to write clear documentation and present design decisions to stakeholders
- Bonus: experience with code (HTML/CSS) or design tokens in production

<Callout tone="info" title="Portfolio required">
Please include a link to your portfolio or a selection of relevant work samples in your application.
</Callout>
</Card>
