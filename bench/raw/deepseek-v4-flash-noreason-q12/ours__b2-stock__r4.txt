<Details title="How the warning works" open="false">
- The threshold is set globally at **20 units** (adjustable above).
- Items are checked at the end of each business day.
- Items at or below the threshold appear in the table above with a **Low** or **Critical** badge (Critical = 10 units or fewer).
- Reordering via the form sends a purchase order to your supplier and updates the inventory once received.
</Details>
