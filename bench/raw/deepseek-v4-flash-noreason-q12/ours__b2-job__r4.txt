# Senior Product Designer — Design Systems

**Team:** Platform Design Group

<Badge tone="info">Full-time</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Remote-friendly</Badge> <Badge tone="success">Hiring now</Badge>

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to own and evolve our design system, partnering with engineers and product managers to craft cohesive, accessible experiences across all of our products.

---

<Columns>
<Card title="About the role">
You'll lead the design system from tokens to components, working closely with frontend engineers to ship high-quality, reusable patterns. You'll mentor other designers, audit product surfaces for consistency, and drive adoption of new standards.
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
- 5+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years focused on design systems
- Strong portfolio demonstrating systematic thinking and polished UI
- Proficiency in Figma, including variables, components, and auto-layout
- Understanding of accessibility (WCAG 2.2) and responsive design
- Experience collaborating with engineering teams on implementation
- Excellent communication and documentation skills
</Card>
</Columns>
