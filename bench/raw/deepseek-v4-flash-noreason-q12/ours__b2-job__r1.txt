# Product Designer — Design Systems

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) or Hybrid in San Francisco**

<Badge tone="success">Open</Badge> <Badge tone="info">Full-time</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Product Designer to own and evolve our design system across web and mobile. You'll work closely with engineering, brand, and product teams to create consistent, accessible, and delightful experiences used by millions of people.

You'll be responsible for turning messy patterns into scalable components, documenting usage guidelines, and championing design quality across the org. If you love the craft of systems thinking and enjoy collaborating across disciplines, this is the role for you.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll bring">
- 4+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years focused on design systems
- Strong portfolio showing component libraries, tokens, and documentation
- Proficiency with Figma, including variables and component properties
- Working knowledge of HTML/CSS and how design translates to code
- Experience with accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) and inclusive design practices
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Ability to balance speed, quality, and long-term scalability

<Callout tone="info" title="Bonus points">
Experience with motion design tokens, theming across brands, or contributing to open-source design systems.
</Callout>
</Card>
