<Card title="About this role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for high-impact features, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for design quality across the team. This role reports to the Head of Design and sits within a cross-functional product squad.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for complex, user-facing features from concept to launch
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights into actionable design decisions
- Build and maintain components in our design system
- Collaborate with PMs and engineers to define product strategy
- Mentor junior designers and contribute to design culture
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience at a software company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products and design process
- Expertise in Figma and modern design tooling
- Experience with design systems and component libraries
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- Comfortable giving and receiving design critique
</Card>

<Card title="Nice to have">
- Experience with B2B SaaS or developer tools
- Motion design and prototyping skills
- Front-end development knowledge (React, CSS)
</Card>
