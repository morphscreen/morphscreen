# Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

<Card title="About this role">
We're looking for a Product Designer to help shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to turn complex problems into intuitive, delightful flows. This is a high-impact role where you'll own features end-to-end—from early discovery through polished delivery—and help raise the bar for design quality across the team.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for key product surfaces: onboarding, workspace settings, and cross-team workflows
- Partner with PMs to define problem spaces, scope solutions, and validate direction with users
- Produce high-fidelity mockups, prototypes, and design specs that engineers love to build from
- Contribute to and evolve our design system (components, patterns, tokens)
- Run lightweight usability tests and synthesize findings into actionable changes
- Mentor junior designers and champion design thinking across the org
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 4+ years of product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products where you owned the end-to-end design process
- Fluency in Figma (prototyping, auto-layout, components, variables)
- Comfortable working in a technical environment—you can read API docs and collaborate with engineers on feasibility
- Excellent written and visual communication; you can articulate the "why" behind your decisions
- Experience with design systems and accessibility standards (WCAG AA)
- Bonus: motion design, illustration, or front-end prototyping (React, CSS)
</Card>
