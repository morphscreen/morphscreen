<Card title="Product Designer" subtitle="Design Team · Full-time · Remote">
We're looking for a thoughtful product designer to help shape the core experience of our platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to turn complex problems into simple, elegant solutions that our customers love.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Own the design process from discovery through delivery for key product areas
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define requirements and validate ideas
- Create user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Contribute to and evolve our design system
- Conduct usability testing and incorporate feedback into iterations
- Advocate for user-centered design across the organization
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 4+ years of product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design work and impact
- Expertise in Figma (or equivalent) and modern design systems
- Comfortable with user research methods and data-informed design
- Excellent communication skills and ability to present design rationale
- Experience designing for developer tools or B2B products is a plus
</Card>
