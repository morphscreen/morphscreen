# Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

<Card title="About this role">
We're looking for a Product Designer to join our Design team and help shape the future of our core platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to define and deliver intuitive, accessible experiences for millions of users. This is a high-impact role where you'll own the end-to-end design process — from discovery and ideation through prototyping, testing, and handoff.
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- **5+ years** of product design experience at a tech company or digital agency
- Strong portfolio showing end-to-end design work (research → ship)
- Expert in Figma; comfortable with prototyping tools (Principle, Framer, or code)
- Deep understanding of design systems, accessibility (WCAG AA), and responsive design
- Excellent communication skills — you can articulate design rationale to non-design stakeholders
- Experience conducting usability testing and synthesizing qualitative insights
- **Nice to have:** motion design, design ops, or front-end development experience (React/CSS)
</Card>
