<Card title="1. What the law says about returns within 30 days">
Under UK consumer law, you have a **30-day right to reject** faulty goods and get a full refund. This applies when goods are:
- Not of satisfactory quality
- Not fit for purpose
- Not as described

After 30 days, you still have rights to repair or replacement (up to 6 years in England/Wales, 5 years in Scotland), but the automatic full-refund right ends at 30 days.

<Callout tone="info" title="Separate 14-day cooling-off period">
For online, phone, or doorstep purchases (distance/off-premises contracts), you also have **14 days to change your mind** and return for any reason — even if the item isn't faulty. This is a separate right from the 30-day faulty-goods refund.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="2. The two statutes this comes from">
<Columns>
<Card title="Consumer Rights Act 2015">
Covers the **30-day right to reject** faulty goods (sections 19–24). Sets out statutory standards: satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, as described. Applies to all goods bought from a trader.
</Card>

<Card title="Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013">
Covers the **14-day cooling-off period** for distance and off-premises contracts (regulations 28–35). Lets you cancel for any reason within 14 days of receipt.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="3. What the customer should do">
<Steps>
<Step title="Stop using the item" status="done">
Set it aside. Continued use can weaken your right to reject.
</Step>
<Step title="Contact the seller in writing" status="current">
Email or write (keep proof). State clearly: "I am rejecting this item under my 30-day right to reject under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 because it is [faulty/not as described/not fit for purpose]. I am entitled to a full refund."
</Step>
<Step title="Arrange return" status="todo">
The seller must refund **and** cover reasonable return costs for faulty goods. Ask for a prepaid label or collection.
</Step>
<Step title="Escalate if refused" status="todo">
- Complain via the seller's formal process
- Use Resolver or Citizens Advice consumer helpline
- Consider court claim (small claims track for ≤£10,000)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="success" title="Quick checklist for your message">
- Order number & date
- What's wrong (photos help)
- "I am exercising my 30-day right to reject under the Consumer Rights Act 2015"
- Request full refund + return costs
- Deadline: "Please confirm within 7 days"
</Callout>
</Card>
