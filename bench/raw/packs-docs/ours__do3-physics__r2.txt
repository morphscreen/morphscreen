# Kinetic Energy Explainer

<Card title="1. Formula" subtitle="Kinetic energy of a moving object">
<Math tex="K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2" label="Kinetic energy formula" />
<p><strong>K</strong> = kinetic energy (J)</p>
<p><strong>m</strong> = mass (kg)</p>
<p><strong>v</strong> = velocity (m/s)</p>
</Card>

<Card title="2. Worked Example" subtitle="A 2 kg ball moving at 10 m/s">
<Steps>
<Step title="Identify known values" status="done">
<p>Mass <strong>m = 2 kg</strong></p>
<p>Velocity <strong>v = 10 m/s</strong></p>
</Step>
<Step title="Substitute into formula" status="done">
<Math tex="K = \frac{1}{2} (2\ \text{kg}) (10\ \text{m/s})^2" label="Substitution" />
</Step>
<Step title="Calculate" status="done">
<Math tex="K = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 100 = 100\ \text{J}" label="Result" />
</Step>
<Step title="Answer" status="current">
<p>The ball has <strong>100 joules</strong> of kinetic energy.</p>
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="3. Units" subtitle="SI units for each quantity">
| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | Unit Symbol |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic Energy | K | joule | J |
| Mass | m | kilogram | kg |
| Velocity | v | metre per second | m/s |
| 1 J = 1 kg·m²/s² | | | |
</Card>
