# Kinetic Energy Explainer

<Card title="Formula" subtitle="The kinetic energy of an object in motion">
<Math tex="K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2" label="Kinetic energy formula" />
<p><strong>K</strong> = kinetic energy (joules)</p>
<p><strong>m</strong> = mass (kilograms)</p>
<p><strong>v</strong> = velocity (meters per second)</p>
</Card>

<Card title="Worked Example" subtitle="A 2 kg ball moving at 10 m/s">
<Steps>
<Step title="Identify known values" status="done">
Mass <strong>m = 2 kg</strong>, Velocity <strong>v = 10 m/s</strong>
</Step>
<Step title="Substitute into formula" status="done">
<Math tex="K = \frac{1}{2} (2\,\text{kg}) (10\,\text{m/s})^2" label="Substitution" />
</Step>
<Step title="Calculate" status="done">
<Math tex="K = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 100 = 100\,\text{J}" label="Result" />
</Step>
<Step title="Answer" status="current">
The ball has <strong>100 joules</strong> of kinetic energy.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Units" subtitle="SI units and common conversions">
<Table>
| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | Unit Symbol | Base Units |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic Energy | K | joule | J | kg·m²/s² |
| Mass | m | kilogram | kg | kg |
| Velocity | v | meter per second | m/s | m/s |
</Table>

<Callout tone="info" title="Key relationships">
1 joule = 1 kg·m²/s² = 1 N·m = 1 W·s
</Callout>

<Callout tone="neutral" title="Other energy units">
- 1 calorie (thermochemical) = 4.184 J
- 1 electronvolt (eV) = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
- 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
</Callout>
</Card>
