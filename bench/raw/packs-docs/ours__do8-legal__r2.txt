# Consumer Rights: 30-Day Returns

Your right to a full refund within 30 days for faulty, misdescribed, or unfit goods.

<Card title="1. What the law says">
You have a **short-term right to reject** goods within **30 days** of taking ownership (or delivery, if later) if they:
- Are not of **satisfactory quality**
- Are not **fit for a particular purpose** you made known
- Do not **match the description**, sample, or model shown

If you reject within 30 days, you are entitled to a **full refund** — not a repair, replacement, or credit note. The refund must be given within 14 days of the trader receiving the returned goods (or proof of postage).

<Badge tone="info">Applies to goods, not services</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">Digital content has a separate repair/replacement right first</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="2. The two statutes">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Consumer Rights Act 2015">
**Primary source** — consolidates and updates UK consumer law for goods, services, and digital content.

| Section | Right |
|---|---|
| s.9 | Goods must be of satisfactory quality |
| s.10 | Goods must be fit for a particular purpose |
| s.11 | Goods must match the description |
| s.13 | Goods must match the sample |
| s.14 | Goods must match the model seen/examined |
| **s.20** | **Short-term right to reject (30 days)** |
| s.22 | Refund must be given without undue delay, within 14 days |
</Tab>

<Tab title="Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013">
**Distance/off-premises contracts** — gives a **14-day cooling-off period** to cancel *any* online, phone, or doorstep purchase for *any* reason (change of mind).

| Regulation | Right |
|---|---|
| Reg. 28 | 14-day cancellation period (starts day after receipt) |
| Reg. 30 | Refund within 14 days of receiving returned goods |
| Reg. 33 | Trader must bear return cost only if they didn't inform you otherwise |

<Callout tone="info" title="Key difference">
CRA 2015: **Fault-based** right (30 days, full refund for defective goods).<br/>
CCRs 2013: **No-fault** right (14 days, any reason, distance/off-premises only).
</Callout>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="3. What the customer should do">
<Steps>
<Step title="Stop using the item" status="done">
Do not continue to use the goods once you decide to reject them — this can waive your right.
</Step>

<Step title="Notify the seller in writing" status="current">
Email or write to the retailer (not the manufacturer) stating you are **rejecting the goods under the Consumer Rights Act 2015** and requesting a **full refund**. Keep a copy.
</Step>

<Step title="Return the goods" status="todo">
Send them back (or make them available for collection). Get **proof of postage**. The trader must refund **original delivery cost** (at least standard delivery).
</Step>

<Step title="Escalate if refused" status="todo">
- Complain to the retailer’s CEO / formal complaints process  
- Use **Resolver** (free) or **Citizens Advice** consumer helpline  
- Consider **Section 75** (credit card £100–£30,000) or **chargeback** (debit card)  
- Small claims court (Money Claim Online) for up to £10,000
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="success" title="Quick template">
> **Subject: Rejection under Consumer Rights Act 2015 — Order #12345**  
> I am rejecting the [item] received on [date] because [fault / does not match description / unfit for purpose]. Under s.20 CRA 2015 I am entitled to a full refund within 14 days of your receipt of the returned goods. I will return the item by [date]. Please confirm the return address.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Edge cases & reminders">
| Situation | Your right |
|---|---|
| **Gift receipt / no receipt** | Proof of purchase (bank statement, email) is enough |
| **Perishables / personalised / sealed hygiene items** | 30-day right to reject **does not apply** |
| **Digital content (downloads, streaming)** | No 30-day reject — right to repair/replacement first |
| **Services** | Right to repeat performance or price reduction |
| **Beyond 30 days, under 6 months** | Presumed faulty at delivery — repair/replacement first, then refund |
| **Beyond 6 months** | You must prove fault existed at delivery |
</Card>
