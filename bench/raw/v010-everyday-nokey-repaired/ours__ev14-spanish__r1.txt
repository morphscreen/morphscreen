# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Callout tone="info" title="Why these 10?">
These are the phrases you'll actually use every day while traveling — greetings, polite words, and getting by in restaurants and shops. Master these and you'll get a much warmer welcome.
</Callout>

<Card title="The 10 phrases">
| # | English | Spanish | When to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello / Hi | **Hola** (OH-lah) | Anytime, anywhere |
| 2 | Please | **Por favor** (por fah-VOR) | Making any request |
| 3 | Thank you | **Gracias** (GRAH-see-as) | After anything |
| 4 | Yes / No | **Sí** (see) / **No** (noh) | The basics! |
| 5 | Excuse me / Sorry | **Perdón** (pehr-DOHN) | Getting attention, apologizing |
| 6 | Do you speak English? | **¿Habla inglés?** (AH-blah een-GLES) | When you're stuck |
| 7 | I don't understand | **No entiendo** (noh en-tee-EN-doh) | When you're lost |
| 8 | How much is it? | **¿Cuánto cuesta?** (KWAHN-toh KWES-tah) | Shopping, markets |
| 9 | Where is the bathroom? | **¿Dónde está el baño?** (DOHN-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyoh) | Essential! |
| 10 | The bill, please | **La cuenta, por favor** (lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR) | At restaurants |
</Card>

<Callout tone="success" title="Pronunciation tip">
Spanish is phonetic — you say it how it's written. The **r** is rolled slightly, and **ll** sounds like a soft "y" (as in "yes"). Don't worry about being perfect; locals appreciate any effort!
</Callout>

<Question text="How do you say 'Thank you' in Spanish?" answer="Gracias">
- Gracias
- Por favor
- Perdón
- Hola

Gracias is used constantly — after every meal, purchase, or kind gesture.
</Question>
<Question text="You're in a restaurant and want to pay. What do you say?" answer="La cuenta, por favor">
- ¿Dónde está el baño?
- La cuenta, por favor
- ¿Cuánto cuesta?
- No entiendo

"La cuenta, por favor" is the polite way to ask for the check.
</Question>
<Question text="What does 'No entiendo' mean?" answer="I don't understand">
- I don't want it
- I don't understand
- Excuse me
- How much is it?

Say "No entiendo" when someone speaks too fast or you're confused.
</Question>
<Question text="Which phrase helps you ask if someone speaks English?" answer="¿Habla inglés?">
- ¿Cuánto cuesta?
- ¿Dónde está el baño?
- ¿Habla inglés?
- La cuenta, por favor

A lifesaver when you need help — most people in tourist areas speak some English.
