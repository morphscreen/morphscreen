<Badge tone="info">Best for first-timers</Badge> — Balanced mix of iconic sights, food, and culture, with easy transit between areas.

<Callout tone="success" title="How to use this plan">
Each day is anchored in one neighborhood so you walk less and see more. The map shows all stops in order — tap any marker for details. Start each morning around 9:00 AM.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1 — Shinjuku, Harajuku, Shibuya">
<Steps>
<Step title="9:00 — Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="Forest in the city">
Walk the gravel path under towering cedars. Arrive early to avoid crowds. Free entry.
</Step>
<Step title="10:30 — Harajuku & Takeshita St" status="done" subtitle="Crepes & kawaii">
Try a rainbow crepe, browse vintage shops on Cat Street. Crowded but fun.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 — Lunch in Shibuya" status="done" subtitle="Shibuya Crossing view">
Eat at a café overlooking the crossing (e.g., Starbucks by the station). Try *gyukatsu* (beef cutlet).
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 — Shibuya Scramble" status="done" subtitle="The famous crossing">
Watch the light change, then walk the crossing yourself. Visit Hachiko statue.
</Step>
<Step title="17:30 — Tokyo Metropolitan Building" status="done" subtitle="Free sunset view">
South tower observatory — see Mount Fuji on clear days. Sunset ~17:00 in October.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 — Dinner in Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Omoide Yokocho">
Tiny alleys with yakitori and beer. Go early to beat the queue.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Callout tone="warning" title="Budget tip">
Shibuya Sky (rooftop) is spectacular but ¥2,000+ and needs booking. The Metropolitan Building is free and nearly as good.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 — Tsukiji, Hamarikyu, teamLab">
<Steps>
<Step title="8:00 — Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="Sushi breakfast">
Try *tamago* (egg) skewers, fresh uni, and coffee. Go hungry — you'll taste 5 things.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — Hamarikyu Gardens" status="done" subtitle="Tea house by the water">
¥300 entry. Walk the tidal pond, sip matcha at Nakajima-tei.
</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Lunch nearby" status="done" subtitle="Local favorites">
Try *monjayaki* (savory pancake) in Tsukishima, a 10-min walk.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 — teamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="Book 2 weeks ahead">
Walk through water and light. Wear shorts — you'll be barefoot in ankle-deep water.
</Step>
<Step title="17:00 — Odaiba Seaside" status="done" subtitle="Optional sunset">
Rainbow Bridge views, Gundam statue. Take the Yurikamome line.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Callout tone="info" title="Tickets">
teamLab Planets: ¥3,200/person. Book on their official site — evening slots are calmer.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 — Asakusa, Skytree, Ueno">
<Steps>
<Step title="9:00 — Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's oldest temple">
Enter through the Kaminarimon Gate. Try *ningyo-yaki* (filled cakes) from street stalls.
</Step>
<Step title="10:30 — Nakamise Shopping St" status="done" subtitle="Souvenirs & snacks">
Pick up lucky charms, fans, and *senbei* (rice crackers).
</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Lunch in Asakusa" status="done" subtitle="Tempura tradition">
Try *tendon* (tempura rice bowl) at a 100-year-old shop.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 — Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="Tembo Deck (350m)">
Book a timed ticket online. On clear days you see all of Tokyo.
</Step>
<Step title="16:00 — Ueno Park" status="done" subtitle="Museums & pond">
Pick one: Tokyo National Museum (¥1,000) or the free National Museum of Nature and Science.
</Step>
<Step title="19:00 — Ameyoko Market" status="done" subtitle="Street food dinner">
Try *yakiniku* skewers, fresh fruit, and cheap beer.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 — Ghibli Museum & Kichijoji">
<Callout tone="danger" title="Book this first">
The **Ghibli Museum** requires advance tickets (¥1,000) — they sell out weeks ahead. Reserve on **Lawson Ticket** (Japanese site) or through a travel agency. You'll need your passport.
</Callout>
<Steps>
<Step title="10:00 — Ghibli Museum" status="done" subtitle="Studio Ghibli magic">
See original animation cells, the catbus, and a short film (changes monthly). No photos inside — enjoy the moment.
</Step>
<Step title="12:30 — Lunch in Kichijoji" status="done" subtitle="Harmonica Yokocho">
Tiny alleys with *izakaya* and *kushikatsu* (fried skewers). Great for people-watching.
</Step>
<Step title="14:30 — Inokashira Park" status="done" subtitle="Boat ride">
Rent a swan boat (¥700/30min). Check out the shrine at the center.
</Step>
<Step title="16:00 — Vintage shopping" status="done" subtitle="Kichijoji's backstreets">
Browse used kimono and vinyl records. More relaxed than Harajuku.
</Step>
<Step title="19:00 — Dinner near your hotel" status="done" subtitle="Try *izakaya* hopping">
Order 2–3 small dishes per place, move on after an hour.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 — Akihabara, Imperial Palace, Ginza">
<Steps>
<Step title="9:30 — Akihabara Electric Town" status="done" subtitle="Anime & gadgets">
Browse multi-floor shops for figures, retro games, and maid cafés (just look, or book one for fun).
</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Lunch in Akihabara" status="done" subtitle="*Yakisoba* or curry">
Try a themed café if you're brave — or a simple ramen shop.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 — Imperial Palace East Gardens" status="done" subtitle="Free castle grounds">
Walk the moat, see the stone walls. Peaceful contrast to the morning.
</Step>
<Step title="16:00 — Ginza" status="done" subtitle="High-end shopping">
Walk Chuo-dori (closed to cars on weekends). Try *shabu-shabu* for dinner.
</Step>
<Step title="19:00 — Final night drinks" status="done" subtitle="Rooftop bar">
Head to a hotel bar for skyline views (e.g., Park Hyatt or Andaz).
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
