Not everyone has a green thumb — and that's okay. These plants forgive missed waterings, low light, and general neglect. Here's a practical guide to the most resilient indoor plants, plus how to keep them thriving with minimal effort.

<Card title="Golden Rules for Keeping Any Plant Alive">
<Steps>
<Step title="Don't overwater" status="done">
This is the #1 killer. Most of these plants prefer their soil to dry out completely between waterings. When in doubt, wait a few more days.
</Step>
<Step title="Match light to the plant" status="done">
Snake, ZZ, and cast iron handle dark corners. Pothos and spider plants like medium indirect light. Jade needs bright sun. Peace lily prefers shade.
</Step>
<Step title="Use well-draining soil" status="done">
A standard potting mix with perlite works for most. Succulents (jade) need a sandier mix.
</Step>
<Step title="Water deeply, then let it drain" status="done">
When you do water, soak the soil until water runs out the bottom, then empty the saucer. Don't let roots sit in water.
</Step>
<Step title="Watch for signs" status="done">
Yellow leaves = too much water. Crispy brown tips = too dry. Drooping = thirsty. Learn each plant's language.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="The #1 mistake">
Overwatering kills more houseplants than anything else. When you're unsure, wait. These plants are adapted to survive dry spells — not soggy roots.
</Callout>

<Card title="Which should you pick?">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Low light room">
Pick a **Snake Plant**, **ZZ Plant**, or **Cast Iron Plant**. These three thrive in dim corners where most plants struggle.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Bright, sunny window">
A **Jade Plant** will reward you with slow, steady growth. **Pothos** and **Spider Plants** also love bright indirect light.
</Tab>
<Tab title="You forget to water">
**Snake Plant** and **ZZ Plant** are your best friends — they can go a month without water and still look fine.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Pets at home">
**Spider Plants** and **Cast Iron Plants** are safe for cats and dogs. Avoid snake, pothos, ZZ, peace lily, and jade if your pets nibble.
</Tab>
<Tab title="You want to see progress">
**Pothos** grows fast and visibly, and **Peace Lilies** produce elegant white blooms. Both give satisfying feedback.
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Where to start">
<Callout tone="success" title="My recommendation">
Start with a **Snake Plant** or **Pothos**. Both are nearly impossible to kill, widely available, and affordable. Once you've kept one alive for a few months, you'll have the confidence to expand your collection.
</Callout>
