## Day 1 — Arrival & Shinjuku

<Card title="Day 1 · Sunday, Oct 25" subtitle="Get your bearings in the neon heart of Tokyo">

<Agenda title="Day 1 schedule">
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 15:00 | Check in to your hotel in Shinjuku |
| 17:00 | Walk through **Omoide Yokochō** (Memory Lane) — tiny yakitori alleys |
| 18:30 | Dinner at **Ishigama-yaki Pizzeria** (or a local izakaya) |
| 20:00 | **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** — free observation deck, views of Mt. Fuji on clear days |
| 21:30 | Drinks at **Golden Gai** — 200+ tiny bars, pick one with a friendly sign |
</Agenda>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Jet lag tip">
If you're arriving from a long flight, take it easy. Golden Gai bars close around 23:00–24:00, so you can head back whenever you're tired.
</Callout>

## Day 4 — Day Trip: Nikko or Kamakura

<Card title="Day 4 · Wednesday, Oct 28" subtitle="Choose your adventure — pick one" >

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Option A: Nikko (nature + temples)">
<Agenda title="Nikko day">
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 07:30 | Take the **Tobu Limited Express** from Asakusa (2h, ¥2,900 round trip) |
| 10:00 | **Tōshō-gū Shrine** — ornate UNESCO site, ¥1,300 |
| 12:30 | Lunch at a local soba shop |
| 14:00 | **Kegon Falls** and **Lake Chuzenji** (bus 40 min) |
| 17:30 | Return train to Tokyo |
</Agenda>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Option B: Kamakura (coastal temples)">
<Agenda title="Kamakura day">
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | **JR Yokosuka Line** from Tokyo Station (1h, ¥920 each way) |
| 10:00 | **Kōtoku-in** — the Great Buddha, ¥300 |
| 11:30 | **Hase-dera Temple** — hillside views of the coast |
| 13:00 | Lunch near **Komachi-dori** street |
| 14:30 | **Tsurugaoka Hachimangū** shrine |
| 16:00 | **Enoshima Island** — sunset views (optional) |
| 18:30 | Return to Tokyo |
</Agenda>
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Which to pick?">
- **Nikko**: more impressive shrines, bigger nature, longer train ride.
- **Kamakura**: easier, more relaxed, mix of temples and beach town.
- If you're into hiking, Nikko wins. If you want a lighter day, Kamakura.
</Callout>

## Practical Tips

<Card title="Getting around">
- **Suica / Pasmo card** — tap on/off trains, buses, and vending machines. Get one at any station (¥500 deposit, refundable).
- **Tokyo Metro 24/48/72h pass** — ¥800 / ¥1,200 / ¥1,500, covers most subway lines. Great value if you use it 3+ times a day.
- **Google Maps** works well for transit directions. Trains run from ~5:00 to ~24:00.
</Card>
