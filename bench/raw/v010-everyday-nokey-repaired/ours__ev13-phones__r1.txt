## Choose the iPhone 16 if you…

<Card title="iPhone 16 strengths">
- **Already in the Apple ecosystem** — seamless with your Mac, iPad, Watch, AirPods
- **Want the best video recording** — 4K Dolby Vision, excellent stabilization
- **Prefer iOS** — smooth, polished, long-term support
- **Care about resale value** — iPhones hold value longer
- **Want a compact feel** — lighter and slightly smaller

## Choose the Pixel 9 if you…

<Card title="Pixel 9 strengths">
- **Want the best camera for still photos** — Google's computational photography is unmatched
- **Love AI features** — Gemini assistant, Magic Editor, Call Screen
- **Prefer stock Android** — clean, fast, no bloatware
- **Want a smoother display** — 120Hz refresh rate
- **Plan to keep it longer** — 7 years of updates vs. 5 on iPhone

Both phones take stunning photos. The **Pixel 9** wins on:

## Which should you buy?

<Card title="My recommendation">
<Callout tone="success" title="Pixel 9 wins for most people">
The Pixel 9 offers a smoother display, better still photos, longer software support, and more capable AI — all at the same price. Unless you're deeply invested in the Apple ecosystem, the Pixel 9 is the better value.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="info" title="iPhone 16 wins if...">
You already own Apple devices, shoot lots of video, or prefer iOS. The iPhone 16 is also the better choice if you want a lighter phone or plan to resell it later.
</Callout>
