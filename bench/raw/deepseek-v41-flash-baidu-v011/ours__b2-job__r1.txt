<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to own and evolve our design system — the shared language of components, tokens, and patterns used by every product team at the company. You'll partner closely with engineers and product designers to ship a system that's beautiful, accessible, and genuinely used.

This is a hands-on role: you'll design components, write usage guidance, run design reviews, and champion adoption across the org. You'll have real influence over how our products look and feel at scale.
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
<Columns>
<Card subtitle="Must-have">
- 5+ years designing digital products, with 2+ years on design systems
- Deep fluency in Figma: components, variants, auto-layout, tokens
- Strong grasp of accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) and inclusive design
- Experience partnering with front-end engineers on component APIs
- A portfolio showing systems thinking, not just screens
</Card>
<Card subtitle="Nice-to-have">
- Familiarity with design tokens (Style Dictionary, Tokens Studio)
- Experience with Storybook or similar documentation tooling
- Background in motion or interaction design
- Comfortable facilitating workshops and design critiques
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
