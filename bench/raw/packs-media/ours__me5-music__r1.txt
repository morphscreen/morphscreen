<Card title="Why these tracks">
<Callout tone="info" title="Designed for cognitive load">
These playlists avoid lyrics, sudden tempo changes, and emotional peaks that pull attention. Instead they use:
</Callout>

- **Consistent BPM** (60–80) to match a calm heart rate
- **Minimal harmonic surprise** — predictable progressions reduce prediction error
- **No vocals** — language processing competes with verbal working memory
- **Long tracks / seamless transitions** — fewer boundaries mean fewer micro-interruptions
- **Low spectral flux** — steady energy across the frequency spectrum

<Callout tone="success" title="Research backing">
Studies show that moderate-arousal, low-complexity music can improve performance on repetitive or creative tasks by masking background noise without taxing executive control (e.g., *J. Exp. Psychol. Appl.*, 2019).
</Callout>
</Card>
