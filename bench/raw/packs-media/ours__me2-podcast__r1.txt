## EP 1247: AI Regulation Hits Congress, Apple's Foldable Leaks, and the Death of Cookies

### Top Stories

**1. Congress Introduces Comprehensive AI Regulation Bill**
The bipartisan **AI Accountability Act** was introduced yesterday, requiring companies developing frontier models to undergo third-party safety audits before deployment. Key provisions include mandatory incident reporting, compute thresholds for covered models, and a new federal oversight board. Tech giants have already begun lobbying—Microsoft and Google support the framework; Meta and xAI oppose the compute thresholds.

**2. Apple's First Foldable iPhone Leaked in Supply Chain Reports**
Multiple analysts confirm Apple has ordered foldable display samples from Samsung Display and LG Display for a **2026 launch**. The device reportedly folds inward like a book, uses a 7.8" inner display, and runs a modified iPadOS. No crease visible in current prototypes. Price target: $1,799.

**3. Google Finally Kills Third-Party Cookies in Chrome**
After four years of delays, Chrome 132 begins rolling out **Tracking Protection** to 1% of users globally, blocking third-party cookies by default. The Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics, Protected Audience, Attribution Reporting) are now the only approved paths for cross-site advertising. Ad tech stocks dipped 3–5% on the news.
