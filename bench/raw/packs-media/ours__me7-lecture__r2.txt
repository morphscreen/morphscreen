## Slides' Main Points

<Card title="Key Concepts Covered" subtitle="Slide deck summary">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Foundations">
- **Definition**: Machine Learning = algorithms that improve automatically through experience
- **Three paradigms**: Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning
- **Core terminology**: Features, labels, training set, test set, model, hypothesis
- **Bias-variance tradeoff**: Underfitting vs overfitting
</Tab>
<Tab title="Supervised Learning">
- **Classification**: Predicting discrete categories (spam detection, medical diagnosis)
- **Regression**: Predicting continuous values (house prices, temperature)
- **Key algorithms**: Linear/Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, SVM, k-NN
- **Evaluation metrics**: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, MSE, MAE
</Tab>
<Tab title="Unsupervised Learning">
- **Clustering**: Grouping similar data points (k-means, hierarchical, DBSCAN)
- **Dimensionality Reduction**: PCA, t-SNE, UMAP for visualization & compression
- **Anomaly Detection**: Identifying outliers (Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM)
- **Association Rules**: Market basket analysis (Apriori algorithm)
</Tab>
<Tab title="Practical Workflow">
- **Data preprocessing**: Cleaning, scaling, encoding, feature engineering
- **Model selection**: Cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning (Grid/Random Search)
- **Deployment considerations**: Monitoring, drift detection, A/B testing
- **Ethics & bias**: Fairness, transparency, privacy, responsible AI
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>
