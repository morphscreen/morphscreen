<Card title="Slides — Main Points">
<Details title="Module 1: Foundations" open="true">
- Definition and scope of machine learning
- Supervised vs. unsupervised vs. reinforcement learning
- Key terminology: features, labels, training/test sets, overfitting
- Bias-variance tradeoff
</Details>
<Details title="Module 2: Linear Models">
- Linear regression with gradient descent
- Logistic regression for classification
- Regularization: L1 (Lasso) and L2 (Ridge)
- Model evaluation metrics: MSE, accuracy, precision, recall, F1
</Details>
<Details title="Module 3: Decision Trees & Ensembles">
- Decision tree construction (entropy, Gini impurity)
- Random forests and bagging
- Gradient boosting (XGBoost, LightGBM)
- Feature importance and interpretability
</Details>
<Details title="Module 4: Neural Networks Basics">
- Perceptrons and multi-layer perceptrons
- Activation functions: ReLU, sigmoid, tanh
- Backpropagation and chain rule
- Common architectures: CNNs, RNNs, Transformers (intro)
</Details>
<Details title="Module 5: Practical Workflow">
- Data preprocessing and feature engineering
- Cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning
- Model deployment considerations
- Ethics, bias, and fairness in ML
</Details>
