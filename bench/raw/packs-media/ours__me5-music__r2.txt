<Card title="Why these tracks">
<Columns>
<Details title="Minimal lyrics" open="true">
Lyrics engage language centers and pull attention away from verbal tasks. These tracks are instrumental or use vocals as texture only.
</Details>
<Details title="Steady, predictable rhythm" open="false">
A consistent tempo (60–80 BPM) helps entrain brainwaves to an alpha state without surprising changes that break flow.
</Details>
<Details title="Low dynamic range" open="false">
Volume and intensity stay even — no sudden drops, builds, or climaxes that demand orientation.
</Details>
<Details title="Familiar but novel" open="false">
Recurring motifs give a sense of predictability; subtle variation prevents habituation and mind-wandering.
</Details>
</Columns>
</Card>
