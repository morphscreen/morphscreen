<Card title="Ingredients">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226g) |
| Granulated sugar | ¾ cup (150g) |
| Light brown sugar, packed | ¾ cup (165g) |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| All-purpose flour | 2¼ cups (280g) |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Semi-sweet chocolate chips | 2 cups (340g) |
</Card>

<Card title="Directions">
<Steps>
<Step title="Preheat & prep" status="done">Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.</Step>
<Step title="Cream butter & sugars" status="done">In a large bowl, beat softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.</Step>
<Step title="Add eggs & vanilla" status="done">Beat in eggs one at a time, then mix in vanilla extract until combined.</Step>
<Step title="Mix dry ingredients" status="done">In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.</Step>
<Step title="Combine" status="done">Gradually add dry mixture to wet mixture, mixing on low speed just until incorporated. Do not overmix.</Step>
<Step title="Fold in chocolate chips" status="done">Fold in chocolate chips with a spatula until evenly distributed.</Step>
<Step title="Scoop dough" status="current">Using a 2-tbsp cookie scoop, place dough balls 2 inches apart on prepared sheets.</Step>
<Step title="Bake" status="todo">Bake 9–11 minutes, until edges are golden but centers still look slightly underbaked.</Step>
<Step title="Cool" status="todo">Cool on baking sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Baker's Tips">
• Chill dough 30 min for thicker cookies  
• Use a mix of milk and dark chocolate for depth  
• Sprinkle flaky sea salt on top before baking  
• Store in airtight container up to 5 days
</Callout>
