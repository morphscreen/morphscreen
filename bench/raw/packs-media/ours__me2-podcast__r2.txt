**Episode 1,247** · January 15, 2024 · 14:32

The EU AI Act officially takes effect today, setting the world's first comprehensive regulatory framework for artificial intelligence. We break down what this means for developers, startups, and enterprise teams — plus the compliance timeline you need to know.

**Guests:** Sarah Chen (Tech Policy Lead, Mozilla) · Marcus Webb (Founder, ComplianceAI)

**Topics covered:**
- Risk tiers explained: prohibited, high-risk, limited, minimal
- What "general purpose AI" obligations mean for LLM providers
- The 6-24 month compliance windows
- Impact on open-source models and fine-tuning
- Enforcement mechanisms and potential fines (up to 7% global revenue)
