# Classic Chicken Parmesan

Crispy breaded chicken cutlets topped with marinara sauce and melted mozzarella. A family favorite that's easier than you think.

<Card title="Ingredients">
### For the Chicken
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Boneless, skinless chicken breasts | 4 (about 1.5 lbs) |
| Salt and black pepper | To taste |
| All-purpose flour | 1 cup |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Panko breadcrumbs | 1.5 cups |
| Grated Parmesan cheese | ½ cup |
| Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil (for frying) | ½ cup |

### For the Topping
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Marinara sauce | 2 cups |
| Fresh mozzarella, sliced | 8 oz |
| Grated Parmesan cheese | ¼ cup |
| Fresh basil leaves | For garnish |
</Card>

<Card title="Instructions">
<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare the chicken" status="done">
Place chicken breasts between plastic wrap and pound to ½-inch thickness. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
</Step>
<Step title="Set up the dredging station" status="done">
Place flour in one shallow dish. Beat eggs in a second dish. In a third dish, combine panko, ½ cup Parmesan, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder.
</Step>
<Step title="Bread the chicken" status="done">
Dredge each cutlet in flour, shaking off excess. Dip in egg, then press into breadcrumb mixture to coat both sides. Place on a wire rack.
</Step>
<Step title="Pan-fry" status="done">
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry cutlets 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
</Step>
<Step title="Assemble and bake" status="current">
Preheat oven to 425°F. Spread ½ cup marinara in a baking dish. Arrange cutlets, top each with remaining sauce, mozzarella slices, and ¼ cup Parmesan. Bake 10-12 minutes until cheese is bubbling and golden.
</Step>
<Step title="Rest and serve" status="todo">
Let rest 5 minutes. Garnish with fresh basil. Serve over pasta or with crusty bread.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="success" title="Pro Tip">
For extra crispy chicken, use a wire rack on a baking sheet instead of paper towels after frying. This prevents steam from making the crust soggy.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="info" title="Make Ahead">
Bread the cutlets up to 4 hours ahead. Keep on a wire rack in the refrigerator, uncovered, until ready to fry.
</Callout>
