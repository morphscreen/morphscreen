<Step title="12:00 — Lunch at Ponto Final (Almada)" status="todo">Take the ferry across the river for grilled fish with a view of Lisbon.</Step>
<Step title="14:00 — Tram 28 to Alfama" status="todo">Ride the classic yellow tram up the steep hills to the old quarter.</Step>
<Step title="14:30 — São Jorge Castle" status="todo">Explore the ramparts and archaeological site. Best city panorama.</Step>
<Step title="16:30 — Miradouro de Santa Luzia" status="todo">Sunset drinks at this tiled viewpoint over Alfama rooftops.</Step>
<Step title="18:30 — Dinner in Bairro Alto" status="todo">Tapas at **Taberna da Rua das Flores** (book ahead) or **Petiscaria Ideal**.</Step>
<Step title="20:30 — Pink Street & Rooftop Bars" status="todo">Rua Nova do Carvalho → **Park Bar** (rooftop) or **Foxtrot** (speakeasy).</Step>

<Card title="Day 2 — Sunday" subtitle="Baixa → Chiado → LX Factory">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Brunch at Nicolau Lisboa" status="todo">Acai bowls, great coffee, bright space in Baixa.</Step>
<Step title="10:30 — Praça do Comércio & Rua Augusta Arch" status="todo">Grand riverside square, climb the arch for 360° views.</Step>
<Step title="11:30 — Elevador de Santa Justa" status="todo">Neo-Gothic lift to Carmo Convent ruins (skip the line: walk up from Carmo).</Step>
<Step title="12:30 — Time Out Market" status="todo">Food hall with 30+ stalls — try **Marisqueira Azul** (seafood) or **Croquetaria**.</Step>
<Step title="14:00 — Tram 15 to Belém (round 2) or LX Factory" status="todo">**Option A:** Revisit Belém for MAAT museum. **Option B:** Creative hub LX Factory — shops, street art, **Ler Devagar** bookshop.</Step>
<Step title="16:30 — Sunset at Miradouro de Santa Catarina" status="todo">"Adamastor" viewpoint — locals, street music, Tagus sunset.</Step>
<Step title="18:30 — Farewell Dinner at Taberna do Calhau" status="todo">Modern Portuguese tasting menu in a 17th-century warehouse (reserve).</Step>

<Callout tone="info" title="Money-saving tips">
- Buy **Lisboa Card (24h €21 / 48h €35)** if you hit 3+ paid sites — covers transit + free entry to Jerónimos, Tower, Castle, Santa Justa, MAAT.
- Eat **prato do dia** (dish of the day) at lunch for €8–12 instead of dinner prices.
- Walk instead of tram 28 when possible — it's often packed; the walk from Baixa to Alfama is 20 min uphill.
- Book **Pastéis de Belém**, **Taberna da Rua das Flores**, and **Taberna do Calhau** 2–3 weeks ahead.
