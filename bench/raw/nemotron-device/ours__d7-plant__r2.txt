### 2. Likely problems
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Fungal Issues">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Powdery Mildew">White powdery spots on leaves, common in humid conditions with poor air circulation</Callout>
<Callout tone="warning" title="Root Rot">Yellowing leaves, mushy roots, foul smell from overwatering or poor drainage</Callout>
<Callout tone="warning" title="Leaf Spot">Brown/black spots with yellow halos, spreads in wet conditions</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Pest Problems">
<Callout tone="danger" title="Spider Mites">Tiny webs, stippled leaves, common in dry indoor air</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Aphids">Clusters of small green/black insects on new growth, sticky honeydew residue</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Mealybugs">White cottony masses in leaf axils and stems</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Nutrient Deficiencies">
<Callout tone="info" title="Nitrogen Deficiency">Older leaves turn pale yellow, stunted growth</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Iron Deficiency">Yellowing between veins on new leaves (interveinal chlorosis)</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Magnesium Deficiency">Yellowing between veins on older leaves, leaf curling</Callout>
</Tab>
</Tabs>

### 3. Care steps
<Steps>
<Step title="Isolate the plant" status="todo">Move affected plant away from healthy plants to prevent spread</Step>
<Step title="Remove affected parts" status="todo">Prune diseased leaves/stems with sterilized scissors, dispose in trash (not compost)</Step>
<Step title="Treat the issue" status="todo">
<Details title="Treatment options" open="false">
- **Fungal**: Neem oil, copper fungicide, or baking soda spray (1 tsp baking soda + 1 tsp mild soap per liter water)
- **Pests**: Insecticidal soap, neem oil, or rubbing alcohol on cotton swab for mealybugs
- **Nutrients**: Balanced fertilizer, chelated iron, or Epsom salt (1 tbsp per gallon) for magnesium
</Details>
</Step>
<Step title="Adjust environment" status="todo">Improve air circulation, adjust watering, check light levels, repot if root-bound</Step>
<Step title="Monitor & follow up" status="todo">Check daily for 2 weeks, reapply treatment per product instructions, watch for new growth</Step>
</Steps>
