<Card title="Day-by-Day Schedule">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 — Alfama & Baixa">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Start at São Jorge Castle" status="todo">Walk up through Alfama's narrow lanes. Buy tickets online to skip the line.</Step>
<Step title="11:00 — Sé de Lisboa" status="todo">Short walk downhill. Gothic cloister, archaeological site.</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Miradouro de Santa Luzia" status="todo">Coffee at the café, azulejo panels, river views.</Step>
<Step title="13:00 — Lunch in Alfama" status="todo">Try *Bacalhau à Brás* at a tasca nearby.</Step>
<Step title="15:00 — Walk down to Praça do Comércio" status="todo">Via Rua da Madalena / Rua da Prata. Arch at Rua Augusta.</Step>
<Step title="17:00 — Riverside stroll to Cais do Sodré" status="todo">Optional: sunset drink at a rooftop bar.</Step>
<Step title="19:30 — Dinner at Time Out Market" status="todo">Multiple vendors, book a seat early.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2 — Belém">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Tram 15 to Belém" status="todo">Buy 24h Carris/Metro pass (€6.80).</Step>
<Step title="09:45 — Mosteiro dos Jerónimos" status="todo">Pre-book 10:00 slot. Cloisters are the highlight.</Step>
<Step title="11:30 — Pastéis de Belém" status="todo">Eat 2 warm tarts with cinnamon & powdered sugar.</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Torre de Belém" status="todo">Walk along the river. Pre-book entry if you want to climb.</Step>
<Step title="13:30 — Lunch at LX Factory" status="todo">Tram 15 back, get off at Santo Amaro. Creative campus, many restaurants.</Step>
<Step title="16:00 — Explore LX Factory" status="todo">Ler Devagar bookshop, street art, shops.</Step>
<Step title="19:00 — Return to center, casual dinner" status="todo">Bairro Alto or Cais do Sodré.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 3 — Baixa, Chiado & Departure">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Elevador de Santa Justa" status="todo">Buy ticket at machine (€5.30 round-trip) or use 24h pass.</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — Carmo Convent ruins" status="todo">Right at the top of the lift.</Step>
<Step title="11:00 — Chiado shopping & coffee" status="todo">A Brasileira, Bertrand Bookshop (oldest in world).</Step>
<Step title="12:30 — Rossio Square & Ginjinha" status="todo">Try the cherry liqueur at a hole-in-the-wall bar.</Step>
<Step title="13:00 — Lunch at Confeitaria Nacional" status="todo">Historic pastry shop, savory options too.</Step>
<Step title="14:30 — Last walk / souvenirs / head to airport" status="todo">Aerobus from Cais do Sodré (€4, 20 min).</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
