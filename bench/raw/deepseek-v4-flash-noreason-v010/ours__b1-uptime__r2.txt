**Incident:** Intermittent 502 errors on `GET /v2/orders`

**Timeline:**
- Started: Tuesday, 14:32 UTC
- Root cause: Degraded connection pool in the payments service
- Resolved: Tuesday, 15:07 UTC

**Impact:** ~2% of API requests failed during the 35-minute window. No data loss occurred.

<Details title="Full postmortem">
During a routine deployment of the payments service, the connection pool was misconfigured, causing the service to exhaust available database connections. This led to intermittent 502 responses for requests routed to that service. The deployment was rolled back and the pool size was corrected. A follow-up fix to the deployment pipeline will validate connection pool settings automatically before rollout.
</Details>
