**Design Systems** · Full-time · Remote (US/EU)

**Core qualifications**

- 5+ years of product design experience, with 2+ years focused on design systems
- Expert-level Figma skills, including variables, components, and auto-layout
- Strong understanding of accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and inclusive design
- Experience documenting and evangelizing systems across teams
- Portfolio showing shipped design system work

</div>

**Nice to have**

- Motion design skills
- Open-source contribution history
