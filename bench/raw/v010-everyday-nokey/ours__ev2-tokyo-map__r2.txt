# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two — A Complete Itinerary

<Callout tone="info" title="Trip at a glance">
This plan balances iconic sights, food, and quiet escapes. It's designed for two people, with easy train access between stops. Day 1 and 5 are lighter to ease into the city and travel out.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day-by-day plan">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 — Arrival & Shibuya">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Arrive & settle" status="done">Check in at your Shinjuku hotel. Grab a konbini breakfast.</Step>
<Step title="Late morning: Meiji Shrine" status="current">A calm forest shrine right off Harajuku station. Walk the gravel path, see the torii gates.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing" status="todo">The famous scramble. Watch from the Starbucks second floor, then explore Shibuya 109 and the backstreets.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Dinner in Shinjuku" status="todo">Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) — tiny yakitori bars. Or a casual ramen spot.</Step>
</Steps>
<Callout tone="success" title="Budget tip">Day 1 is free except food and transport. Meiji Shrine has no entry fee.</Callout>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2 — Art & Food">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: TeamLab Planets" status="todo">Book tickets in advance (¥3,200 each). Water-based immersive art — go barefoot through the installations.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="todo">Fresh sushi, tamago skewers, grilled scallops. Go early to beat crowds.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ginza & Tokyo Station" status="todo">Window shop in Ginza, see the Tokyo Station brick facade.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Rooftop views" status="todo">Head to the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observatory — free, 45th floor.</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Book TeamLab Planets tickets for day 2">Book TeamLab tickets</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3 — Old Tokyo">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="todo">Tokyo's oldest temple. Walk Nakamise-dori for snacks and souvenirs.</Step>
<Step title="Midday: Ueno Park & Museums" status="todo">Stroll the park, visit the Tokyo National Museum (¥1,000) if you like history.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Akihabara" status="todo">Electric town — arcades, anime shops, gachapon. Even if you're not into anime, it's a spectacle.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Monjayaki in Tsukishima" status="todo">Cook your own savory pancake at a local monjayaki restaurant.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 4 — Ghibli & Gardens">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Ghibli Museum" status="todo">In Mitaka, 20 min west. Book tickets a month ahead — they sell out. No photos inside.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Kichijoji" status="todo">A relaxed neighborhood next to the museum. Try the famous satou beef croquettes.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shinjuku Gyoen" status="todo">A beautiful national garden — perfect for a slow walk. Entry ¥500.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai" status="todo">Tiny bars in Shinjuku, each seating 5-8 people. Great for a drink and people-watching.</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="How do I book Ghibli Museum tickets?">Help me book Ghibli</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 5 — Harajuku & Departure">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Harajuku" status="todo">Takeshita Street — crepes, quirky fashion, and the backstreets for vintage shops.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Ramen in Harajuku" status="todo">Try a trendy spot like Afuri for yuzu shio ramen.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Last-minute shopping" status="todo">Pick up souvenirs at Don Quijote in Shinjuku — tax-free with passport.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Depart" status="todo">Head to Narita or Haneda depending on your flight. Leave 2-3 hours for the trip.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Practical tips">
<Checklist title="Before you go" name="prep-list">
- [ ] Book Ghibli Museum tickets (1 month ahead)
- [ ] Book TeamLab Planets tickets (1 week ahead)
- [ ] Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card (works on all trains)
- [ ] Download Google Maps offline for Tokyo
- [ ] Check if you need a JR Pass (only if traveling beyond Tokyo)
- [ ] Bring cash — many small shops and restaurants don't take cards
</Checklist>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Train etiquette">
Don't talk on the phone on trains. Eat only at designated spots. Queue at the marked lines on platforms.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Where to stay">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Shinjuku (recommended)">
Best for first-timers — endless dining, nightlife, and the main train hub. Expect ¥12,000–¥25,000/night for two.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Shibuya">
Trendy and central, close to the crossing and Harajuku. Slightly more expensive, louder at night.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Ueno">
Budget-friendly, near the park and museums. Quieter, and a direct train to Narita Airport.
</Tab>
</Tabs>
