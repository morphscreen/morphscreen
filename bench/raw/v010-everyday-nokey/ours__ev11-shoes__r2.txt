<Checklist title="Fit checklist" name="fit-checks">
- [ ] Half a thumb's width of space between your longest toe and the shoe's end
- [ ] Heel doesn't slip when you walk — lace snugly, not tightly
- [ ] No pinching at the widest part of your foot
- [ ] Toe box feels roomy enough to wiggle your toes
- [ ] Try them on in the evening (feet swell during the day)
</Checklist>
</Card>
