## The 10 Phrases

<Card title="Learn Them in Order" subtitle="From most to least essential">

| # | English | Spanish | When to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello / Hi | **Hola** | Greeting anyone, anytime |
| 2 | Please | **Por favor** | Adding politeness to any request |
| 3 | Thank you | **Gracias** | Showing appreciation |
| 4 | Yes / No | **Sí / No** | Confirming or declining |
| 5 | Excuse me / Sorry | **Perdón** | Getting attention, apologizing |
| 6 | Do you speak English? | **¿Habla inglés?** | Finding common ground |
| 7 | How much does it cost? | **¿Cuánto cuesta?** | Shopping, taxis, markets |
| 8 | Where is the bathroom? | **¿Dónde está el baño?** | Urgent needs! |
| 9 | Help! | **¡Ayuda!** | Emergencies |
| 10 | Goodbye | **Adiós** | Ending conversations politely |

</Card>

</Flashcards>

## Quick Pronunciation Tips

<Card title="Sound More Natural">
<Steps>
<Step title="Roll your R's" status="done">The single R in "por favor" is a quick tap, not a roll. Practice: "pero" (but) vs "perro" (dog).</Step>
<Step title="Stress the right syllable" status="done">Words ending in a vowel, N, or S stress the second-to-last syllable: "GRAH-see-ahs", "KWAHN-toh".</Step>
<Step title="The 'll' sounds like 'y'" status="done">"Baño" has a soft "ny" sound, like in "canyon". "Ll" in "llamo" sounds like "y" in "yes".</Step>
<Step title="Question intonation rises" status="done">In Spanish, questions rise in pitch at the end: "¿Habla inglés?" goes up like "ing-LÉS?"</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Put Them Together

<Card title="Real-Life Combinations" subtitle="These phrases work even better together">

| Situation | What to say |
|---|---|
| Ordering food | "Hola, **por favor**…" + point at menu |
| Buying something | "**¿Cuánto cuesta?**" + "**Gracias**" after |
| Lost | "**Perdón**, **¿habla inglés?**" |
| Emergency | "**¡Ayuda!**" (loud and clear) |
| Leaving | "**Gracias**, **adiós**!" |

</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pro tip">Even if your pronunciation isn't perfect, Spanish speakers appreciate the effort. A smile and "por favor" go a long way!</Callout>
