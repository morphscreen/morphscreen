# The Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system has **eight planets**, split into two groups: the four rocky **inner planets** and the four gas/ice **giant outer planets**. Here's a tour from the Sun outward.

<Card title="How far apart are they?">
The distances between planets are enormous. If the Sun were a basketball in New York, Earth would be a peppercorn 26 meters away — and Neptune would be a marble 780 meters away.

<Card title="Did you know?">
<Callout tone="info" title="Pluto is not a planet">
Pluto was reclassified as a **dwarf planet** in 2006 because it hasn't cleared its orbit of debris. The solar system now has 5 recognized dwarf planets: Pluto, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="success" title="A day on Venus is longer than its year">
Venus takes 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun. So a single Venusian day lasts longer than its entire year.
</Callout>
</Card>
