# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is a hiker's paradise, with world-class trails within an hour's drive of downtown. Here are the top picks, from easy coastal strolls to challenging summit pushes.

<Callout tone="info" title="Trail conditions">Always check weather and trail conditions before heading out. Many trails have seasonal closures or require bear spray. The BC Parks website and local trail reports are your best sources.</Callout>

<Card title="1. Stawamus Chief (The Chief)" subtitle="Squamish, 45 min from Vancouver">
<Columns>
<div>

**The classic summit hike.** Three granite peaks with incredible views over Howe Sound. The second peak offers the best panoramic vista, while the third is quieter and wilder.

- **Distance:** 11 km round trip (Peak 2)
- **Elevation gain:** 700 m
- **Time:** 5-7 hours
- **Difficulty:** Hard — steep, with chains and ladders on some sections
- **Best time:** May–October, early morning to avoid crowds

</div>
<div>

<KeyValue title="Quick facts">
| | |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Stawamus Chief Provincial Park, Squamish |
| Parking | Large lot, $7/day (arrive by 8am on weekends) |
| Facilities | Washrooms, picnic tables |
| Dog friendly | Yes, on leash |
| Shade | Mostly exposed |
</KeyValue>

</div>
</Columns>

<Details title="Route options">
- **Peak 1:** Shortest (3.5 km round trip), great views, very crowded
- **Peak 2:** Most popular, best views, requires some scrambling
- **Peak 3:** Longest (5.5 km round trip), quietest, more forested
</Details>

<Card title="2. Garibaldi Lake" subtitle="Garibaldi Provincial Park, 1.5 hrs from Vancouver">
<Columns>
<div>

**The turquoise jewel of the Coast Mountains.** A steady, well-graded climb leads to one of the most stunning alpine lakes in British Columbia. The glacial-fed water is an unreal shade of blue-green.

- **Distance:** 18 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 810 m
- **Time:** 6-8 hours
- **Difficulty:** Moderate — long but not technical
- **Best time:** July–October (trail is snow-free)

</div>
<div>

<KeyValue title="Quick facts">
| | |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Rubble Creek Lot, off Hwy 99 |
| Parking | Large lot, free (fills by 8-9am on weekends) |
| Facilities | Outhouses at lake, camping nearby |
| Dog friendly | No |
| Shade | Mostly forested lower half |
</KeyValue>

</div>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Day-use permit required">Since 2023, a free day-use permit is required for Garibaldi Lake. Reserve online up to 48 hours in advance. Camping requires a paid reservation.</Callout>

<Card title="3. Joffre Lakes" subtitle="Pemberton, 2 hrs from Vancouver">
<Columns>
<div>

**Three glacier-fed lakes, each more stunning than the last.** The hike is short but steep, and the payoff is immense — you'll pass three lakes ranging from milky blue to deep turquoise, with glaciers looming overhead.

- **Distance:** 10 km round trip (to Upper Lake)
- **Elevation gain:** 370 m
- **Time:** 4-5 hours
- **Difficulty:** Moderate — steep but manageable
- **Best time:** July–October

</div>
<div>

<KeyValue title="Quick facts">
| | |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Joffre Lakes Parking Lot, off Hwy 99 |
| Parking | Large lot, free (fills by 7-8am on weekends) |
| Facilities | Outhouses, picnic areas |
| Dog friendly | No |
| Shade | Mixed forest and open areas |
</KeyValue>

</div>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Permit required">Joffre Lakes now requires a free day-use permit, available online 48 hours in advance. Weekend slots fill fast — set a reminder for the release time.</Callout>

<Card title="4. Quarry Rock" subtitle="Deep Cove, 30 min from Vancouver">
<Columns>
<div>

**The perfect quick escape.** A short, family-friendly hike through lush forest leads to a rocky viewpoint overlooking Deep Cove and Indian Arm. It's the easiest hike on this list, making it ideal for beginners or when you're short on time.

- **Distance:** 3.8 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 120 m
- **Time:** 2-2.5 hours
- **Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate — some stairs and roots
- **Best time:** Year-round (snow-free most winters)

</div>
<div>

<KeyValue title="Quick facts">
| | |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Panorama Drive, Deep Cove |
| Parking | Limited street parking (arrive early) |
| Facilities | Washrooms at trailhead |
| Dog friendly | Yes, on leash |
| Shade | Mostly forested |
</KeyValue>

</div>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="success" title="Combine with a swim">After the hike, reward yourself with a swim at Deep Cove's beach or a famous Honey Doughnut from the local bakery.</Callout>

<Card title="5. Bowen Lookout" subtitle="Cypress Provincial Park, 30 min from Vancouver">
<Columns>
<div>

**Sunset views without the summit push.** This trail climbs through forest and subalpine meadows to a viewpoint overlooking Howe Sound, Bowen Island, and the Gulf Islands. It's a shorter alternative to the nearby (and much harder) St. Marks Summit.

- **Distance:** 10 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 530 m
- **Time:** 3-4 hours
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Best time:** June–October (snow-free)

</div>
<div>

<KeyValue title="Quick facts">
| | |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Cypress Mountain Ski Resort lot |
| Parking | Free at the main lot |
| Facilities | Washrooms at the resort |
| Dog friendly | Yes, on leash |
| Shade | Mixed forest and open ridges |
</KeyValue>

</div>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="info" title="Best for sunset">This is the go-to sunset hike near Vancouver. The viewpoint faces west, so you get golden-hour light over the ocean. Bring a headlamp for the descent.</Callout>

<Card title="My recommendation for first-timers">
If you're visiting Vancouver and can only do one hike, make it **Garibaldi Lake**. It's the most iconic British Columbia experience — a manageable climb through old-growth forest that opens up to a turquoise lake framed by snow-capped peaks. Start early, bring a picnic, and spend an hour just sitting by the water.
