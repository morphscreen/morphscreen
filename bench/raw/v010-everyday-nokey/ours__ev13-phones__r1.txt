## Which one suits you? Pick your priority

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Camera & photos">
<Callout tone="success" title="Pixel wins for stills">The Pixel 9's computational photography is unmatched — Night Sight, Magic Eraser, and Best Take are genuinely magical.</Callout>

**Pixel 9 strengths:**
- Better low-light photos with less noise
- Magic Eraser removes unwanted objects flawlessly
- 48MP ultrawide is sharper than iPhone's 12MP
- Astrophotography mode for night skies

**iPhone 16 strengths:**
- More natural, true-to-life colors
- Better video stabilization (Action Mode)
- Spatial video for Vision Pro
- ProRes support for creators

**Pixel 9 wins clearly:**

- Larger battery means you'll rarely hit 20% by evening
- Reverse wireless charging for your earbuds

**iPhone 16 note:** MagSafe is more convenient if you have Apple chargers everywhere, but the battery life is average.
