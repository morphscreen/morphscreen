# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Callout tone="info" title="Why these 10?">
These are the phrases you'll actually use daily — greeting, ordering, asking for help, and being polite. Master these and you'll get by in any Spanish-speaking country.
</Callout>

<Card title="The 10 phrases">
| # | English | Spanish | When to use it |
|---|---------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Hello / Hi | **Hola** (OH-lah) | Any time, any place |
| 2 | Please | **Por favor** (por fah-VOR) | Adding politeness to any request |
| 3 | Thank you | **Gracias** (GRAH-see-as) | After someone helps you |
| 4 | Yes / No | **Sí / No** (see / noh) | Simple answers |
| 5 | Excuse me / Sorry | **Perdón** (per-DOHN) | Getting attention, apologizing |
| 6 | How much does it cost? | **¿Cuánto cuesta?** (KWAN-toh KWES-tah) | Shopping, markets, taxis |
| 7 | Where is the bathroom? | **¿Dónde está el baño?** (DON-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyo) | Essential! |
| 8 | I don't understand | **No entiendo** (noh en-tee-EN-doh) | When you need help |
| 9 | Do you speak English? | **¿Habla inglés?** (AH-blah een-GLES) | Finding someone who can help |
| 10 | The bill, please | **La cuenta, por favor** (lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR) | At restaurants and cafés |
</Card>

## Pronunciation Tips

<Card title="Sound it out">
- **H is always silent** — "hola" sounds like "OH-lah", not "HOH-lah"
- **LL sounds like Y** — "baño" has a soft "ny" sound, like in "canyon"
- **Stress the capitalized syllable** — put emphasis where the guide shows it in caps
- **The "r" is rolled lightly** — don't worry about perfecting this; native speakers will understand you anyway
</Card>

## Make It Stick

<Card title="Your practice plan">
<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1: Learn 5 phrases" status="done" subtitle="Start with Hola, Por favor, Gracias, Sí/No, Perdón">
Say each one out loud 5 times. Write them on sticky notes around your home.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 2: Add 5 more" status="current" subtitle="Learn the questions and requests">
Practice the full set with the flashcards above. Cover the Spanish and try to recall it.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 3: Use them in context" subtitle="Role-play common situations">
Pretend you're ordering at a café: "La cuenta, por favor." Ask "¿Dónde está el baño?" at a restaurant.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 4: Test yourself" subtitle="Take the quiz again">
Aim for all correct. Then practice with a friend or language app.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
