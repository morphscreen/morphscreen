# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which Should You Get?

Both are excellent phones, but they're built for different kinds of people. Here's the breakdown to help you decide.

<Card title="At a glance">
<Compare title="iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9">
| Feature | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 6.1" OLED, 60Hz | 6.3" OLED, 120Hz |
| Chip | A18 | Tensor G4 |
| Camera | 48MP main + 12MP ultrawide | 50MP main + 48MP ultrawide |
| Battery life | ~22h video playback | ~24h mixed use |
| Charging | 25W wired, 15W MagSafe | 27W wired, 15W wireless |
| AI features | Apple Intelligence (coming) | Gemini AI (built-in) |
| Software updates | 5+ years | 7 years |
| Price | $799 | $799 |
</Compare>
</Card>

<Columns>
<Card title="Choose iPhone 16 if...">
- You're already in the Apple ecosystem (AirPods, Mac, iPad)
- You prefer iOS simplicity and polish
- You want the best video recording on a phone
- You like MagSafe accessories
- You want a more compact, lighter phone (170g vs 199g)
</Card>

<Card title="Choose Pixel 9 if...">
- You want the smoothest display (120Hz vs 60Hz)
- You care about AI features like Call Screening and Magic Editor
- You take lots of photos — Pixel's processing is still magic
- You want longer software support (7 years vs 5)
- You prefer stock Android with no bloatware
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="info" title="Camera note">
The Pixel 9 has a better ultrawide sensor (48MP vs 12MP) and generally produces more "punchy" photos. The iPhone 16 is better for video — it shoots 4K Dolby Vision at up to 60fps.
</Callout>

<Card title="Which should you pick?">
<Steps>
<Step title="Are you in the Apple ecosystem?" status="current" subtitle="iMessage, AirDrop, Apple Watch, Mac">
If yes, the iPhone 16 is the obvious choice — it just works with your other devices. If no, keep going.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you take more photos or videos?" status="todo" subtitle="Photos → Pixel, Videos → iPhone">
For still photography, the Pixel 9 is the better camera phone. For video, the iPhone 16 wins hands-down.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you care about AI features?" status="todo" subtitle="Gemini vs Apple Intelligence">
Pixel's AI is here today (Call Screening, Magic Eraser, Circle to Search). Apple Intelligence is still rolling out and will arrive in stages.
</Step>
<Step title="Want the best value?" status="todo" subtitle="Pixel 9 wins on features per dollar">
At the same price, the Pixel 9 gives you a 120Hz display, more RAM, a bigger battery, and 7 years of updates.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="The verdict">
<Callout tone="success" title="Recommendation">
- **Get the iPhone 16** if you're an Apple user, care about video, or want the most polished, reliable experience.
- **Get the Pixel 9** if you want the best camera for photos, love AI features, and want the smoothest display and longest support.
</Callout>
</Card>
