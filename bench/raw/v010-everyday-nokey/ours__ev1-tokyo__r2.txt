# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

<Badge tone="success">Perfect for first-timers & food lovers</Badge>

<Callout tone="info" title="Trip at a glance">
A balanced mix of iconic sights, hidden gems, great food, and romantic evenings — paced so you never feel rushed.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1 — Arrival & Shinjuku">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check in at your hotel" status="done" subtitle="Shinjuku area — great base for exploring">
Settle in, drop your bags, and grab a quick lunch nearby.
</Step>
<Step title="Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden" status="done" subtitle="Afternoon">
A peaceful escape in the middle of the city. Perfect for a stroll and photos.
</Step>
<Step title="Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Evening">
Tiny lantern-lit alleys packed with bars and yakitori stalls. Great for a casual dinner hopping.
</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building" status="done" subtitle="Night view — free!">
Head up to the observation deck for a panoramic night view of the city.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 — Asakusa & Sumida River">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="Morning — arrive early to beat crowds">
Walk through the iconic Kaminarimon Gate and Nakamise shopping street.
</Step>
<Step title="Sumida River cruise" status="done" subtitle="Late morning">
Take a water bus to Hamarikyu Gardens — a lovely way to see the skyline from the water.
</Step>
<Step title="Hamarikyu Gardens" status="done" subtitle="Midday">
A beautiful landscape garden with a teahouse on an island. Stop for matcha and a sweet.
</Step>
<Step title="TeamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="Afternoon — book tickets in advance">
An immersive digital art museum. Go barefoot through water and light installations.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 — Shibuya & Harajuku">
<Steps>
<Step title="Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="Morning — serene forest in the city">
A calm start before the crowds.
</Step>
<Step title="Takeshita Street" status="done" subtitle="Late morning">
Colorful, quirky, and full of crepes and vintage shops.
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko Statue" status="done" subtitle="Lunchtime">
Experience the famous scramble crossing, then grab lunch at one of the nearby department store food halls.
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Sky" status="done" subtitle="Sunset — book in advance">
Rooftop observation deck with 360° views. Amazing at golden hour.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 — Day trip to Hakone">
<Callout tone="info" title="Getting there">
Take the Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (~90 min). Consider the Hakone Free Pass for transport and attractions.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Hakone Open-Air Museum" status="done" subtitle="Morning">
Sculptures set in nature, plus a foot bath with mountain views.
</Step>
<Step title="Lake Ashi cruise" status="done" subtitle="Midday">
Pirate ship across the lake with views of Mount Fuji on clear days.
</Step>
<Step title="Hakone Shrine & Torii Gate" status="done" subtitle="Afternoon">
The iconic red torii gate standing in the water.
</Step>
<Step title="Onsen (hot spring)" status="done" subtitle="Evening — the highlight">
Soak in a traditional ryokan or public bath. Many offer private options.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Hakone transport options">
| Option | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hakone Free Pass (2-day) | ¥6,100/person | Includes Romancecar + all transport in Hakone |
| Odakyu Romancecar only | ¥2,470/person | Pay separately for local buses/boats |
| Private car & driver | ~¥30,000 | Flexible but pricey |
</Card>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 — Imperial Palace & Ginza">
<Steps>
<Step title="Imperial Palace East Gardens" status="done" subtitle="Morning — free">
Walk the grounds and see the historic moats and gates.
</Step>
<Step title="Ginza shopping street" status="done" subtitle="Late morning">
Browse flagship stores, from Uniqlo to high-end boutiques.
</Step>
<Step title="Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="Lunch">
Fresh sushi, tamago, and street food — a foodie paradise.
</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Station area" status="done" subtitle="Afternoon — before departure">
See the historic red-brick facade and grab last-minute souvenirs.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
