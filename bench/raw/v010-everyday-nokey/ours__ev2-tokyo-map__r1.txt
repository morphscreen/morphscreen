# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

Tokyo is a city of contrasts — ancient temples beside neon skyscrapers, serene gardens hidden behind busy streets, and some of the best food in the world. Here's a balanced 5-day itinerary that mixes iconic sights, local neighborhoods, and romantic moments for two.

<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Your best friend will be the Tokyo Metro and Toei Subway. Grab a **Suica or Pasmo card** at any station — it works on all trains, buses, and even at convenience stores. Most sights are within a 10-minute walk of a station.
</Callout>

## Day 1 — Arrival & Shibuya/Sinjuku

<Card title="Day 1: Iconic Tokyo Start">
<Steps>
<Step title="Arrive & check in" status="done" subtitle="Shinjuku — your home base">
Drop your bags at your hotel in Shinjuku. This area has endless food options and is perfectly central.
</Step>
<Step title="Meiji Shrine" status="current" subtitle="Morning">
A peaceful forested shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji. Walk the gravel path under towering cedar trees — a calm start to your trip. **Free entry.**

<Button variant="primary" ask="How do we get to Meiji Shrine from Shinjuku?">Get directions</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Crossing" status="todo" subtitle="Late morning">
Walk (or train one stop) to the world's busiest pedestrian crossing. Watch the famous scramble from the Starbucks on the second floor of the Shibuya TSUTAYA building.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="What's the best time to see Shibuya Crossing without crowds?">Best time to visit</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch in Shibuya" status="todo" subtitle="Noon">
Try a casual **sushi-go-round** (kaiten-zushi) or a steaming bowl of ramen. Look for lines — locals queue for a reason!
</Step>
<Step title="Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="todo" subtitle="Evening">
Back in Shinjuku, explore the tiny alleys of **Golden Gai** (over 200 micro-bars) and **Omoide Yokocho** ("Memory Lane") for yakitori skewers and a nostalgic atmosphere.

## Day 2 — Old Tokyo: Asakusa & Ueno

<Card title="Day 2: Temples, Gardens & Anime">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple" status="current" subtitle="Morning">
Tokyo's oldest temple. Enter through the massive **Kaminarimon Gate**, stroll down **Nakamise-dori** (shopping street with traditional snacks), and explore the temple grounds.

<Callout tone="info" title="Pro tip">
Visit at 8 AM to beat the crowds — the temple is quieter and more atmospheric.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Ueno Park" status="todo" subtitle="Late morning">
A 5-minute train ride away. Visit the **Ueno Zoo** (home to giant pandas) or just wander the leafy paths. In spring, this is one of Tokyo's best cherry blossom spots.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="What's worth seeing in Ueno Park besides the zoo?">Park highlights</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Akihabara" status="todo" subtitle="Afternoon">
The electric town — anime, manga, retro video games, and maid cafés. Even if you're not into anime, the neon chaos is an experience.

<Button variant="primary" ask="What should two first-timers do in Akihabara?">Top things to do</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Dinner: Monjayaki in Tsukishima" status="todo" subtitle="Evening">
Take the train to Tsukishima's **Monja Street** — a narrow lane packed with restaurants serving *monjayaki*, a savory Tokyo-style pancake you cook yourself on a hot griddle. Fun, interactive, and delicious.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="Which monjayaki restaurant do you recommend?">Pick a restaurant</Button>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 3 — Tsukiji, Hamarikyu & Ginza

<Card title="Day 3: Markets & Modern Elegance">
<Steps>
<Step title="Tsukiji Outer Market" status="current" subtitle="Early morning">
Wake up early for the bustling outer market (the inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu). Sample fresh seafood — try **tamago** (egg omelette on a stick), uni, and toro sushi.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Cash only">
Many stalls are cash-only, so bring ¥5,000–¥10,000 per person.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Hamarikyu Gardens" status="todo" subtitle="Late morning">
A stunning Japanese landscape garden with a tidal pond and a **teahouse** on an island. It's a 10-minute walk from Tsukiji — a peaceful contrast to the market chaos.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="How long should we spend at Hamarikyu Gardens?">Planning time</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Ginza" status="todo" subtitle="Afternoon">
Tokyo's most elegant district — luxury boutiques, art galleries, and the iconic **Wako building** with its clock tower. Even window-shopping is fun here.

<Button variant="primary" ask="What are the best affordable things to do in Ginza?">Budget-friendly Ginza</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Kabuki-za Theatre" status="todo" subtitle="Evening (optional)">
If you're curious about traditional Japanese theater, catch a **single-act kabuki performance** at the historic Kabuki-za. English audio guides are available.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="How do I book kabuki tickets?">Book tickets</Button>
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 4 — Shinjuku Gyoen, Harajuku & Roppongi

<Card title="Day 4: Nature, Kawaii & Night Views">
<Steps>
<Step title="Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden" status="current" subtitle="Morning">
One of Tokyo's most beautiful parks — formal French gardens, English landscapes, and a traditional Japanese garden with tea house. It's a 10-minute walk from Shinjuku Station.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="What's the entry fee for Shinjuku Gyoen?">Entry info</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Harajuku & Takeshita Street" status="todo" subtitle="Late morning">
The heart of Tokyo's youth culture — quirky fashion, crepes, and colorful stores. Nearby **Omotesando** offers a more upscale, tree-lined shopping street.

<Button variant="primary" ask="What are the must-try snacks on Takeshita Street?">Snack guide</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Roppongi Hills & Mori Tower" status="todo" subtitle="Sunset">
Take the elevator to the **Tokyo City View** observation deck (52nd floor) for a 360° panorama. Time it for sunset — the sky turns pink over Mount Fuji on clear days.

<Callout tone="success" title="Date night tip">
The deck has a cozy café and lounge — perfect for a drink while the city lights come up.
</Callout>
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 5 — Skytree, Odaiba & Departure

<Card title="Day 5: High Views & Waterfront">
<Steps>
<Step title="Tokyo Skytree" status="current" subtitle="Morning">
The world's tallest tower (634m). The **Tembo Deck** at 350m gives a jaw-dropping view, and the **Tembo Galleria** at 450m is a glass-walled skywalk.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="Is it worth going to both Skytree decks?">Which deck?</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Odaiba" status="todo" subtitle="Afternoon">
A futuristic man-made island across the bay. Visit **teamLab Borderless** (digital art museum), walk the beach, and see the **Rainbow Bridge** from the deck of the **Yurikamome** driverless train.

<Button variant="primary" ask="Should we book teamLab Borderless tickets in advance?">Ticket advice</Button>
</Step>
<Step title="Farewell dinner" status="todo" subtitle="Evening">
End your trip with a memorable meal — perhaps **teppanyaki** cooked at your table, or a kaiseki (traditional multi-course) dinner in Shinjuku.

<Button variant="secondary" ask="Recommend a romantic dinner spot for our last night">Find a restaurant</Button>
</Step>
</Steps>

- **Eat like a local**: convenience store breakfasts (onigiri, sandwiches) cost ~¥300–500.
- **Free sights**: Meiji Shrine, Senso-ji, Shinjuku Gyoen (¥500), and most parks are free or cheap.
- **Skip the taxi**: Tokyo's trains are fast, punctual, and much cheaper.

## Essential Prep Checklist

<Checklist title="Before you go" name="tokyo-prep">
- [ ] Book flights (Narita or Haneda)
- [ ] Reserve hotel in Shinjuku or Shibuya
- [ ] Get a **Suica/Pasmo** card (or use your iPhone's Wallet)
- [ ] Download **Google Maps** offline + **Japan Travel** app
- [ ] Pack comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 10–15 km/day!)
- [ ] Bring a portable charger
- [ ] Learn a few phrases: *Arigato* (thank you), *Sumimasen* (excuse me), *Kore kudasai* (this please)
- [ ] Consider a **Japan Rail Pass** if you plan day trips (e.g., Nikko, Kamakura)
</Checklist>
