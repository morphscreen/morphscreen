# Should you rent or buy a flat?

It depends on how long you'll stay put, what you can afford upfront, and how you feel about flexibility vs. building equity. Let's work through it with your numbers.

<Callout title="The short answer" tone="info">
If you plan to stay **5+ years** and can put down a **10–20% deposit**, buying is likely to beat renting financially. If you value flexibility or can't save a deposit quickly, renting remains a smart choice.
</Callout>
