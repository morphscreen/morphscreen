# Climate Summit Reaches Historic Agreement on Emissions

**By Sarah Chen · 2 hours ago · 5 min read**

World leaders at the COP29 summit in Baku have finalized a landmark agreement committing 198 nations to accelerate the transition away from fossil fuels, with binding targets for 2030 and 2035.

<Card title="What was agreed">
The final text calls for "transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems, in a just, orderly and equitable manner, accelerating action in this critical decade." Key provisions include:

- **Tripling renewable energy capacity** globally by 2030
- **Doubling energy efficiency improvements** by 2030
- **Phasing down unabated coal power** with no new permits after 2025
- **$300 billion annually** in climate finance for developing nations by 2035
- **Loss and damage fund** operationalized with $700M in initial pledges
