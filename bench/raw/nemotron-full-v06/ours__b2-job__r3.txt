# Product Designer — Growth Team

<Card title="Product Designer" subtitle="Growth Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)">
<Callout tone="info" title="About this role">
We're looking for a product designer to join our Growth team and help shape the user journey from first visit to long-term engagement. You'll work closely with engineers, PMs, and data analysts to run experiments, improve activation, and reduce churn across web and mobile.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Design end-to-end flows for onboarding, upgrade paths, and re-engagement campaigns
- Collaborate with PMs and engineers to define, prototype, and ship experiments
- Use qualitative and quantitative insights to identify opportunities and validate solutions
- Contribute to and maintain our design system as it scales
- Present work to stakeholders and incorporate feedback iteratively
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications we're looking for">
- 4+ years of product design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS or consumer tech
- Strong portfolio showing shipped work with measurable impact
- Fluency in Figma and modern design systems
- Comfortable working with data — you can read experiment results and ask good questions
- Excellent written and visual communication skills
- Experience with A/B testing and growth methodologies is a plus
</Card>
