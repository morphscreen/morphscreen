# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for key user flows, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for design quality across the team. This is a high-impact role where your work directly influences how thousands of teams collaborate daily.
</Card>

<Card title="Key qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products with measurable impact
- Expertise in Figma, design systems, and prototyping
- Comfortable conducting user research and usability testing
- Excellent communication skills — you can articulate design decisions to non-design stakeholders
- Experience mentoring junior designers and contributing to design culture
</Card>
