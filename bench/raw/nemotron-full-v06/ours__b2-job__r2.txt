# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

---

<Card title="About the Role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design team and help shape the future of our core product. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to define and deliver intuitive, accessible experiences for millions of users. This is a high-impact role where you'll own the design process end-to-end—from discovery and ideation through to polished execution and iteration.
</Card>

<Card title="Main Qualifications">
<Columns>
<Details title="5+ years product design experience" open="false">
Proven track record shipping consumer or B2B products at scale. Portfolio demonstrating end-to-end ownership.
</Details>
<Details title="Strong systems thinking" open="false">
Experience building and maintaining design systems. Comfortable defining patterns, components, and guidelines used across teams.
</Details>
<Details title="User-centered process" open="false">
Deep experience with qualitative and quantitative research methods. You validate assumptions before investing in solutions.
</Details>
<Details title="Collaboration & communication" open="false">
You partner effectively with PMs, engineers, and stakeholders. You give and receive feedback well and can articulate design rationale clearly.
</Details>
<Details title="Proficiency with modern tools" open="false">
Figma (required), prototyping tools, and familiarity with front-end implementation constraints (HTML/CSS/JS basics a plus).
</Details>
</Columns>
</Card>
