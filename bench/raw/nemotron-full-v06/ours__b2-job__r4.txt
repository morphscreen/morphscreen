# Product Designer — Design Team

<Card title="About this role" subtitle="Full-time · Remote (US/Canada) · Posted 3 days ago">
We're looking for a Product Designer to help shape the core experience of our platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to turn complex problems into simple, elegant solutions. This is a high-impact role where you'll own features end-to-end — from early concepts through polished delivery.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for key product areas, collaborating with cross-functional teams
- Conduct user research and usability testing to validate ideas
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups in Figma
- Build and maintain components in our design system
- Partner with engineering to ensure quality implementation
- Contribute to design culture through critiques, mentorship, and process improvements
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- **5+ years** of product design experience at a tech company or digital agency
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products and end-to-end design process
- Expert-level Figma skills; comfortable with prototyping and design systems
- Experience conducting and synthesizing user research
- Excellent visual design sense — typography, layout, color, motion
- Ability to give and receive constructive feedback in design critiques
- Bonus: experience with data visualization, B2B SaaS, or accessibility (WCAG)
</Card>
