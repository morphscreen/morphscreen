<Details title="Budget assumptions & tips" open="false">
- **Accommodation**: €90/night for a well-rated private room in a guesthouse or 2-person Airbnb in Baixa/Chiado (split if sharing).
- **Food**: €50/day – breakfast €5, lunch €15, dinner €25, snacks/drinks €5. Time Out Market ~€20-25.
- **Transport**: Viva Viagem card zapping €1.50/ride; 24h pass €6.60 if heavy use. Airport metro €1.50.
- **Attractions**: Jerónimos €10, Belém Tower €6, São Jorge Castle €10, Santa Justa Lift €5.50. Many churches free.
- **Save money**: Free entry Sunday mornings (until 14:00) at many monuments; walk instead of tram where possible; buy pastries at non-tourist bakeries (€1.20 vs €2.50).
- **Splurge options**: Fado show €25-40, day trip to Sintra +€30, private tuk-tuk tour €50.
</Details>

<Callout tone="success" title="Quick packing reminders">
Comfortable walking shoes (cobblestones) · Light layers (spring temps 15-22°C) · Compact umbrella · Portable charger · Euro cash for small cafés/tascas
</Callout>
