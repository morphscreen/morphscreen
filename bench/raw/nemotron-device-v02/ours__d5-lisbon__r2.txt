### Day 1 Walking Route (Belém → Alfama)

<Callout tone="info" title="Walking route note">
The Belém → Alfama leg is **not walkable** (5.5 km, 1h10m). The plan uses tram/bus between districts. Walking routes below are *within* each district.
</Callout>

<Details title="Belém Walking Loop (2.1 km, ~30 min walk time)" open="true">
| From | To | Distance | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Belém Tower | Jerónimos Monastery | 800 m | 10 min | Av. Brasília → Praça do Império |
| Jerónimos Monastery | Pastéis de Belém | 350 m | 4 min | Praça do Império → R. de Belém |
| Pastéis de Belém | MAAT | 600 m | 8 min | R. de Belém → Av. Brasília |
| **Total** | | **1.75 km** | **~22 min** | Flat, riverside promenade |
</Details>

<Details title="Alfama Walking Loop (1.8 km, ~35 min walk time)" open="false">
| From | To | Distance | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bus stop (Alfama) | São Jorge Castle | 600 m | 15 min | Steep uphill, cobblestones |
| São Jorge Castle | Miradouro Santa Luzia | 300 m | 5 min | Downhill through castle walls |
| Miradouro Santa Luzia | Lisbon Cathedral | 400 m | 6 min | Largo Santa Luzia → Largo da Sé |
| Lisbon Cathedral | Alfama dinner area | 500 m | 8 min | Winding medieval streets |
| **Total** | | **1.8 km** | **~34 min** | **Steep hills, wear good shoes** |
</Details>
