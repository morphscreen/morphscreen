<Card title="Likely Problems" subtitle="Based on your photo analysis">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Fungal Issues">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Powdery Mildew">White powdery spots on leaves, common in humid conditions with poor air circulation</Callout>
<Callout tone="warning" title="Leaf Spot">Brown or black spots with yellow halos, often caused by overhead watering</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Root Rot">Wilting despite wet soil, mushy brown roots — check drainage immediately</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Pest Damage">
<Callout tone="danger" title="Spider Mites">Fine webbing, stippled yellow leaves, tiny moving dots on undersides</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Aphids">Clusters of small green/black insects on new growth, sticky honeydew residue</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Mealybugs">White cottony masses in leaf axils and stem joints</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Nutrient Deficiencies">
<Callout tone="info" title="Nitrogen">Older leaves yellow first, stunted growth</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Iron">Young leaves yellow with green veins (interveinal chlorosis)</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Magnesium">Older leaf edges yellow/brown, curling upward</Callout>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Care Steps" subtitle="Recommended treatment plan">
<Steps>
<Step title="Isolate the plant" status="current">
Move affected plant away from others to prevent spread. Quarantine for 2–3 weeks while treating.
</Step>
<Step title="Remove damaged foliage" status="todo">
Prune heavily affected leaves/stems with sterilized scissors. Dispose in sealed bag — don't compost.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply targeted treatment" status="todo">
<Badge tone="info">Fungal</Badge> Neem oil or copper fungicide every 7–10 days<br />
<Badge tone="danger">Pests</Badge> Insecticidal soap or neem oil, repeat in 5 days<br />
<Badge tone="success">Nutrients</Badge> Balanced fertilizer + specific supplement per deficiency
</Step>
<Step title="Adjust environment" status="todo">
Improve air circulation, avoid overhead watering, ensure proper drainage, check light levels match plant needs.
</Step>
<Step title="Monitor & follow up" status="todo">
Inspect daily for 2 weeks. New growth should look healthy. Re-treat if needed per product instructions.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
