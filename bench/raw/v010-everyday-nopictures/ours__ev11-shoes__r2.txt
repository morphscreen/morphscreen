<Callout tone="warning" title="Half-size up tip">
For road running, most runners prefer a half size up from their casual shoe size to avoid toe jamming on downhills. If between sizes, go larger.
</Callout>

<Card title="What to check before buying">
<Checklist title="Sizing checklist" name="sizing">
- [ ] Your heel is snug, not slipping
- [ ] You have a thumb's width (about 1 cm) from your longest toe to the shoe end
- [ ] The shoe bends at the ball of your foot, not mid-arch
- [ ] No pressure points on the sides when laced normally
- [ ] Walk a few steps: no heel rub or toe pinch
</Checklist>
</Card>
