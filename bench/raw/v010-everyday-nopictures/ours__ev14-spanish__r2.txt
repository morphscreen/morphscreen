# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Card title="Your Essential Spanish Survival Kit" subtitle="Master these 10 phrases and you'll get by in most situations">
<Callout tone="success" title="Quick tip">Practice the pronunciation out loud — Spanish is phonetic, so what you see is what you say!</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="The 10 Phrases">
| # | English | Spanish | When to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello / Hi | Hola | Any time, any place |
| 2 | Please | Por favor | Adding politeness to any request |
| 3 | Thank you | Gracias | Showing appreciation |
| 4 | Yes / No | Sí / No | Simple answers |
| 5 | Excuse me / Sorry | Perdón | Getting attention or apologizing |
| 6 | Where is…? | ¿Dónde está…? | Asking for directions |
| 7 | How much is it? | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | Asking prices at markets or shops |
| 8 | I don't understand | No entiendo | When you need help or repetition |
| 9 | Do you speak English? | ¿Habla inglés? | Finding someone who can help |
| 10 | Help! | ¡Ayuda! | In an emergency |
</Card>

<Card title="Two Bonus Phrases Worth Knowing">
<Columns>
<Card title="The polite extra">
**"De nada"** — *You're welcome*

When someone says "gracias," reply with "de nada." It costs nothing and makes a great impression.
</Card>
<Card title="The practical extra">
**"La cuenta, por favor"** — *The bill, please*

At restaurants, catch the waiter's eye and say this to get the check without awkward waving.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
