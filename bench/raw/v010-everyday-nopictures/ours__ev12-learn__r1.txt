# How Photosynthesis Works

Photosynthesis is the process plants, algae, and some bacteria use to turn sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into food (glucose) and oxygen. It's the foundation of nearly all life on Earth.

<Callout tone="info" title="The big picture">
In plain terms: plants drink water, breathe in CO₂, soak up sunlight, and use that energy to build sugar — releasing oxygen as a byproduct.
</Callout>

<Card title="The overall equation">
<Math tex="6CO_2 + 6H_2O + light \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2" label="Balanced equation" />
In words: **six carbon dioxide molecules + six water molecules + light energy → one glucose molecule + six oxygen molecules**.
</Card>

<Card title="Where it happens">
Photosynthesis takes place in the **chloroplasts** of plant cells — tiny green factories packed with **chlorophyll**, the pigment that absorbs light (and gives leaves their green color).

<KeyValue title="Key players">
| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Chloroplasts | Organelles where photosynthesis occurs |
| Chlorophyll | Green pigment that captures light energy |
| Stomata | Tiny pores on leaves for gas exchange (CO₂ in, O₂ out) |
| Xylem | Tubes that carry water from roots to leaves |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="The two stages">
Photosynthesis happens in two linked stages:

<Steps>
<Step title="Light-dependent reactions" status="current" subtitle="In the thylakoid membranes">
Sunlight energizes electrons in chlorophyll. This energy splits water molecules (photolysis), releasing oxygen gas. The energy is stored as ATP and NADPH — temporary energy carriers.
</Step>
<Step title="Calvin cycle (light-independent)" status="todo" subtitle="In the stroma">
Using the ATP and NADPH from stage 1, the plant fixes CO₂ from the air into glucose. This stage doesn't need light directly, but it depends on the products of the light reactions.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Anatomy of a leaf">
Think of a leaf as a solar panel:

| Leaf part | Job |
|---|---|
| Cuticle | Waxy protective coating, prevents water loss |
| Epidermis | Outer cell layer |
| Palisade mesophyll | Dense layer of chloroplast-rich cells — main photosynthesis site |
| Spongy mesophyll | Looser cells with air spaces for gas exchange |
| Stomata | Pores that let CO₂ in and O₂ out |
| Veins | Transport water (xylem) and sugar (phloem) |
</Card>

<Card title="Factors that affect the rate">
Photosynthesis speeds up or slows down depending on conditions:

<Columns>
<Stat label="Light intensity" value="Higher = faster" note="Up to a saturation point" tone="info" icon="sun" />
<Stat label="CO₂ concentration" value="More = faster" note="Until other limits kick in" tone="info" icon="wind" />
<Stat label="Temperature" value="Optimal 25-35°C" note="Too hot denatures enzymes" tone="warning" icon="thermometer" />
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Why it matters to you">
- **Oxygen**: Every breath you take contains oxygen released by photosynthesis.
- **Food**: The glucose plants make becomes the base of the food chain — including the grains, fruits, and vegetables you eat.
- **Climate**: Plants absorb CO₂, helping regulate the atmosphere.

<Callout tone="success" title="Fun fact">
About 20% of Earth's oxygen comes from the Amazon rainforest alone. The rest comes mostly from ocean phytoplankton doing the same chemistry!
</Callout>
</Card>
