Making a proper Neapolitan pizza at home is absolutely achievable, even without a wood-fired oven. The key is mastering the dough, using high-quality ingredients, and getting your home oven as hot as possible. Here’s your complete guide.

<Card title="The Perfect Dough (2 pizzas)">
This is the heart of it. It needs time, but the hands-on work is minimal.

<Steps>
<Step title="Mix" status="done" subtitle="10 minutes">In a large bowl, combine <strong>500g “00” flour</strong> (or bread flour), <strong>325g cold water</strong>, <strong>10g fine sea salt</strong>, and <strong>2g fresh yeast</strong> (or 1g active dry yeast). Mix with a spoon until a shaggy dough forms, then knead for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic.</Step>
<Step title="Bulk Ferment" status="done" subtitle="8-24 hours">Cover the bowl and let it rest at room temperature for 8-24 hours. The longer, the better for flavor. You’ll see it double in size.</Step>
<Step title="Shape & Proof" status="done" subtitle="2 hours">Gently divide the dough into two balls. Place each on a floured surface, cover with a damp towel, and let them proof for another 2 hours at room temperature.</Step>
<Step title="Stretch" status="done" subtitle="2 minutes per pizza">Flour your hands and the dough ball. Gently press it flat, then stretch from the center outward, leaving the rim thicker. Don’t use a rolling pin – it knocks out the air.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="info" title="Why '00' flour?">
“00” flour is finely milled Italian flour with a high protein content, giving you that soft, chewy, and slightly charred crust. If you can’t find it, bread flour is a good substitute.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="The Secret to High Heat at Home">
A wood-fired oven hits 800-900°F. Your home oven likely maxes out at 500-550°F. Here’s how to bridge the gap:

<Columns>
<Card title="The Method">
1. **Preheat** your oven with a pizza stone or steel on the top rack at its highest setting (500°F+) for at least 1 hour. The stone needs to absorb maximum heat.
2. **Turn on the broiler** for the last 10-15 minutes. This creates a blast of heat from above, mimicking the dome of a pizza oven.
3. **Assemble and launch** your pizza quickly. Use a pizza peel dusted with semolina flour to slide it off easily.
4. **Bake** for 4-7 minutes. The top should be blistered and the bottom golden. If the top is browning too fast, move the pizza to a lower rack for a minute.
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Don't skip the preheat">
A cold stone will make your pizza doughy and soggy. The long preheat is non-negotiable for a proper crust.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Choosing Your Toppings">
Neapolitan pizza is about simplicity. Less is more.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Margherita">
The classic. <Badge tone="success">Beginner-friendly</Badge>
- **Sauce:** Crushed San Marzano tomatoes with a pinch of salt. No cooking needed.
- **Cheese:** Fresh mozzarella (fior di latte), torn into pieces.
- **Finish:** Fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil after baking.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Marinara">
No cheese, just pure tomato goodness. <Badge tone="info">Vegan</Badge>
- **Sauce:** The same crushed San Marzano tomatoes, with minced garlic and oregano mixed in.
- **Cheese:** None.
- **Finish:** A drizzle of olive oil.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Diavola">
Spicy and satisfying. <Badge tone="warning">Spicy</Badge>
- **Sauce:** A light layer of tomato sauce.
- **Cheese:** Fresh mozzarella.
- **Topping:** Spicy salami (like 'nduja or soppressata) added before baking.
- **Finish:** A drizzle of chili oil or fresh chili flakes.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout tone="neutral" title="Pro tip: Less is more">
Don't overload your pizza. A wet, heavy topping will weigh down the dough and prevent it from crisping. Two to three quality ingredients are plenty.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Step-by-Step: From Oven to Table">
<Steps>
<Step title="Preheat & Prep" status="done" subtitle="60+ minutes">Get your oven and stone screaming hot. Prep your sauce, cheese, and any toppings so they're ready to go.</Step>
<Step title="Stretch & Top" status="done" subtitle="5 minutes">Stretch your dough into a 10-12 inch round. Spread a thin layer of sauce, leaving a 1-inch border. Add your cheese and toppings.</Step>
<Step title="Bake" status="done" subtitle="4-7 minutes">Carefully slide the pizza onto the hot stone. Bake until the crust is puffed and charred in spots, and the cheese is melted and bubbling.</Step>
<Step title="Finish & Serve" status="done" subtitle="1 minute">Remove from the oven. Drizzle with olive oil, add fresh basil, and slice. Eat immediately!</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
