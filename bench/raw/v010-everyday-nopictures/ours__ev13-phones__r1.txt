# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which should you get?

Both are excellent phones, but they're built for different people. Here's a side-by-side comparison to help you decide.

<Card title="The deciding questions">
<Checklist title="Ask yourself" name="deciding">
- [ ] Do I use iMessage, FaceTime, or AirDrop with family/friends?
- [ ] Do I want the absolute best video recording?
- [ ] Do I want the most flexible camera for photos (especially night or zoom)?
- [ ] Do I care about getting updates for as long as possible?
- [ ] Do I want AI features built into the phone now (Gemini, Magic Eraser)?
- [ ] Do I want to customise my home screen and default apps?
</Checklist>

<Callout tone="success" title="The short answer">
Choose the **iPhone 16** if you're in the Apple ecosystem, shoot a lot of video, or just want something that "just works". Choose the **Pixel 9** if you want the best still camera, longest software support, or prefer Android's flexibility.
</Callout>
</Card>
