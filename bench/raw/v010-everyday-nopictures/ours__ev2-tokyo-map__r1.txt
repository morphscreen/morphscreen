<Card title="Daily plan">
<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1 — Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Arrival, neon, and skyline">
Check in, then explore Shinjuku: Observation deck at the Metropolitan Government Building (free), Omoide Yokocho for yakitori, and Golden Gai for bars.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 2 — Shibuya & Harajuku" status="done" subtitle="Crossing, culture, and crepes">
Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko statue, Meiji Shrine, Takeshita Street, and a sunset view from Shibuya Sky.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 3 — Asakusa & Ueno" status="done" subtitle="Old Tokyo and museums">
Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street, river cruise to Hamarikyu, then Ueno Park and museums.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 4 — Ginza & Odaiba" status="done" subtitle="Shopping and bayside views">
Ginza luxury shopping, teamLab Planets (book ahead), Odaiba seaside park, and the Rainbow Bridge at night.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 5 — Flexible favorites" status="current" subtitle="Pick your pace">
Choose one: Studio Ghibli Museum (book weeks ahead), a day trip to Kamakura, or relaxed coffee-hopping in Kichijoji.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
