# Houseplants that are hard to kill

Whether you're a beginner, a frequent traveler, or just someone with a busy life, these plants forgive neglect, low light, and irregular watering.

<Callout tone="success" title="The golden rule">
Most "unkillable" plants die from *too much love* (overwatering) rather than neglect. When in doubt, let the soil dry out.
</Callout>

## The 3-step survival method

<Steps>
<Step title="Check the soil before watering" status="done" subtitle="The finger test">
Stick your finger 2 cm into the soil. Only water if it feels dry. For succulents (Jade), wait until it's fully dry.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the right pot" status="current" subtitle="Drainage is everything">
Use pots with drainage holes. Terracotta dries faster; plastic holds moisture longer. Never let the plant sit in standing water.
</Step>
<Step title="Match light to the plant" status="todo" subtitle="Read the label">
Snake, ZZ, Pothos, and Cast Iron tolerate low light. Jade needs a sunny windowsill. Spider and Peace Lily prefer bright, indirect light.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What kills "unkillable" plants

<Card title="Top 5 killers">
<Checklist title="Avoid these mistakes" name="killers">
- [ ] Overwatering — the #1 cause of death
- [ ] No drainage holes in the pot
- [ ] Direct hot afternoon sun on Jade or Peace Lily
- [ ] Cold drafts from windows or AC vents
- [ ] Repotting too often or into too-large pots
</Checklist>
</Card>
