# The Art of Neapolitan Pizza at Home

Making a true Neapolitan pizza at home is absolutely achievable. The secret lies in three pillars: **the dough**, **the heat**, and **the toppings**. Let's break it down.

<Card title="The Dough — Your Foundation">
The dough is where 80% of the magic happens. Here's the authentic formula from the Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana (AVPN).

<KeyValue title="Dough Formula (for 4 pizzas)">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| "00" Flour (high protein) | 500g |
| Water (room temp) | 325ml |
| Fresh yeast | 2g (or 0.5g dry) |
| Fine sea salt | 10g |
</KeyValue>

**Method:**

<Steps>
<Step title="Mix" status="done">Dissolve salt in water, then add flour and yeast. Mix until shaggy.</Step>
<Step title="Knead" status="done">Knead 15-20 minutes until smooth and elastic. Dough should pass the windowpane test.</Step>
<Step title="Bulk Ferment" status="current">Cover and rest 8-12 hours at room temperature (18-22°C).</Step>
<Step title="Ball & Proof" status="todo">Divide into 250g balls, rest another 6-12 hours in the fridge.</Step>
<Step title="Shape" status="todo">Take out 1 hour before use. Stretch by hand — never use a rolling pin!</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Key Tip">
The dough should be **very wet and sticky** (hydration ~65%). Resist adding extra flour — a wet dough creates that airy, leopard-spotted crust.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="The Heat — Getting Closer to Naples">
Traditional Neapolitan pizza bakes at **450°C for 60-90 seconds**. At home, you'll need to get creative.

<Compare title="Oven Options">
| Factor | Home Oven | Pizza Steel | Ooni/Koda | Wood-Fired |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Max Temp | 250°C | 250°C | 450°C+ | 500°C+ |
| Bake Time | 5-8 min | 4-6 min | 90 sec | 60 sec |
| Crust | Chewy | Crisper | Authentic | Most authentic |
| Cost | $0 | $50-100 | $300-500 | $2000+ |
| Verdict | Good start | Better | Best value | Dream setup |
</Compare>

<Callout tone="info" title="The Home Oven Hack">
If using a regular oven: place a **pizza steel** on the top rack, preheat at max for **1 hour**, then switch to broil for 5 minutes before baking. This gives you a fighting chance at leopard spotting.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="The Toppings — Less is More">
A true Neapolitan pizza respects its ingredients. Here's the classic Margherita:

<Columns>
<Card title="San Marzano Tomatoes">
Crushed by hand. No cooking, no seasoning — just tomatoes, salt, and a basil leaf.
</Card>
<Card title="Fior di Latte Mozzarella">
Fresh cow's milk mozzarella, torn into pieces. Buffalo mozzarella (Mozzarella di Bufala) is even better but more watery — drain it well.
</Card>
<Card title="Extra Virgin Olive Oil">
A drizzle before baking. Use a high-quality oil — you'll taste the difference.
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="danger" title="Common Mistakes">
- **Overloading** — a soggy center is the #1 killer. Use just 80-100g of mozzarella per pizza.
- **Cold toppings** — bring everything to room temperature before assembling.
- **Pre-sliced cheese** — it contains anti-caking agents that won't melt properly.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Step-by-Step Assembly">
<Steps>
<Step title="Shape the Dough" status="current">Flatten with fingertips, then stretch from the center outward. Leave a 1-2cm rim.</Step>
<Step title="Sauce First" status="todo">Spread 2-3 tablespoons of crushed tomatoes in a spiral, leaving the rim clean.</Step>
<Step title="Cheese & Basil" status="todo">Add torn mozzarella pieces and 2-3 basil leaves.</Step>
<Step title="Drizzle & Launch" status="todo">Finish with olive oil, then slide onto your hot surface.</Step>
<Step title="Bake & Rotate" status="todo">Bake 90 seconds (or 5 min in home oven), rotating for even color. The rim should puff and char slightly.</Step>
<Step title="Rest & Serve" status="todo">Rest 2 minutes, then slice. The center should be soft, not soggy.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Recipe Card — Margherita D.O.P.">
<Plan name="Classic Margherita" price="4 pizzas" period="~24h total" featured="true">
- **Dough:** 500g "00" flour, 325ml water, 2g fresh yeast, 10g salt
- **Sauce:** 200g San Marzano tomatoes, pinch of salt
- **Cheese:** 300g fior di latte mozzarella
- **Finish:** 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, fresh basil
- **Bake:** 450°C for 90 seconds (or max home oven for 5 min)
</Plan>
</Card>

<Card title="Troubleshooting">
<Details title="My crust is pale, not leopard-spotted" open="false">
Your oven isn't hot enough or the dough is too thick. Preheat longer, move the rack higher, and stretch the dough thinner.
</Details>
<Details title="The center is soggy">
Too much sauce or cheese, or the mozzarella was too watery. Drain the mozzarella on paper towels for 30 minutes before using.
</Details>
<Details title="The dough tears when stretching">
It's not rested enough or too cold. Let it sit at room temperature for an extra hour before shaping.
</Details>
<Details title="No oven spring (flat pizza)">
Your yeast is dead or the dough over-proofed. Check your yeast freshness and reduce proofing time.
</Details>
</Card>

<Card title="Your First Attempt Checklist">
<Checklist title="Before you start" name="pizza-checklist">
- [ ] "00" flour (or bread flour as backup)
- [ ] San Marzano tomatoes (canned is fine)
- [ ] Fresh mozzarella (not pre-shredded)
- [ ] Pizza steel or stone (or heavy baking sheet)
- [ ] Oven preheated 1 hour at max
- [ ] Dough made 24 hours ahead
- [ ] Toppings at room temperature
- [ ] Pizza peel or flat baking sheet for launch
</Checklist>
</Card>

<Callout tone="success" title="The 80/20 Rule">
Don't obsess over perfection on your first try. Even a "good" homemade pizza beats most takeout. The more you practice the stretch and the launch, the closer you'll get to Naples.
</Callout>
