<Card title="Before you buy — quick fit check">
<Checklist title="Sizing checklist" name="fit-checklist">
- [ ] Try them on in the evening (feet swell during the day)
- [ ] Leave a thumb's width (about 1 cm) between your longest toe and the shoe's end
- [ ] Wiggle your toes freely — no pinching at the sides
- [ ] Walk and jog a few steps; no heel slipping
- [ ] If between sizes, go up half a size
</Checklist>
</Card>
