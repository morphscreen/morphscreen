## Day-by-day breakdown

<Card title="Day 1 — Shibuya & Shinjuku: The Iconic Core" subtitle="Sunday: bustling energy, neon, and gardens">
<Steps>
<Step title="Shibuya Crossing" status="done" subtitle="9:00 AM — Beat the crowds, watch the scramble">Start at the world's busiest crossing. Grab coffee at Starbucks above the intersection for the classic view.</Step>
<Step title="Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="10:30 AM — Forest in the city">A 5-min walk. Serene torii gates and a quiet forest path — a perfect contrast to the chaos outside.</Step>
<Step title="Harajuku — Takeshita Street" status="done" subtitle="12:00 PM — Lunch & quirky fashion">Crepes, vintage shops, and eccentric street style. Try the famous crepe stands.</Step>
<Step title="Shinjuku Gyoen Garden" status="done" subtitle="2:00 PM — Picnic & rest">One of Tokyo's best gardens. Bring snacks from Harajuku and sit by the pond.</Step>
<Step title="Golden Gai" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM — Dinner in tiny bars">Over 200 micro-bars in six alleys. Pick one with 4–6 seats for an intimate evening.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 — Asakusa & Ueno: Old Tokyo" subtitle="Monday: temples, markets, and skyline views">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="8:30 AM — Beat the tour groups">Tokyo's oldest temple. Arrive before 9 AM for peaceful photos of the lantern and pagoda.</Step>
<Step title="Nakamise Shopping Street" status="done" subtitle="9:30 AM — Traditional snacks">Try ningyo-yaki (sweet bean cakes) and senbei (rice crackers). Great souvenirs.</Step>
<Step title="Ueno Park & Museums" status="done" subtitle="11:30 AM — Culture & street food">Tokyo National Museum (samurai armor, ukiyo-e) or the National Museum of Nature and Science. Ameya-Yokocho market for lunch.</Step>
<Step title="Akihabara Electric Town" status="done" subtitle="3:00 PM — Anime & gadgets">Multi-story arcades, maid cafés, and electronics. Even if you're not an otaku, the energy is worth it.</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="6:30 PM — Sunset views">The 634m tower. Book the Tembo Deck (350m) in advance for sunset. The illuminations below are stunning.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 — Tsukiji, Ginza & Roppongi: Food & Nightlife" subtitle="Tuesday: markets, luxury, and city lights">
<Steps>
<Step title="Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="8:00 AM — Fresh sushi breakfast">The inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market still has 400+ stalls. Try uni (sea urchin) or toro (fatty tuna) at a tiny counter.</Step>
<Step title="Hamarikyu Gardens" status="done" subtitle="10:30 AM — Tea house by the bay">A feudal lord's garden with a tidal pond. Matcha and sweet at the teahouse.</Step>
<Step title="Ginza Shopping" status="done" subtitle="12:30 PM — Luxury & department stores">Uniqlo flagship (12 floors!), Ginza Six, and artisan shops. Lunch at a basement food hall (depachika).</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Tower" status="done" subtitle="4:00 PM — Retro views">Less crowded than Skytree, more nostalgic. The Main Deck at 150m is cheaper and charming.</Step>
<Step title="Roppongi Hills" status="done" subtitle="7:00 PM — Dinner & Mori Tower">Mori Art Museum (open late), the 52nd-floor observation deck, and dozens of restaurants. Try izakaya hopping.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 — Ghibli, Kichijoji & Shimokitazawa: Creative Tokyo" subtitle="Wednesday: anime magic, parks, and vintage finds">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Book Ghibli tickets in advance">Tickets sell out months ahead. Book on the official Lawson site exactly one month before your date (10 AM Japan time).</Callout>
<Steps>
<Step title="Ghibli Museum" status="done" subtitle="10:00 AM — Studio Ghibli's whimsy">A must for Spirited Away fans. No photos inside, but the rooftop garden and Catbus are worth it. Plan 2–3 hours.</Step>
<Step title="Kichijoji & Inokashira Park" status="done" subtitle="1:00 PM — Lunch & lake stroll">Take the train 5 min from Mitaka. Rent a swan boat on the lake, browse vintage shops, and eat at the famous CoCo Curry or a local ramen spot.</Step>
<Step title="Shimokitazawa Vintage" status="done" subtitle="4:00 PM — The Brooklyn of Tokyo">Two streets of vintage clothing, indie record stores, and quirky cafés. Great for unique souvenirs.</Step>
<Step title="Koenji Night" status="done" subtitle="7:30 PM — Punk rock & curry">Known for its punk scene and cheap curry. Try the famous "Buddha Curry" or catch live music at a tiny venue.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 — Odaiba, TeamLab & Shibuya Sky: The Grand Finale" subtitle="Thursday: art, tech, and one last sunset">
<Steps>
<Step title="TeamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="9:00 AM — Immersive art">Walk through water and digital installations. Wear shorts or roll up pants — you'll wade through knee-deep water. Book tickets online (¥3,200 each).</Step>
<Step title="Toyosu Market" status="done" subtitle="11:30 AM — New wholesale market">Watch tuna auctions (early morning, limited spots) or just eat at the sushi counters. The observation deck is free.</Step>
<Step title="Odaiba Seaside" status="done" subtitle="2:00 PM — Rainbow Bridge & Gundam">Relax on the artificial beach, see the life-size Unicorn Gundam, and shop at DiverCity. The view of the Rainbow Bridge is postcard-perfect.</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Sky (sunset)" status="done" subtitle="5:30 PM — Golden hour finale">The rooftop observation deck at Shibuya Scramble Square. Book 4:45 PM slot to catch sunset (around 5:15 PM in late October).</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Getting around">
| Transport | Cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Suica / Pasmo IC card** | ¥500 deposit + balance | Everything — tap in/out, convenience stores |
| **Tokyo Metro 24/48/72-hour pass** | ¥800/¥1,200/¥1,500 | Heavy subway days (Days 1–3) |
| **JR Pass (7-day)** | ¥29,650 | Only if also visiting Kyoto/Osaka |
| **Taxis** | ¥500–1,500 per trip | Late nights, luggage days |

<Callout tone="success" title="Pro tip">Get a Suica card at any station machine (English available). It works on trains, buses, and even vending machines. You can refund the deposit at the end.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Final tips">
- **Start early** (8–9 AM) to beat crowds at major sights.
- **Book online** for Ghibli, TeamLab, Shibuya Sky, and Skytree — walk-ups often sell out.
- **Use the subway app** (Google Maps or Japan Travel by Navitime) — it tells you exact platforms and exit numbers.
- **Taxis are for late nights** — the last train is around midnight, and taxis cost ¥2,000+ for short trips.
- **Stay hydrated** — vending machines are everywhere, ¥100–150 per bottle.

<Callout tone="success" title="You've got this">Tokyo is incredibly safe, clean, and easy to navigate even without Japanese. The hardest part will be deciding what to eat next!</Callout>
