# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A relaxed, well-paced itinerary balancing iconic sights, local neighborhoods, great food, and a bit of romance. <Badge tone="info">Best for first-timers</Badge>

<Card title="Day-by-day plan">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 – Arrival & Shinjuku">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Arrive & check in" status="todo">Take the Narita Express (1 hr) to Shinjuku. Drop bags at hotel, grab a quick coffee.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shinjuku Gyoen" status="todo">Lunch at the park's teahouse, then wander the gardens. Entry ¥500 each.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="todo">Dinner at a tiny yakitori bar in Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane). Then bar-hop in Golden Gai's 200+ micro-bars.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2 – Asakusa & Sumida">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="todo">Beat the crowds — arrive by 8am. Walk Nakamise-dori shopping street for souvenirs.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Sumida River cruise" status="todo">Take the water bus (¥1,200) to Hamarikyu Gardens. Have matcha at the teahouse on the pond.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Skytree" status="todo">Sunset from the observation deck (¥2,100). Book a window seat at the sky restaurant for dinner.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 3 – Shibuya & Harajuku">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Meiji Shrine" status="todo">A serene forest shrine, 10 min from Harajuku station. Free entry.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Harajuku & Omotesando" status="todo">Lunch at a crepe stand, browse Takeshita-dori, then walk the tree-lined Omotesando for shopping.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Shibuya Crossing & dinner" status="todo">Watch the scramble crossing from Starbucks. Dinner at a hidden izakaya in Nonbei Yokocho.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 4 – Day trip: Hakone">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Romancecar to Hakone" status="todo">Take the 8am Limited Express (1.5 hrs, ¥2,400 each).</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Lake Ashi & Mt. Fuji view" status="todo">Cruise the lake, ride the ropeway to Owakudani volcano valley (black eggs!).</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Onsen & return" status="todo">Soak at an onsen with Mt. Fuji views (Yunessun or Hakone-Yuryo). Return by 8pm.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 5 – TeamLab & Departure">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: teamLab Planets" status="todo">Book the 9:30am slot. Immersive digital art, barefoot in water. ¥3,200 each.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Toyosu Market & last lunch" status="todo">Sushi breakfast at the market (arrive by 11am), then stroll the waterfront.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Departure" status="todo">Pick up bags, take the Narita Express to the airport (allow 2 hrs before flight).</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>
