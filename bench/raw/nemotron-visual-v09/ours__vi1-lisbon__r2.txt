<Tab title="Day 1: Belém & Riverside">
<Card title="Day 1 — Belém & Riverside" subtitle="Historic monuments, pastries, and river views">
**Morning**
- 09:00 — Start at **Pastéis de Belém** for the original custard tarts (expect a queue)
- 10:00 — **Jerónimos Monastery** (Mosteiro dos Jerónimos) — UNESCO site, Manueline architecture
- 11:30 — **Belém Tower** (Torre de Belém) — iconic fortress on the Tagus

**Lunch**
- 13:00 — **Restaurante À Margem** (riverfront, seafood rice) or **Casa do Leão** (inside castle walls, book ahead)

**Afternoon**
- 15:00 — **Padrão dos Descobrimentos** — monument to the Age of Discovery, elevator to viewpoint
- 16:00 — **MAAT** (Museum of Art, Architecture & Technology) — contemporary exhibitions, riverside walk
- 18:00 — Sunset drinks at **Topo Belém** rooftop or **Darwin's Café** (MAAT)

**Evening**
- 20:00 — Dinner at **Feitoria** (Michelin-star, Altis Belém) or **Tasca do Joel** (traditional, great bacalhau)
</Card>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2: Alfama, Castle & Mouraria">
<Card title="Day 2 — Alfama, Castle & Mouraria" subtitle="Oldest districts, fado, and viewpoints">
**Morning**
- 09:00 — Coffee at **Café da Fábrica** (Mouraria)
- 09:30 — **São Jorge Castle** (Castelo de São Jorge) — explore walls, cameras obscura, peacocks
- 11:30 — Wander **Alfama** maze: Sé Cathedral, Casa dos Bicos, narrow alleys

**Lunch**
- 13:00 — **Taberna da Rua das Flores** (book early) or **O Trigueirinho** (home-style Portuguese)

**Afternoon**
- 15:00 — **National Pantheon** (Santa Engrácia) — dome views
- 16:00 — **Fado Museum** (Museu do Fado) or **Miradouro de Santa Luzia** / **Portas do Sol** for photos
- 17:30 — Tram 28 ride (board at Martim Moniz, get off at Graça) — or walk up to **Miradouro da Graça**

**Evening**
- 19:30 — Fado show + dinner at **Clube de Fado** (Alfama) or **Mesa de Frades** (intimate, former chapel)
- 22:00 — Nightcap at **Foxtrot** (speakeasy) or **Pavilhão Chinês** (eclectic bar-museum)
</Card>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3: Baixa, Chiado & Modern Lisbon">
<Card title="Day 3 — Baixa, Chiado & Modern Lisbon" subtitle="Grand squares, elevators, creative hubs">
**Morning**
- 09:00 — **Praça do Comércio** — arch, riverfront, Lisboa Story Centre
- 10:00 — **Rua Augusta Arch** elevator to viewpoint
- 10:30 — **Santa Justa Lift** (Elevador de Santa Justa) — neo-Gothic iron lift, city views
- 11:00 — **Carmo Convent** ruins & archaeological museum

**Lunch**
- 13:00 — **Time Out Market** (Mercado da Ribeira) — 30+ stalls, try **Marisqueira Azul** (seafood) or **Croquetaria**

**Afternoon**
- 15:00 — **Chiado**: Bertrand Bookstore (world's oldest), São Roque Church, shopping
- 16:00 — **Bairro Alto** — street art, vintage shops, **Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara**
- 17:30 — **LX Factory** (tram 15 or Uber) — creative campus: Ler Devagar bookshop, shops, street art

**Evening**
- 19:30 — Dinner at **Taberna da Esquina** (modern Portuguese) or **O Asiático** (Portuguese-Asian fusion)
- 21:30 — Drinks at **Park Bar** (rooftop on parking garage) or **Red Frog** (speakeasy)
</Card>
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Details title="Money-saving tips" open="false">
- Buy a **24h Carris/Metro pass (€6.80)** instead of single tickets — covers trams, buses, metro, lifts
- **Lisboa Card** (24h €21 / 48h €35 / 72h €44) — free entry to many monuments + unlimited transport; worth it if you hit 4+ paid sites
- Eat **prato do dia** (dish of the day) at lunch — €10–15 for soup, main, drink, coffee
- **Free viewpoints**: Portas do Sol, Santa Luzia, Graça, São Pedro de Alcântara, Parque Eduardo VII
- Walk — central Lisbon is compact; hills are steep but save transport costs
- Book **Jerónimos Monastery & Belém Tower** online to skip lines
</Details>
