# Low-Light Houseplants for a Dark Flat

Four reliable choices that tolerate minimal light, with care notes and cat safety.

<Card title="Snake Plant (Sansevieria)" subtitle="Very low light tolerant">
![Snake plant with upright variegated leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (let soil dry completely)
<Badge tone="danger">Toxic to cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)" subtitle="Thrives in shade">
![ZZ plant with glossy dark green leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (drought tolerant)
<Badge tone="danger">Toxic to cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)" subtitle="Adaptable, trails or climbs">
![Pothos vine with heart-shaped green leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 1–2 weeks (when top inch of soil is dry)
<Badge tone="danger">Toxic to cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Cast Iron Plant (Aspidistra elatior)" subtitle="Nearly indestructible">
![Cast iron plant with broad dark green leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (keep evenly moist, not soggy)
<Badge tone="success">Safe for cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick tips">
- All four prefer indirect light but survive in dark corners.
- Water less in winter; overwatering is the main risk.
- Rotate pots occasionally for even growth.
</Callout>
