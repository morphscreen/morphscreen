<Card title="Tips for Avoiding Crowds">
<Details title="Visit early or late" open="true">
- Arrive at **9 am opening** (or 9:30 am on weekends) and head straight to the Denon Wing for Mona Lisa and Winged Victory before the tour groups arrive.
- Alternatively, come **after 4 pm** on Wednesdays or Fridays (open until 9:45 pm); crowds thin noticeably in the last two hours.
</Details>

<Details title="Buy a timed-entry ticket online">
- Reserve a specific entry slot on the Louvre website. Skip the Pyramid security line by using the **Porte des Lions** or **Carrousel du Louvre** entrances (both accept timed tickets).
</Details>

<Details title="Use the “secret” routes">
- From the Pyramid, take the **escalator to the Sully Wing** first for Venus de Milo—most visitors turn right toward Denon.
- After Mona Lisa, exit Room 711 toward the **Grand Galerie** and loop back via the **French Paintings** rooms; you’ll bypass the main return bottleneck.
</Details>

<Details title="Visit on a weekday">
- Tuesdays are closed—avoid Mondays (busiest) and weekends. **Wednesday and Thursday mornings** are consistently quietest.
</Details>

<Details title="See the “second-tier” masterpieces first">
- The **Coronation of Napoleon**, **Liberty Leading the People**, and **The Raft of the Medusa** are in nearby rooms and often have shorter lines. Visit them while waiting for the Mona Lisa queue to ebb.
</Details>
</Card>
