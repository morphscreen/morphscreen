## 🗺️ Best Order for a Single Day

<Steps>
<Step title="08:00 – Kinkaku-ji" status="done" subtitle="Northwest Kyoto • Golden Pavilion">
Arrive at opening to beat tour buses. Walk the circuit around the pond for the classic reflection shot. Allow 60–75 min.
</Step>
<Step title="09:30 – Ryoan-ji" status="done" subtitle="5-min bus/taxi from Kinkaku-ji">
Contemplate the 15-rock Zen garden from the veranda. Visit the tea garden and Kyoyochi Pond. Allow 45–60 min.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Ginkaku-ji" status="current" subtitle="Northeast Kyoto • Silver Pavilion">
Take bus 101/204 or taxi (~30 min). Stroll the moss garden, sand cone (Ginshanada), and the Philosopher's Path toward Nanzen-ji if time allows. Allow 90 min.
</Step>
<Step title="13:30 – Lunch near Ginkaku-ji" status="todo" subtitle="Yudofu (tofu hot pot) or soba on the Philosopher's Path">
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – Kiyomizu-dera" status="todo" subtitle="East Kyoto • Wooden Stage">
Bus 100 or taxi (~20 min). Explore the main hall stage, Jishu Love Shrine, and drink from Otowa Waterfall's three streams. Stay for sunset if season permits. Allow 2+ hours.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="info" title="Transport Tips">
- **Bus day pass** (¥700) covers all routes above. Buy on any city bus.
- **Taxi** between northwest and northeast temples saves 20–30 min per hop (~¥1,500–2,000 each).
- **IC card** (ICOCA/Suica) works on buses and subways if you prefer pay-as-you-go.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Seasonal Notes">
- **Cherry blossoms** (late Mar–early Apr): Kiyomizu-dera and Philosopher's Path are spectacular but crowded.
- **Autumn foliage** (mid–late Nov): All four temples glow; arrive 30 min earlier at each.
- **New Year** (Jan 1–3): Temples open early for hatsumode; expect massive crowds.
</Callout>
