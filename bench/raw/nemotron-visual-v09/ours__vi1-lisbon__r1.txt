<Card title="Day 1 — Historic Belém & Riverside" subtitle="Morning to evening">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Pastéis de Belém" status="done" subtitle="Try the original custard tarts at the historic bakery">
Warm pastéis de nata with cinnamon and powdered sugar. Arrive early to avoid the queue.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — Jerónimos Monastery" status="done" subtitle="Manueline masterpiece, UNESCO World Heritage">
Allow 1.5 hours for the church and cloisters. Buy tickets online to skip the line.
</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Belém Tower" status="done" subtitle="Iconic fortress on the Tagus River">
Climb to the top for river views. Combined ticket with Jerónimos saves €2.
</Step>
<Step title="13:30 — Lunch at Ponto Final (Almada)" status="current" subtitle="Ferry across the river for riverside seafood">
Take the ferry from Belém to Cacilhas (€1.50). Grilled fish, house wine, Lisbon skyline views.
</Step>
<Step title="15:30 — MAAT or Coach Museum" status="todo" subtitle="Modern art museum or royal carriages">
MAAT for contemporary art and architecture; Coach Museum for gilded royal carriages.
</Step>
<Step title="18:00 — Sunset at Miradouro de Santa Catarina" status="todo" subtitle="Drinks with a view over the 25 de Abril Bridge">
Known as "Adamastor," popular sunset spot with kiosk bars.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 — Alfama, Castle & Mouraria" subtitle="Lisbon's oldest neighborhoods">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Breakfast at Café da Garagem" status="done" subtitle="Hidden gem with castle views">
Coffee and toast on the terrace overlooking the city.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — São Jorge Castle" status="done" subtitle="Moorish castle with panoramic views">
Walk the ramparts, visit the archaeological site, peek at the camera obscura.
</Step>
<Step title="12:30 — Wander Alfama's alleys" status="current" subtitle="Get lost in Lisbon's oldest district">
Follow Rua de São Miguel, find hidden miradouros (Portas do Sol, Santa Luzia).
</Step>
<Step title="13:30 — Lunch at Casa de Linhares" status="todo" subtitle="Fado house with traditional Portuguese food">
Try bacalhau à brás or grilled sardines. Fado shows start around 19:00.
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 — National Tile Museum (Museu do Azulejo)" status="todo" subtitle="Stunning azulejo panels in a former convent">
Highlight: the 23-meter Lisbon panorama before the 1755 earthquake.
</Step>
<Step title="17:00 — Mouraria & Ginjinha" status="todo" subtitle="Birthplace of fado, cherry liqueur tasting">
Stop at Ginjinha Sem Rival (€1.50 per shot) or Ginjinha do Carmo.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 — Dinner at Taberna da Rua das Flores" status="todo" subtitle="Small plates, natural wine, no reservations">
Arrive by 19:00 or join the waitlist. Seasonal menu changes daily.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 — Baixa, Chiado & Modern Lisbon" subtitle="Downtown, shopping & creative hubs">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Coffee at Fábrica Coffee Roasters" status="done" subtitle="Specialty coffee in Chiado">
Excellent espresso, pastries, and brunch options.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — Elevador de Santa Justa & Carmo Convent" status="done" subtitle="Neo-Gothic lift and ruined convent">
Buy the €5.50 combo ticket for lift + convent. Views from the top platform.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 — Praça do Comércio & Rua Augusta Arch" status="current" subtitle="Grand riverside square and triumphal arch">
Climb the arch for a 360° city view. Walk the riverside promenade.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 — Lunch at Time Out Market" status="todo" subtitle="Food hall with 30+ stalls from top chefs">
Try Sea Me (sushi), Marisqueira Azul (seafood), or Croquetaria (croquettes).
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 — LX Factory" status="todo" subtitle="Creative campus in a former industrial complex">
Bookshops, concept stores, street art, Ler Devagar bookshop, rooftop bars.
</Step>
<Step title="17:30 — Tram 28 ride" status="todo" subtitle="Classic yellow tram through historic hills">
Board at Martim Moniz or Prazeres. Watch for pickpockets; hold bags in front.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 — Farewell dinner at Bairro do Avillez" status="todo" subtitle="José Avillez's multi-concept food court">
Taberna (tapas), Páteo (seafood), Mini Bar (tasting menu). Book ahead.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Money-Saving Tips">
- **Lisboa Card** (€45/48h): free entry to 30+ museums, unlimited public transport — worth it if you hit 4+ paid sites
- **Free entry**: Many museums free on Sunday mornings until 14:00
- **Walk**: Alfama, Baixa, Chiado are best explored on foot; saves tram fares
- **Eat like a local**: Prato do dia (dish of the day) at tasca for €8–12 including drink
- **Water**: Tap water is safe; refill bottles at fountains
</Callout>
