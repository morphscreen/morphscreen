# Spaghetti Carbonara

![Spaghetti carbonara in a white bowl with creamy sauce, pancetta, and parsley](find)

<Card title="Time">
<Columns>
<Stat label="Prep" value="10 min" icon="clock" />
<Stat label="Cook" value="15 min" icon="fire" />
<Stat label="Total" value="25 min" icon="timer" />
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Ingredients (serves 4)">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Spaghetti | 400 g (14 oz) |
| Guanciale or pancetta | 150 g (5 oz), diced |
| Egg yolks | 4 large |
| Whole egg | 1 large |
| Pecorino Romano | 80 g (¾ cup), finely grated |
| Parmigiano Reggiano | 40 g (⅓ cup), finely grated |
| Black pepper | 1–2 tsp, freshly cracked |
| Salt | For pasta water |
</Card>

<Card title="Steps">
<Steps>
<Step title="Heat water" status="done">
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti until al dente (about 1 minute less than package). Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
</Step>
<Step title="Crisp the guanciale" status="done">
While pasta cooks, place guanciale in a cold large pan over medium heat. Render fat until golden and crisp, 5–7 minutes. Remove from heat.
</Step>
<Step title="Make the sauce" status="done">
In a bowl, whisk egg yolks, whole egg, both cheeses, and plenty of black pepper into a thick paste.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine" status="current">
Add drained spaghetti to the pan with guanciale and rendered fat. Toss over **very low heat** (or off heat) to coat. Remove from heat completely.
</Step>
<Step title="Finish" status="todo">
Quickly pour egg-cheese mixture over pasta, tossing vigorously. Add splashes of reserved pasta water until a glossy, creamy sauce forms. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Key tips">
- **No cream** — the sauce emulsifies from eggs, cheese, and pasta water  
- **Off heat** when adding eggs prevents scrambling  
- **Guanciale** is traditional; pancetta works well too  
- Work fast at the end — the residual heat cooks the eggs gently
</Callout>
