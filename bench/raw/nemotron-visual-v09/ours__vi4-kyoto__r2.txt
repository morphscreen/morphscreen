<Card title="Opening Hours & Entry Fees">
| Temple | Hours | Entry Fee |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion) | 9:00–17:00 daily | ¥500 |
| **Kiyomizu-dera** | 6:00–18:00 (varies seasonally) | ¥400 |
| **Fushimi Inari Taisha** | 24 hours (shrine grounds) | Free |
| **Ryoan-ji** | 8:00–17:00 (Mar–Nov), 8:30–16:30 (Dec–Feb) | ¥600 |
</Card>

<Card title="Best One-Day Order" subtitle="Start early, move north to south">
<Steps>
<Step title="7:00 – Fushimi Inari Taisha" status="done" subtitle="Beat the crowds at the torii gates">
Arrive at opening. Hike to the summit (2–3 hrs round trip) or stop at Yotsutsuji intersection for city views. No entry fee.
</Step>
<Step title="10:30 – Kiyomizu-dera" status="current" subtitle="Walk Higashiyama's preserved lanes">
Bus/taxi 20 min. Explore the main hall, Jishu Shrine, and Otowa Waterfall. Allow 1.5 hrs. Nearby Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka for snacks.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch in Gion" status="todo" subtitle="Tofu, yudofu, or kaiseki near Yasaka Shrine">
Walk 10 min from Kiyomizu-dera. Reserve if aiming for kaiseki.
</Step>
<Step title="14:30 – Kinkaku-ji" status="todo" subtitle="Golden Pavilion at its brightest">
Bus 30 min (Kyoto City Bus 101/205). Visit takes 45–60 min. Gardens are compact; late afternoon light hits the gold leaf beautifully.
</Step>
<Step title="16:00 – Ryoan-ji" status="todo" subtitle="End with Zen stillness">
Bus 10 min (Bus 59) or taxi 5 min. Sit at the rock garden viewing platform. Grounds close 17:00 (16:30 winter).
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Transit Tips">
- Buy a **Kyoto City Bus & Subway One-Day Pass** (¥900) for unlimited rides.
- Buses 101 and 205 connect Kyoto Station → Kiyomizu → Kinkaku-ji → Ryoan-ji.
- Taxis between northern temples (Kinkaku-ji ↔ Ryoan-ji) cost ~¥1,000 and save 20 min.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Seasonal Notes">
- **Cherry blossom (late Mar–early Apr)** and **autumn foliage (mid–late Nov)**: arrive 30 min earlier at each site; expect queues.
- Kiyomizu-dera extends hours for night illuminations in spring/autumn (check dates).
- Ryoan-ji closes 30 min earlier Dec–Feb.
</Callout>
