# Louvre Quick Guide

Three icons you can't miss, where to find them, and how to beat the queues.

---

<Card title="Mona Lisa" subtitle="Leonardo da Vinci · c. 1503–1519">
![Mona Lisa](find)

**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 711 (Salle des États)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → follow signs for "Peintures italiennes"
</Card>

<Card title="Venus de Milo" subtitle="Alexandros of Antioch · c. 130–100 BC">
![Venus de Milo](find)

**Location:** Sully Wing, Level 0, Room 346 (Galerie des Antiques)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → Sully Wing → follow signs for "Antiquités grecques"
</Card>

<Card title="Winged Victory of Samothrace" subtitle="Hellenistic · c. 200–190 BC">
![Winged Victory of Samothrace](find)

**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 703 (top of the Daru staircase)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → Denon Wing → head straight for the grand staircase
</Card>

<Card title="Crowd-smart tips" subtitle="Visit calmer, see more">
<Columns>
<Details title="Time it right" open="false">
- **First hour** (9–10 am) or **last two hours** before closing are quietest  
- **Wednesday & Friday evenings** (open until 9:45 pm) are far less packed  
- Avoid Tuesdays (closed) → Monday & Saturday are busiest
</Details>

<Details title="Buy smart" open="false">
- **Pre-book a timed ticket** online (official site or partners)  
- **Paris Museum Pass** holders still need a free time-slot reservation  
- Use the **Porte des Lions** or **Carrousel** entrances when open — shorter lines than the Pyramid
</Details>

<Details title="Route hacks" open="false">
- See the **Mona Lisa first** (or last) — the room clears fast once the tour groups move on  
- **Venus de Milo** and **Winged Victory** are on opposite wings; hit one wing fully before crossing  
- Download the **official Louvre app** for real-time room density and audio guides
</Details>

<Details title="Quick comfort" open="false">
- Wear comfortable shoes — the palace is 72,000 m² of marble floors  
- Bring a water bottle (refill at fountains) and a light layer — galleries vary in temperature  
- Free lockers at each entrance for bags > 55 × 35 × 20 cm
</Details>
</Columns>
</Card>
