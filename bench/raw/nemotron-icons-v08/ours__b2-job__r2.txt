# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

---

<Card title="About this role" icon="briefcase">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design team and help shape the future of our core product. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to define and deliver intuitive, accessible experiences for millions of users. This is a high-impact role where you'll own end-to-end design for key product areas, contribute to our design system, and mentor junior designers.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do" icon="list">
- Lead design for complex product features from concept through launch
- Collaborate with cross-functional partners to define problem spaces and success metrics
- Create user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Conduct and synthesize user research to validate design decisions
- Contribute to and evolve our design system components and patterns
- Mentor and provide feedback to other designers on the team
- Advocate for accessibility, usability, and design quality across the organization
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications" icon="star">
<Columns>
<Tab title="Required">
- 5+ years of product design experience at a tech company or digital agency
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design process and shipped products
- Expertise in Figma (or equivalent) and modern design tools
- Deep understanding of user-centered design, interaction patterns, and accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- Experience working in agile, cross-functional teams
</Tab>
<Tab title="Preferred">
- Experience designing for B2B SaaS or developer-facing products
- Familiarity with design tokens and design system governance
- Prototyping with code (React, HTML/CSS, or Framer)
- Background in design research or content design
- Experience mentoring or managing other designers
</Tab>
</Columns>
</Card>
