# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team** · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)

---

<Card title="About the role" subtitle="Shape the future of our core product experience">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to lead design for our core collaboration features. You'll own the end-to-end design process—from research and ideation through high-fidelity prototypes and handoff—working closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers. This is a high-impact role where your work directly shapes how thousands of teams collaborate daily.
</Card>

---

<Card title="Qualifications" icon="list-checks">
<Columns>
<Tab title="Must have">
- 5+ years product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products with measurable impact
- Expert in Figma; comfortable with design systems and component libraries
- Experience running user research and usability testing
- Excellent written and visual communication skills
</Tab>
<Tab title="Nice to have">
- Experience with B2B collaboration tools
- Motion design / prototyping in Principle or After Effects
- Front-end development knowledge (HTML/CSS/React)
- Design ops or design system contribution experience
</Tab>
</Columns>
</Card>
