# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

---

<Card title="About the role" icon="briefcase">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for high-impact features, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for craft across the team. This is a high-autonomy role with direct influence on product strategy.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do" icon="list-checks">
- Lead design for complex, cross-functional projects from discovery through launch
- Collaborate with PMs, engineers, and researchers to define problems and validate solutions
- Contribute to and evolve our design system
- Mentor other designers and run design critiques
- Advocate for user needs while balancing business goals and technical constraints
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications" icon="star">
| Required | Preferred |
|---|---|
| 5+ years product design experience | Experience with B2B SaaS |
| Strong portfolio showing shipped products | Design systems expertise |
| Fluency in Figma and prototyping tools | Front-end development basics (HTML/CSS/JS) |
| Excellent communication and storytelling | Motion design / micro-interactions |
| Experience working in agile, cross-functional teams | Prior remote-first experience |
</Card>
