# Senior Product Designer
**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

<Card title="About this role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for major features, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for craft across the team. This is a high-autonomy role with direct impact on how thousands of teams work together every day.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for complex, cross-functional product initiatives from discovery through delivery
- Collaborate with PMs, engineers, and researchers to define problems and validate solutions
- Contribute to and evolve our design system, patterns, and processes
- Mentor other designers and participate in design reviews
- Advocate for user needs while balancing business goals and technical constraints
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
<Columns>
<Details title="Must have" open="true">
- 6+ years of product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products with measurable impact
- Expert-level Figma skills and fluency with design systems
- Excellent written and visual communication
- Experience conducting and synthesizing user research
</Details>
<Details title="Nice to have" open="false">
- Experience with B2B collaboration or productivity tools
- Front-end development knowledge (React, CSS)
- Motion design and prototyping with Principle or Framer
- Design management or mentorship experience
</Details>
</Columns>
</Card>
