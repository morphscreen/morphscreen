# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

---

<Card title="About the role" subtitle="Shape the future of our core product experience">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design team and help craft intuitive, delightful experiences for millions of users. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to define and ship features that solve real problems. This is a high-impact role where you'll own the end-to-end design process—from discovery and ideation through to polished execution and iteration.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for major product initiatives across web and mobile
- Collaborate with PMs and engineers to define product strategy and roadmap
- Conduct user research and usability testing to validate decisions
- Build and maintain our design system and component library
- Mentor junior designers and advocate for design excellence
- Present work to stakeholders and incorporate feedback thoughtfully
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- **5+ years** of product design experience at a tech company or agency
- Strong portfolio showing shipped consumer or B2B products
- Expertise in Figma, prototyping, and design systems
- Deep understanding of user-centered design processes
- Excellent communication and storytelling skills
- Experience mentoring designers and collaborating cross-functionally
- **Nice to have:** motion design, accessibility expertise, frontend coding skills
</Card>
