# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

---

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for high-impact features, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for craft across the team. This is a high-autonomy role with direct influence on product strategy.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for complex, user-facing features from concept to launch
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights into actionable designs
- Build and maintain design system components and patterns
- Mentor junior designers and contribute to design culture
- Collaborate with PMs and engineers to define product strategy
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products and design process
- Expert in Figma; comfortable with prototyping and motion
- Experience with design systems and component libraries
- Excellent written and visual communication skills
- Ability to give and receive constructive design feedback
</Card>
