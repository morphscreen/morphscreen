# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team** · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)

---

<Card title="About the Role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our growing Design team. You'll own the end-to-end design process for core product features—from discovery and research through high-fidelity prototypes and handoff. You'll collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to shape intuitive, accessible experiences that help our customers succeed.

This is a high-impact role where you'll help define our design system, mentor junior designers, and contribute to the overall design strategy.
</Card>

<Card title="Key Qualifications">
- **5+ years** of product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products and measurable impact
- Expertise in Figma, prototyping, and design systems
- Experience conducting user research and usability testing
- Excellent communication and cross-functional collaboration skills
- Passion for accessibility and inclusive design
- Bonus: experience with design tokens, motion design, or frontend development (React/CSS)
</Card>
