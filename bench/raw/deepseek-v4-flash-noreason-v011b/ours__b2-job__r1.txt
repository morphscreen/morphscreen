# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team** · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a product designer who thrives on turning complex problems into simple, elegant experiences. You'll own end-to-end design for our core platform, working closely with product managers and engineers to ship features that serve millions of users.
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- **5+ years** of product design experience (web or mobile)
- **Strong portfolio** showing end-to-end product work
- **Proficient in Figma** and prototyping tools
- **Experience with design systems** and component libraries
- **User research** and usability testing skills
- **Excellent communication** and cross-functional collaboration
</Card>
