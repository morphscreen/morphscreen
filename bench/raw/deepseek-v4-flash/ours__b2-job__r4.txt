# Product Designer
**Design Systems Team** · Remote (North America) · Full-time

<Badge tone="info">Full-time</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Remote</Badge> <Badge tone="success">Accepting applications</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a product designer to own and evolve the Acme design system — the tokens, components, and guidelines that power every product. You'll partner with engineers and brand designers to craft accessible, consistent experiences across web, iOS, and Android. You'll be the third member of the Design Systems Team, reporting to the Head of Design.
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
- **4+ years in product design**, with a portfolio that shows end-to-end thinking from problem to shipped interface
- **Deep Figma fluency**, including component libraries, variables, and token workflows
- **Accessibility chops** — you design with WCAG 2.1 AA in mind and advocate for inclusive patterns
- **Engineering empathy** — you can speak the language of themes, tokens, and design-to-code handoff
- **Clear communication** — you write crisp documentation and present decisions with confidence
</Card>
