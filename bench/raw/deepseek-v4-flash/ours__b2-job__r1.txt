# Product Designer

<Badge tone="info">Design Systems Team</Badge> · Acme Studio · Remote (US)

<Card title="The role" subtitle="Shape the visual language across all Acme products">
We're looking for a product designer to help us build a cohesive, accessible design language that powers everything we ship. You'll partner closely with engineers, product managers, and researchers — taking work from rough concept to polished, shippable experience. This is a hands-on role for someone who loves detail, systems thinking, and making complex things feel simple.
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for" subtitle="Main qualifications for the role">
- **4+ years** of experience in product design, with a strong portfolio showing end-to-end work
- **Deep proficiency in Figma**, prototyping tools, and modern design workflows
- **Experience building or maintaining design systems**, tokens, and component libraries
- **Collaborative instincts** — you're comfortable giving and receiving critique, and working in the open
- **A working knowledge of accessibility** (WCAG) and inclusive design practices
</Card>
