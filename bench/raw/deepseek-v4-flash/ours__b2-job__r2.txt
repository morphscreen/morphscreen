# Senior Product Designer

<Badge tone="info">Design Systems Team</Badge> <Badge tone="success">Full-time</Badge>

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to help us build and scale the design language used across every product at our company.

<Card title="About the role">
You'll partner with product managers, engineers, and brand designers to define and maintain a cohesive design system — from tokens and components to documentation and governance. You'll work across the full design process: auditing, prototyping, testing, and shipping. If you're someone who sweats the details and thinks in systems, this role is for you.
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
We're looking for a designer who combines craft, empathy, and systems thinking. Here's what we consider essential:

- 5+ years of product design experience, with at least 1 year dedicated to design systems
- A portfolio that demonstrates end-to-end product work and a clear design process
- Deep proficiency with Figma, including variables, component properties, and libraries
- Experience writing design documentation and building adoption across teams
- Strong understanding of accessibility (WCAG 2.2) and inclusive design practices
- Comfort facilitating design critiques and giving feedback that raises the bar
</Card>
