<Card title="Day 1 — Arrival & Shinjuku Neon">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check in & settle" status="done" subtitle="Shinjuku area">Drop bags at your hotel. Pick a spot near Shinjuku Station for convenience — it's Tokyo's busiest hub.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Memory Lane">Tiny yakitori stalls under train tracks. Try the grilled chicken skewers and beer — a classic Tokyo welcome.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shinjuku Gyoen" status="done" subtitle="National Garden">A peaceful 144-acre park with Japanese, French, and English gardens. Perfect for a stroll after lunch.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building" status="done" subtitle="Free observation deck">Head to the 45th floor for a free panoramic view of Tokyo — Mount Fuji visible on clear days.</Step>
<Step title="Night: Golden Gai" status="done" subtitle="Tiny bars">Over 200 miniature bars crammed into six alleys. Pick one with space — most seat 4–6 people. Great for a drink.</Step>
</Steps>
<Callout tone="info" title="Pro tip">Get an IC card (Suica or Pasmo) at the airport — it works on all trains, buses, and most convenience stores. Load ¥5,000 each to start.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 — Old Tokyo: Asakusa & Ueno">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's oldest temple">Start at the iconic Kaminarimon Gate, walk through Nakamise shopping street, and reach the main hall. Try a fortune (omikuji) — ¥100.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Tempura near the temple" status="done" subtitle="Traditional lunch">Try tendon (tempura rice bowl) at a local spot. Dai-ichi Tenyasu is a favorite — expect a short line.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ueno Park & Museum" status="done" subtitle="Culture & pandas">Stroll Ueno Park, visit the Tokyo National Museum (¥1,000) for Japanese art, or see the pandas at Ueno Zoo (¥600).</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Ameya-Yokocho Market" status="done" subtitle="Street food & bargains">A lively market street with food stalls, discount shops, and street snacks. Try the taiyaki (fish-shaped cake with red bean).</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 3 — Youth Culture: Shibuya & Harajuku">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shibuya Crossing" status="done" subtitle="The world's busiest intersection">Watch the scramble crossing from the Starbucks in Shibuya Tsutaya (2F) or the MAGNET building. Then visit the Hachiko statue.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Uobei Shibuya" status="done" subtitle="Fun conveyor belt sushi">Order via touchscreen — plates zip to your seat. Great value, ¥100–¥500 per plate.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Harajuku & Takeshita Street" status="done" subtitle="Kawaii culture">Walk down Takeshita Street for quirky fashion and crepes. Visit Meiji Shrine nearby — a serene forest in the city.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Yoyogi Park" status="done" subtitle="People-watching">On weekends, catch street performers and cosplayers. Grab a drink and relax.</Step>
<Step title="Night: Omoide Yokocho or Golden Gai revisit" status="done" subtitle="Or try something new">Head to Shibuya Nonbei Yokocho — another tiny bar alley, less crowded than Golden Gai.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 — Ginza Shopping & Tsukiji Fish Market">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="Fresh seafood & street food">The inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market still has 400+ stalls. Try fresh sushi, tamagoyaki, and uni.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Sushi at Tsukiji" status="done" subtitle="The classic experience">Pick a sushi-ya for a set (omakase). Expect ¥2,000–¥5,000 per person for a quality meal.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ginza shopping" status="done" subtitle="Luxury & department stores">Walk Ginza's main avenue — flagship stores, art galleries, and the iconic Wako building. Visit Ginza Six for a rooftop garden.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Kabuki-za Theatre" status="done" subtitle="Traditional Japanese theater">Catch a kabuki performance — single-act tickets (¥3,000–¥4,000) let you watch one act without the full program.</Step>
</Steps>
<Callout tone="warning" title="Tsukiji tip">Go early — many stalls close by 2 PM. Arrive by 8 AM for the freshest selection and shortest lines.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 — Odaiba: Waterfront & Futuristic Tokyo">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: teamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="Immersive digital art">Walk through water and digital installations. Book tickets online in advance (¥3,200–¥3,800). Allow 2 hours.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Aqua City Odaiba" status="done" subtitle="Waterfront dining">Food courts and restaurants with views of the Rainbow Bridge. Try the ramen or okonomiyaki.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Odaiba Seaside Park & Gundam" status="done" subtitle="Statue & beach">See the life-size Unicorn Gundam statue at DiverCity, then walk the beach promenade.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Rainbow Bridge views" status="done" subtitle="Sunset & lights">Watch the sunset over the bridge from the park. Take the Yurikamome line back for elevated views.</Step>
</Steps>
