# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Callout tone="info" title="Why these 10?">
These are the phrases you'll actually use daily — greeting, asking, buying, and thanking. Master these and you'll get by in any Spanish-speaking country.
</Callout>

## The Essential Phrases

<Card title="Your 10 Must-Know Phrases">
<Columns>
<Card>
**1. Hola** — Hello
- Pronounced: *OH-lah*
- Use it anytime, anywhere. Universal greeting.
</Card>
<Card>
**2. Gracias** — Thank you
- Pronounced: *GRAH-see-ahs*
- Add "muchas" (MOO-chahs) for "thank you very much."
</Card>
</Columns>
<Columns>
<Card>
**3. Por favor** — Please
- Pronounced: *por fah-VOR*
- Always pair with a request: "Un café, por favor."
</Card>
<Card>
**4. ¿Cuánto cuesta?** — How much does it cost?
- Pronounced: *KWAN-toh KWES-tah*
- Essential for markets, taxis, and restaurants.
</Card>
</Columns>
<Columns>
<Card>
**5. ¿Dónde está...?** — Where is...?
- Pronounced: *DON-deh es-TAH*
- Fill in the blank: "el baño" (bathroom), "la playa" (beach).
</Card>
<Card>
**6. No entiendo** — I don't understand
- Pronounced: *no en-tee-EN-doh*
- Honest and useful. People will slow down or rephrase.
</Card>
</Columns>
<Columns>
<Card>
**7. ¿Hablas inglés?** — Do you speak English?
- Pronounced: *AH-blahs een-GLES*
- Great backup when you're stuck.
</Card>
<Card>
**8. La cuenta, por favor** — The check, please
- Pronounced: *lah KWEN-tah, por fah-VOR*
- Say this at restaurants when you're ready to pay.
</Card>
</Columns>
<Columns>
<Card>
**9. Perdón** — Excuse me / Sorry
- Pronounced: *per-DON*
- For bumping into someone, getting attention, or apologizing.
</Card>
<Card>
**10. Buenos días** — Good morning
- Pronounced: *BWEH-nos DEE-ahs*
- Swap for "Buenas tardes" (afternoon) or "Buenas noches" (evening).
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

## How to Remember Them

<Card title="Quick Memory Tricks">
- **Hola** — sounds like "oh-la" as in "oh, la!" — easy.
- **Gracias** — think "grace" (as in grace before a meal).
- **Por favor** — "for favor" — do me a favor.
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** — imagine asking "how much does this *quest* a?"
- **¿Dónde está?** — "donde" sounds like "don" (as in Don Juan) + "está" (like "estar").
- **No entiendo** — "I don't *understand*" — just remember "no" + "entend" (like "understand").
- **¿Hablas inglés?** — "hablas" = "you speak" (like "habla" in "habla español").
- **La cuenta** — "the account" — the bill is the account.
- **Perdón** — "pardon" — almost the same word!
- **Buenos días** — "good days" — literally "good days."
</Card>

<Card title="Practice Quiz">
<Quiz title="Test yourself">
<Question text="How do you say 'Thank you' in Spanish?" answer="Gracias">
- Hola
- Gracias
- Por favor
- Perdón
**Explanation:** "Gracias" means "thank you." "Hola" is "hello," "por favor" is "please," and "perdón" is "excuse me."
</Question>
<Question text="You're at a market and want to ask the price. What do you say?" answer="¿Cuánto cuesta?">
- ¿Dónde está?
- No entiendo
- ¿Cuánto cuesta?
- La cuenta, por favor
**Explanation:** "¿Cuánto cuesta?" literally means "How much does it cost?" — perfect for shopping.
</Question>
<Question text="Which phrase would you use to ask for the bathroom?" answer="¿Dónde está el baño?">
- Buenos días
- ¿Dónde está el baño?
- ¿Hablas inglés?
- Perdón
**Explanation:** "¿Dónde está el baño?" means "Where is the bathroom?" — combine phrase #5 with "el baño."
</Question>
<Question text="You bumped into someone on the street. What do you say?" answer="Perdón">
- Gracias
- Por favor
- Perdón
- Hola
**Explanation:** "Perdón" means "excuse me" or "sorry" — perfect for brief apologies.
</Question>
<Question text="It's 8 AM and you're greeting your hotel host. What do you say?" answer="Buenos días">
- Buenas noches
- Buenos días
- Hola
- Perdón
**Explanation:** "Buenos días" is "good morning," used until around noon. "Buenas noches" is for the evening.
</Question>
</Quiz>
</Card>

## Your Practice Plan

<Card title="5 Minutes a Day">
<Steps>
<Step status="done" title="Day 1: Listen and repeat">
Say each phrase out loud 5 times. Focus on pronunciation.
</Step>
<Step status="done" title="Day 2: Pair them up">
Practice "Hola, gracias" and "Perdón, ¿dónde está?" — phrases you'd use together.
</Step>
<Step status="current" title="Day 3: Use them in context">
Imagine scenarios: ordering coffee, asking for directions, paying at a restaurant.
</Step>
<Step status="todo" title="Day 4: Test yourself">
Cover the English and try to recall the Spanish. Use the quiz above.
</Step>
<Step status="todo" title="Day 5: Go live">
Use at least 5 phrases in real conversations, even if it's just "Hola" and "Gracias" to a shopkeeper.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
