# Houseplants That Are Hard to Kill

<Callout tone="success" title="Good news">Even self-described "plant killers" can keep these 10 houseplants alive. They tolerate neglect, low light, and irregular watering — perfect for beginners or busy people.</Callout>

## The Top 10 Tough Plants

<Columns>
<Card title="1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria)">
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 2-3 weeks (when soil is dry)
- **Why it's tough:** Survives weeks without water, tolerates almost any light
- **Bonus:** Purifies air at night
<Badge tone="success">Easiest</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="2. Pothos (Devil's Ivy)">
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** When leaves start to droop
- **Why it's tough:** Grows in water or soil, recovers quickly from neglect
- **Bonus:** Fast-growing trailing vine
</Card>

<Card title="3. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas)">
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 3-4 weeks
- **Why it's tough:** Stores water in rhizomes, can survive months without
- **Bonus:** Glossy, architectural leaves
</Card>

<Card title="4. Spider Plant">
- **Light:** Bright indirect
- **Water:** When top inch of soil is dry
- **Why it's tough:** Tolerates missed waterings, produces babies (spiderettes) you can propagate
- **Bonus:** Safe for pets
</Card>

<Card title="5. Aloe Vera">
- **Light:** Bright, direct sun
- **Water:** Every 2-3 weeks, deeply
- **Why it's tough:** Succulent stores water in leaves
- **Bonus:** Healing gel for burns
</Card>

<Card title="6. Peace Lily">
- **Light:** Low to medium
- **Water:** When leaves droop (they'll tell you!)
- **Why it's tough:** Dramatic droop recovers within hours of watering
- **Bonus:** White flowers, air-purifying
</Card>

<Card title="7. Jade Plant">
- **Light:** Bright, direct sun
- **Water:** Every 2-3 weeks
- **Why it's tough:** Succulent, survives neglect
- **Bonus:** Long-lived, can become a "family heirloom"
</Card>

<Card title="8. Cast Iron Plant">
- **Light:** Very low
- **Water:** Every 2-3 weeks
- **Why it's tough:** Named for its indestructibility, survives dark corners
- **Bonus:** Great for rooms with no windows
</Card>

<Card title="9. Chinese Evergreen">
- **Light:** Low to medium
- **Water:** When top 2 inches are dry
- **Why it's tough:** Tolerates low light, dry air, and missed waterings
- **Bonus:** Colorful foliage varieties
</Card>

<Card title="10. Philodendron">
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** When top inch is dry
- **Why it's tough:** Similar to pothos, very forgiving
- **Bonus:** Heart-shaped leaves, easy to propagate
</Card>
</Columns>

## The Golden Rules for Keeping Any Plant Alive

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the right pot" status="done">Drainage holes are non-negotiable. Terra cotta dries faster; plastic retains moisture.</Step>
<Step title="Water less than you think" status="done">Stick a finger 2 inches into the soil. If it's dry, water. If damp, wait. Overwatering kills more plants than underwatering.</Step>
<Step title="Match light to the plant" status="done">Read the label. A low-light plant in a sunny window will burn; a sun-lover in a dark corner will stretch and fade.</Step>
<Step title="Ignore them a little" status="done">The best thing you can do is not fuss. Most tough plants thrive on benign neglect.</Step>
</Steps>

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

<Callout tone="warning" title="The #1 killer: overwatering">
Most plants die from too much love. When in doubt, don't water. Yellow, mushy leaves mean too much water; brown, crispy leaves mean too little.
</Callout>
