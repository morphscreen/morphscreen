# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Card title="The Essential 10" subtitle="Learn these and you'll get by in most situations">

<Card title="When to Use Each Phrase">
<Steps>
<Step title="Hola" status="done" subtitle="Greeting">Say this any time of day when you meet someone — shopkeepers, waiters, locals on the street.</Step>
<Step title="Por favor" status="done" subtitle="Politeness">Add this to any request: "Un café, por favor" (A coffee, please).</Step>
<Step title="Gracias" status="done" subtitle="Politeness">Always thank people — it goes a long way. "Muchas gracias" means "thank you very much."</Step>
<Step title="Sí / No" status="done" subtitle="Basics">These two words will answer most questions you're asked.</Step>
<Step title="¿Dónde está...?" status="done" subtitle="Finding things">Follow with a place: "¿Dónde está el baño?" (Where is the bathroom?) or "¿Dónde está la estación?" (Where is the station?)</Step>
<Step title="¿Cuánto cuesta?" status="done" subtitle="Shopping">Use this at markets, souvenir shops, or restaurants before you commit.</Step>
<Step title="La cuenta, por favor" status="done" subtitle="At restaurants">When you're ready to pay, this gets you the bill.</Step>
<Step title="No entiendo" status="done" subtitle="When lost">If someone speaks too fast, say this, then ask "¿Habla inglés?"</Step>
<Step title="¿Habla inglés?" status="done" subtitle="Help">A lifesaver when you need help in English. The answer is often "Sí, un poco" (Yes, a little).</Step>
<Step title="Adiós" status="done" subtitle="Farewell">A warm goodbye. You can also say "Hasta luego" (See you later).</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick Tip">
Spanish is phonetic — words are pronounced exactly as they're written. Once you know the sounds of the letters, you can read any Spanish word aloud correctly. The "ll" sounds like a "y" (like in "yellow"), and "j" sounds like an "h" (like in "hello").
</Callout>
