# The Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets, split into two groups: the rocky inner planets and the gas/ice giants of the outer solar system. Here's a tour, from the Sun outward.

<Card title="The Inner Rocky Planets" subtitle="Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars">
These four are small, dense, and made mostly of rock and metal. They have solid surfaces and few or no moons.

<Columns>
<Plan name="Mercury" price="0.39 AU" period="from Sun" note="Smallest & fastest">
- Diameter: 4,879 km
- Day length: 59 Earth days
- Year length: 88 Earth days
- Moons: 0
- Temp: -173°C to 427°C
</Plan>
<Plan name="Venus" price="0.72 AU" period="from Sun" note="Hottest planet">
- Diameter: 12,104 km
- Day length: 243 Earth days (retrograde)
- Year length: 225 Earth days
- Moons: 0
- Temp: 462°C (lead-melting)
</Plan>
<Plan name="Earth" price="1.00 AU" period="from Sun" note="Our home">
- Diameter: 12,742 km
- Day length: 24 hours
- Year length: 365.25 days
- Moons: 1
- Temp: -88°C to 58°C
</Plan>
<Plan name="Mars" price="1.52 AU" period="from Sun" note="The Red Planet">
- Diameter: 6,779 km
- Day length: 24.6 hours
- Year length: 687 Earth days
- Moons: 2 (Phobos, Deimos)
- Temp: -153°C to 20°C
</Plan>
</Columns>

<Card title="The Outer Giant Planets" subtitle="Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune">
These are huge, cold, and mostly gas or ice. They all have ring systems and many moons.

<Columns>
<Plan name="Jupiter" price="5.20 AU" period="from Sun" note="Largest planet">
- Diameter: 139,820 km
- Day length: 9.9 hours
- Year length: 11.9 Earth years
- Moons: 95
- Temp: -108°C (cloud tops)
</Plan>
<Plan name="Saturn" price="9.58 AU" period="from Sun" note="The ringed beauty">
- Diameter: 116,460 km
- Day length: 10.7 hours
- Year length: 29.4 Earth years
- Moons: 146
- Temp: -139°C
</Plan>
<Plan name="Uranus" price="19.19 AU" period="from Sun" note="Tipped on its side">
- Diameter: 50,724 km
- Day length: 17.2 hours (retrograde)
- Year length: 84 Earth years
- Moons: 28
- Temp: -197°C
</Plan>
<Plan name="Neptune" price="30.07 AU" period="from Sun" note="Windiest planet">
- Diameter: 49,244 km
- Day length: 16.1 hours
- Year length: 165 Earth years
- Moons: 16
- Temp: -201°C
</Plan>
</Columns>
