<Card title="Key Tips for Using These Phrases">
- **Hola** works any time of day, casual or formal.
- **Perdón** gets attention politely—use it before asking someone something.
- **¿Dónde está…?** always pairs with a place: *¿Dónde está el baño?* (Where is the bathroom?)
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** is for asking prices in markets or shops.
- Smiling while saying **Gracias** goes a long way everywhere.

<Callout tone="info" title="Pronunciation secret">Spanish vowels are consistent: **a** (ah), **e** (eh), **i** (ee), **o** (oh), **u** (oo). Once you know these, you can read almost any Spanish word.</Callout>
</Card>
