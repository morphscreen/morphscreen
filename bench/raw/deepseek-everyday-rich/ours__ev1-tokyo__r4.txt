# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

Tokyo is a city that rewards curiosity—temples and neon alleys, quiet gardens and bustling fish markets. This itinerary balances iconic sights with local neighborhoods, gives you a taste of both tradition and pop culture, and includes plenty of time to wander (and eat).

<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Pick up a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at the airport—it works on all trains, subways, and buses, and even at convenience stores. You can also add it to your phone's wallet. The metro system is excellent, and Google Maps works great for navigating.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1 – Arrival & Shinjuku Nights">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check in & settle" status="done">Drop bags at your hotel. If you land early, grab a quick lunch in the neighborhood—try a *ramen* shop or a convenience store onigiri (rice ball).</Step>
<Step title="Explore Shinjuku" status="current">Wander the neon-lit streets around Kabukicho (the entertainment district), grab a coffee in a quiet back alley, and see the massive Godzilla head on the Toho building.</Step>
<Step title="Golden hour views" status="todo">Head to the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** (free!) for sunset views over the city. On a clear day you can see Mt. Fuji.</Step>
<Step title="Dinner in Omoide Yokocho" status="todo">End the night in this nostalgic alley of tiny yakitori (grilled chicken) stalls. It's cozy, smoky, and utterly Tokyo. Expect to squeeze in!</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="success" title="Pro tip">
Book a hotel with a view of **Shinjuku** or **Shibuya** for the best vibes. If you prefer quiet, stay near **Tokyo Station** or **Ginza**.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 – Old Tokyo: Asakusa & Ueno">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple" status="done">Arrive early (before 9am) to beat the crowds. Walk through the giant red **Kaminarimon** gate and down Nakamise Street, a shopping lane full of snacks and souvenirs.</Step>
<Step title="Sumida River walk" status="current">Stroll along the river toward the **Tokyo Skytree**. Cross the bridge for photo ops, or take a short river cruise.</Step>
<Step title="Ueno Park & museums" status="todo">Grab lunch near Ueno Station, then explore the park—home to the **Tokyo National Museum** (samurai armor, ukiyo-e prints) and the **Ueno Zoo** (pandas!).</Step>
<Step title="Evening in Yanaka" status="todo">Walk through **Yanaka Ginza**, a charming shopping street with old-school shops and cat statues. It's a glimpse of "shitamachi" (downtown) Tokyo.</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 3 – Shibuya & Harajuku: Pop Culture Central">
<Steps>
<Step title="Meiji Shrine" status="done">A peaceful forest shrine right in the middle of the city. Walk the gravel path, write a wish on an *ema* (wooden plaque), and soak in the calm before the chaos.</Step>
<Step title="Harajuku & Takeshita Street" status="current">Explore the colorful, quirky street of crepes, vintage shops, and kawaii culture. For a quieter side, wander **Cat Street** for trendy boutiques.</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Crossing" status="todo">Experience the famous scramble crossing—join the crowd, then watch from the **Starbucks** window or the **Shibuya Sky** observation deck.</Step>
<Step title="Dinner in a depachika" status="todo">End the day at **Shibuya Hikarie** or **Tokyu Food Show**—the basement food halls of department stores are a feast for the eyes and stomach. Grab sushi, tempura, or a bento to go.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Heads up">
Shibuya Sky is popular—**book your sunset slot online** a few days ahead. The view is worth it!
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 – Tsukiji, Ginza & Akihabara">
<Steps>
<Step title="Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done">Wake up early and head to the outer market (the inner wholesale market has moved to Toyosu). Grab a fresh seafood bowl, tamagoyaki (egg omelet), and matcha ice cream.</Step>
<Step title="Ginza stroll" status="current">Walk through Tokyo's upscale shopping district. Check out the flagship stores, the **Kabukiza Theatre**, and the **Uniqlo** flagship (12 floors!).</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Station & Marunouchi" status="todo">See the beautiful red-brick station building, then walk the **Marunouchi** area for classic architecture and high-end shops.</Step>
<Step title="Akihabara Electric Town" status="todo">End the day in the anime, manga, and electronics district. Try a *maid café* (if you're curious), browse retro game shops, or just soak in the neon glow.</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 5 – Choose Your Adventure">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Hakone Day Trip (Mt. Fuji & Onsen)">
<Steps>
<Step title="Romancecar to Hakone" status="done">Take the Odakyu **Romancecar** from Shinjuku (90 min). Book the front-row seats for panoramic views.</Step>
<Step title="Lake Ashi & the pirate ship" status="current">Cruise across the lake with Mt. Fuji as your backdrop (if the weather cooperates!).</Step>
<Step title="Hakone Open-Air Museum" status="todo">A beautiful sculpture garden with a footbath onsen. Picasso exhibition included!</Step>
<Step title="Soak in an onsen" status="todo">End the day at a ryokan (traditional inn) with a private bath, or a day-onsen like **Yunessun**.</Step>
</Steps>
<Callout tone="info" title="Note">
The **Hakone Free Pass** covers trains, buses, cable car, and the lake cruise. Worth it if you're going all the way.
</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="teamLab Planets & Odaiba">
<Steps>
<Step title="teamLab Planets" status="done">An immersive digital art museum where you walk through water and mirror rooms. Book tickets online in advance.</Step>
<Step title="Odaiba Seaside Park" status="current">See the mini Statue of Liberty, the Rainbow Bridge, and the futuristic buildings.</Step>
<Step title="DiverCity Plaza" status="todo">Check out the giant Gundam statue and the shopping mall.</Step>
<Step title="Sunset at the beach" status="todo">Grab a drink at a waterfront café and watch the sky change colors.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>
