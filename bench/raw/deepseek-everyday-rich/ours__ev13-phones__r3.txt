# iPhone 16 vs. Pixel 9: Which should you buy?

Both are excellent phones, but they're built for different people. Here's a side-by-side look to help you decide.

## What each does best

<Columns>
<Card title="Choose the iPhone 16 if…">
- You're already in the Apple ecosystem (AirDrop, iMessage, Apple Watch)
- You want the best app quality and long-term support
- You prefer a more polished, mature OS with fewer bugs
- You like MagSafe accessories and the Apple Watch
- You shoot a lot of video — it's the best in class
- You want a compact, light phone (170g)
</Card>

<Card title="Choose the Pixel 9 if…">
- You want the smoothest display (120Hz) at the same price
- You care about AI features like Call Screen, Magic Editor, and Gemini
- You want a bigger battery and faster charging
- You prefer stock Android with clean, bloat-free software
- You want 7 years of updates vs. 5
- You take lots of photos in tricky light — Pixel's camera is still the smartest
</Card>
</Columns>

Both have excellent main cameras. The iPhone tends to produce warmer, punchier colors; the Pixel leans natural with better HDR in high-contrast scenes. For video, the iPhone 16 wins clearly — 4K/60 with better stabilization and audio. For stills, the Pixel 9 often edges ahead in low light and zoom (though neither has a telephoto).

## Performance and battery

<Card title="Real-world performance">
<Columns>
<div>
**iPhone 16 (A18)**
- Geekbench 6: ~3,300 single / ~8,200 multi
- Gaming: excellent, sustained performance
- Battery: ~15–18 hours mixed use
- Charging: 25W wired, 50% in 30 min
</div>
<div>
**Pixel 9 (Tensor G4)**
- Geekbench 6: ~2,100 single / ~4,500 multi
- Gaming: good, but not flagship level
- Battery: ~18–22 hours mixed use
- Charging: 27W wired, 55% in 30 min
</div>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="info" title="Battery reality">
The Pixel 9 has a bigger battery and lasts longer on a full charge. The iPhone 16 is more efficient per watt, but the Pixel's larger cell wins in day-to-day use.
</Callout>
</Card>

## Which one should you pick?

<Card title="Decision guide">
<Steps>
<Step title="Are you locked into Apple?" status="current">
If you use an iPhone, Apple Watch, or rely on iMessage/AirDrop, the iPhone 16 is the obvious choice. Switching costs are real.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you care about AI and photography?" status="todo">
The Pixel 9's AI features and smarter camera processing are ahead. If you want the best point-and-shoot photos, go Pixel.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you want the smoothest screen and longest battery?" status="todo">
The Pixel 9's 120Hz display and bigger battery are clear wins. The iPhone's 60Hz feels dated at this price.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you game or shoot video?" status="todo">
The iPhone 16 is the better gaming phone and the best video camera on any phone. Choose it for those.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## My recommendation

<Callout tone="success" title="If you want the best all-rounder">
Get the **Pixel 9**. It matches the iPhone on price, beats it on screen, battery, and AI smarts, and takes better photos. The iPhone's advantages (video, gaming, ecosystem) matter mainly if you're already an Apple user.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="info" title="If you're in the Apple ecosystem">
Get the **iPhone 16**. The seamless integration with your other Apple devices is worth more than any spec sheet difference.
</Callout>
