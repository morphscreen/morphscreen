# The Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets, split into two groups: the four rocky **inner planets** and the four gas/ice **giant outer planets**. Here's the full lineup, from closest to farthest from the Sun.

<Card title="Ordering the planets from the Sun">
<Steps>
<Step title="1. Mercury" status="done">Smallest, fastest orbit, no atmosphere.</Step>
<Step title="2. Venus" status="done">Hottest, thick CO₂ atmosphere, spins backwards.</Step>
<Step title="3. Earth" status="done">Liquid water oceans, plate tectonics, life.</Step>
<Step title="4. Mars" status="done">Iron oxide dust, extinct volcanoes, polar ice.</Step>
<Step title="5. Jupiter" status="done">Gas giant, 2.5x mass of all other planets combined.</Step>
<Step title="6. Saturn" status="done">Lowest density: would float in water.</Step>
<Step title="7. Uranus" status="done">Ice giant, methane gives blue-green color.</Step>
<Step title="8. Neptune" status="done">Farthest, discovered by math before telescope.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
