# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which should you get?

Both phones are excellent, but they excel in different areas. Here's a breakdown to help you decide based on what matters most to you.

<Card title="Quick comparison">
<Compare title="iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9">
| Feature | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 6.1" OLED, 60Hz | 6.3" OLED, 120Hz |
| Chip | A18 Bionic | Tensor G4 |
| RAM | 8GB | 12GB |
| Base storage | 128GB | 128GB |
| Main camera | 48MP | 50MP |
| Ultrawide | 12MP | 48MP |
| Telephoto | No | No |
| Battery | ~3,561 mAh | 4,700 mAh |
| Charging | 20W wired, 15W MagSafe | 27W wired, 15W wireless |
| Software updates | 5+ years | 7 years |
| Starting price | $799 | $799 |
</Compare>
</Card>

<Card title="Design & feel">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">iPhone 16</Badge>
- Aluminum frame, Ceramic Shield front
- Colors: Black, White, Pink, Teal, Ultramarine
- 6.1" screen, 170g — compact and light
- Action Button and Camera Control
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Pixel 9</Badge>
- Aluminum frame, Gorilla Glass Victus 2
- Colors: Obsidian, Porcelain, Wintergreen, Peony
- 6.3" screen, 198g — slightly larger and heavier
- Flat sides, polished camera bar
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Performance & battery">
<Columns>
<div>
<Stat label="iPhone 16" value="A18" note="3nm, very fast" icon="cpu" />
- Blazing fast for gaming, video editing, and heavy apps
- Battery lasts a full day, but not much more
- Charging: 20W wired (need to buy a charger), 15W MagSafe
</div>
<div>
<Stat label="Pixel 9" value="Tensor G4" note="Focus on AI, not raw speed" icon="cpu" />
- Plenty fast for everyday use, but lags on heavy games
- Bigger battery, easily lasts a day and a half
- Charging: 27W wired, 15W wireless
</div>
</Columns>
<Callout tone="warning" title="If you're a heavy gamer">The iPhone 16's A18 chip is significantly faster than the Tensor G4 for sustained gaming and rendering.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Cameras: which one suits you?">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="iPhone 16">
- **Main**: 48MP, great detail and color
- **Ultrawide**: 12MP, good but not class-leading
- **Video**: Best-in-class 4K 60fps with Dolby Vision, great stabilization
- **Photographic Styles**: Real-time color grading you can tweak
- **Spatial video**: Records 3D video for Vision Pro
- Photos look natural and true-to-life
</Tab>
<Tab title="Pixel 9">
- **Main**: 50MP, excellent dynamic range
- **Ultrawide**: 48MP, much sharper than iPhone's
- **Video**: Good, but not as smooth as iPhone
- **Magic Editor**: Remove objects, reframe, and relight photos with AI
- **Best Take**: Swap faces in group shots so everyone smiles
- **Super Res Zoom**: Digital zoom that's surprisingly good
- Photos are punchy and processed by AI
</Tab>
</Tabs>
<Callout tone="info" title="Bottom line">If you shoot video or want natural photos → iPhone 16. If you take lots of photos, especially of people, and love AI editing → Pixel 9.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="AI & software experience">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">iPhone 16</Badge>
- Apple Intelligence: Writing Tools, Clean Up in Photos, notification summaries
- Siri is getting smarter, but still behind
- Tight integration with iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch
- App Store: largest selection of quality apps
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Pixel 9</Badge>
- Gemini: far more capable AI assistant, on-device and in the cloud
- Call screening, scam detection, live translation
- Pixel Studio: generate images on-device
- 7 years of OS updates (vs Apple's ~5-6)
- Clean, customizable Android — more freedom
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="What should you pick?">
<Columns>
<Card title="Choose the iPhone 16 if…">
- You're already in the Apple ecosystem (AirPods, Mac, Watch)
- You shoot lots of video or do creative work
- You value app quality and long-term resale value
- You want a compact, light phone
- You prefer iOS's simplicity and polish
</Card>
<Card title="Choose the Pixel 9 if…">
- You love AI features like Magic Editor and Gemini
- You take lots of photos of people and want the best stills
- You want a bigger battery and faster charging
- You prefer Android's customizability
- You want the longest software support (7 years)
- You want a better value for the money
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="My recommendation">
<Callout tone="info" title="If you're still undecided">
- **Stick with your ecosystem**: If you have an iPhone, get the 16. If you have an Android, get the Pixel.
- **Switch if you want a change**: Both are great, so you won't regret either. The Pixel offers more AI and battery; the iPhone offers more power and video.
- **If you're coming from an older phone (3+ years)**: Either will feel like a massive upgrade. Go with whichever OS you prefer.
</Callout>
