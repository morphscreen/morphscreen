# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

¡Hola! Learning these essential phrases will help you navigate common situations with confidence. Let's start with the most useful ones.

<Card title="The 10 Essential Phrases">

|---|---------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | Hello | Hola | OH-la |
| 2 | Goodbye | Adiós | ah-DYOHS |
| 3 | Please | Por favor | por fah-VOR |
| 4 | Thank you | Gracias | GRAH-see-as |
| 5 | Yes / No | Sí / No | see / noh |

| 7 | I don't understand | No entiendo | noh en-TYEN-doh |

<Card title="Quick Pronunciation Tips">
<Callout tone="info" title="Key sounds">
- **ll** sounds like a soft "y" (baño = BAH-nyoh)
- **ñ** is like "ny" in canyon
- **j** is a guttural "h" sound (like in "loch")
- **r** is rolled slightly at the front of the mouth
- Stress usually falls on the second-to-last syllable
</Callout>
</Card>
