# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two — Your Map & Itinerary

<Callout tone="info" title="How to use this plan">
This itinerary is built around two people exploring Tokyo together — balancing iconic sights, local neighborhoods, great food, and enough breathing room to enjoy it. Each day focuses on one area so you spend less time commuting and more time experiencing.
</Callout>

<Card title="Key details for two">
<KeyValue title="Essentials">
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Best time to visit | Late March–April (cherry blossoms) or Oct–Nov (autumn colors) |
| Budget per day (for two) | ¥15,000–25,000 ($100–170) excluding hotel |
| Transportation | Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card — tap on/off trains and buses |
| Best area to stay | Shinjuku or Shibuya for nightlife; Asakusa for old-town charm |
| Language | English is limited; download Google Translate with Japanese offline |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="Getting around">
<Callout tone="success" title="Pro tip">
The Yamanote Line loops through Shinjuku, Shibuya, Harajuku, Ueno, and Akihabara — it connects most of your days. A one-day pass for Tokyo Metro is ¥600 per person.
</Callout>
