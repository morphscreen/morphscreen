# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is a hiker's paradise — within a 90-minute drive you can be on a mountain ridge, beside a waterfall, or in an old-growth forest. Here are the standout day hikes, chosen for scenery, accessibility, and payoff.

## The Big Three (Iconic and Worth the Hype)

<Card title="Stawamus Chief">
The Chief is a granite monolith rising above Squamish — three peaks, each with jaw-dropping views of the fjord and surrounding mountains. The **Second Peak** is the most popular, with chains and ladders for a fun scramble section.

<Gallery>
![Stawamus Chief granite cliffs seen from Shannon Falls viewpoint](find)
![Looking down at Howe Sound from the Second Peak summit](find)
![The wooden ladder section on the Chief trail](find)
</Gallery>

<KeyValue title="Trail details">
| Label | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 11 km round trip (to Second Peak) |
| Elevation gain | 700 m |
| Time | 4–6 hours |
| Trailhead | Stawamus Chief Provincial Park, Squamish (1 hr from Vancouver) |
| Parking | Large lot, but fills by 8 AM on weekends |
| Best time | Year-round; dry and clear days in winter are spectacular |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Permit & safety">No permit needed, but the trail is **extremely busy** — go early or on a weekday. The summit has steep drop-offs; keep children close.</Callout>

<Card title="Panorama Ridge (Garibaldi Provincial Park)">
This is the ultimate payoff hike. You climb through forest and meadows to a ridge that looks down on **Garibaldi Lake** — a turquoise gem framed by Black Tusk and Mount Garibaldi. In late summer, the meadows are full of wildflowers.

<Gallery>
![Panorama Ridge summit with Garibaldi Lake turquoise below](find)
![Wildflower meadows on the trail in August](find)
![Black Tusk seen from the ridge](find)
</Gallery>

<KeyValue title="Trail details">
| Label | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 30 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 1,500 m |
| Time | 8–10 hours |
| Trailhead | Rubble Creek parking lot (1.5 hrs from Vancouver) |
| Parking | Limited — arrive by 7 AM on weekends |
| Best time | July–October (snow-free) |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="danger" title="Long and demanding">This is a **full-day commitment**. Pack 3+ litres of water, food, layers, and a headlamp. Check trail conditions before heading out — snow can linger into July.</Callout>

<Card title="Joffre Lakes">
Three glacier-fed lakes in a row, each more stunning than the last. The trail is well-maintained with boardwalks and bridges, and the final lake sits under a massive glacier. The water is an unreal milky turquoise.

<Gallery>
![Middle Joffre Lake with glacier behind and turquoise water](find)
![The wooden bridge crossing between lakes](find)
![Upper Joffre Lake with glacier face in view](find)
</Gallery>

<KeyValue title="Trail details">
| Label | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 10 km round trip (to Upper Lake) |
| Elevation gain | 370 m |
| Time | 4–5 hours |
| Trailhead | Joffre Lakes Provincial Park (2.5 hrs from Vancouver) |
| Parking | **Day-use permit required** (free, online) — book ahead |
| Best time | July–October; winter requires snowshoes |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="info" title="Day-use passes">You must reserve a free day-use pass online before arriving, even in winter. Passes release 48 hours ahead at 7 AM — they go fast.</Callout>

## The Underrated Gems (Fewer Crowds, Great Views)

<Card title="Eagle Bluffs (Cypress Provincial Park)">
A hidden gem 30 minutes from downtown. The trail climbs through forest, then opens onto granite slabs with sweeping views of **Vancouver, the harbour, and Bowen Island**. On clear days you can see Mount Baker to the south.

<Gallery>
![Eagle Bluffs summit looking over Howe Sound and Bowen Island](find)
![Granite slabs near the summit with city below](find)
![Vancouver skyline visible from the trail](find)
</Gallery>

<KeyValue title="Trail details">
| Label | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 8 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 460 m |
| Time | 3–4 hours |
| Trailhead | Cypress Mountain ski area parking (30 min from Vancouver) |
| Parking | Free, plenty of space |
| Best time | May–November (snow-free) |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="success" title="Best sunset hike">This is the perfect after-work or sunset hike. Go on a clear evening and watch the city lights come on.</Callout>

<Card title="Quarry Rock (Deep Cove)">
The easiest hike on this list, but the views are anything but small. A gentle forest trail ends at a granite lookout over **Deep Cove and Indian Arm** — a fjord lined with mountains. Perfect for families or a quick nature escape.

<Gallery>
![Quarry Rock lookout over Deep Cove and Indian Arm](find)
![The forested trail with boardwalks](find)
![Kayakers below the viewpoint on a sunny day](find)
</Gallery>

<KeyValue title="Trail details">
| Label | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 3.8 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 100 m |
| Time | 1.5–2 hours |
| Trailhead | Panorama Drive, Deep Cove (30 min from Vancouver) |
| Parking | Very limited — use the Park & Ride shuttle on weekends |
| Best time | Year-round |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Go early">This trail gets **packed** by 10 AM. Arrive before 8:30 AM or take the weekend shuttle from Parkgate Community Centre.</Callout>

<Button ask="Where exactly is the Quarry Rock trailhead?">Find the trailhead</Button>

<Card title="Bowen Lookout (Cypress Provincial Park)">
A quiet alternative to Eagle Bluffs, starting from the same parking lot. Less elevation, more forest, and a viewpoint looking over **Bowen Island and the Strait of Georgia**. Great for a peaceful morning or a family hike.

## How to Choose

<Card title="Pick your hike">
<Columns>
<div>
<Callout tone="info" title="You want the best views per effort">
**Eagle Bluffs** or **Quarry Rock**. Both are short, sweet, and give you city-and-sea views.
</Callout>
</div>
<div>
<Callout tone="warning" title="You want a challenge">
**Panorama Ridge** is the king. But if you want something shorter but still tough, **Stawamus Chief** delivers.
</Callout>
</div>
<div>
<Callout tone="success" title="You want turquoise lakes">
**Joffre Lakes** — no contest. The colour is unreal.
</Callout>
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Essential gear checklist">
<Checklist title="Pack for a day hike" name="hike-gear">
- [x] Sturdy hiking shoes or boots
- [ ] 2–3 litres of water (more for Panorama Ridge)
- [ ] High-energy snacks and lunch
- [ ] Layers (weather changes fast in the mountains)
- [ ] Rain jacket (Vancouver, after all)
- [ ] Sunscreen and sunglasses
- [ ] Headlamp or flashlight (even for short hikes)
- [ ] Map or downloaded trail offline
- [ ] First-aid kit (small)
- [ ] Bear spray (for Garibaldi and Joffre areas)
</Checklist>
