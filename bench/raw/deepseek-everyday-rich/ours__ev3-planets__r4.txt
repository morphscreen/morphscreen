# The Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets, split into two main groups: the four rocky **inner planets** (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) and the four gas/ice giants **outer planets** (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune). Here's your tour, starting from the Sun.

<Card title="The Inner Planets" subtitle="Rocky worlds close to the Sun">
<Columns>
<Plan name="Mercury" price="0.39 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Smallest planet, no atmosphere
- Extreme temperature swings (-180°C to 430°C)
- 88-day year
</Plan>
<Plan name="Venus" price="0.72 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Hottest planet (465°C), thick CO2 atmosphere
- Spins backwards, day longer than year
- Brightest object in night sky after Moon
</Plan>
<Plan name="Earth" price="1.00 AU" note="from Sun" featured=true>
- Only known life-bearing world
- 71% water, active plate tectonics
- One Moon stabilizes climate
</Plan>
<Plan name="Mars" price="1.52 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Red from iron oxide dust
- Olympus Mons – tallest volcano (21.9 km)
- Two small moons, Phobos and Deimos
</Plan>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="The Outer Planets" subtitle="Gas and ice giants beyond the asteroid belt">
<Columns>
<Plan name="Jupiter" price="5.20 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Largest planet, 2.5× mass of all others combined
- Great Red Spot – 400-year-old storm
- 95 known moons, including Ganymede
</Plan>
<Plan name="Saturn" price="9.58 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Iconic rings made of ice and rock
- Least dense planet – would float in water
- 146 moons, Titan has a thick atmosphere
</Plan>
<Plan name="Uranus" price="19.2 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Rotates on its side (98° tilt)
- Pale cyan from methane in atmosphere
- 27 known moons, named after Shakespeare characters
</Plan>
<Plan name="Neptune" price="30.05 AU" note="from Sun" featured=false>
- Farthest planet, strong winds (2,100 km/h)
- Deep blue from methane
- Discovered by math before telescope (1846)
</Plan>
</Columns>
</Card>
