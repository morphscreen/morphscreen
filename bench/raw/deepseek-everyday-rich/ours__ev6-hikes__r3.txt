# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is a hiker's paradise—within an hour you can go from sea level to alpine ridges, old-growth forests, and waterfalls. Here are the top day hikes, ranked by scenery-to-effort ratio, plus insider tips for each.

<Card title="The Top 5 Picks">
<Columns>
<Card title="1. Stawamus Chief">
<Badge tone="success">Classic</Badge>
- **Distance:** 11 km round trip  
- **Elevation gain:** 700 m  
- **Time:** 4–6 hours  
- **Difficulty:** Moderate–Hard  
- **Best for:** Panoramic views of Howe Sound and the Sea-to-Sky corridor.
</Card>
<Card title="2. Joffre Lakes">
<Badge tone="info">Most Instagrammed</Badge>
- **Distance:** 10 km round trip  
- **Elevation gain:** 400 m  
- **Time:** 4–5 hours  
- **Difficulty:** Moderate  
- **Best for:** Three turquoise glacier-fed lakes, especially the third.
</Card>
<Card title="3. Garibaldi Lake">
<Badge tone="warning">Big Reward</Badge>
- **Distance:** 18 km round trip  
- **Elevation gain:** 900 m  
- **Time:** 6–8 hours  
- **Difficulty:** Hard  
- **Best for:** A massive emerald lake with a volcanic backdrop.
</Card>
<Card title="4. Lynn Canyon / Norvan Falls">
<Badge tone="neutral">Easy Escape</Badge>
- **Distance:** 14 km round trip (to falls)  
- **Elevation gain:** 300 m  
- **Time:** 4–5 hours  
- **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate  
- **Best for:** Lush rainforest, a suspension bridge, and a waterfall.
</Card>
<Card title="5. Dog Mountain">
<Badge tone="success">Quick Views</Badge>
- **Distance:** 5.8 km round trip  
- **Elevation gain:** 350 m  
- **Time:** 2–3 hours  
- **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate  
- **Best for:** Sunset views over Vancouver and the harbour from Mount Seymour.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Pro Tips from Locals">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Start Early">
- **Stawamus Chief:** Arrive by 7 AM on weekends; the lot is full by 8:30 AM. Go on a weekday if possible.
- **Joffre Lakes:** Parking fills by 7 AM in summer; take the shuttle from Squamish if you miss it.
- **Garibaldi Lake:** The trail is steep—bring trekking poles to save your knees on the descent.
- **Lynn Canyon:** The suspension bridge is free; avoid it mid-day when it's packed with tourists. Hike to Norvan Falls for solitude.
- **Dog Mountain:** Best at sunset—bring a headlamp for the walk back down.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Weather & Safety">
<KeyValue title="Quick Safety Check">
| Condition | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Rain (heavy) | Avoid rock slabs (slippery) on Stawamus Chief; streams may flood on Joffre |
| Heatwave (>30°C) | Start at dawn, carry 3L water, take breaks in shade |
| Lightning | Leave summits immediately; wait 30 min after last strike |
| Snow (even patches) | Use microspikes; turn back if trail is icy or whiteout |
| Bear activity | Make noise, carry bear spray, keep dogs leashed |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="If You Have Less Time">
<Columns>
<Card title="Quick 1-Hour Hikes">
- **Quarry Rock (Deep Cove):** 3.8 km, 100 m gain, 1.5 hrs—views of Indian Arm.
- **Lighthouse Park (West Vancouver):** 5 km, 100 m gain, 1.5 hrs—old-growth forest and ocean views.
- **Bowen Lookout (Cypress):** 3 km, 200 m gain, 1 hr—panorama of Vancouver and the Gulf Islands.
</Card>
<Card title="If You Want a Challenge">
- **The Grouse Grind:** 2.9 km, 853 m gain, 1–1.5 hrs up—a stairmaster of nature. Take the gondola down ($15).
- **Panorama Ridge (Garibaldi):** 30 km, 1,500 m gain, 10–12 hrs—alpine views over Garibaldi Lake and Black Tusk.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
