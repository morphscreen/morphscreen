# Best Road Running Shoes Under $120

<Callout tone="info" title="What to look for">
A good road shoe under $120 balances cushioning, weight, and durability. For most runners, a neutral shoe with moderate drop (8–12mm) and a snug heel fit is the safest choice. If you overpronate (arches collapse inward), look for a stability shoe.
</Callout>

<Card title="How to choose">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check your foot type" status="current">
Wet your foot and step on paper. Flat arch = overpronator (need stability). High arch = supinator (need more cushioning). Normal = neutral.
</Step>
<Step title="Match to your runs">
Daily miles → Gel-Cumulus or 880. Tempo/speed → Ride 17. Recovery/easy → Ghost 15.
</Step>
<Step title="Try them on late in the day">
Feet swell during the day. Bring your running socks, and leave a thumb's width (about 1cm) between your longest toe and the shoe's end.
</Step>
<Step title="Consider your weight">
Heavier runners (200+ lbs) generally need more cushioning — look at the Gel-Cumulus or Ghost. Lighter runners may prefer the responsiveness of the Ride.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Buying tips">
<Callout tone="success" title="Save more">
Check previous models (Gel-Cumulus 25, 880 v13, Ride 16, Ghost 14) — they're often 30–50% off and nearly identical. Look for last season's colors on sale.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Break-in period">
New shoes may feel stiff for the first 15–20 miles. Alternate them with your old pair for the first week to avoid blisters and soreness.
</Callout>
</Card>
