# iPhone 16 vs. Pixel 9: Which Should You Choose?

Both phones are excellent, but they excel in different areas. Here's a clear breakdown to help you decide based on what matters most to you.

<Card title="Quick comparison">
<Compare title="iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9 at a glance">
| Feature | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 6.1" OLED, 60Hz | 6.3" OLED, 120Hz |
| Chip | A18 Bionic | Google Tensor G4 |
| Main camera | 48MP f/1.6 | 50MP f/1.7 |
| Battery life | Up to 22 hrs video | Up to 24 hrs mixed use |
| Charging | 20W wired, 15W MagSafe | 27W wired, 15W wireless |
| Software updates | 5+ years | 7 years |
| Starting price | $799 | $799 |
| AI features | Apple Intelligence | Gemini AI (more advanced) |
</Compare>
</Card>

<Card title="Which one fits you?">
<Columns>
<Card title="Choose the iPhone 16 if...">
- You're already in the Apple ecosystem (watch, Mac, AirPods)
- You value video recording quality and consistency
- You prefer a more polished, refined OS experience
- You want the best third-party app support and accessories
- You care about resale value
</Card>

<Card title="Choose the Pixel 9 if...">
- You want the best camera for photos, especially in low light
- You love AI features like Magic Eraser, Call Screening, and Gemini
- You want a smoother 120Hz display
- You plan to keep the phone for 6+ years (7 years of updates)
- You prefer stock Android with no bloatware
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Camera showdown">
Both take stunning photos, but they have different strengths.

<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16 camera">
- **Photos**: Natural, true-to-life colors; excellent dynamic range
- **Video**: Best-in-class, with 4K Dolby Vision and great stabilization
- **Low light**: Good, but slightly behind Pixel
- **Selfies**: 12MP, very reliable
</Card>

<Card title="Pixel 9 camera">
- **Photos**: Punchy, vibrant colors; superb HDR and low-light (Night Sight is magic)
- **Video**: Good, but not quite iPhone-level
- **Low light**: Best-in-class, especially for night shots
- **Selfies**: 10.5MP with ultra-wide option
</Card>
</Columns>

<Card title="Performance & daily use">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">
- **Chip**: A18 Bionic — incredibly fast, efficient, and future-proof
- **Display**: 60Hz is smooth, but not as fluid as the Pixel's 120Hz
- **Battery**: Solid, typically lasts a full day
- **Charging**: MagSafe is convenient if you have Apple accessories
- **OS**: iOS 18 — polished, secure, and easy to use
</Card>

<Card title="Pixel 9">
- **Chip**: Tensor G4 — good, but not as powerful for heavy gaming
- **Display**: 120Hz is noticeably smoother for scrolling and gaming
- **Battery**: Slightly better endurance in mixed use
- **Charging**: Faster wired charging (27W vs 20W)
- **OS**: Android 14 — customizable, with the best AI integration
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="AI features: The big differentiator">
<Callout tone="info" title="This is where they split">
- **Pixel 9** has Gemini AI built deep into the system: Magic Eraser for photos, Call Screening, Summarize in Recorder, and Circle to Search. It's genuinely useful every day.
- **iPhone 16** has Apple Intelligence: Writing Tools, notification summaries, and a smarter Siri. It's good, but Google is further ahead right now.
</Callout>
</Card>
