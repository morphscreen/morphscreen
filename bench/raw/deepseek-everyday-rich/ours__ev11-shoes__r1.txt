<Card title="How to pick the right one">
<Columns>
<Card title="Your mileage">
- **Under 15 miles/week** → Nike Revolution 7 is plenty.
- **15–30 miles/week** → ASICS Gel-Excite gives you more cushion for longer runs.
- **30+ miles/week or training for a race** → Brooks Ghost 15 is the most durable and protective.
</Card>
<Card title="Fit tips">
- Buy **half a size up** from your dress shoes — your feet swell when running.
- Try them on in the **evening** when feet are largest.
- Leave a **thumb's width** of space from your longest toe to the end.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

- [ ] Measure your foot at a store (don't rely on memory)
