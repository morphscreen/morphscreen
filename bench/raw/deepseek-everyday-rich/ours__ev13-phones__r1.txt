# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which should you buy?

Two excellent phones, but they suit very different people. Here's the honest breakdown to help you decide.

<Card title="Quick verdict">
<Callout tone="info" title="Pick iPhone 16 if…">You're already in the Apple ecosystem (AirDrop, iMessage, Apple Watch), want the smoothest video recording, and value long-term software support with a polished, consistent experience.</Callout>
<Callout tone="success" title="Pick Pixel 9 if…">You love photography (especially low-light and zoom), want the smartest AI features like Call Screen and Magic Eraser, and prefer a clean Android experience with faster updates from Google.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Which one fits your life?">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="iPhone 16">
- **Best for**: Apple users, content creators, those who want a simple, reliable phone
- **Pros**: Superior video stabilization, seamless integration with AirPods/MacBook/Apple Watch, better resale value, FaceTime and iMessage
- **Cons**: 60Hz screen feels dated, slower charging, no telephoto zoom, less RAM
- **Verdict**: A safe, polished choice that just works

- **Cons**: Tensor chip runs warmer, no ProRes video, weaker ecosystem outside Google apps
