## Quick Fit Guide

<Card title="Find your size">
<Steps>
<Step title="Measure your foot" status="done">Stand on a piece of paper, trace your foot, and measure from heel to longest toe. Do this at the end of the day when feet are slightly swollen.</Step>
<Step title="Check the fit" status="current">There should be about a thumb's width (½ inch) between your longest toe and the end of the shoe. Your heel shouldn't slip when you walk.</Step>
<Step title="Try both shoes" status="todo">Feet can differ in size. Try on both shoes and go with the larger size if one foot is bigger.</Step>
<Step title="Take a test jog" status="todo">Run a few steps in the store or around your home. The shoe should feel comfortable immediately — no break-in period needed.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
