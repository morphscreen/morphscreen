# Houseplants that are hard to kill

Some plants thrive on neglect — they forgive missed waterings, low light, and dry air. Here are the most resilient houseplants, with tips for each.

<Callout tone="success" title="Golden rule for all of these">
Most of these plants prefer to dry out completely between waterings. When in doubt, wait a few more days before watering.
</Callout>

<Card title="The top 6 unkillable houseplants">
<Columns>
<div>
**Snake Plant (Sansevieria)**  
Almost impossible to kill. Tolerates low light, drought, and irregular care. Water every 2–4 weeks.  
<Badge tone="success">Easiest</Badge>
</div>
<div>
**Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)**  
Grows in low light and survives missed waterings. Cut a stem, put it in water — it roots in weeks.  
<Badge tone="success">Great starter</Badge>
</div>
<div>
**ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)**  
Stores water in thick rhizomes. Only water when soil is completely dry — every 2–3 weeks.  
<Badge tone="info">Low light champ</Badge>
</div>
<div>
**Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)**  
Tolerates almost anything, produces baby plants. Water when the top 2 cm of soil is dry.  
<Badge tone="info">Easy and fun</Badge>
</div>
<div>
**Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum)**  
Dramatic when thirsty — leaves droop, then bounce back after watering. Low light OK.  
<Badge tone="warning">Needs a little more water</Badge>
</div>
<div>
**Aloe Vera**  
Succulent that stores water in thick leaves. Sunny spot, water every 3 weeks.  
<Badge tone="info">Also useful</Badge>
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="How to choose the right one for your space">
<Columns>
<div>
**Your light situation:**
- **Dark corner** → Snake plant, ZZ plant, Peace Lily
- **Bright but indirect** → Pothos, Spider plant
- **Direct sun (south window)** → Aloe Vera
</div>
<div>
**Your watering habits:**
- **Forgetful** → Snake plant, ZZ plant, Aloe (drought-tolerant)
- **Weekly routine** → Pothos, Spider plant, Peace Lily
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="The 3 most common ways people kill even these">
<Steps>
<Step title="Overwatering" status="warning">The #1 killer. These plants rot quickly if soil stays wet. Always check the soil before watering.</Step>
<Step title="No drainage" status="warning">A pot without holes traps water. Use a pot with drainage holes, or add a layer of pebbles at the bottom.</Step>
<Step title="Cold drafts" status="warning">Keep them away from heating vents, open windows in winter, and air conditioners.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
