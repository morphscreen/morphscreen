## Day 1 — Shinjuku & Shibuya Icons

<Card title="Day 1: West Tokyo Highlights">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku & Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="~3 hours">
Start at Shinjuku Station (busy but central). Walk to Meiji Shrine (10 min) — a serene forest in the city. Arrive by 9 AM to beat crowds. Free entry.
</Step>
<Step title="Late Morning: Harajuku & Takeshita Street" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
From Meiji, walk 10 min to Harajuku. Explore Takeshita Street for quirky fashion, crepes, and photo booths. Try a crepe (¥500–800).
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Walk 15 min to Shibuya. See the famous scramble crossing from the Starbucks second floor, then visit Hachiko statue. Explore Shibuya Center-gai for lunch.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="~3 hours">
Return to Shinjuku. Dinner at Omoide Yokocho (memory lane) — tiny yakitori stalls. Then bar-hop in Golden Gai (6-seat bars). Great for a couple's night.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 2 — Old Tokyo & Otaku Culture

<Card title="Day 2: Asakusa, Ueno & Akihabara">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise" status="done" subtitle="~2.5 hours">
Take the Ginza Line to Asakusa (30 min from Shinjuku). Enter through the Kaminarimon Gate, walk Nakamise shopping street, and see Senso-ji. Try a melon pan or ningyo-yaki.
</Step>
<Step title="Midday: Ueno Park & Museums" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Walk 20 min (or one stop) to Ueno. Stroll Ueno Park, visit the Tokyo National Museum (¥1,000) or Ueno Zoo (¥600). Grab lunch at Ameyoko market street.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Akihabara Electric Town" status="done" subtitle="~3 hours">
Take the JR Yamanote line to Akihabara (10 min). Explore electronics shops, anime stores, and arcades. Try a maid café experience (¥1,500–2,000) if you're curious.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Return to Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="~1 hour">
Take the JR Chuo line back to Shinjuku (20 min). Dinner at a local izakaya or ramen shop.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 budget tip">
<Callout tone="success" title="Save money">
Buy a Tokyo Metro 24-hour ticket (¥800) if you'll use the subway 3+ times. It covers most of Day 2 and 3 routes.
</Callout>
</Card>

## Day 3 — Markets, Ginza & Imperial Palace

<Card title="Day 3: Tsukiji, Ginza & Roppongi">
<Steps>
<Step title="Early Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Take the Hibiya Line to Tsukiji (25 min). Arrive by 8 AM for fresh sushi breakfast (¥2,000–3,000 per person). Try uni, toro, and tamago.
</Step>
<Step title="Late Morning: Ginza Shopping" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Walk 10 min to Ginza. Browse flagship stores like Uniqlo, Muji, and Ginza Six. Check out the Kabukiza Theatre facade.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Imperial Palace & Tokyo Station" status="done" subtitle="~2.5 hours">
Walk 20 min to the Imperial Palace East Gardens (free). See the Nijubashi Bridge. End at Tokyo Station for the beautiful red-brick Marunouchi exit.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Roppongi Hills & Tokyo Tower views" status="done" subtitle="~3 hours">
Take the Oedo Line to Roppongi (15 min). Visit the Mori Art Tower observation deck (¥2,000) for panoramic views. See Tokyo Tower lit up at night.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 4 — Parks, Skyline & Odaiba

<Card title="Day 4: Nature & Futuristic Tokyo">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen Garden" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Walk 10 min from Shinjuku Station. This large park has Japanese, French, and English gardens. Entry ¥500. Great for a picnic (buy food at a konbini).
</Step>
<Step title="Late Morning: Shibuya Sky" status="done" subtitle="~1.5 hours">
Take the JR Yamanote to Shibuya (5 min). Go to Shibuya Sky rooftop (¥2,000) for 360° views. Book tickets online the day before.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Odaiba & TeamLab" status="done" subtitle="~4 hours">
Take the Yurikamome line from Shimbashi to Odaiba (20 min). Visit teamLab Planets (¥3,200, book ahead) — immersive digital art. Walk the waterfront, see the Gundam statue, and Rainbow Bridge.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Return & relax" status="done" subtitle="~1 hour">
Take the Yurikamome back to Shimbashi, then JR to Shinjuku. Dinner at a local spot.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 5 — Hidden Gems & Departure

<Card title="Day 5: Nakameguro & Daikanyama">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Nakameguro Canal" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Take the Hibiya Line to Nakameguro (20 min from Shinjuku). Walk along the Meguro River — lovely in any season, magical with cherry blossoms in spring. Visit small boutique cafes.
</Step>
<Step title="Late Morning: Daikanyama & T-Site" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Walk 10 min to Daikanyama. Browse the famous Tsutaya Books T-Site, explore fashion boutiques, and have a coffee at a specialty roaster.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya final shopping" status="done" subtitle="~2 hours">
Walk 15 min back to Shibuya. Shop for souvenirs at Shibuya 109 or Loft. Pick up omiyage (gift snacks) at a department store basement.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Departure or final dinner" status="done" subtitle="Flexible">
If flying late, grab a final ramen or sushi dinner. Head to Haneda (30 min by monorail) or Narita (1 hr by Skyliner) airport.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
