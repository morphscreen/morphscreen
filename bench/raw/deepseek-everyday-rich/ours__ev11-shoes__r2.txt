<Card title="How to Choose Your Fit">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check the drop" status="done" subtitle="Heel-to-toe offset">Higher drop (10-12 mm) suits heel strikers; lower drop (8 mm) feels more natural for mid-foot runners.</Step>
<Step title="Know your width" status="done" subtitle="Standard vs wide">Most brands offer wide (2E/4E) versions at no extra cost. Measure your foot at the end of the day.</Step>
<Step title="Leave a thumb's width" status="done" subtitle="Toe room">Your longest toe should have about a thumb's width of space from the shoe's end.</Step>
<Step title="Try them on in the evening" status="done" subtitle="Feet swell during the day">Shop after work or a run to get the right size for your actual running foot.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Common Fit Issues to Avoid">
<Details title="Heel slipping">
Tie a runner's knot (lace through the top eyelets twice) to lock your heel in place. If it still slips, try a half-size down.
</Details>
<Details title="Toe numbness">
Usually means the shoe is too narrow. Look for wide versions or brands like Altra with anatomical toe boxes.
</Details>
<Details title="Blisters on arches">
Often caused by too much motion. Consider insoles with arch support if this persists after break-in.
</Details>
</Card>
