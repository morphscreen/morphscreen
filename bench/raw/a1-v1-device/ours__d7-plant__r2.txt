<Details title="Fungal Leaf Spot (High Confidence)" open="true">
**Symptoms:** Circular brown spots with yellow halos on lower leaves
**Cause:** Excess moisture, poor air circulation, contaminated soil
**Spread:** Splashing water, infected tools, wind-borne spores
</Details>

<Details title="Nitrogen Deficiency (Medium Confidence)" open="false">
**Symptoms:** Yellowing of older leaves, stunted growth, pale green color
**Cause:** Depleted soil, high pH, excessive watering leaching nutrients
**Affected:** Older leaves first, progressing upward
</Details>

<Details title="Overwatering (Medium Confidence)" open="false">
**Symptoms:** Wilting despite wet soil, yellowing leaves, root rot smell
**Cause:** Poor drainage, watering too frequently, compacted soil
**Risk:** Root suffocation, fungal growth, nutrient lockout
</Details>
</Card>

<Card title="Care Steps" subtitle="Follow in order for best results">
<Steps>
<Step title="Isolate the Plant" status="current">
Move affected plant away from healthy plants to prevent fungal spread. Improve air circulation with a fan if indoors.
</Step>
<Step title="Remove Affected Leaves" status="todo">
Using sterilized scissors, cut off all leaves with visible spots. Dispose in trash (not compost). Sterilize tools after each cut.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply Fungicide Treatment" status="todo">
Spray copper-based fungicide or neem oil on all leaf surfaces (top and bottom). Repeat every 7-10 days for 3 applications.
</Step>
<Step title="Adjust Watering Schedule" status="todo">
Allow top 2 inches of soil to dry between waterings. Ensure pot has drainage holes. Water at soil level, not on leaves.
</Step>
<Step title="Fertilize with Balanced Feed" status="todo">
Apply half-strength balanced fertilizer (10-10-10) after 2 weeks. Switch to nitrogen-rich formula if yellowing persists.
</Step>
<Step title="Monitor & Prevent" status="todo">
Check daily for new spots. Maintain 40-60% humidity, good airflow. Rotate plant weekly for even light exposure.
</Step>
</Steps>
