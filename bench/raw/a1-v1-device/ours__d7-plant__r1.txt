<Card title="Likely Problems" subtitle="Based on common symptoms">
<Badge tone="warning">Yellowing leaves</Badge> – Often indicates **nitrogen deficiency** or **overwatering**.
<Badge tone="danger">Brown spots</Badge> – Could be **fungal infection** (e.g., leaf spot) or **sunburn**.
<Badge tone="warning">Wilting</Badge> – Typically **underwatering**, **root rot**, or **heat stress**.
<Badge tone="info">White powder</Badge> – Classic sign of **powdery mildew**.
<Badge tone="danger">Sticky residue</Badge> – Suggests **aphids**, **scale**, or **mealybugs**.

<Card title="Care Steps" subtitle="Follow these steps in order">
<Steps>
<Step title="Isolate the plant" status="current">Move the affected plant away from others to prevent spread of pests or disease.</Step>
<Step title="Remove damaged foliage" status="todo">Trim off severely yellowed, spotted, or dead leaves with clean scissors.</Step>
<Step title="Treat the specific issue" status="todo">
- **Fungal issues:** Apply neem oil or copper fungicide.
- **Pests:** Spray insecticidal soap or introduce beneficial insects.
- **Nutrient deficiency:** Feed with a balanced fertilizer.
- **Watering problems:** Adjust schedule; ensure proper drainage.
</Step>
<Step title="Monitor and repeat" status="todo">Check daily for improvement. Reapply treatment after 7–10 days if needed.</Step>
<Step title="Prevent recurrence" status="todo">Improve air circulation, avoid overhead watering, and inspect new plants before adding them.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
