<Details title="Money-saving tips" open=false>
- **Lisboa Card** (24h €21 / 48h €35 / 72h €44) — covers most museums + unlimited transport. Worth it if you hit 3+ paid sites per day.
- **Free viewpoints**: Miradouro da Graça, Miradouro da Senhora do Monte, Portas do Sol — skip paid elevators.
- **Pastéis de nata**: Buy at local pastelarias (Manteigaria, Fábrica da Nata) for €1.20–1.50 vs €2+ at tourist spots.
- **Walk everywhere** in Alfama/Baixa/Chiado — saves tram/metro fares.
- **Tap water** is safe and free — bring a bottle.
</Details>
