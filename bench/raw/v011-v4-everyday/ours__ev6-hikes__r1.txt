<Card title="Top 5 day hikes near Vancouver">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="1. Stawamus Chief">
<Badge tone="success">Most popular</Badge>

**Location:** Squamish, 1 hour north of Vancouver  
**Distance:** 11 km return  
**Elevation gain:** 500 m  
**Time:** 4–5 hours  
**Difficulty:** Moderate–Hard

Three granite peaks with breathtaking views of Howe Sound and the surrounding mountains. The trail is well-maintained but steep in sections, with some chain-assisted climbing near the top.

</Tab>

<Tab title="2. Grouse Grind">
<Badge tone="warning">Very steep</Badge>

**Location:** North Vancouver, 30 minutes from downtown  
**Distance:** 2.9 km one way  
**Elevation gain:** 853 m  
**Time:** 1.5–2.5 hours up  
**Difficulty:** Hard

Known as "Mother Nature's Stairmaster," this is a challenging uphill-only trail. Take the Skyride gondola down ($15 CAD). Best for fitness, not scenery during the climb — the views come at the top.

<Button ask="Tell me more about the Grouse Grind">More on Grouse Grind</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="3. Lighthouse Park">
<Badge tone="info">Easy & family-friendly</Badge>

**Location:** West Vancouver, 30 minutes from downtown  
**Distance:** 5–8 km (various loops)  
**Elevation gain:** 150 m  
**Time:** 2–3 hours  
**Difficulty:** Easy

Old-growth forest trails leading to a historic lighthouse on a rocky headland. Great for families, with stunning views of Burrard Inlet and the Vancouver skyline. Multiple loop options so you can choose your distance.

<Button ask="Tell me more about Lighthouse Park">More on Lighthouse Park</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="4. Garibaldi Lake">
<Badge tone="success">Iconic alpine lake</Badge>

**Location:** Squamish, 1.5 hours north of Vancouver  
**Distance:** 18 km return  
**Elevation gain:** 920 m  
**Time:** 6–7 hours  
**Difficulty:** Moderate–Hard

Turquoise alpine lake surrounded by snow-capped peaks and glaciers. The trail is long but steady, and the payoff is one of the most photographed views in BC. Bring plenty of water and snacks.

<Button ask="Tell me more about the Garibaldi Lake hike">More on Garibaldi Lake</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="5. Lynn Canyon & Norvan Falls">
<Badge tone="info">Forest & waterfall</Badge>

**Location:** North Vancouver, 25 minutes from downtown  
**Distance:** 14 km return  
**Elevation gain:** 300 m  
**Time:** 4–5 hours  
**Difficulty:** Moderate

Follow Lynn Creek through lush coastal rainforest to a 30-metre waterfall. The trail is mostly flat with gentle ups and downs. Start at Lynn Canyon Suspension Bridge (free, unlike Capilano) for an added thrill.

</Tab>

<Card title="Safety notes">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Trail conditions">
Always check current trail conditions before heading out. Snow can linger on alpine trails (Garibaldi Lake, Chief) into July. Some trails require a **BC Parks day pass** in peak season — book online in advance.
</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Getting there">
All hikes are accessible by car. Grouse Grind, Lighthouse Park and Lynn Canyon are also reachable by public transit from downtown Vancouver. Parking fills early on weekends — arrive before 8 AM.
</Callout>
