# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which should you get?

Both are excellent phones, but they excel in different areas. Let's compare them across the features that matter most.

<Card title="Which one fits you?">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="You take lots of photos">
<Callout tone="info">Pixel 9 wins for most people who love photography</Callout>

The Pixel 9 has a **48MP ultra-wide** (vs 12MP on iPhone) and Google's legendary computational photography. You get:
- Best-in-class Night Sight
- Magic Eraser and Best Take
- Real Tone for accurate skin tones
- Astrophotography mode

The iPhone 16 is no slouch, but its ultra-wide is much lower resolution.
</Tab>
<Tab title="You want the smoothest experience">
<Callout tone="info">iPhone 16 wins for polish and app quality</Callout>

The A18 Bionic is faster in raw benchmarks, and iOS apps tend to be better optimised. You get:
- Smoother animations (even at 60Hz)
- Better third-party app support
- iMessage and FaceTime
- Seamless integration with other Apple devices

The Pixel 9's 120Hz screen is smoother for scrolling, but the overall experience feels less refined.
</Tab>
<Tab title="You keep phones for years">
<Callout tone="info">Pixel 9 wins for longevity guarantees</Callout>

Google promises **7 years of OS and security updates** for the Pixel 9. Apple guarantees 5+ years, but often delivers longer. However:
- Pixel 9: 7 years guaranteed from launch
- iPhone 16: 5+ years, typically 6-7 in practice

Both are great, but Google's written promise gives more peace of mind.
</Tab>
<Tab title="You care about battery and charging">
<Callout tone="info">Pixel 9 wins for battery life, iPhone for wireless charging</Callout>

- Pixel 9: ~24h mixed use, 27W wired, 12W wireless
- iPhone 16: ~20h video playback, 20W wired, 15W MagSafe

The Pixel lasts longer on a charge and charges faster wired. The iPhone's MagSafe is more convenient with its magnetic attachment.
</Tab>
</Tabs>
