# The Solar System: Our Cosmic Neighborhood

Our solar system consists of the Sun and everything that orbits it: 8 planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, comets, and more. The planets are divided into two main groups.

<Card title="The Inner (Terrestrial) Planets">
Small, rocky worlds close to the Sun.

<Columns>
<Plan name="Mercury" price="0.39 AU from Sun" period="88 days" note="Smallest planet">
- No atmosphere to speak of
- Extreme temperatures: -180°C to 430°C
- No moons
- Heavily cratered, like our Moon
</Plan>

<Plan name="Venus" price="0.72 AU from Sun" period="225 days" note="Hottest planet">
- Thick, toxic CO₂ atmosphere
- Surface hot enough to melt lead (465°C)
- Rotates backwards (sunrise in the west)
- No moons
</Plan>

<Plan name="Earth" price="1.00 AU from Sun" period="365.25 days" featured="true" note="Our home">
- Only known planet with life
- Liquid water on the surface
- Protective magnetic field
- 1 moon (the Moon)
</Plan>

<Plan name="Mars" price="1.52 AU from Sun" period="687 days" note="The Red Planet">
- Iron oxide (rust) gives it a red color
- Largest volcano in the solar system: Olympus Mons
- Thin CO₂ atmosphere
- 2 small moons: Phobos and Deimos
</Plan>
</Columns>

<Card title="The Outer (Gas & Ice) Giants">
Huge worlds made mostly of gas and ice, with rings and many moons.

<Columns>
<Plan name="Jupiter" price="5.20 AU from Sun" period="11.86 years" note="Largest planet">
- Great Red Spot (a storm bigger than Earth)
- 95 known moons (including Ganymede, the largest moon in the solar system)
- Faint ring system
- A day is only 10 hours
</Plan>

<Plan name="Saturn" price="9.54 AU from Sun" period="29.46 years" note="The Ringed Planet">
- Spectacular ring system made of ice and rock
- Least dense planet (would float in water!)
- 146 known moons (Titan has a thick atmosphere)
- 6-sided storm at its north pole
</Plan>

<Plan name="Uranus" price="19.19 AU from Sun" period="84.01 years" note="The Sideways Planet">
- Rotates on its side (axial tilt of 98°)
- Pale blue-green from methane in its atmosphere
- 27 known moons
- Faint, dark rings
</Plan>

<Plan name="Neptune" price="30.07 AU from Sun" period="164.8 years" note="The Windiest Planet">
- Strongest winds in the solar system (up to 2,100 km/h)
- Deep blue color
- 16 known moons (Triton orbits backwards)
- Faint, incomplete rings
</Plan>
</Columns>

<Card title="What about Pluto?">
<Callout tone="info" title="Pluto is a dwarf planet">
In 2006, the IAU redefined what a planet is. Pluto was reclassified as a **dwarf planet** because it hasn't cleared its orbital neighborhood of other debris. It's still a fascinating world with mountains, glaciers, and a heart-shaped plain!
</Callout>
