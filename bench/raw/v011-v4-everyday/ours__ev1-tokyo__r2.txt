## Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku Neon

<Card title="Day 1 — Sunday, Oct 25 (Evening)" subtitle="Settle in & explore Shinjuku">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check into hotel" status="todo">Drop bags in Shinjuku area. Recommended: <Badge>Hotel Gracery Shinjuku</Badge> (Godzilla view) or <Badge>Citadines Central Shinjuku</Badge> (apartment-style for two).</Step>
<Step title="Dinner: Omoide Yokocho" status="todo">Narrow alley of tiny yakitori grills. Try <Badge>Piss Alley</Badge> for authentic atmosphere. Budget: ¥3,000–5,000 for two.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building" status="todo">Free observation deck until 22:00. See the city lights from 202m.</Step>
<Step title="Nightcap: Golden Gai" status="todo">Tiny bars in six narrow alleys. Each seats 4–8 people — perfect for couples. Try Bar Albatross for the chandelier ceiling.</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 2: Classic Tokyo — Asakusa & Akihabara

<Card title="Day 2 — Monday, Oct 26">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="todo" subtitle="Asakusa">Japan's oldest temple. Walk Nakamise-dori shopping street (try <Badge>ningyo-yaki</Badge> — little filled cakes).</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Kappabashi Kitchen Street" status="todo">Buy realistic plastic food samples as souvenirs. Try <Badge>Kamiya Bar</Badge> for historic Western-Japanese fusion.</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Akihabara Electric Town" status="todo">Anime, manga, electronics. Visit <Badge>Yodobashi Camera</Badge> for gadgets, <Badge>Radio Kaikan</Badge> for figure shops.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Sumida River Cruise" status="todo">From Asakusa to Hamarikyu Gardens (¥1,200/pp). See Tokyo Skytree lit up from the water.</Step>
<Step title="Dinner: Monja in Tsukishima" status="todo">Cook your own savory pancake at <Badge>Monja Kura</Badge>. Fun for two! Budget: ¥4,000–6,000.</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 3: Culture & Nature — Ueno & Harajuku

<Card title="Day 3 — Tuesday, Oct 27">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Ueno Park & Museums" status="todo">Visit <Badge>Tokyo National Museum</Badge> (samurai armor, ukiyo-e). Free entry to the park — autumn leaves are stunning.</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Ameya-Yokocho Market" status="todo">Street food market under the train tracks. Try <Badge>taiyaki</Badge> (fish-shaped red bean cake).</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Harajuku & Takeshita Street" status="todo">Youth fashion hub. Visit <Badge>Daiso Harajuku</Badge> (¥100 shop), <Badge>Kiddy Land</Badge> for character goods.</Step>
<Step title="Late afternoon: Meiji Jingu Shrine" status="todo">Peaceful forest shrine right next to Harajuku. See the giant torii gates and sake barrel wall.</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Shibuya Crossing & Dinner" status="todo">See the famous scramble crossing from Starbucks Tsutaya (2F). Dinner at <Badge>Uobei Shibuya</Badge> — conveyor belt sushi (¥100/plate).</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 4: Day Trip — Hakone or Kamakura

<Card title="Day 4 — Wednesday, Oct 28" subtitle="Choose one day trip">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Hakone (Recommended)">
<Callout tone="info" title="Romantic choice">Onsen, Mount Fuji views, and the ropeway — perfect for couples.</Callout>
<Steps>
<Step title="Take Romancecar train from Shinjuku" status="todo">1h25min. Reserve seats online (¥2,300/pp).</Step>
<Step title="Hakone Loop" status="todo">Cable car → Ropeway (see Owakudani volcanic valley) → Pirate ship cruise on Lake Ashi → Back to Hakone-Yumoto.</Step>
<Step title="Onsen experience" status="todo">Try a day-use onsen like <Badge>Tenzan Onsen</Badge> (¥1,500/pp). Private baths available for couples.</Step>
<Step title="Return to Tokyo" status="todo">Last trains around 21:00. Dinner at <Badge>Hakone-Yumoto station area</Badge>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tab title="Kamakura">
<Callout tone="neutral" title="Budget-friendly">Cheaper and closer — only 1 hour from Tokyo.</Callout>

<Step title="Komachi-dori Street" status="todo">Snack on <Badge>kamakura vegetables</Badge> and <Badge>shirasu-don</Badge> (whitebait rice bowl).</Step>
<Step title="Enoshima Island" status="todo">Cross the bridge, climb to the lighthouse for panoramic views. Sea Candle observation deck (¥500).</Step>

## Practical Tips

<Card title="Essentials for Two">
<Columns>
<Card title="Money">
- Cash is still king — many small shops don't take cards
- ATMs at 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, and post offices work with foreign cards
- Budget: ¥8,000–12,000/day for two (food + transport + small purchases)
</Card>
<Card title="Transport">
- Get **Suica or Pasmo** IC cards (¥500 deposit, refundable)
- Covers all trains, buses, and convenience store payments
- JR Pass not worth it for just Tokyo
- Taxis are expensive — avoid unless late at night
</Card>
<Card title="Etiquette">
- No tipping — it's considered rude
- Eat while walking is frowned upon — finish at the stall
- Shoes off in temples and some restaurants
- Quiet on trains — no phone calls
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

## Packing Checklist

<Card title="What to Pack for Two">
<Checklist title="Packing list" name="tokyo-packing">
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 15,000+ steps/day)
- [ ] Light jacket or sweater (15–20°C in October)
- [ ] Small umbrella (sudden rain is common)
- [ ] Power bank (you'll use maps and translation apps)
- [ ] Pocket WiFi or eSIM (get at airport or online before)
- [ ] Reusable shopping bag (plastic bags cost ¥3–5)
- [ ] Passport (carry at all times — required by law)
- [ ] Cash in yen (get at airport ATM)
