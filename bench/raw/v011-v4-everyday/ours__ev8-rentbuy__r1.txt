<Card title="Decision guide">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check your deposit" status="current" subtitle="You need at least 10%">
If you have less than £25,000 saved, renting is the only option right now. Save up while renting.
</Step>
<Step title="How long will you stay?" subtitle="5+ years favours buying">
Buying costs ~£8,000 in fees. If you move within 3 years, renting is usually cheaper.
</Step>
<Step title="Can you handle repairs?" subtitle="A new boiler costs £3,000+">
As a buyer, you're responsible for everything. Have an emergency fund of at least £5,000.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you value flexibility?" subtitle="Renting wins for freedom">
If your job or life might change in the next 3 years, rent. If you're settled, buy.
</Step>
</Steps>
