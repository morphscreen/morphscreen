# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

¡Vamos! Let's get you ready with the essential phrases you'll use every day.

<Card title="Your Core Phrases">
<Flashcards title="10 Spanish Phrases to Know">
| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hola | Hello |
| Gracias | Thank you |
| Por favor | Please |
| Sí / No | Yes / No |
| Perdón / Disculpe | Excuse me / Sorry |
| ¿Dónde está…? | Where is…? |
| La cuenta, por favor | The check, please |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | How much does it cost? |
| No entiendo | I don't understand |
| ¿Habla inglés? | Do you speak English? |
</Flashcards>
</Card>

<Card title="When to Use Each Phrase">
<Steps>
<Step title="Greeting" status="current" subtitle="Start every interaction">
**Hola** — Use it morning, noon, and night. It's friendly and works everywhere.

<Button ask="Teach me more greetings in Spanish">More greetings</Button>
</Step>

<Step title="Politeness" status="todo" subtitle="Essential manners">
**Gracias** and **Por favor** go a long way. Always say *gracias* after someone helps you.

<Button ask="Show me polite phrases for dining out">Dining phrases</Button>
</Step>

<Step title="Getting help" status="todo" subtitle="When you need something">
- **Perdón / Disculpe** — to get someone's attention or apologise for bumping into them
- **¿Dónde está…?** — add a place: *¿Dónde está el baño?* (Where is the bathroom?)
- **¿Habla inglés?** — ask before assuming someone speaks English

<Button ask="Give me more questions for finding places">More location phrases</Button>
</Step>

<Step title="Shopping & dining" status="todo" subtitle="Paying and asking prices">
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** — point at the item
- **La cuenta, por favor** — when you're ready to leave a restaurant

<Button ask="Show me restaurant phrases in Spanish">Restaurant phrases</Button>
</Step>

<Step title="When you're stuck" status="todo" subtitle="Don't panic">
**No entiendo** — people will slow down or try English.

<Button ask="What do I say if someone speaks too fast?">Help! Too fast</Button>
</Step>
</Steps>
