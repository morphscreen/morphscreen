<Card title="Camera comparison">
Both phones take great photos, but they have different strengths.

| Aspect | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Color science | Natural, true-to-life | More vibrant, punchy |
| Low light | Good, with Night mode | Excellent, best in class |
| Portrait mode | Reliable edge detection | More natural bokeh |
| Video | Best in class, 4K 60fps Dolby Vision | Good, 4K 60fps HDR |
| AI editing | Clean Up tool | Magic Editor, Best Take, Audio Magic Eraser |

<Callout tone="info" title="Photography tip">
If you take mostly photos, especially of people and in low light, the Pixel 9 wins. If you shoot a lot of video, the iPhone 16 is the clear choice.
</Callout>
