# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

<Card title="Overview">
The Vancouver area offers world-class hiking within a short drive from the city. Whether you're looking for coastal views, mountain peaks, or forest trails, there's something for every fitness level. Below are the top day hikes, organized by difficulty and experience.

<Card title="What to Bring">
<Checklist title="Hiking essentials" name="hiking-gear">
- [ ] Sturdy hiking boots or trail runners
- [ ] Layers (temperature drops quickly)
- [ ] Rain jacket (Vancouver weather!)
- [ ] 1–2 litres of water per person
- [ ] Snacks and lunch
- [ ] Sunscreen and hat
- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Headlamp or flashlight (even for day hikes)
- [ ] Map or GPS (cell service is spotty)
- [ ] Bear spray (especially Garibaldi and Chief)
</Checklist>
</Card>

<Card title="Safety & Tips">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Before you go">
- Check trail conditions on the BC Parks website or AllTrails
- Start early — afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer
- Tell someone your route and expected return time
- No cell service on many trails
- Pack out all garbage
</Callout>
</Card>
