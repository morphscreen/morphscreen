# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Badge tone="success">Essential for travel</Badge>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pronunciation tip">
Spanish pronunciation is very consistent. Once you learn how each letter sounds, you can read any word aloud. The key rule: **every vowel has one sound** — A (ah), E (eh), I (ee), O (oh), U (oo). The letter **H** is always silent.
</Callout>

<Card title="Key Questions">
| Spanish | English | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **¿Dónde está el baño?** | Where is the bathroom? | Any restaurant, cafe, or shop |
| **¿Cuánto cuesta?** | How much does it cost? | Markets, souvenirs, taxis |
| **¿Habla inglés?** | Do you speak English? | When you're stuck |
| **¿Puede ayudarme?** | Can you help me? | Asking for assistance |

<Callout tone="warning" title="Important">
The upside-down question mark **¿** is used in Spanish at the start of every question. It helps you know the intonation before you begin speaking.
</Callout>

<Card title="Practice Your Pronunciation">
<Steps>
<Step title="Hello / Goodbye" status="current">
- **Hola** — OH-lah
- **Adiós** — ah-dee-OHS
- **Chao** — CHOW (informal goodbye)
</Step>
<Step title="Thank you / Please">
- **Gracias** — GRAH-seeahs
- **Por favor** — por fah-VOR
- **De nada** — deh NAH-dah
</Step>
<Step title="Where is the bathroom?">
- **¿Dónde está el baño?** — DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl BAH-nyoh
- Say it confidently — locals will appreciate the effort!
</Step>
<Step title="How much?">
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** — KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah
- Useful at markets, street stalls, and taxis
</Step>
<Step title="I don't understand">
- **No entiendo** — noh ehn-tee-EHN-doh
- Follow it with **¿Habla inglés?** (AH-blah een-GLEHS) — "Do you speak English?"
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
