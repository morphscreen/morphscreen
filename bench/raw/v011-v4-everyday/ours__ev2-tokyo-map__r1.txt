# 5-Day Tokyo Itinerary for Two

Tokyo blends ancient temples, neon-lit streets, world-class food, and peaceful gardens. This plan balances iconic sights with local experiences, keeping you moving efficiently across the city.

<Card title="Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku Neon">
**Theme:** Settle in, see Tokyo's futuristic side

<Steps>
<Step title="Check into hotel" status="done" subtitle="Shinjuku area recommended">Drop luggage, freshen up</Step>
<Step title="Lunch at Omoide Yokocho" status="current" subtitle="Piss Alley">Narrow lanes of tiny yakitori grills — pick a stall, order skewers and beer</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building" subtitle="Free observation deck">Sunset view over the whole city, Mt. Fuji on clear days</Step>
<Step title="Dinner & Kabukicho" subtitle="Robot Restaurant area">Walk the neon-lit entertainment district; grab ramen at Ichiran or a sushi conveyor belt</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 2: Traditional Tokyo (Asakusa & Ueno)">
**Theme:** Old Tokyo, temples, gardens, and street food

<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple" status="current" subtitle="Asakusa">Tokyo's oldest temple; walk Nakamise-dori shopping street for souvenirs and melon pan</Step>
<Step title="Ueno Park & Museums" subtitle="Cherry blossoms in spring">Stroll the park, visit a museum (Tokyo National Museum recommended)</Step>
<Step title="Ameya-Yokocho Market" subtitle="Ueno">Bustling market street for street food, clothes, and bargains</Step>
<Step title="Sumida River cruise" subtitle="To Hamarikyu Gardens">Relaxing boat ride with skyline views, ends at a beautiful feudal garden</Step>
</Steps>

**Theme:** Youth culture, fashion, and the famous crossing

<Steps>
<Step title="Meiji Jingu Shrine" status="current" subtitle="Harajuku">Peaceful forested shrine, a short walk from Harajuku station</Step>
<Step title="Takeshita Street" subtitle="Harajuku">Kawaii culture, crepes, and quirky shops</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Scramble Crossing" subtitle="The iconic intersection">View from Starbucks above or the new Shibuya Sky rooftop</Step>
<Step title="Dinner in Shibuya" subtitle="Nonbei Yokocho">Tiny bar alley for drinks and small plates</Step>

<Card title="Day 4: Akihabara & Ginza">
**Theme:** Electronics, anime, and high-end Tokyo

<Steps>
<Step title="Akihabara Electric Town" status="current" subtitle="Otaku culture">Browse electronics shops, anime stores, and themed cafes (Maid Cafe, Gundam Cafe)</Step>

<Card title="Quick Tips">
<Callout tone="info" title="Etiquette">
- No tipping ever — it's considered rude
- Bow instead of handshake
- Eat while walking is frowned upon (stand near the stall)
- Shoes off in temples and some restaurants
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Transport">
- Get a Suica card at Narita/Haneda airport
- Trains stop around midnight — last train is ~12:30 AM
- Avoid rush hour (7:30–9 AM, 5–7 PM)
- Google Maps works perfectly for routes
</Callout>
</Card>
