# 5-Day Tokyo Itinerary for Two

<Badge tone="success">Best for first-time visitors</Badge> <Badge tone="info">~¥150,000–200,000 per person</Badge>

<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at any station — tap on/off trains, buses, and even convenience stores. The Yamanote loop line connects most major areas.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1: Shibuya & Shinjuku — Neon & Culture">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Meiji Jingu & Harajuku" status="current">
Start at the serene Meiji Shrine (free), then walk to Takeshita Street in Harajuku for quirky fashion and crepes. <Badge tone="warning">Crowded on weekends</Badge>
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Ramen in Shibuya" status="todo">
Try **Ichiran Shibuya** (individual booths, ~¥1,500/person) or **Afuri** for yuzu shio ramen.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya Scramble & Hachiko" status="todo">
See the famous crossing from Starbucks above, snap Hachiko statue, then shop at Shibuya Parco (Nintendo, Pokémon centers).
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Shinjuku Golden Gai" status="todo">
Explore tiny bars in Golden Gai alleys — each seats 4–8 people. Great for a drink with your partner. <Badge tone="success">Try Omoide Yokocho for yakitori</Badge>
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 2: Asakusa & Ueno — Old Tokyo">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise Street" status="current">
Tokyo's oldest temple. Walk through the thunder gate, browse the shopping street for souvenirs, and get a fortune (omikuji).
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Monjayaki in Tsukishima" status="todo">
Cook your own savory pancake at **Monja Street** — interactive and fun for two (~¥2,000/person).
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ueno Park & Museums" status="todo">
Visit Ueno Zoo (pandas!) or Tokyo National Museum. Cherry blossoms in spring. <Badge tone="info">Free entry to park</Badge>
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Sumida River Cruise" status="todo">
Take a water bus from Asakusa to Hamarikyu Gardens or Odaiba — see Tokyo from the water at sunset.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Plan day 2 in more detail">More on day 2</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3: Akihabara & Ginza — Tech & Luxury">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Akihabara Electric Town" status="current">
Browse electronics, anime goods, and retro games at **Yodobashi Camera** and **Radio Kaikan**. <Badge tone="success">Maid cafes nearby</Badge>
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Tonkatsu at Maisen" status="todo">
Famous pork cutlet in Omotesando — crispy, juicy, ~¥2,500/person.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ginza & Kabukiza" status="todo">
Walk Ginza's luxury stores, see the Kabuki theater (1-act tickets from ¥1,000), or visit the **Art Aquarium**.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Rooftop bar with Tokyo Tower view" status="todo">
**The Moon** or **New York Bar** at Park Hyatt (from *Lost in Translation*) — cocktails ~¥2,000.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Plan day 3 in more detail">More on day 3</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4: Day Trip to Hakone — Mt. Fuji Views">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Romancecar to Hakone" status="current">
Take the Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (~85 min, ¥2,300). Get the **Hakone Free Pass** (¥6,100) for unlimited transport.
</Step>
<Step title="Sightseeing: Ropeway & Pirate Ship" status="todo">
Ride the cable car up to **Owakudani** (volcanic valley, black eggs), then cruise across Lake Ashi on a pirate ship with Mt. Fuji views.
</Step>
<Step title="Relax: Onsen & Kaiseki Dinner" status="todo">
Stay at a ryokan with private onsen (e.g., **Hakone Ginyu**, ~¥50,000/night for two) or day-use onsen like **Yunessun**.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Plan day 4 in more detail">More on day 4</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5: TeamLab & Departure">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: teamLab Borderless (or Planets)" status="current">
Immersive digital art — book tickets in advance (¥3,200/person). Borderless is in Azabudai Hills; Planets is in Toyosu.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="todo">
Fresh sushi, tamagoyaki, and grilled seafood. Try **Sushi Dai** (long queue) or grab street food.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Last shopping & Departure" status="todo">
Pick up omiyage (gifts) at **Don Quijote** or **Tokyo Station** — they have everything. Head to Narita/Haneda airport.
</Step>
</Steps>

Use **convenience store meals** (onigiri, sandwiches) for breakfast — cheap and delicious. Many museums offer discounts with **Tokyo Metro 24-hour pass** (¥600).

<Card title="Packing Checklist for Two">
<Checklist title="What to bring" name="tokyo-packing">
- [x] Suica/Pasmo IC card (get at airport)
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 15k+ steps/day)
- [ ] Portable Wi-Fi or eSIM (e.g., Ubigi, Airalo)
- [ ] Power bank (no public outlets in stations)
- [ ] Cash (many small shops are cash-only)
- [ ] Reusable shopping bag (plastic bags cost ¥3–5)
- [ ] Light jacket (October is 15–20°C)
- [ ] Passport (for tax-free shopping over ¥5,000)
</Checklist>
</Card>
