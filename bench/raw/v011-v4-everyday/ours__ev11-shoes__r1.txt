<Card title="How to Choose the Right Fit">
<Steps>
<Step title="Know your arch" status="current" subtitle="Flat, normal, or high">
Visit a running store for a gait analysis, or do the wet test: wet your foot and step on paper. A full footprint means flat arches, a moderate one means normal, and a narrow one means high arches.
</Step>
<Step title="Measure your size" status="todo" subtitle="Shoes stretch, feet swell">
Your running shoe size is usually ½ to 1 size larger than your casual shoe size. Leave a thumb's width between your longest toe and the shoe's end.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the return policy" status="todo" subtitle="Test them at home">
Most brands offer a 30-day trial. Run on a treadmill or carpet first — if they don't feel right, exchange them.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
