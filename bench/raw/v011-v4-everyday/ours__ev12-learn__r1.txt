# How Photosynthesis Works

<Card title="Photosynthesis in a Nutshell">
Plants convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into food (glucose) and oxygen. It's how they eat and how we breathe.

<KeyValue title="The Big Picture">
| Ingredient | Role |
|---|---|
| Sunlight | Energy source |
| Water (H₂O) | Electron donor, split apart |
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | Carbon source for sugar |
| Chlorophyll | Pigment that captures light |
| Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) | Food for the plant |
| Oxygen (O₂) | Byproduct, released into air |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="The Chemical Equation">
<Math tex="6CO_2 + 6H_2O + light \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2" label="Photosynthesis equation" />
<Stat label="Input" value="6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O" icon="arrow-circle-down" />
<Stat label="Output" value="1 Glucose + 6 O₂" icon="arrow-circle-up" />
</Card>

<Card title="The Two Stages" subtitle="Photosynthesis happens in two linked parts">
<Columns>
<Card title="Light-Dependent Reactions">
<Badge tone="info">Needs light</Badge>

1. **Light hits chlorophyll** in the thylakoid membranes.
2. **Water splits** (photolysis): H₂O → 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ + ½O₂.
3. **Electrons move** through an electron transport chain.
4. **ATP and NADPH** are produced — energy carriers.

<Stat label="Products" value="ATP + NADPH + O₂" icon="lightning" />
</Card>

<Card title="Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent)">
<Badge tone="info">Can happen in dark</Badge>

1. **CO₂ is fixed** (attached to RuBP by RuBisCO).
2. **ATP and NADPH** from the light reactions power the conversion.
3. **3-PGA → G3P** (a 3-carbon sugar).
4. **Glucose is built** from two G3P molecules.

<Stat label="Product" value="Glucose (sugar)" icon="flask" />
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Where It Happens">
<KeyValue title="Inside the leaf">
| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Stomata | Tiny pores that let CO₂ in and O₂ out |
| Mesophyll cells | Main site of photosynthesis |
| Chloroplasts | Organelles where it all happens |
| Thylakoid | Disc-shaped sacs inside chloroplasts; light reactions here |
| Stroma | Fluid around thylakoids; Calvin cycle here |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="Step by Step: How a Plant Makes Food">
<Steps>
<Step title="1. Capture sunlight" status="done" subtitle="Chlorophyll absorbs photons">
Light energy excites electrons in chlorophyll molecules.
</Step>
<Step title="2. Split water" status="done" subtitle="Photolysis">
Water molecules break apart, releasing oxygen as a waste product.
</Step>
<Step title="3. Make ATP & NADPH" status="current" subtitle="Electron transport chain">
Electrons flow through proteins, pumping protons and building energy molecules.
</Step>
<Step title="4. Fix carbon" status="todo" subtitle="Calvin cycle begins">
CO₂ from the air is attached to a 5-carbon molecule (RuBP).
</Step>
<Step title="5. Build sugar" status="todo" subtitle="Reduction phase">
Using ATP and NADPH, 3-carbon sugars are made and combined into glucose.
</Step>
<Step title="6. Regenerate RuBP" status="todo" subtitle="Cycle continues">
Some G3P molecules rebuild RuBP so the cycle can keep going.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Why It Matters to You">
<Columns>
<Card title="Oxygen we breathe">
<Stat label="O₂ from photosynthesis" value="~100%" icon="wind" />
The oxygen in every breath you take came from a plant, algae, or cyanobacterium doing photosynthesis.
</Card>
<Card title="Food we eat">
<Stat label="Calories from photosynthesis" value="100%" icon="bread" />
All food energy ultimately comes from the sun, captured by plants.
</Card>
<Card title="Climate impact">
<Stat label="CO₂ removed yearly" value="~120 Gt" icon="globe" />

</Card>
