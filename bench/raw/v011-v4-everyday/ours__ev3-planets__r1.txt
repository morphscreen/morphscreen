# The Planets in Our Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets orbiting the Sun, divided into two groups: the inner rocky planets and the outer gas/ice giants.

<Card title="The Inner Rocky Planets">
<Columns>
<Card title="Mercury" subtitle="Smallest & closest to Sun">
- **Distance from Sun:** 57.9 million km
- **Diameter:** 4,879 km
- **Day length:** 59 Earth days
- **Year length:** 88 Earth days
- **Temperature:** -180°C to 430°C
- **Moons:** 0
- A cratered, airless world like our Moon
<Button ask="Tell me more about Mercury">Learn about Mercury</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Venus" subtitle="Hottest planet">
- **Distance from Sun:** 108.2 million km
- **Diameter:** 12,104 km
- **Day length:** 243 Earth days (longer than its year!)
- **Year length:** 225 Earth days
- **Temperature:** 465°C (average)
- **Moons:** 0
- Shrouded in thick, toxic clouds; rotates backwards
<Button ask="Tell me more about Venus">Learn about Venus</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Earth" subtitle="Our home">
- **Distance from Sun:** 149.6 million km
- **Diameter:** 12,756 km
- **Day length:** 24 hours
- **Year length:** 365.25 days
- **Temperature:** -89°C to 57°C
- **Moons:** 1 (the Moon)
- Only known planet with liquid water and life
<Button ask="Tell me more about Earth">Learn about Earth</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Mars" subtitle="The Red Planet">
- **Distance from Sun:** 227.9 million km
- **Diameter:** 6,792 km
- **Day length:** 24.6 hours
- **Year length:** 687 Earth days
- **Temperature:** -87°C to -5°C
- **Moons:** 2 (Phobos, Deimos)
- Home to the tallest mountain (Olympus Mons) and largest canyon (Valles Marineris)
<Button ask="Tell me more about Mars">Learn about Mars</Button>
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="The Outer Gas & Ice Giants">
<Columns>
<Card title="Jupiter" subtitle="Largest planet">
- **Distance from Sun:** 778.5 million km
- **Diameter:** 142,984 km
- **Day length:** 9.9 hours (fastest rotation)
- **Year length:** 11.9 Earth years
- **Temperature:** -110°C (cloud top)
- **Moons:** 95 (including Ganymede, the largest moon in the solar system)
- Famous for the Great Red Spot, a storm larger than Earth
<Button ask="Tell me more about Jupiter">Learn about Jupiter</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Saturn" subtitle="The Ringed Planet">
- **Distance from Sun:** 1.4 billion km
- **Diameter:** 120,536 km
- **Day length:** 10.7 hours
- **Year length:** 29.5 Earth years
- **Temperature:** -140°C
- **Moons:** 146 (including Titan, with a thick atmosphere)
- Its ring system spans up to 282,000 km but is only ~10 meters thick
<Button ask="Tell me more about Saturn">Learn about Saturn</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Uranus" subtitle="The sideways planet">
- **Distance from Sun:** 2.9 billion km
- **Diameter:** 51,118 km
- **Day length:** 17.2 hours
- **Year length:** 84 Earth years
- **Temperature:** -195°C
- **Moons:** 27
- Rotates on its side, likely from a massive ancient collision
<Button ask="Tell me more about Uranus">Learn about Uranus</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Neptune" subtitle="Farthest & windiest">
- **Distance from Sun:** 4.5 billion km
- **Diameter:** 49,528 km
- **Day length:** 16.1 hours
- **Year length:** 165 Earth years
- **Temperature:** -200°C
- **Moons:** 16 (including Triton, which orbits backwards)
- Winds reach 2,100 km/h — the fastest in the solar system
<Button ask="Tell me more about Neptune">Learn about Neptune</Button>
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
