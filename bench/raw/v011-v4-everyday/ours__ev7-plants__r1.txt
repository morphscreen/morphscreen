<Card title="How to Keep Them Alive (Minimal Effort)">
<Steps>
<Step title="Water sparingly" status="current" subtitle="Less is more">
<Callout tone="info">Only water when the top 2–3 inches of soil feel dry. For Snake and ZZ plants, wait until the soil is fully dry — they prefer drought to soggy roots.</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Give them any light (except direct sun)" status="todo">
These plants tolerate low light but grow faster in bright, indirect light. Avoid placing them in direct afternoon sun, which can scorch leaves.
</Step>
<Step title="Don't repot often" status="todo">
They prefer being slightly root-bound. Repot only every 2–3 years, or when roots push out the drainage holes.
</Step>
<Step title="Ignore them a little" status="todo">
The #1 killer of these plants is over-care — too much water, too much fertilizer, too much attention. Let them be.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Common Mistakes to Avoid">
<Checklist title="Don't do these" name="mistakes">
- [ ] Watering on a fixed schedule — check the soil first
- [ ] Using a pot without drainage holes
- [ ] Placing in direct, hot sunlight
- [ ] Fertilizing in winter (they rest then)
- [ ] Repotting into a huge pot — go just 1–2 inches wider
</Checklist>
</Card>
