# Senior Product Designer

<Badge tone="info">Design Team</Badge> · <Badge tone="neutral">Full-time</Badge> · <Badge tone="neutral">Remote / San Francisco</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design Team and help shape the future of how people collaborate. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to craft intuitive, beautiful experiences that delight millions of users. If you're passionate about design systems, user research, and pixel-perfect execution, we'd love to hear from you.
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years of experience in product design (or equivalent portfolio strength)
- Expert-level Figma skills (components, auto layout, prototyping)
- Strong understanding of interaction design, visual hierarchy, and accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Experience shipping consumer or B2B SaaS products across web and mobile
- Ability to present and defend design decisions to stakeholders
- Bonus: motion design (Lottie, Rive) or front-end prototyping (HTML/CSS)
</Card>
