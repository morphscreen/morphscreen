summaryHeader = InlineHeader("Period Summary", "The French Revolution in brief")
summaryContent = MarkDownRenderer("# The French Revolution (1789–1799)\n\nThe French Revolution was a decade of radical political and societal change in France that dismantled the **Ancien Régime** — the absolute monarchy, feudal privileges, and the dominance of the Catholic Church — and replaced it with a republic based on the principles of **liberty, equality, and fraternity**.\n\n## Phases\n\n1. **Moderate Phase (1789–1792)** — The Estates-General convenes, the Third Estate forms the National Assembly, the Bastille falls, feudalism is abolished, and the *Declaration of the Rights of Man* is adopted. A constitutional monarchy is created in 1791.\n\n2. **Radical Phase (1792–1794)** — War with Austria and Prussia, the monarchy is overthrown, the First Republic proclaimed, Louis XVI executed, and the Committee of Public Safety under Robespierre launches the **Reign of Terror** (≈16,000 official executions).\n\n3. **Thermidorian Reaction & Directory (1794–1799)** — Robespierre falls, the Terror ends, a new constitution (Year III) creates the five-man **Directory**. Corruption, inflation, and military dependence weaken the regime.\n\n4. **Napoleonic Coup (1799)** — General Napoleon Bonaparte stages the **Coup of 18 Brumaire**, ending the Revolution and establishing the Consulate.\n\n## Legacy\n- Ended feudalism and aristocratic privilege in France\n- Spread revolutionary ideals across Europe via the Napoleonic Wars\n- Inspired later revolutions (1830, 1848, 1917)\n- Produced enduring symbols: the tricolour flag, *La Marseillaise*, Bastille Day\n- Established the metric system and secular civil law (later the Napoleonic Code)")
