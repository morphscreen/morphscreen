## Sizing and fit tips

<Card title="Get the right fit">
<Checklist title="Before you buy" name="fit-checklist">
- [x] Try them on in the afternoon (feet swell during the day)
- [x] Leave a thumb's width (about 1 cm) between your longest toe and the shoe's end
- [x] Wear the same socks you'll run in
- [ ] Check that your heel doesn't slip when you walk
- [ ] Run a few strides in the store, not just walk
</Checklist>
</Card>
