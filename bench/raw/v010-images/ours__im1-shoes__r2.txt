<Callout tone="info" title="Sizing tip">
All three run true to size in most cases. The Pegasus 41 runs slightly snug — if you're between sizes, size up. The Ghost 16 has a roomy toe box, so true-to-size works well for most.

Also, remember to leave a thumb's width (about 1 cm) between your longest toe and the end of the shoe — your feet swell on runs.
</Callout>
