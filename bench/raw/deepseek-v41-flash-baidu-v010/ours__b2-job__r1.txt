<Card title="About the role" subtitle="What you'll be doing">
You'll own the surface area where our product and our brand meet: the component library, the patterns teams reach for every day, and the craft standards that make our interface feel like one product instead of twelve.

Day to day you'll partner with two product squads, run design reviews, and ship work that other designers build on. We're looking for someone who treats a well-named token as a feature, not a chore — and who can argue for it in a room full of engineers.

<Callout tone="info" title="Why this role is open">
We're splitting the design systems function out of the platform team so it gets dedicated ownership. You'd be the second designer on it, with a clear path to leading it.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
<Details title="Required qualifications" open="true">
- 5+ years designing shipped digital products, including 2+ on a design system or platform team
- Fluency in Figma: variables, component properties, auto layout at scale
- Comfort reading and writing front-end code (HTML/CSS, and enough JS/TS to be dangerous)
- A portfolio showing systems thinking, not just screens — before/after, constraints, tradeoffs
- Experience running critique and aligning designers and engineers behind a shared standard
</Details>

<Details title="Nice to have">
- Built or maintained a public design system or open-source component library
- Accessibility depth: WCAG 2.2, screen reader testing, keyboard-first flows
- Motion and interaction prototyping
- Experience in a B2B SaaS or developer-tools context
</Details>

<Details title="How we hire">
1. Intro call with the hiring manager — 30 min
2. Portfolio walkthrough with two designers — 60 min
3. Systems exercise (paid, ~4 hours, done async) — reviewed live in 60 min
4. Team and values conversations — 45 min
5. Offer
</Details>
