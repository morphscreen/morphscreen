<Details title="How runway is calculated">
Runway = cash on hand ÷ trailing 3-month average burn.

- Cash on hand: $412,800 (operating account + money market, as of Oct 31)
- Trailing 3-month burn: ($63,200 + $67,300 + $65,400) ÷ 3 = $65,300
- Reported runway uses current-month burn ($67,300) for a conservative view → **6.1 months**

Excludes committed but unbilled Q4 contract renewals and the $50k unused credit line.
