<Card title="Day-by-Day Breakdown">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 — West Tokyo Icons">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen Garden" status="done" subtitle="9:00 – 11:30">
Start your trip in this tranquil park — perfect for a stroll among cherry blossoms (spring) or maples (autumn). Entry: ¥500.
</Step>
<Step title="Midday: Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="12:00 – 13:30">
A peaceful Shinto shrine in the heart of the forest. Walk the gravel path and write a wish on an ema plaque.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Harajuku Street Food" status="done" subtitle="13:30 – 14:30">
Grab crepes and crêperie treats on Takeshita Street — a carnival of quirky fashion and sweets.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing" status="done" subtitle="15:00 – 17:00">
Watch the world-famous scramble crossing from the Starbucks by the station, then explore Shibuya's shops and backstreets.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai for dinner" status="done" subtitle="19:00 – 22:00">
Tiny bars and izakayas in Shinjuku's nostalgic alleys — perfect for a cozy dinner for two.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2 — Old & New Tokyo">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="9:00 – 11:00">
Tokyo's oldest temple, with a giant red lantern and incense smoke. Visit before the crowds.
</Step>
<Step title="Midday: Nakamise Shopping Street" status="done" subtitle="11:00 – 12:30">
Browse 200-year-old stalls selling ningyo-yaki (sweet cakes) and souvenirs.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Asakusa Eats" status="done" subtitle="12:30 – 13:30">
Try monjayaki (savory pancake) or tempura at a local spot.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="14:00 – 16:00">
Ride to 350m for panoramic views. Book sunset slot for golden-hour photos.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Sumida River Walk" status="done" subtitle="17:00 – 19:00">
Stroll along the river back toward Asakusa — the Skytree lights up at dusk.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3 — Art & Modern Wonders">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: TeamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="9:00 – 11:00">
An immersive digital art museum — walk through water and mirror rooms. Book tickets online in advance.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="11:30 – 13:00">
Fresh sushi, grilled scallops, and tamago — a foodie paradise.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Tokyo Tower" status="done" subtitle="14:00 – 16:00">
The iconic red tower with a retro-futuristic vibe. Go up to the Main Deck for city views.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Roppongi Hills" status="done" subtitle="18:00 – 21:00">
Dinner at a rooftop restaurant with a view of Tokyo Tower, then Mori Art Museum if open late.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 4 — Studio Ghibli & Nature">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Ghibli Museum" status="done" subtitle="10:00 – 12:00">

<Step title="Lunch: Kichijoji" status="done" subtitle="12:30 – 14:00">

<Step title="Afternoon: Inokashira Park" status="done" subtitle="14:00 – 16:00">

<Step title="Evening: Shimokitazawa" status="done" subtitle="17:00 – 21:00">

<Card title="Practical Tips">
<Details title="Getting around" open="true">
Get a Suica or PASMO IC card at the airport — tap on/off all trains and buses. The Yamanote Line loops the main hubs. Google Maps works well for transit directions.
</Details>
<Details title="When to go">
Spring (late March–early April) for cherry blossoms; autumn (Oct–Nov) for mild weather and fall colors. Avoid Golden Week (late April–early May) and New Year.
</Details>
<Details title="Packing">
Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 15k+ steps/day), a reusable water bottle, a power bank, and a light jacket for evenings.
</Details>
<Details title="Etiquette">
Bow slightly when greeting, don't tip, and keep noise low on trains. Onsen and ryokan have rules — read signs before entering.
</Details>
</Card>
