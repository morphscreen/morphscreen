# The Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets orbiting the Sun, split into two groups: the rocky **inner planets** and the gas/ice giants of the **outer solar system**. Here's a guided tour, from closest to farthest from the Sun.

## Fun Facts

<Callout tone="info" title="Did you know?">
- **Venus spins backwards** — the Sun rises in the west there.
- **Jupiter's Great Red Spot** is a storm larger than Earth that has raged for centuries.
- **Saturn is less dense than water** — it would float in a big enough bathtub.
- **Uranus rolls on its side**, likely from an ancient collision.
- **Neptune has the fastest winds** in the solar system, reaching 2,100 km/h.
- **A day on Mercury** (sunrise to sunrise) lasts 176 Earth days — twice its year!
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="What about Pluto?">
Pluto was reclassified as a **dwarf planet** in 2006 because it hasn't cleared its orbital neighborhood. Other dwarf planets include Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake.
