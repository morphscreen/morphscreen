<Card title="Day 1 – Arrival & Shinjuku Neon" subtitle="Settle in, get lost, and eat your first bowl of ramen">
<Steps>
<Step title="Land at Narita/Haneda" status="done" subtitle="Airport to hotel via Narita Express or Limousine Bus (60–90 min)">
Pick a hotel in Shinjuku, Shibuya, or Ginza – all are well-connected. Drop bags, shower, and head out.
</Step>
<Step title="Stroll through Shinjuku Gyoen" status="done" subtitle="2:00 PM – 4:00 PM | ¥500 entry">
A huge park with a French garden, English landscape, and Japanese tea house. Quiet even in the middle of the city.
</Step>
<Step title="Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM – 9:00 PM | Dinner + drinks">
Golden Gai: tiny, dimly lit bars in narrow alleys – perfect for a pre-dinner drink. Then Omoide Yokocho (“Memory Lane”) for yakitori skewers and beer. Go early to avoid the longest queues.
</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Metropolitan Building Observatory" status="done" subtitle="9:30 PM | Free">
Free night view of the city lights. On clear days, you can see Mt. Fuji in the distance.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 – Asakusa & Sumida River" subtitle="Old Tokyo, temples, and a riverside walk">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise Street" status="done" subtitle="9:00 AM – 11:30 AM | Free">
Arrive early to beat the crowds. Walk through the giant Kaminarimon Gate, browse the souvenir stalls, and draw a fortune at the temple.
</Step>
<Step title="Sumida River walk to Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="11:30 AM – 1:00 PM | Free walk, Skytree ¥2,100">
Walk along the river past the old warehouses and the Asahi Beer Hall (the golden “flame” on top). Cross the Sakurabashi Bridge for a postcard shot of the Skytree.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Monjayaki in Tsukishima" status="done" subtitle="1:30 PM – 3:00 PM | ¥2,500–3,500 per person">
Tsukishima is a tiny island of monjayaki restaurants – a runny, savory pancake you cook yourself on a hot plate. Fun and interactive.
</Step>
<Step title="Hama-rikyu Gardens" status="done" subtitle="4:00 PM – 6:00 PM | ¥300">
A tidal garden with a teahouse on an island. Watch the sunset over the skyscrapers from the water.
</Step>
<Step title="Dinner: Sushi in Ginza" status="done" subtitle="7:30 PM | ¥4,000–8,000 per person">
Book a counter seat at a mid-range sushi spot (e.g., Kyubey or Uogashi Nihon-Ichi). Let the chef choose – omakase style.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 – Shibuya, Harajuku & a quiet walk" subtitle="Pop culture, street style, and a hidden forest">
<Steps>
<Step title="Hachiko Statue & Shibuya Crossing" status="done" subtitle="9:30 AM | Free">
Join the crowd at the world’s busiest intersection. For a better view, head to the Starbucks on the second floor of the Tsutaya building.
</Step>
<Step title="Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="10:30 AM – 12:00 PM | Free">
A serene Shinto shrine in the middle of a 170-acre forest. Watch a traditional wedding procession if you’re lucky.
</Step>
<Step title="Harajuku: Takeshita Street & backstreets" status="done" subtitle="12:00 PM – 2:00 PM | Street food ¥500–1,000">
Takeshita Street for crepes and kawaii shops. Then wander the quieter backstreets of Cat Street for vintage stores and small cafés.
</Step>
<Step title="Yoyogi Park picnic" status="done" subtitle="2:30 PM – 4:00 PM | Free">
Grab takeaway sandwiches or onigiri and sit on the grass. Watch the street performers and the weekend crowds.
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Sky observation deck" status="done" subtitle="5:00 PM | ¥2,000">
Sunset view from the rooftop. Book tickets a few days ahead – it sells out.
</Step>
<Step title="Dinner: Izakaya hop" status="done" subtitle="7:30 PM | ¥3,000–5,000 each">
Pick a few small plates at different izakayas (Japanese pubs). Try takoyaki, karaage, and a highball.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 – Day trip: Nikko or Hakone" subtitle="Choose your adventure – mountains or hot springs">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Option A: Nikko (nature + temples)">
<Steps>
<Step title="Train from Asakusa" status="done" subtitle="7:30 AM departure | Tobu Limited Express, ~2h">
Reserve a seat the day before.
</Step>
<Step title="Toshogu Shrine & Rinno-ji" status="done" subtitle="10:00 AM – 1:00 PM | ¥1,300">
Japan’s most lavishly decorated shrine – gold leaf, carvings, and the famous “see no evil” monkeys.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Yuba (tofu skin) dishes" status="done" subtitle="1:30 PM | ¥1,500–2,500">
A local specialty – fresh, delicate, and vegetarian-friendly.
</Step>
<Step title="Kegon Falls & Lake Chuzenji" status="done" subtitle="2:30 PM – 5:00 PM | Bus + elevator ¥570">
A 97-metre waterfall and a calm lake. If time allows, take the boat cruise (¥1,250).
</Step>
<Step title="Return to Tokyo" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM departure | Back by 8:30 PM">
Grab a bento for the train.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Option B: Hakone (onsen + Mt Fuji)">
<Steps>
<Step title="Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="7:30 AM departure | ~90 min">
Book the “Romancecar” for reserved seats with big windows.
</Step>
<Step title="Hakone Open-Air Museum" status="done" subtitle="9:30 AM – 11:30 AM | ¥1,600">
Sculptures, a Picasso pavilion, and an outdoor footbath with a view.
</Step>
<Step title="Ropeway over volcanic valley" status="done" subtitle="12:00 PM – 1:30 PM | ¥1,500 one-way">
See sulfur vents and, on clear days, Mt. Fuji. Stop at Owakudani for black eggs (¥500 for 5).
</Step>
<Step title="Lake Ashi cruise + shrine" status="done" subtitle="2:00 PM – 4:30 PM | Pirate ship ¥1,000">
A gondola ride across the lake with the torii gate of Hakone Shrine in the water.
</Step>
<Step title="Onsen before the train back" status="done" subtitle="5:00 PM – 6:30 PM | ¥1,500–2,500">
A quick soak at a day-use onsen. Many have a “no tattoo” policy – check before entering.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 – Art, gardens & last-minute finds" subtitle="A slower day before you fly home">
<Steps>
<Step title="TeamLab Planets (or Borderless)" status="done" subtitle="9:30 AM – 11:30 AM | ¥3,200">
A digital art museum where you walk through water and light. Book the earliest slot to avoid queues. Wear shorts – you’ll be barefoot.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Kaisendon (seafood rice bowl) in Toyosu" status="done" subtitle="12:00 PM | ¥2,000–3,500">
The market area has casual sushi and donburi spots. No need to queue for the tuna auction – just eat well.
</Step>
<Step title="Shopping: Ginza or Nakameguro" status="done" subtitle="2:00 PM – 5:00 PM | Free">
Ginza for department stores and artisan shops. Nakameguro for vintage clothing and riverside cafés.
</Step>
<Step title="Final walk: Rikugien Garden" status="done" subtitle="5:30 PM – 7:00 PM | ¥300">
A strolling garden with a central pond. If you’re here in late March, the weeping cherry blossoms are spectacular.
</Step>
<Step title="Airport transfer" status="done" subtitle="Leave hotel by 8:00 PM">
Give yourself 90 minutes to Narita, 60 to Haneda.
</Step>
</Steps>
