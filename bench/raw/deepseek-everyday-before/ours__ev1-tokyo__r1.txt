# Tokyo: A 5-Day Itinerary for Two

A balanced mix of iconic sights, tranquil gardens, delicious food, and local experiences.

## Day 1: Arrival & Shibuya

<Card title="Day 1 – Shibuya & Harajuku" subtitle="Iconic Tokyo energy">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Arrive & Check-in" status="done" subtitle="Narita/Haneda → Shinjuku Hotel">
Take the Narita Express (N'EX) or Airport Limousine Bus to your hotel. Drop your bags, grab a quick coffee, and get ready to explore.
</Step>
<Step title="Late Morning: Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="Serenity in the city">
Walk through the towering torii gate and forested path to this peaceful Shinto shrine. Perfect first stop to center yourself.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Harajuku Street Food" status="done" subtitle="Crepes & more">
Stroll down Takeshita Street, famous for colorful crepes, quirky fashion, and vibrant youth culture. Try a savory crepe for lunch.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing" status="done" subtitle="The world's busiest intersection">
Experience the famous scramble crossing. For the best view, head to the free observation deck at Shibuya Scramble Square (SHIBUYA SKY).
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Dinner & Neon Night" status="done" subtitle="Izakaya hopping">
Explore the backstreets of Shibuya for a cozy izakaya (Japanese pub). Try yakitori (grilled skewers) and sake. End with a walk through the neon-lit streets.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

---

## Day 2: Asakusa & Sumida River

<Card title="Day 2 – Old Tokyo & River Views" subtitle="Tradition meets the skyline">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's oldest temple">
Start early to beat crowds at this iconic Buddhist temple. Walk through the Thunder Gate (Kaminarimon) and the bustling Nakamise shopping street.
</Step>
<Step title="Late Morning: Kimono Experience" status="done" subtitle="Optional but memorable">
Rent a kimono for a few hours and wander the historic streets of Asakusa. Many shops offer packages with hair styling.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Tempura or Soba" status="done" subtitle="Traditional lunch">
Try a classic tempura set or hand-made soba noodles in a local restaurant near the temple.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Sumida River Cruise" status="done" subtitle="To Hamarikyu Gardens">
Take a relaxing river cruise down the Sumida River to the beautiful Hamarikyu Gardens, a tranquil landscape garden with a teahouse.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Ginza & Dinner" status="done" subtitle="High-end & high-energy">
Explore the upscale district of Ginza. For dinner, try a *kaiseki* (multi-course) meal or a high-end sushi counter. End with a drink at a rooftop bar.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

---

## Day 3: Shinjuku & Nature

<Card title="Day 3 – Shinjuku & Nature Escape" subtitle="Urban park & neon nights">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen Garden" status="done" subtitle="A green oasis">
One of Tokyo's most beautiful parks, featuring Japanese, French, and English gardens. Perfect for a peaceful morning stroll.
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Ramen in Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="A local favorite">
Shinjuku is ramen heaven. Try a rich tonkotsu (pork bone) broth or a spicy miso ramen at a famous shop.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Free Time" status="done" subtitle="Shopping or anime">
Choose your adventure: explore the massive department stores (Isetan, Lumine), visit the anime mecca of Akihabara, or relax in a *kaiten* (conveyor belt) sushi restaurant.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Neon-lit alleys">
Experience Shinjuku's famous narrow alleys packed with tiny bars and eateries. Golden Gai is great for cocktails; Omoide Yokocho ("Memory Lane") is perfect for grilled skewers and beer.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 4: Day Trip – Hakone or Nikko

<Card title="Day 4 – Day Trip Option" subtitle="Choose your adventure">

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Hakone (Onsen & Mt. Fuji)">
- **Morning:** Take the Romancecar train from Shinjuku (about 85 min) to Hakone-Yumoto.
- **Midday:** Ride the Hakone Tozan Railway, cable car, and ropeway for panoramic Mt. Fuji views (weather permitting).
- **Afternoon:** Cruise Lake Ashi on a pirate ship and visit the volcanic Owakudani Valley with its black eggs (boiled in sulfur springs).
- **Evening:** Return to Tokyo in the late afternoon. Or, stay overnight at a *ryokan* (traditional inn) with an onsen (hot spring) for the full experience.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Nikko (Temples & Nature)">
- **Morning:** Take the Tobu Limited Express from Asakusa (about 2 hours) to Nikko.
- **Midday:** Visit the UNESCO World Heritage Toshogu Shrine, the lavishly decorated mausoleum of Tokugawa Ieyasu.
- **Afternoon:** Walk through the cedar-lined paths of Rinno-ji and Futarasan Shrine. If time permits, see the Kegon Waterfall.
- **Evening:** Return to Tokyo by late afternoon.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout tone="info" title="Weather note">
Check the Mt. Fuji visibility forecast before heading to Hakone. It's often hidden by clouds. Nikko is more reliable for temple architecture regardless of weather.
</Callout>
</Card>

## Day 5: TeamLab & Departure

<Card title="Day 5 – Digital Art & Last-Minute Souvenirs" subtitle="A magical finale">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: teamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="Immersive art experience">
Visit this incredible digital art museum where you walk through water and interact with light installations. Book tickets in advance. (Note: You'll be barefoot and may get wet – wear shorts.)
</Step>
<Step title="Lunch: Toyosu Market" status="done" subtitle="Fresh sushi & seafood">
Take a short taxi ride to the new Toyosu Market. Try a fresh sushi breakfast/lunch at one of the many stalls.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Last-Minute Shopping" status="done" subtitle="Souvenirs & snacks">
Head back to your hotel area for final souvenir shopping – think KitKat flavors, matcha tea, and unique stationery. Don't forget to pick up a bottle of sake or whisky.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Departure" status="done" subtitle="To the airport">
Take the Narita Express or Limousine Bus to the airport. If you have time, grab a final bowl of udon or a bento box for the flight.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Practical Tips" subtitle="Make it smooth">

<Columns>
<Card title="Transportation">
- **Suica/Pasmo IC card** – Load with cash, tap on/off. Works on all trains, buses, and even vending machines.
- **Tokyo Metro 72-hour pass** – Great value if you stay central.
- **Google Maps** – Use it for train times and transfers; it's reliable.
- **Avoid rush hour** – 7:30–9:30 AM and 5:00–7:00 PM are packed.
</Card>
<Card title="Etiquette & Culture">
- **No eating while walking** – Stand near a vending machine or step aside.
- **No tipping** – It's not expected; service charge is included.
- **Keep quiet on trains** – No phone calls; keep conversations soft.
- **Take off shoes** – In temples, traditional restaurants, and some shops.
- **Carry cash** – Many small shops and restaurants don't take cards.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
