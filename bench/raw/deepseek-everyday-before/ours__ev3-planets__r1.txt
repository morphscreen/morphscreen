# The Planets of Our Solar System

Our solar system is home to eight planets, each with its own unique characteristics. They orbit the Sun in two main groups: the inner rocky planets and the outer gas giants.

## The Inner Rocky Planets

These four planets are composed mostly of rock and metal, with solid surfaces.

<Columns>
<Card title="Mercury">
- **Size**: Smallest planet, 4,879 km diameter
- **Day**: 59 Earth days (rotation)
- **Year**: 88 Earth days
- **Temperature**: -173°C to 427°C
- **Notable**: Closest to the Sun, extreme temperature swings, no atmosphere to speak of
</Card>

<Card title="Venus">
- **Size**: Similar to Earth, 12,104 km diameter
- **Day**: 243 Earth days (retrograde)
- **Year**: 225 Earth days
- **Temperature**: ~465°C (hottest planet)
- **Notable**: Thick CO₂ atmosphere, crushing pressure, sulfuric acid clouds
</Card>
</Columns>

<Columns>
<Card title="Earth">
- **Size**: 12,742 km diameter
- **Day**: 24 hours
- **Year**: 365.25 days
- **Temperature**: Avg 15°C
- **Notable**: Only known planet with life, liquid water oceans, protective magnetic field
</Card>

<Card title="Mars">
- **Size**: 6,779 km diameter
- **Day**: 24.6 hours
- **Year**: 687 Earth days
- **Temperature**: Avg -63°C
- **Notable**: The "Red Planet", has polar ice caps, Olympus Mons (largest volcano), past evidence of liquid water
</Card>
</Columns>

## The Outer Gas and Ice Giants

These planets are much larger, with no solid surface and extensive ring systems.

<Columns>
<Card title="Jupiter">
- **Size**: Largest planet, 139,820 km diameter
- **Day**: 9.9 hours (fastest rotation)
- **Year**: 11.9 Earth years
- **Notable**: Great Red Spot (giant storm), strongest magnetic field, 95 known moons
</Card>

<Card title="Saturn">
- **Size**: 116,460 km diameter
- **Day**: 10.7 hours
- **Year**: 29.5 Earth years
- **Notable**: Famous ring system made of ice and rock, lowest density (would float in water), 146 moons
</Card>
</Columns>

<Columns>
<Card title="Uranus">
- **Size**: 50,724 km diameter
- **Day**: 17.2 hours (retrograde)
- **Year**: 84 Earth years
- **Notable**: Rotates on its side (98° tilt), methane gives it a blue-green color, coldest atmosphere (-224°C)
</Card>

<Card title="Neptune">
- **Size**: 49,244 km diameter
- **Day**: 16.1 hours
- **Year**: 165 Earth years
- **Notable**: Strongest winds (up to 2,100 km/h), discovered by math before observation, deep blue color
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="info" title="What about Pluto?">
Pluto was reclassified as a **dwarf planet** in 2006 because it hasn't cleared its orbit of other debris. The solar system also has four other recognized dwarf planets: Ceres, Haumea, Makemake, and Eris.
</Callout>

<Card title="Key Takeaways">
- The inner planets are small, rocky, and have few or no moons
- The outer planets are huge, gaseous, and have many moons
- Jupiter is so large that over 1,300 Earths could fit inside it
- All planets orbit the Sun in the same direction, on roughly the same plane
- Only Earth is confirmed to support life
</Card>
