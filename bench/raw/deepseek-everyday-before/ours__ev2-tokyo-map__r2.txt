<Card title="Day-by-Day Plan" subtitle="Pace: relaxed but full — perfect for two">
<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1 — Old Tokyo & Green Spaces" status="done" subtitle="Asakusa → Ueno">
- Morning: Senso-ji Temple (arrive before 9am to avoid crowds), stroll Nakamise-dori for street snacks like *ningyo-yaki* and *senbei*.
- Lunch: *Tempura* at a riverside restaurant near Sumida Park.
- Afternoon: Walk along Sumida River, cross to Ueno Park. Visit Tokyo National Museum or just relax under the trees.
- Evening: *Yakitori* dinner in Ueno's *Ameyoko* market street, then early rest.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 2 — Youth Culture & Icons" status="done" subtitle="Shibuya → Harajuku">
- Morning: Shibuya Crossing (view from Starbucks second floor), Hachiko statue photo, shopping at Shibuya 109 or Loft.
- Lunch: Ramen in a back alley near Center Gai.
- Afternoon: Meiji Shrine (quiet forest in the city), then Takeshita Street in Harajuku for crepes and quirky fashion. Walk Omotesando for architecture and cafes.
- Evening: *Izakaya* dinner in Shibuya, then a craft cocktail bar.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 3 — Gardens & Neon Nights" status="done" subtitle="Shinjuku">
- Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden — pack a picnic, spend 2–3 hours among the lawns, ponds, and greenhouses.
- Lunch: Casual *soba* or *udon* near the garden's east exit.
- Afternoon: Explore Shinjuku's department stores (Isetan, Takashimaya), or visit the observation deck of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (free).
- Evening: Golden Gai's tiny bars (go early, around 6pm, to find seats), then Omoide Yokocho for grilled skewers and beer.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 4 — Market Morning & Glamour" status="done" subtitle="Tsukiji → Ginza">
- Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market — grab *tamagoyaki*, fresh *uni* sushi, and matcha. Arrive by 8am.
- Lunch: *Kaisendon* (seafood rice bowl) in the market.
- Afternoon: Walk to Ginza — flagship stores, art galleries, and the Imperial Palace East Gardens (free, closes 4pm).
- Evening: *Kaiseki* dinner in Ginza (book ahead), or a teppanyaki counter show.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 5 — Otaku & Skyline Farewell" status="done" subtitle="Akihabara → Roppongi">
- Morning: Akihabara — browse electronics at Yodobashi, explore anime stores, try a *maid café* for laughs.
- Lunch: *Curry* or *omurice* at a themed café.
- Afternoon: Head to Roppongi Hills — Mori Art Museum, then the 52nd-floor Sky Deck for panoramic views (sunset is magical).
- Evening: Farewell dinner at a *robatayaki* or *sushi* counter in Roppongi, then a nightcap with the city lights below.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Money-saving tips">
- Buy a **Suica or Pasmo** IC card for trains and buses — tap on/off, no tickets.
- Lunch sets are far cheaper than dinner menus — make lunch your big meal.
- Many shrines and gardens are free; paid ones are usually ¥300–600 per person.
</Callout>

<Card title="Packing & Practical Notes" subtitle="Small things that make the trip smoother">
<Columns>
<Card title="Essentials">
- Comfortable walking shoes (you'll do 20k+ steps daily)
- Power bank (you'll use your phone for maps)
- Small cash — many street stalls and tiny bars are cash-only
- A foldable shopping bag (plastic bags cost ¥3–5)
</Card>
<Card title="Good to know">
- Trains stop around midnight — check last train times
- Many restaurants have English menus; point-and-order works fine
- Tipping is not customary in Japan
- Free Wi-Fi is available at major stations and convenience stores
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
