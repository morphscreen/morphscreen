<Card title="Day 1 – Classic Tokyo: Shrines & Neon" subtitle="Neighborhoods: Harajuku → Shibuya">
<Steps>
<Step title="9:00 – Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="Tranquil forest in the city">
Walk the gravel path under towering cedars. Don't miss the sake barrel wall. Free entry; 20 min visit.
</Step>
<Step title="11:00 – Takeshita Street" status="done" subtitle="Harajuku's colorful chaos">
Crepes, vintage thrift, and crazy fashion. Try the rainbow cotton candy. Budget ¥1,000 for snacks.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch: Uobei Shibuya" status="done" subtitle="Conveyor belt sushi">
High-speed sushi at ¥120/plate. Order via touchscreen — the train zooms to your table. ~¥2,500 for two.
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – Shibuya Crossing" status="done" subtitle="The world's busiest intersection">
Watch the scramble from the Starbucks second floor, then cross it yourself. Go up to Shibuya Sky for sunset.
</Step>
<Step title="19:00 – Dinner: Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Retro alley of tiny yakitori bars">
Head back to Shinjuku. Squeeze into a 10-seat bar, order skewers & beer. ~¥4,000 for two.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 – Old & New: Temples and Towers" subtitle="Neighborhoods: Asakusa → Sumida">
<Steps>
<Step title="8:30 – Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's oldest temple">
Enter through the giant red Kaminarimon gate. Burn incense and pull a fortune (omikuji). Free.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Nakamise Street" status="done" subtitle="200m of street food & crafts">
Try ningyo-yaki (sweet bean cakes) and fresh senbei. Pick up a folding fan souvenir. ¥1,500.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Sumida River Walk" status="done" subtitle="20-min scenic stroll">
Walk north along the river toward the Skytree. Great photo ops of the tower and the river boats.
</Step>
<Step title="12:30 – Lunch: Kanda Yabusoba" status="done" subtitle="Legendary soba since 1880">
A 5-min detour from the river. Hand-made buckwheat noodles, hot or cold. ~¥3,000 for two.
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="350m observation deck">
Pre-book a sunset slot online (¥2,300/person). Watch the city light up below. Stay for golden hour.
</Step>
<Step title="19:00 – Dinner: Sumida Aquarium" status="done" subtitle="Night view with fish?">
Actually, grab a bowl of ramen at the Skytree Town food floor — try the matcha ramen. ~¥2,800 for two.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 – Foodie Day: Market to Ginza" subtitle="Neighborhoods: Tsukiji → Ginza">
<Steps>
<Step title="7:00 – Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="Eat like a local at dawn">
Arrive early for the freshest sushi breakfast. Try tamago (egg) skewers, uni, and grilled scallops. ¥4,000 for two.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Hamarikyu Gardens" status="done" subtitle="A feudal lord's strolling garden">
Tide pond, pine trees, and a tea house in the middle. Watch the falconry demonstration at 11:00. ¥300/person.
</Step>
<Step title="12:30 – Lunch: Ginza Kagari" status="done" subtitle="Michelin-starred chicken broth ramen">
A 20-min walk from the gardens. Rich, creamy tori paitan broth. Line moves fast. ¥2,000 for two.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 – Ginza Shopping District" status="done" subtitle="Flagship stores & art galleries">
Browse the 12-story Uniqlo, the Sony showroom, and the Wako clock tower. Free window shopping; budget ¥5,000 if you buy.
</Step>
<Step title="17:30 – Kabuki-za Theatre" status="done" subtitle="Traditional drama, 1-act ticket">
Get a single-act "makumi" ticket (¥1,500–2,000) for a glimpse of kabuki — even without understanding Japanese, the costumes and staging wow.
</Step>
<Step title="20:00 – Dinner: Izakaya in Ginza" status="done" subtitle="Late-night small plates">
Try "Ginza no Hikari" for seasonal small dishes and sake. ~¥6,000 for two.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 – Studio Ghibli & Laid-Back Suburbs" subtitle="Neighborhoods: Mitaka → Kichijoji">
<Steps>
<Step title="10:00 – Ghibli Museum" status="done" subtitle="Book tickets 1 month ahead!">
Reserve via Lawson tickets (¥1,000/person). Explore the whimsical exhibits, watch the exclusive short film. No photos inside — but the rooftop robot is fair game.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch: Inokashira Park" status="done" subtitle="Picnic by the pond">
Grab bento boxes from the Kichijoji station depachika (food hall). Row a swan boat (¥700/30min) between bites.
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – Kichijoji Sunroad" status="done" subtitle="Covered shopping arcade">
Vintage record stores, cheap clothing, and the famous "Satou" beef croquettes (¥250 each). Perfect for souvenir hunting.
</Step>
<Step title="17:30 – Harmonica Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Tiny retro bars & eateries">
A maze of 100+ micro-izakayas. Pick one with locals — point at what looks good. ~¥5,000 for two with drinks.
</Step>
<Step title="20:00 – Nightcap: Kichijoji Jazz" status="done" subtitle="Live music in a basement bar">
"Jazz Bar Meg" hosts local players nightly. Cover charge ¥1,000/person includes a drink.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 – Futuristic Tokyo: Art, Bay & Tower" subtitle="Neighborhoods: Toyosu → Odaiba → Minato">
<Steps>
<Step title="9:30 – teamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="Walk through water and light">
Pre-book online (¥3,200/person). You'll wade through knee-deep water and touch digital art. Allow 1.5–2 hours.
</Step>
<Step title="12:00 – Lunch: Toyosu Market" status="done" subtitle="The new wholesale market">
The inner market is for pros, but the outer "Senkyaku" area has 70+ restaurants. Try the tuna bowl (maguro don). ¥3,500 for two.
</Step>
<Step title="14:00 – Odaiba Seaside Park" status="done" subtitle="Beach with a skyline view">
Walk the boardwalk, snap a photo with the mini Statue of Liberty, and watch the Rainbow Bridge. Free.
</Step>
<Step title="16:30 – Tokyo Tower" status="done" subtitle="The iconic red tower">
Take the elevator to the 150m Main Deck (¥1,200/person). Stay for sunset — the city glows gold, then neon.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Farewell Dinner: Roppongi" status="done" subtitle="One last splurge">
Head to "Afuri" for yuzu-shio ramen (¥1,200/bowl), then a rooftop bar at the Mori Tower for skyline views. ~¥8,000 for two with cocktails.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Practical Tips for Your Trip">
<Columns>
<Card title="Getting Around">
- **Suica/PASMO card**: Load ¥5,000 at any station machine. Covers trains, buses, and convenience stores.
- **JR Yamanote Line**: The green loop connects Shinjuku, Shibuya, and Tokyo Station — your best friend.
- **Google Maps works great** for transit times and exits.
</Card>
<Card title="Money & Language">
- **Cash is king** at small shops and temples. Withdraw at 7-Eleven ATMs (English available).
- **Learn these phrases**: *Arigatou* (thank you), *Sumimasen* (excuse me), *Oishii* (delicious).
- **No tipping** — it can cause confusion.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
