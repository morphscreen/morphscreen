# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A balanced itinerary mixing iconic sights, local neighborhoods, food, and a day trip — all mapped out.

<Map title="Tokyo 5-Day Route" route="true">
| Stop | Address | Day / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 – Shibuya & Harajuku | Shibuya Crossing, 2 Chome-2-1 Dogenzaka, Shibuya City | Start: iconic scramble crossing |
| Meiji Shrine | 1-1 Yoyogikamizonocho, Shibuya City | Peaceful forest in the city |
| Takeshita Street | 1 Chome-1 Jingumae, Shibuya City | Quirky fashion & crepes |
| Day 2 – Asakusa & Ueno | Senso-ji Temple, 2 Chome-3-1 Asakusa, Taito City | Ancient temple & Nakamise shopping street |
| Ueno Park & Museums | 5-20 Uenokoen, Taito City | Cherry blossoms, zoo, museums |
| Day 3 – Tsukiji & Ginza | Tsukiji Outer Market, 4 Chome-16-2 Tsukiji, Chuo City | Fresh sushi breakfast |
| Ginza Shopping | 4 Chome-6-16 Ginza, Chuo City | High-end stores & galleries |

<Card title="Day-by-Day Breakdown">
<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1 – Shibuya & Harajuku" status="done" subtitle="Neon lights & youth culture">
- Morning: Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko statue
- Late morning: Meiji Shrine (quiet, free)
- Afternoon: Takeshita Street, crepes, vintage shops
- Evening: Dinner in Shibuya (izakaya or ramen)
</Step>
<Step title="Day 2 – Asakusa & Ueno" status="done" subtitle="Old Tokyo & museums">
- Morning: Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise-dori
- Lunch: Tempura or monjayaki nearby
- Afternoon: Ueno Park, Tokyo National Museum
- Evening: Ameyoko market street for street food
</Step>
<Step title="Day 3 – Tsukiji & Ginza" status="done" subtitle="Fresh fish & luxury">
- Early morning: Tsukiji Outer Market (sushi breakfast)
- Morning: Ginza flagship stores, art galleries
- Afternoon: Kabukiza Theatre (if interested)
- Evening: Rooftop bar in Ginza
</Step>
<Step title="Day 4 – Shinjuku & Mt. Takao" status="done" subtitle="Gardens & mountain air">
- Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen Garden (half day)
- Afternoon: Train to Mt. Takao (1 hr), cable car, hike
- Evening: Onsen at Takao, return to Shinjuku for night views
</Step>
<Step title="Day 5 – Akihabara & Odaiba" status="done" subtitle="Geek culture & future islands">
- Morning: Akihabara arcades, anime shops
- Lunch: Maid café or curry
- Afternoon: teamLab Planets (book ahead)
- Evening: Rainbow Bridge views from Odaiba
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Getting Around">
<Callout tone="info" title="Public transport is your friend">
Tokyo's train and subway system is fast, safe, and punctual. The **JR Yamanote Line** loops the main hubs (Shibuya, Shinjuku, Tokyo, Ueno, Akihabara). Use **Google Maps** or **Japan Travel by Navitime** for routes. Avoid taxis for long distances — they're pricey.
</Callout>
