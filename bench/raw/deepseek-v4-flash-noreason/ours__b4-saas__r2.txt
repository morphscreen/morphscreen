<Details title="View methodology">
<Callout tone="info" title="How metrics are calculated">
- MRR and ARR are based on active subscriptions as of the last day of the reporting period.
- Churn is calculated as MRR lost from cancellations and downgrades ÷ MRR at the start of the period.
- Seat utilization is seats in use ÷ seats purchased, averaged across the reporting period.
- NPS is collected from in-app surveys sent in the last 30 days.
</Callout>
</Details>
