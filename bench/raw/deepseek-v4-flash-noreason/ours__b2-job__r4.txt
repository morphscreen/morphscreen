# Senior Product Designer

**Design Systems Team** · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)  

<Badge tone="success">Open</Badge> <Badge tone="info">Remote</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Full-time</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design Systems team. You'll own the component library used by 40+ product teams, partner with engineers and brand designers, and set the standard for accessible, high-quality UI across our platform. This is a high-leverage role for someone who loves systems thinking, craft, and collaboration.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Design, document, and maintain reusable components in Figma and code
- Partner with engineering to ensure design-to-code parity
- Run design critiques and contribute to our accessibility standards
- Drive adoption of the system through office hours, docs, and onboarding
- Mentor mid-level designers on systems thinking and interaction design
</Card>

<Card title="Who you are">
- 5+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years on design systems
- A portfolio showing systematic thinking and polished UI craft
- Deep understanding of accessibility (WCAG 2.2) and responsive patterns
- Experience working in code (HTML/CSS, or React) — you don't need to ship production code, but you should be comfortable reading it
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Bonus: experience with tokens, theming, or multi-brand systems
</Card>
