# Senior Product Designer

**Design Systems Team** · Full-time · Remote (US & EU)

<Badge tone="success">● Hiring now</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Remote</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Full-time</Badge>

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to own and evolve our design system across web and mobile. You'll partner with engineering and brand to ship accessible, scalable UI that powers products used by millions.

<Card title="Role overview">
Work with a cross-functional team of 12 designers and engineers to maintain and grow our component library, documentation, and design tooling. You'll drive consistency, mentor junior designers, and champion accessibility across every screen.
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
- 5+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years focused on design systems
- Strong portfolio showing component-driven work and systematic thinking
- Deep proficiency in Figma, including variables, tokens, and auto-layout
- Working knowledge of HTML/CSS and handoff to engineering
- Experience with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA or better)
- Excellent communication and documentation skills

<Callout tone="info" title="Nice to have">Experience with React, Storybook, or token-transformer tooling is a plus.</Callout>
</Card>
