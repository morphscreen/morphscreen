**Design Systems · Platform Team** <Badge tone="info">Full-time</Badge> <Badge tone="success">Remote-friendly</Badge>

<Columns>
<div>

- 5+ years of product design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating systems thinking
- Proficiency in Figma, including variables and component libraries
- Experience with design tokens and theming
- Excellent cross-functional communication skills
</div>
<div>
**Nice-to-have**

- Experience with WCAG accessibility standards
- Front-end coding skills (HTML/CSS)
- Experience scaling design systems across multiple products
- Familiarity with motion design principles
