# Senior Product Designer

**Design Systems Team** <Badge tone="info">Full-time · Remote</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a product designer who loves building and maintaining design systems. You'll work closely with engineers, brand, and product managers to create scalable, accessible components that power our entire platform.

<Callout tone="neutral" title="Why join us">You'll own the design system end-to-end — from token architecture to component documentation — and see your work used by every product team.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
<Columns>
<div>

**Must-haves:**
- 4+ years of product design experience
- Strong portfolio showing design system work
- Proficiency in Figma (variables, components, auto-layout)
- Understanding of accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- Experience working with engineers on component handoff

</div>
<div>

**Nice-to-have:**
- Experience with token pipelines (Style Dictionary, Tokens Studio)
- Familiarity with React component libraries
- Motion design skills
- Experience mentoring junior designers

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>
