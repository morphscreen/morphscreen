# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A first-timer's route that keeps each day in one area, so you spend your time in places, not on trains. Base yourself near **Shinjuku** or **Ueno** — both sit on the Yamanote loop line.

<Callout tone="info" title="Before you go">
Get a **Suica/Pasmo** card at the airport (or add it to Apple Wallet) — it works on every train, bus and convenience store. Two people, five days: budget roughly **¥60,000–90,000 each** excluding flights and hotel.
</Callout>

Out of the city for a change of pace.

Temples and cedar forest, ~2h by Tobu line. Buy the Nikko All Area Pass.

- Kegon Falls
- Lake Chuzenji
</Card>

Coastal, easy, ~1h by JR. Better for a relaxed day.

- Great Buddha at Kotoku-in
- Hase-dera temple
- Enoshima sunset
