# Hard-to-kill houseplants

The trick isn't finding a plant that survives neglect — it's matching the plant to the light and watering habits you already have. Here are the ones that forgive the most.

<Card title="The forgiving five" subtitle="Ranked by how much abuse they shrug off">

<Columns>
<Card title="1. Snake plant" subtitle="Sansevieria / Dracaena trifasciata">
Water every 2–4 weeks, less in winter. Tolerates low light, dry air, and being forgotten for a month. The main killer is overwatering.
</Card>
<Card title="2. ZZ plant" subtitle="Zamioculcas zamiifolia">
Stores water in its rhizomes, so it forgives missed waterings. Happiest in medium indirect light but survives a dim corner. Slow grower.
</Card>
</Columns>

<Columns>
<Card title="3. Pothos" subtitle="Epipremnum aureum">
Trails or climbs, roots easily in water, and visibly droops when thirsty so it tells you what it needs. Grows in anything from low to bright indirect light.
</Card>
<Card title="4. Spider plant" subtitle="Chlorophytum comosum">
Nearly indestructible, and it makes babies you can pot up or give away. Brown tips usually mean tap water or dry air, not a dying plant.
</Card>
</Columns>

<Card title="5. Cast-iron plant" subtitle="Aspidistra elatior">
The name says it. Handles deep shade, temperature swings, and long gaps between waterings. Slow, so buy the size you want.
</Card>

</Card>

<Card title="Pick by your weak spot">

<Compare title="Which one fits how you actually live">
| Your habit | Snake plant | ZZ plant | Pothos | Spider plant | Cast-iron plant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forget to water for weeks | yes | yes | no | no | yes |
| Only a dark corner available | yes | yes | yes | no | yes |
| Bright, sunny window | yes | no | yes | yes | no |
| Small apartment, no floor space | yes | yes | yes | yes | no |

</Compare>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Toxic to pets">
Snake plant, ZZ plant, and pothos are all toxic to cats and dogs if chewed. Spider plant and cast-iron plant are the safe picks in a pet household.
</Callout>

</Card>

<Card title="The two rules that matter more than the plant">

<Steps>
<Step title="Water only when the soil is dry" status="current" subtitle="Stick a finger in 2–3 cm down">
Overwatering kills more houseplants than neglect does. If the soil is still damp, wait. When in doubt, wait another week.
</Step>
<Step title="Match the plant to the light you have" subtitle="Not the light you wish you had">
A low-light plant in a blazing south window will scorch. A sun-lover in a dark hallway will stretch and fade. Move the plant, don't fight the room.
</Step>
<Step title="Repot only when rootbound" subtitle="Every 1–2 years at most">
Roots circling the pot and water running straight through are the signals. A pot one size up, fresh soil, done.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Checklist title="Starting out" name="plant-start">
- [ ] Pick one plant from the list above that fits your light
- [ ] Buy a pot with a drainage hole
- [ ] Set a phone reminder every 14 days to check the soil
- [ ] Keep it away from radiators and cold drafts
- [ ] Wait a month before buying a second one
</Checklist>
