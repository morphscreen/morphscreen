# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A loop that starts and ends in central Tokyo, moving east to west so you're never doubling back. Two people, five days, one map.

<Callout tone="info" title="How to use this">
Each day is a compact area. Pick the days in the order you like — the map is drawn as a route, but nothing forces you to walk it in that order.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1 — Shinjuku, arrive and settle">
<Steps>
<Step title="Land and drop bags" status="todo" subtitle="Narita or Haneda to Shinjuku">
Narita Express or the Keisei Skyliner + Yamanote; from Haneda the Keikyu line to Shinjuku. Roughly 60–90 minutes either way.
</Step>
<Step title="Shinjuku Gyoen" status="todo" subtitle="Late afternoon">
A big, calm garden — the easiest first stop after a flight. ¥500 each.
</Step>
<Step title="Omoide Yokocho" status="todo" subtitle="Dinner">
Tiny yakitori counters under the tracks. Sit at two seats, order skewers and beer, keep it cheap and early.
</Step>
<Step title="Kabukicho lights" status="todo" subtitle="Optional, on foot">
A walk through the neon on the way back to the hotel. No need to book anything.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 2 — Meiji Jingu, Harajuku, Shibuya">
<Steps>
<Step title="Meiji Jingu before 9am" status="todo" subtitle="Shrine forest">
Free, quiet, and best before the crowds. Enter from the Harajuku side.
</Step>
<Step title="Takeshita Street and Omotesando" status="todo" subtitle="Midday">
Crepes and chaos on Takeshita, then the tree-lined designer stretch on Omotesando for a slower walk.
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Crossing and Miyashita Park" status="todo" subtitle="Afternoon">
Watch the scramble from a second-floor window, then coffee on the rooftop park.
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Sky at sunset" status="todo" subtitle="Book ahead">
Timed tickets sell out. Book the slot about 30 minutes before sunset.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Book-style plan for Day 2 with Shibuya Sky timing">Day 2 in detail</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 — Asakusa, Skytree, Ueno">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji at opening" status="todo" subtitle="Around 6–7am">
The temple and Nakamise street are near-empty early. By 10am it is shoulder to shoulder.
</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Skytree" status="todo" subtitle="Late morning">
Book a timed ticket online to skip the queue. Sumida Park along the river is a good walk between the two.
</Step>
<Step title="Ueno Park museums" status="todo" subtitle="Afternoon">
Pick one: Tokyo National Museum, or the science museum if you have kids along. Ameyoko market for snacks after.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Plan Day 3 around Asakusa and Ueno with food stops">Day 3 in detail</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 — Tsukiji, teamLab, Ginza">
<Steps>
<Step title="Tsukiji Outer Market" status="todo" subtitle="Arrive by 8am">
Tamagoyaki, uni, and grilled scallops from the stalls. Most close by early afternoon.
</Step>
<Step title="teamLab Planets" status="todo" subtitle="Book a timed slot">
In Toyosu. Barefoot, water rooms, about 90 minutes. Book weeks ahead.
</Step>
<Step title="Ginza" status="todo" subtitle="Evening">
Department store food halls (depachika) for dinner-to-go, or a proper sit-down. Itto-ya and Mitsukoshi are the big two.
</Step>
</Steps>
<Button ask="Day 4 plan with Tsukiji and teamLab timings">Day 4 in detail</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 — Nakameguro, Tokyo Station, depart">
<Steps>
<Step title="Nakameguro canal walk" status="todo" subtitle="Morning">
Cafes along the canal, no crowds on a weekday. Good last slow morning.
</Step>
<Step title="Tokyo Station and Marunouchi" status="todo" subtitle="Midday">
Character Street under the station for souvenirs, then the brick station facade for a photo.
</Step>
<Step title="Head to the airport" status="todo" subtitle="Allow 3 hours">
Narita Express from Tokyo Station, or the Skyliner from Ueno. Leave buffer for luggage.
</Step>
</Steps>
