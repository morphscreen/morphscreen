# Hard-to-kill houseplants

These are the plants that forgive missed waterings, low light, and general neglect. The trick is matching the plant to your actual conditions, not the conditions you wish you had.

<Callout tone="success" title="The golden rule">
Most houseplants die from overwatering, not neglect. When in doubt, wait a week.
</Callout>

<Card title="The tough six" subtitle="Ranked by how much abuse they shrug off">
| Plant | Light | Water | Why it survives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snake plant | Any, even a dark corner | Every 2–3 weeks | Stores water in its leaves; rot-resistant |
| ZZ plant | Low to bright indirect | Every 2–3 weeks | Waxy leaves hold moisture; rhizomes store it |
| Pothos | Low to bright | When top soil dries | Wiltes dramatically, then revives |
| Spider plant | Medium to bright | Weekly-ish | Sends out babies you can ignore for months |
| Cast iron plant | Low light | Every 1–2 weeks | Genuinely hard to kill, hence the name |
| Aloe vera | Bright, direct | Every 2–3 weeks | Loves being forgotten; hates wet feet |
</Card>

<Columns>
<Card title="Snake plant" subtitle="Sansevieria">
The most forgiving plant on this list. Tolerates a dark hallway, a north window, or a bright room. Water when the soil is bone dry — in winter that can mean once a month.
</Card>
<Card title="ZZ plant" subtitle="Zamioculcas zamiifolia">
Nearly indestructible. Grows slowly, so buy the size you want. Drooping stems usually mean too much water, not too little.
</Card>
</Columns>

<Details title="Signs you're overwatering (the real killer)" open="false">
- Yellowing lower leaves that feel soft
- Mushy or black stems at the base
- Soil still damp a week after watering
- Fungus gnats hovering around the pot
- A sour smell from the soil

Fix: stop watering, move to brighter light, and repot into dry, gritty mix if the roots are brown and mushy.
</Details>

<Details title="Signs you're underwatering" open="false">
- Crispy brown tips on leaf edges
- Leaves curling inward or folding
- Soil pulling away from the pot sides
- Whole plant drooping but stems still firm

Fix: water thoroughly until it runs out the drainage hole, then wait. Most of these plants recover within a day.
</Details>

<Checklist title="Getting started" name="plant-setup">
- [ ] Pick one plant that matches your light
- [ ] Buy a pot with a drainage hole
- [ ] Use a gritty, fast-draining mix
- [ ] Water once, then wait until the soil is dry
- [ ] Set a phone reminder for two weeks out
- [ ] Check the soil before every watering, not the calendar
</Checklist>
