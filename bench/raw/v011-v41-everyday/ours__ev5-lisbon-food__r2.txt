# What to eat in Lisbon, and where

Lisbon eating is mostly a walking sport: grilled fish, custard tarts still warm, and small tascas where the menu is chalked on the wall. Below are the dishes worth planning a day around, then specific places to eat them.

<Callout tone="info" title="Two rules that save you money and disappointment">
Eat lunch, not dinner — the *prato do dia* (dish of the day) is often €8–12 with wine, while the same kitchen charges double after 19:00. And if a restaurant has a person outside waving a menu at you in English, keep walking.
</Callout>

## Where to eat it

<Card title="Places, grouped by what they're best at" subtitle="Prices are per person, without drinks">
| Place | Best for | Neighbourhood | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cervejaria Ramiro | Garlic prawns, tiger prawns, the whole shellfish show | Intendente | €30–45 |
| Time Out Market | Trying many things at once, with kids, in the rain | Cais do Sodré | €12–20 |
| A Merendinha do Arco | Old-school tasca, bacalhau à Brás, no fuss | Baixa | €10–15 |
| O Velho Eurico | Modern cooking in an old room, hard to book | Alfama | €25–35 |
| Casa do Pastel de Belém | The original nata, straight from the oven | Belém | €2–4 |
| Manteigaria | Nata, best eaten standing at the counter | Chiado | €2–4 |
| Sol e Pesca | Tinned fish with bread and wine, tiny bar | Cais do Sodré | €15–20 |
| Os Gazeteiros | Vegetarian-leaning, changing daily menu | Graça | €18–25 |
</Card>

<Card title="Booking and timing">
<Steps>
<Step title="Book the two that need it" status="todo">O Velho Eurico and Cervejaria Ramiro take bookings weeks ahead; Ramiro only for early slots. Do this first.</Step>
<Step title="Know the closing days" status="todo">Many tascas close Sunday and Monday. Belém's pastry shop is open daily; Manteigaria too.</Step>
<Step title="Eat lunch late" status="todo">Lisbon lunch runs 13:00–15:00. Arrive at 12:30 and you'll walk in; at 13:15 you'll queue.</Step>
<Step title="Order the house wine" status="done">A jug of *vinho da casa* is €3–5 and usually better than the cheapest bottle.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Watch for">
Couverts (bread, olives, cheese brought unasked) are charged — €2–6 per person. You can send them back. Also: prices in Baixa and along Rua Augusta run about 40% above the same food in Alfama or Graça.
</Callout>
