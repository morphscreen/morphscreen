# Neapolitan Pizza at Home

The real thing comes down to three things: a **very wet, slow-fermented dough**, a **ripping-hot oven**, and **restraint with the toppings**. Everything else is detail.

<Callout tone="info" title="The honest constraint">
A home oven tops out around 250–290°C. True Neapolitan bakes at 430–480°C in 60–90 seconds. You can get 85% of the way there with a preheated steel or stone on the top rack plus the broiler — that's the plan below.
</Callout>

<Card title="What you're aiming for" subtitle="The target, so you know when you've hit it">
<KeyValue title="Neapolitan standard">
| Feature | Target |
|---|---|
| Crust | Puffy, leopard-spotted, blistered rim (*cornicione*) |
| Base | Soft, thin, no crunch — floppy enough to fold |
| Bake time | 60–90 s at 430–480°C (home: 4–6 min at max) |
| Dough hydration | 62–65% |
| Ferment | 24–48 h cold, then 2–4 h room temp |
| Toppings | Tomato, mozzarella, basil, olive oil. That's it. |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="The dough" subtitle="Makes 4 × 250 g balls — start 2 days ahead">
<Steps>
<Step title="Mix the dough" status="todo">
500 g "00" flour (or bread flour), 325 g cold water (65%), 10 g fine sea salt, 1–2 g fresh yeast (or 0.5 g instant dry). Dissolve yeast in water, add flour, rest 20 min, then add salt and mix/knead until smooth and elastic — 8–10 min in a stand mixer on low.
</Step>
<Step title="Bulk ferment" status="todo">
Cover and leave 1 h at room temperature, then move to the fridge for 24–48 h. Longer = more flavour and better digestibility. This is the step people skip, and it's the one that matters most.
</Step>
<Step title="Ball it" status="todo">
Divide into 4 × ~210 g pieces. Shape into tight balls with a smooth top, using a bench scraper and no extra flour on the top surface. Place in a covered tray or individual containers.
</Step>
<Step title="Final proof" status="todo">
2–4 h at room temperature until they've relaxed and roughly doubled. They should be soft, jiggly, and easy to stretch — not springing back.
</Step>
<Step title="Stretch, top, bake" status="todo">
Press from the centre outward on a floured bench, leaving a 2 cm rim untouched. Never use a rolling pin. Top and bake (below).
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="The bake" subtitle="Home-oven method">
<Checklist title="Pre-bake setup" name="pizza-prep">
- [x] Pizza steel or stone on the **top** rack
- [x] Oven at maximum for **45–60 min** (steel needs the full hour)
- [ ] Switch to **broiler/grill** 10 min before baking
- [ ] Semolina or flour on the peel; shake the topped pizza to check it slides
- [ ] Sauce and cheese **at room temperature**, not cold
</Checklist>

Then: slide onto the steel, broil 4–6 minutes, rotating once. The rim should puff and spot before the base dries out. If the top browns before the base sets, move it down a rack.
</Card>

<Card title="Toppings" subtitle="Less than you think">
<Columns>
<Card title="Do">
- Crushed San Marzano tomatoes, **uncooked**, salted
- Fresh mozzarella (fior di latte), torn, **drained 30 min** on paper towel
- Fresh basil — added **after** the bake
- A drizzle of good olive oil, before and after
</Card>
<Card title="Don't">
- Pre-cooked or heavily reduced sauce
- Grated hard cheese on a Margherita
- Piled toppings — it steams instead of bakes
- Wet mozzarella straight from the brine
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Troubleshooting">
<Compare title="What went wrong">
| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| Pale, flat rim | Dough under-proofed | Longer room-temp final proof |
| Rim blows out, wet centre | Oven not hot enough | Longer preheat, use a steel |
| Tough, chewy base | Over-kneaded, or too much flour on the bench | Knead less, use semolina to dust |
| Soggy middle | Wet mozzarella or too much sauce | Drain cheese, use ~80 g sauce per pizza |
| Bitter, burnt spots | Too close to the broiler element | Move down one rack |
</Compare>
</Card>

<Card title="Timeline" subtitle="Working back from dinner">
<Timeline title="48 hours out">
- **T-48 h:** Mix dough, 1 h room temp, into the fridge
- **T-4 h:** Ball the dough, cover
- **T-2 h:** Take dough out, let it relax at room temp
- **T-60 min:** Turn oven to max with the steel inside
- **T-30 min:** Drain mozzarella, crush and salt tomatoes
- **T-10 min:** Switch on the broiler, flour the peel
- **T-0:** Stretch, top, bake, 4–6 min each
</Timeline>
</Card>
