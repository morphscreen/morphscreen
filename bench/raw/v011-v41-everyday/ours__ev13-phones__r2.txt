# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9

Both are 2024 flagships at similar prices, but they're built for different priorities. Here's the honest breakdown.

<Callout tone="info" title="The 10-second answer">
Pick the **iPhone 16** if you're in (or want into) Apple's ecosystem, value long-term resale and update support, and want the most consistent camera. Pick the **Pixel 9** if you want the smarter assistant/AI features, a cleaner Android experience, and better value for money.
</Callout>

<Card title="Where each one wins">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">
- **Chip performance** — A18 is meaningfully faster than Tensor G4 in raw benchmarks
- **Video** — best-in-class stabilization and consistency
- **Ecosystem** — seamless with Mac, iPad, Watch, AirPods
- **Resale** — holds value far better over 2-3 years
- **Support** — Apple Stores everywhere, easy repairs
</Card>
<Card title="Pixel 9">
- **Display** — 120Hz vs 60Hz is a real, daily difference
- **AI features** — Gemini, Magic Editor, Call Screen, Best Take
- **Battery & charging** — bigger cell, faster top-up
- **Software** — clean Android, 7 years of updates
- **Value** — frequent discounts, often $100-200 below iPhone
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
