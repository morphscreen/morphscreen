# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9

Both are 2024 flagships with 6.1–6.3" screens, ~$799 starting price, and 7 years of OS updates promised. The real difference is the ecosystem you're already in and how much you value camera AI vs. polished hardware.

<Callout tone="info" title="The honest answer">
If you already own a Mac, iPad, or Apple Watch, buy the iPhone 16 — the ecosystem is worth more than any spec gap. If you don't, the Pixel 9 gives you a better screen, bigger battery, and smarter camera for the same price. Neither is a mistake.
</Callout>
