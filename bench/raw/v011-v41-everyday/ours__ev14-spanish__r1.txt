# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

The ten that carry you through most situations. Learn the sound, not just the spelling — Spanish is read almost exactly as written, with every vowel short and clear.

<Card title="The 10 phrases" subtitle="Say them out loud twice each">
| # | Spanish | Sounds like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hola | OH-lah | Hello |
| 2 | Buenos días | BWEH-nos DEE-as | Good morning |
| 3 | Por favor | por fah-VOR | Please |
| 4 | Gracias | GRAH-see-as | Thank you |
| 5 | De nada | deh NAH-dah | You're welcome |
| 6 | Perdón | per-DON | Sorry / excuse me |
| 7 | ¿Habla inglés? | AH-blah een-GLÉS | Do you speak English? |
| 8 | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah | How much does it cost? |
| 9 | La cuenta, por favor | lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR | The bill, please |
| 10 | ¿Dónde está el baño? | DON-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyo | Where is the bathroom? |
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Two sounds that matter">
The **ñ** in *baño* is a "ny" sound (BAH-nyo). The **h** is always silent — *hola* starts with a plain "o" sound.
</Callout>

<Card title="When you'll use each one">
<Timeline title="A day of travel">
- Arriving somewhere: **Hola** + **Buenos días** as you walk in
- Asking for anything: add **Por favor** to the end
- Getting help: **Perdón**, then **¿Habla inglés?**
- Shopping: point and ask **¿Cuánto cuesta?**
- Eating out: **La cuenta, por favor** when you're done
- Anywhere urgent: **¿Dónde está el baño?**
</Timeline>
</Card>

<Card title="Two bonus phrases worth the extra minute">
- **No entiendo** (no en-TYEN-doh) — I don't understand. Say it with a shrug and people slow down.
- **Mucho gusto** (MOO-choh GOOS-toh) — Nice to meet you. Good for hosts, shopkeepers, anyone you're introduced to.
</Card>
