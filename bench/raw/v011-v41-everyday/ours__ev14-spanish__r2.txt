# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

Here are the ten that will carry you through most situations — greetings, ordering, asking for help, and being polite.

<Card title="The 10 phrases" subtitle="Say them out loud a few times each">
| # | Spanish | English | Say it like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hola | Hello | OH-lah |
| 2 | Buenos días | Good morning | BWEH-nos DEE-as |
| 3 | Por favor | Please | por fah-VOR |
| 4 | Gracias | Thank you | GRAH-see-as |
| 5 | De nada | You're welcome | deh NAH-dah |
| 6 | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | How much does it cost? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah |
| 7 | La cuenta, por favor | The bill, please | lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR |
| 8 | ¿Dónde está el baño? | Where is the bathroom? | DON-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyo |
| 9 | No entiendo | I don't understand | noh en-TYEN-doh |
| 10 | ¿Habla inglés? | Do you speak English? | AH-blah een-GLES |
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Two quick tips">
- The **ñ** in *baño* sounds like the "ny" in "canyon."
- In *gracias*, the "c" before "i" sounds like "s" — GRAH-see-as, not GRAH-kee-as.
</Callout>
