<Callout tone="info" title="The one thing that matters most">
Fit beats spec sheets. Leave a thumbnail's width at the toe, and make sure the heel doesn't slip when you lace up. If a shoe is uncomfortable in the store, it won't get better at mile 10.
</Callout>

<Card title="How to pick between them">
<Steps>
<Step title="Count your weekly miles" status="done">
Under 15 miles a week: Ghost 16 is plenty. 15–30 miles: Ride 17. 30+ or you race: Novablast 4.
</Step>
<Step title="Check your gait" status="current">
If you overpronate heavily or have had shin/arch issues, none of these are stability shoes — look at the Brooks Adrenaline or ASICS GT-2000 instead.
</Step>
<Step title="Buy the size you actually run in" status="todo">
Feet swell. Many runners go a half size up from their street shoe.
</Step>
<Step title="Rotate after ~400 miles" status="todo">
Midsole foam dies before the outsole wears through. Track your mileage.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
