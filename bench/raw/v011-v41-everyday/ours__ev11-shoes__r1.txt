<Card title="Fit check before you buy">
<Steps>
<Step title="Measure late in the day" status="todo" subtitle="Feet swell up to half a size">
Trace your foot standing on paper, heel to longest toe.
</Step>
<Step title="Leave a thumb's width" status="todo">
Roughly 1 cm of space past the longest toe. Toes shouldn't touch the front.
</Step>
<Step title="Go up half a size for running" status="todo">
Running shoes run smaller than casual shoes. Most people need +0.5 from their street size.
</Step>
<Step title="Test on a treadmill or hard floor" status="todo">
If the shop allows, run 2–3 minutes. Heel should lock in, no slipping.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
