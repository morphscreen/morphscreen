# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

The North Shore mountains and the Sea-to-Sky corridor pack a lot of trail into a short drive. Here are the standouts, sorted by how much effort they ask of you.

<Card title="Quarry Rock" subtitle="Deep Cove, North Vancouver">
The classic easy one. A well-maintained climb through second-growth forest to a rocky outcrop over Indian Arm. Busy on weekends, so go before 9am or after 4pm.
<KeyValue title="Details">
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 3.8 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 100 m |
| Difficulty | Easy |
| Best season | Year-round (microspikes in winter) |
</KeyValue>
<Button ask="How do I get to the Quarry Rock trailhead by transit?">Getting there</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Tunnel Bluffs" subtitle="Sea-to-Sky Highway">
A newer trail with one of the best effort-to-view ratios around — a big ledge looking straight down Howe Sound. The first 1.5 km is a steep grunt through clearcut, then it eases into forest.
<KeyValue title="Details">
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 7.5 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 450 m |
| Difficulty | Moderate |
| Parking | Small lot; fills by 8am on sunny days |
</KeyValue>
<Button ask="What should I know about parking at Tunnel Bluffs?">Parking tips</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Dog Mountain" subtitle="Mount Seymour, North Vancouver">
Snowshoeing in winter, wildflowers in summer, and a view over the entire city from the top. The trail is rooty and rocky — wear shoes with grip.
<KeyValue title="Details">
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 5 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 200 m |
| Difficulty | Easy–moderate |
| Note | Parking fee at Mount Seymour in winter |
</KeyValue>
<Button ask="Is Dog Mountain doable with kids?">With kids?</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="St. Mark's Summit" subtitle="Cypress Provincial Park">
The best half-day hike on the North Shore. A rolling ridge walk with several viewpoints before the final pull to the summit, which looks over Howe Sound and the Sunshine Coast.
<KeyValue title="Details">
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 11 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 600 m |
| Difficulty | Moderate |
| Best season | July–October |
</KeyValue>
<Button ask="What's the trail like to St. Mark's Summit in early season?">Early season conditions</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Garibaldi Lake" subtitle="Garibaldi Provincial Park">
The big one, and worth the drive. A steady switchback climb through old-growth forest to a turquoise alpine lake ringed by glaciers. Start early — you want 6 hours of daylight.
<KeyValue title="Details">
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 18 km round trip |
| Elevation gain | 820 m |
| Difficulty | Hard |
| Note | Day-use pass may be required in peak season |
</KeyValue>
<Button ask="Do I need a day-use pass for Garibaldi Lake?">Permits & passes</Button>
</Card>
