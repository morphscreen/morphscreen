<Card title="Why these tracks" icon="lightbulb">
**Designed for sustained attention.** Each track is selected for:
- **Minimal lyrics** — vocals (if any) are wordless or in unfamiliar languages so language centers stay quiet
- **Steady tempo** — 60–80 BPM mirrors a calm heart rate, reducing physiological arousal
- **Predictable structure** — no sudden drops, builds, or jarring transitions that pull you out of flow
- **Neutral emotional valence** — not melancholic, not euphoric; just a consistent, supportive backdrop

The playlist leans ambient, modern classical, and lo-fi electronic — genres research links to improved working-memory performance during cognitively demanding tasks.
</Card>
