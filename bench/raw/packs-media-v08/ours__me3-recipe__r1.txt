*Yields 24 cookies • Prep 15 min • Bake 10 min*

<Card title="Ingredients" icon="list">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226 g) |
| Granulated sugar | ¾ cup (150 g) |
| Light brown sugar, packed | ¾ cup (165 g) |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| All-purpose flour | 2¼ cups (280 g) |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Semisweet chocolate chips | 2 cups (340 g) |
| Optional: chopped walnuts | 1 cup |
</Card>

<Card title="Directions" icon="book-open">
<Steps>
<Step title="Preheat and prep" status="done">
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
</Step>
<Step title="Cream butter and sugars" status="done">
In a large bowl, beat softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="Add eggs and vanilla" status="done">
Beat in eggs one at a time, then mix in vanilla extract until combined.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine dry ingredients" status="done">
In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Gradually add to wet mixture, mixing on low just until incorporated.
</Step>
<Step title="Fold in chocolate chips" status="current">
Stir in chocolate chips (and nuts, if using) with a spatula until evenly distributed.
</Step>
<Step title="Scoop dough" status="todo">
Drop rounded tablespoons of dough 2 inches apart on prepared sheets.
</Step>
<Step title="Bake" status="todo">
Bake 9–11 minutes, until edges are golden but centers still look soft. Cool on sheet 2 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.
</Step>
<Step title="Enjoy!" status="todo">
Store in an airtight container up to 5 days, or freeze dough balls for up to 3 months.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pro Tips">
• Use room-temperature butter for better creaming<br>• Chill dough 30 min for thicker cookies<br>• Underbake slightly for chewy centers<br>• Sprinkle flaky sea salt on top before baking
</Callout>
