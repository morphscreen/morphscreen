<Card title="Ingredients">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226g) |
| Granulated sugar | ¾ cup (150g) |
| Light brown sugar, packed | ¾ cup (165g) |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| All-purpose flour | 2¼ cups (280g) |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Chocolate chips | 2 cups (340g) |
</Card>

<Card title="Steps">
<Steps>
<Step title="Preheat & Prep" status="done">
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
</Step>
<Step title="Cream Butter & Sugars" status="done">
In a large bowl, beat softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="Add Eggs & Vanilla" status="done">
Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla extract.
</Step>
<Step title="Mix Dry Ingredients" status="done">
In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine" status="done">
Gradually add dry ingredients to wet mixture, mixing until just combined. Do not overmix.
</Step>
<Step title="Fold in Chocolate Chips" status="done">
Gently fold in chocolate chips with a spatula until evenly distributed.
</Step>
<Step title="Scoop & Bake" status="current">
Scoop 1.5 tbsp dough balls onto prepared sheets, spacing 2 inches apart. Bake 9–11 minutes until edges are golden but centers look slightly underbaked.
</Step>
<Step title="Cool & Enjoy" status="todo">
Cool on baking sheet 2 minutes, then transfer to wire rack. Store in airtight container up to 5 days.
</Step>
</Steps>
