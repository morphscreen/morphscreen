<Card title="Why these tracks" icon="lightbulb">
**Designed for cognitive flow**

- **No lyrics** — Language processing competes with verbal working memory; instrumental music avoids that interference.
- **Steady tempo (60–80 BPM)** — Mirrors a calm heart rate, helping regulate arousal without distraction.
- **Low dynamic range** — Minimal sudden volume changes or dramatic drops that pull attention.
- **Repetitive, evolving textures** — Ambient, post-classical, and minimal electronic pieces give the brain a predictable pattern it can "ignore" while staying engaged.
- **Curated by Spotify's editorial team** — Updated regularly with new releases that fit the profile, so the playlist stays fresh without breaking the mood.

<Callout tone="info" title="Tip">Play at low volume — just loud enough to mask background noise. If you notice the music, it's too loud.</Callout>
</Card>
