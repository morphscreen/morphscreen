<Card title="Why These Tracks" subtitle="The science behind the selection">
**Minimal lyrics** — Vocals compete for language-processing bandwidth. Instrumental tracks avoid this interference.

**Steady tempo (60–80 BPM)** — Mirrors resting heart rate, promoting calm alertness without arousal spikes.

**Consistent dynamics** — No sudden drops, builds, or surprises that pull attention away from the task.

**Neutral emotional valence** — Avoids strongly melancholic or euphoric passages that trigger mind-wandering.

**Repetitive, evolving textures** — Ambient, post-classical, and minimal electronic works provide just enough novelty to sustain engagement without demanding it.

<Callout tone="info" title="Tip">Use noise-cancelling headphones at moderate volume. The playlist works best as a "sonic wallpaper" — present enough to mask distraction, subtle enough to ignore.</Callout>
</Card>
