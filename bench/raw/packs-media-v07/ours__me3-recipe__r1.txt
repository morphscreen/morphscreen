# Chicken Parmesan Recipe

Classic Italian-American dish with crispy breaded chicken, marinara sauce, and melted mozzarella.

<Card title="Ingredients">
**For the chicken:**
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken breasts | 4 (6 oz each) |
| Salt and black pepper | To taste |
| All-purpose flour | 1 cup |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Panko breadcrumbs | 1 ½ cups |
| Grated Parmesan cheese | ½ cup |
| Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil (for frying) | ½ cup |

**For the topping:**
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Marinara sauce | 2 cups |
| Fresh mozzarella, sliced | 8 oz |
| Grated Parmesan cheese | ¼ cup |
| Fresh basil leaves | For garnish |
</Card>

<Card title="Instructions">
<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare the chicken" status="done">
Place chicken breasts between plastic wrap and pound to ½-inch thickness. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
</Step>
<Step title="Set up dredging station" status="done">
Place flour in shallow dish. Beat eggs in second dish. Mix panko, ½ cup Parmesan, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder in third dish.
</Step>
<Step title="Bread the chicken" status="done">
Dredge each cutlet in flour, dip in egg, then press into panko mixture. Place on wire rack and let rest 10 minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="Fry the cutlets" status="done">
Heat olive oil in large skillet over medium-high. Fry cutlets 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown. Transfer to paper towels.
</Step>
<Step title="Assemble and bake" status="current">
Preheat oven to 450°F. Spread ½ cup marinara in baking dish. Arrange cutlets, top each with remaining marinara, mozzarella slices, and ¼ cup Parmesan. Bake 10-12 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden.
</Step>
<Step title="Rest and serve" status="todo">
Let rest 5 minutes. Garnish with fresh basil. Serve over spaghetti or with crusty bread.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pro Tip">
For extra crispy chicken, use a wire rack on a baking sheet when frying so air circulates underneath.
</Callout>
