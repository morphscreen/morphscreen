<Card title="Why these tracks">
<Columns>
<Column>
### Designed for cognitive load
Tracks are selected for **low lyrical density**, **stable tempo (60–80 BPM)**, and **minimal dynamic shifts** — reducing the brain's prediction-error signals that pull attention away.
</Column>
<Column>
### Consistent sonic texture
Ambient drones, soft piano, and slow synth pads create a **steady auditory background** that masks office noise without becoming a distraction itself.
</Column>
<Column>
### No surprises, no hooks
No sudden drops, vocal choruses, or memorable melodies. The music **loops transparently** so you stay in flow instead of anticipating the next section.
</Column>
</Columns>

<Details title="Research backing">
- **Mozart Effect meta-analyses** (Pietschnig et al., 2010): small but reliable arousal/mood boost from pleasant instrumental music.
- **Cognitive load theory** (Sweller, 1988): extraneous load drops when background audio lacks semantic content.
- **Spotify's own "Focus" editorial guidelines**: prioritize instrumental, >2 min track length, <80 BPM, low spectral flux.
</Details>
</Card>
