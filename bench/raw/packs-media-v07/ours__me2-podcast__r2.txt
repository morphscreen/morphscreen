<p>In this episode, we dive deep into how artificial intelligence is transforming daily routines — from smart home assistants to personalized healthcare. Our guest, Dr. Maya Chen, shares insights on ethical AI development and what the next decade holds.</p>
<Details title="Full description">
<p>Artificial intelligence isn't just for tech giants anymore. It's in your phone, your car, your thermostat, and soon, your doctor's office. Dr. Maya Chen, lead researcher at the Institute for Human-Centered AI, joins us to discuss:</p>
<ul>
<li>How generative AI is reshaping creative work</li>
<li>The privacy trade-offs of always-on assistants</li>
<li>Regulatory frameworks emerging worldwide</li>
<li>Practical steps to stay informed and in control</li>
</ul>
<p>Plus: our weekly tech news roundup and a listener question about AI hallucinations.</p>
