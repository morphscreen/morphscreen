# Classic Chicken Parmesan

Crispy breaded chicken cutlets topped with marinara and melted mozzarella. Served over spaghetti.

<Embed src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZTgZzCjRqJQ" title="How to Make Classic Chicken Parmesan" />

<Card title="Ingredients" subtitle="Serves 4">
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken breasts (boneless, skinless) | 4 (about 6 oz each) |
| All-purpose flour | 1 cup |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Panko breadcrumbs | 1½ cups |
| Grated Parmesan cheese | ¾ cup + 2 tbsp for topping |
| Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil (for frying) | ½ cup |
| Marinara sauce | 2 cups |
| Fresh mozzarella (sliced) | 8 oz |
| Fresh basil leaves | for garnish |
| Spaghetti | 1 lb |
</Card>

<Card title="Directions">
<Steps>
<Step title="Prep the chicken" status="done">
Place each chicken breast between plastic wrap and pound to ½-inch thickness. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
</Step>
<Step title="Set up breading station" status="done">
Place flour in one shallow dish. Beat eggs in a second dish. Mix panko, ¾ cup Parmesan, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder in a third dish.
</Step>
<Step title="Bread the chicken" status="done">
Dredge each cutlet in flour (shake off excess), dip in egg, then press into panko mixture to coat evenly.
</Step>
<Step title="Pan-fry" status="done">
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Fry cutlets 3–4 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a paper-towel-lined plate.
</Step>
<Step title="Assemble and bake" status="current">
Preheat oven to 450°F. Spread ½ cup marinara in a baking dish. Arrange chicken, top each with remaining marinara, mozzarella slices, and 2 tbsp Parmesan. Bake 10–12 minutes until cheese is bubbly and browned.
</Step>
<Step title="Cook pasta" status="todo">
While chicken bakes, cook spaghetti in salted boiling water per package directions. Drain and toss with a little marinara.
</Step>
<Step title="Serve" status="todo">
Plate pasta, top with chicken, spoon extra sauce over, and garnish with fresh basil.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pro tip">
For extra-crispy chicken, use a wire rack on a baking sheet instead of a baking dish — air circulates underneath.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="success" title="Make ahead">
Bread the cutlets up to 4 hours ahead; keep refrigerated on a rack. Fry just before baking.
</Callout>
