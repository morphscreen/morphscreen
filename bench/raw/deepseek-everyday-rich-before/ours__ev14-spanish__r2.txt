# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick tip">
Spanish pronunciation is very phonetic — if you can read it, you can usually say it. The "ll" sounds like a "y", and the "j" sounds like an "h".
</Callout>

<Card title="The 10 essential phrases">
<Columns>
<div>

**1. Hola** — Hello
*Pronounced: OH-lah*

**2. Por favor** — Please
*Pronounced: por fah-VOR*

**3. Gracias** — Thank you
*Pronounced: GRAH-see-ahs*

**4. Sí / No** — Yes / No
*Pronounced: see / noh*

**5. ¿Cuánto cuesta?** — How much does it cost?
*Pronounced: KWAHN-toh KWES-tah*

</div>
<div>

**6. ¿Dónde está...?** — Where is...?
*Pronounced: DON-deh es-TAH*

**7. No entiendo** — I don't understand
*Pronounced: noh en-tee-EN-doh*

**8. ¿Habla inglés?** — Do you speak English?
*Pronounced: AH-blah een-GLES*

**9. La cuenta, por favor** — The bill, please
*Pronounced: lah KWEHN-tah por fah-VOR*

**10. ¡Salud!** — Cheers! / Bless you!
*Pronounced: sah-LOOD*

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="When to use them">
<Agenda title="A day out with these phrases">
| Time | What to say |
|---|---|
| Morning | **Hola** to greet your host or shopkeeper |
| Café | **Por favor** when ordering coffee |
| Market | **¿Cuánto cuesta?** for souvenirs |
| Street | **¿Dónde está...?** + "el baño" (the bathroom) |
| Restaurant | **La cuenta, por favor** at the end of the meal |
| Evening | **¡Salud!** when toasting with new friends |
</Agenda>
