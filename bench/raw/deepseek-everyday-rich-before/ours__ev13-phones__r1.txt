# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which should you buy?

Both phones are excellent, but they suit different people. Here's a clear breakdown to help you decide.

<Card title="Quick verdict">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">Choose iPhone 16 if…</Badge>

- You're already in the Apple ecosystem (AirPods, Mac, iPad)
- You want the smoothest, most consistent software experience
- You value long-term software updates (6+ years)
- You prefer a simpler, more reliable camera experience
- You care about the best video recording on a phone

</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Choose Pixel 9 if…</Badge>

- You want the best AI features built right into the phone
- You love Google's clean Android experience
- You take lots of photos and want the most natural-looking shots
- You want the best value for your money
- You like customising your phone more freely

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Design and feel">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">iPhone 16</Badge>

- **Aluminium frame** with a glass back, available in 5 colours (black, white, pink, teal, ultramarine)
- **Lighter and slimmer** than the Pixel — easier to hold one-handed
- **Action button** replaces the mute switch — customisable shortcuts
- **Camera Control** button on the side for quick camera access
- Feels premium and solid, but less distinctive

</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Pixel 9</Badge>

- **Polished aluminium frame** with a matte glass back, available in 4 colours (obsidian, porcelain, wintergreen, peony)
- **Larger and heavier**, but the camera bar makes it stand out
- **No extra buttons** — clean, minimalist design
- The camera bar makes it sit flat on a table without wobble
- More personality, but takes up more pocket space

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Camera comparison">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">iPhone 16</Badge>

- **48MP main camera** with 2x optical-quality zoom
- **12MP ultra-wide** — decent but not class-leading
- **Photographic Styles** let you adjust the look of photos in real-time
- **Excellent video** — best-in-class stabilisation and HDR
- **Spatial video** recording for Apple Vision Pro
- Colours are punchy and vibrant, sometimes a bit warm

</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Pixel 9</Badge>

- **50MP main camera** with 2x optical-quality zoom
- **48MP ultra-wide** — significantly sharper than the iPhone's
- **Magic Editor** — remove objects, reframe, and fix photos with AI
- **Best Take** — swap faces from a burst to get everyone looking
- **Video Boost** (coming later) for AI-enhanced video
- Colours are natural and true-to-life, excellent in low light

</div>
</Columns>

<Card title="Performance and battery">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">iPhone 16</Badge>

- **A18 chip** — blazing fast, handles everything with ease
- **8GB RAM** — plenty for iOS
- **iOS 18** — smooth, fluid, and consistent
- **Battery**: ~22 hours video playback; easily lasts a full day
- **Charging**: 25W wired (50% in 30 min), 15W MagSafe wireless
- **Apple Intelligence** arrives in 2025 — AI features like notification summaries and image generation

</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Pixel 9</Badge>

- **Tensor G4** — fast for everyday tasks, but not as powerful for heavy gaming
- **12GB RAM** — great for multitasking and AI
- **Android 14 (upgradable to 15)** — clean, customisable, with Gemini built in
- **Battery**: ~24 hours mixed use; slightly better than the iPhone
- **Charging**: 27W wired (50% in 30 min), 15W wireless
- **Gemini AI** — already live: call screening, photo editing, and voice assistance

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Software and updates">
<Columns>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">iPhone 16</Badge>

- **iOS 18** — polished, secure, and easy to use
- **6+ years of updates** — Apple supports phones for a very long time
- **Deep ecosystem integration** — iMessage, AirDrop, Handoff, and more
- **App Store** — the best curated apps, but more restricted

</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="success">Pixel 9</Badge>

- **Android 14** — clean, fast, and customisable
- **7 years of updates** — the longest promise in the industry
- **Google ecosystem** — Gmail, Photos, Drive, and Assistant work beautifully
- **Play Store** — more open, but with occasional quality issues

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Which one should you pick?">
<Steps>
<Step title="You use an iPhone now" status="done" subtitle="Stick with Apple">
If you already have an iPhone, switching is a hassle. The iPhone 16 is a great upgrade with a better camera button, faster chip, and the upcoming Apple Intelligence.
</Step>
<Step title="You use an Android phone now" status="done" subtitle="The Pixel is the natural upgrade">
If you're on Android, the Pixel 9 is the best Android experience you can get. It's faster, smarter, and more polished than most other Android phones.
</Step>
<Step title="You're switching from one to the other" status="todo" subtitle="Think about your priorities">
Ask yourself: do I want the best video and ecosystem (iPhone) or the best AI and photos (Pixel)? Both are excellent, so you can't go wrong.
</Step>
<Step title="You care about value" status="todo" subtitle="Pixel wins on price-to-features">
The Pixel 9 gives you a 120Hz screen, more RAM, a better ultra-wide camera, and Gemini AI — for the same $799 starting price.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Final recommendation">
<Callout tone="info" title="My take">
Get the **iPhone 16** if you want the most reliable, polished phone with the best video and a long update promise. Get the **Pixel 9** if you want the best camera, the smartest AI, and better value — and you don't mind a slightly heavier phone.
</Callout>
