<Card title="How to Choose">
<Steps>
<Step title="Know your mileage" status="done">
If you run 20+ miles per week, prioritize durability and cushioning (Cumulus or 880). Under 20 miles, the Ride or Pegasus will last plenty long.
</Step>
<Step title="Consider your weight" status="done">
Runners over 180 lbs will appreciate the extra cushioning of the 880 or Ghost. Lighter runners can go with the firmer Ride.
</Step>
<Step title="Match your stride" status="done">
All five are neutral shoes. If you overpronate (feet roll inward), look for stability versions like the ASICS GT-1000 or Brooks Adrenaline—but those are usually $130+.
</Step>
<Step title="Try them on" status="done">
Buy from a store with free returns (Running Warehouse, Zappos, or your local shop). Run in them on a treadmill or sidewalk for a few minutes before deciding.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
