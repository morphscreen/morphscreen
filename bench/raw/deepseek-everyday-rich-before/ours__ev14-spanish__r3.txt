# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Card title="Essential Phrases" subtitle="Memorize these before you go">
<Columns>
<Card title="Greetings & Politeness">
- **Hola** — Hello
- **Buenos días** — Good morning
- **Buenas tardes** — Good afternoon
- **Gracias** — Thank you
- **Por favor** — Please
</Card>
<Card title="Getting Around">
- **¿Dónde está…?** — Where is…?
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** — How much does it cost?
- **No entiendo** — I don't understand
- **¿Habla inglés?** — Do you speak English?
- **La cuenta, por favor** — The bill, please
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Pronunciation Guide">
| Phrase | How to say it | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Hola | OH-lah | Any time, casual greeting |
| Buenos días | BWEH-nos DEE-as | Until noon |
| Buenas tardes | BWEH-nas TAR-des | Noon to evening |
| Gracias | GRAH-see-as | Say it often — politeness goes far |
| Por favor | por fah-VOR | Add to any request |
| ¿Dónde está…? | DON-deh es-TAH | Point at your map or phone |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAN-to KWES-ta | At markets, shops, taxis |
| No entiendo | no en-tee-EN-do | When you're lost in conversation |
| ¿Habla inglés? | AH-blah een-GLES | Ask politely before assuming |
| La cuenta, por favor | la KWEN-ta por fah-VOR | At restaurants, when you're ready to leave |
</Card>

<Card title="Tips for Using These Phrases">
<Steps>
<Step title="Start with Hola" status="done">
Always greet first — it sets a friendly tone.
</Step>
<Step title="Add Por favor and Gracias" status="done">
These two words will make every interaction warmer.
</Step>
<Step title="Don't be afraid to ask" status="done">
¿Habla inglés? is a lifesaver. Most people in tourist areas speak some English.
</Step>
<Step title="Point and ask" status="done">
Combine ¿Dónde está…? with pointing at a map or your phone for instant clarity.
</Step>
<Step title="Practice the bill phrase" status="done">
In Spain, they won't bring the bill until you ask. In Latin America, it's more common to ask too.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
