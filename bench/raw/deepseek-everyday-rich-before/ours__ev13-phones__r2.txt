# iPhone 16 vs. Pixel 9: Which should you buy?

<Callout tone="info" title="The short answer">
Choose the **iPhone 16** if you're deep in Apple's ecosystem, want the smoothest video recording, and value long-term software support. Choose the **Pixel 9** if you want the best AI features, a brighter display, and prefer a more flexible camera system.
</Callout>

<Card title="Quick comparison">
<Compare title="iPhone 16 vs. Pixel 9">
| Feature | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 6.1" OLED, 60Hz | 6.3" OLED, 120Hz |
| Chip | A18 | Tensor G4 |
| Main camera | 48MP + 12MP ultrawide | 50MP + 48MP ultrawide |
| Video | 4K 60fps, spatial video | 4K 60fps, 8K on main |
| Battery life | ~22h video playback | ~24h mixed use |
| Charging | 20W wired, 25W MagSafe | 45W wired, 15W wireless |
| Software support | 5+ years | 7 years |
| AI features | Apple Intelligence (2025) | Gemini, Call Screen, Magic Editor |
| Price (128GB) | $799 | $799 |
</Compare>
</Card>

<Card title="Which one fits you?">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Choose iPhone 16 if...">
- You own an **Apple Watch**, AirPods, or Mac
- You **record lots of video** (especially for social media)
- You want **FaceTime** and iMessage with family
- You prefer **iOS's simplicity** and app quality
- You don't mind a **60Hz display** (it's still smooth)
- You want **MagSafe** accessories (wallets, chargers)
</Tab>
<Tab title="Choose Pixel 9 if...">
- You want **120Hz smooth scrolling** for the same price
- You value **AI features** like Call Screen and Magic Editor
- You take lots of **photos** and want flexible editing
- You want **7 years of updates** (longer than Apple's)
- You prefer **stock Android** with no bloatware
- You want **faster wired charging** (45W vs. 20W)
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Final verdict">
<Callout tone="success" title="Our pick: Pixel 9 for most people">
The Pixel 9 offers a **better display, faster charging, and more useful AI features** at the same price. It's the smarter value in 2025.
</Callout>
<Callout tone="info" title="Choose iPhone 16 if...">
You're **invested in Apple's ecosystem**, shoot video professionally, or simply prefer iOS. The A18 chip will stay fast for years, and video quality is still unmatched.
