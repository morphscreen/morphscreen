**Know your arch type**

- **Normal arch (neutral):** Any of the three work; pick the Cohesion for daily training or the Revolution for speed.  
- **Flat arch (overpronation):** The Cohesion offers mild stability; the Gel-Excite is forgiving but not corrective.  
- **High arch (underpronation):** Go with the Gel-Excite for extra cushioning under the heel and forefoot.

</div>
<div>

**Check the fit**

- Buy a half size larger than your dress shoes — your feet swell on runs.  
- Leave a thumb's width between your longest toe and the shoe's end.  
- Try them on in the evening, when feet are at their largest.

</div>
<div>

- **Daily miles (30+ per week):** Cohesion 17 — durable and consistent.
