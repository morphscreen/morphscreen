# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Card title="Why These Phrases Matter" subtitle="A little Spanish goes a long way">
These ten phrases cover the essentials: greetings, politeness, and getting by in everyday situations. Even if your pronunciation isn't perfect, locals will appreciate the effort, and you'll navigate restaurants, shops, and directions with much more confidence.
</Card>

<Card title="The 10 Phrases" subtitle="Learn these before you go">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="All Phrases">
| # | English | Spanish | When to Use It |
|---|---------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Hello / Hi | Hola | Any time, any place |
| 2 | Please | Por favor | Asking for anything |
| 3 | Thank you | Gracias | Showing appreciation |
| 4 | Yes / No | Sí / No | Simple answers |
| 5 | Excuse me / Sorry | Perdón | Getting attention, apologizing |
| 6 | Do you speak English? | ¿Hablas inglés? | Finding common ground |
| 7 | I don't understand | No entiendo | When you're lost in conversation |
| 8 | How much is it? | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | Shopping, markets, taxis |
| 9 | Where is the bathroom? | ¿Dónde está el baño? | Urgent need! |
| 10 | Goodbye | Adiós | Leaving politely |
</Tab>
<Tab title="Pronunciation Tips">
| Spanish | Say It Like | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Hola | OH-lah | The "h" is silent |
| Por favor | por fah-VOR | Roll the "r" lightly |
| Gracias | GRAH-see-as | "c" sounds like "s" |
| Sí | see | Accent makes it "ee" |
| No | noh | Same as English |
| Perdón | per-DON | Stress the second syllable |
| ¿Hablas inglés? | AH-blahs een-GLES | Rising tone at the end |
| No entiendo | noh en-tee-EN-doh | "en" like in "end" |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah | Both "c's" are hard |
| ¿Dónde está el baño? | DON-deh es-TAH el BAN-yo | "ñ" sounds like "ny" |
| Adiós | ah-dee-OS | Stress the last syllable |
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="How to Learn Them Fast" subtitle="Tips that actually work">
<Steps>
<Step title="Learn in pairs" status="done">Group phrases by situation: greetings together, questions together. Your brain remembers context better than lists.</Step>
<Step title="Use them daily" status="done">Practice with a friend, or even talk to yourself. Say "Hola" to your mirror, ask your cat "¿Cuánto cuesta?"</Step>
<Step title="Write them on your phone" status="current">Keep this list in your notes app. Glance at it during the flight, at the hotel, before you order coffee.</Step>
<Step title="Don't fear mistakes" status="todo">Locals won't judge you. A smile plus "Por favor" works wonders, even with imperfect grammar.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Common Situations">
<Columns>
<div>
<Callout tone="info" title="At a Restaurant">
When the waiter approaches, try: "Hola, ¿hablas inglés?" If not, point at the menu and say "Por favor" — then "Gracias" when they help.
</Callout>
</div>
<div>
<Callout tone="info" title="At a Market">
To ask prices, say "¿Cuánto cuesta?" and hold up the item. If you want to bargain, a friendly "Perdón" first gets attention without being rude.
</Callout>
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>
