# Best Road Running Shoes Under $120

Here are the top picks for road running shoes that won't break the bank, based on current pricing and expert reviews.

<Card title="Top Pick: Saucony Ride 17">
<Columns>
<div>
The **Saucony Ride 17** is the best all-rounder under $120. It offers a balanced, cushioned ride that works for daily training, tempo runs, and even longer distances. The PWRRUN midsole is responsive yet plush, and the updated upper is breathable and comfortable.

- **Price:** ~$110 (often on sale for less)
- **Drop:** 8mm
- **Weight:** ~9.2 oz (men's)
- **Best for:** Daily training, easy miles, beginners

<Card title="Runner-up: Brooks Ghost 15">
The Ghost is a classic daily trainer known for its soft, smooth ride. The 15th version is slightly lighter and more breathable than its predecessor, and it's often discounted below $120.

- **Price:** ~$100–$120 (depending on colorway/sale)
- **Drop:** 12mm
- **Weight:** ~9.1 oz (men's)
- **Best for:** Neutral runners, heel strikers, road comfort

<Callout tone="info" title="Fit note">The Ghost runs true to size with a roomy toe box—great if you prefer a little extra space.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Budget Pick: Nike Revolution 7">
If you want a reliable shoe for less, the Revolution 7 delivers a soft, springy ride at a very friendly price. It's not built for speedwork, but it's perfect for easy runs and everyday wear.

- **Price:** ~$65–$75
- **Drop:** 9.5mm
- **Weight:** ~8.8 oz (men's)
- **Best for:** Beginners, short runs, walking/running hybrids

<Badge tone="success">Best budget</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Speed Option: New Balance FuelCell Propel v4">
For runners who want a bit of pop, the Propel v4 features a FuelCell foam that feels energetic and responsive. It's a great do-it-all shoe that can handle faster efforts without sacrificing comfort.

- **Price:** ~$100–$110
- **Drop:** 6mm
- **Weight:** ~9.3 oz (men's)
- **Best for:** Tempo runs, faster daily miles, runners who like a firmer feel

<Callout tone="warning" title="Not for wide feet">The Propel runs narrow—consider sizing up half a size if you have wider feet.</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="How to choose">
<Steps>
<Step title="Know your gait" status="current">If you overpronate (arches collapse inward), look for stability features. All four shoes above are neutral—if you need support, consider the **Brooks Adrenaline GTS 23**, often around $110.</Step>
<Step title="Consider your mileage" status="todo">Daily runners logging 20+ miles/week should prioritize durability (Ride or Ghost). Occasional runners can save with the Revolution.</Step>
<Step title="Try them on" status="todo">Feet swell during runs—buy a half size up if between sizes. Wear your running socks when trying on.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
