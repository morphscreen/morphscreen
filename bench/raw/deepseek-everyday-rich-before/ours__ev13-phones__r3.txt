# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9: Which should you buy?

<Callout tone="info" title="Short answer">If you're already in the Apple ecosystem, get the iPhone 16. If you want the best Android camera and AI features for less, get the Pixel 9.</Callout>

<Card title="Quick comparison">
<Compare title="iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9">
| Feature | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 6.1" OLED, 60Hz | 6.3" OLED, 120Hz |
| Chip | A18 | Tensor G4 |
| RAM | 8GB | 12GB |
| Main camera | 48MP | 50MP |
| Ultrawide | 12MP | 48MP |
| Telephoto | No | No |
| Battery | ~3,561 mAh | ~4,700 mAh |
| Charging | 25W wired, 15W MagSafe | 27W wired, 15W wireless |
| AI features | Apple Intelligence | Gemini AI |
| Software updates | 5+ years | 7 years |

</Card>

<Card title="Design and display">
<Columns>
<div>
**iPhone 16** — 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR display at 60Hz. Aluminum frame, Ceramic Shield front, IP68. Available in pink, teal, ultramarine, and black. Lighter at 170g. The 60Hz refresh rate is a noticeable omission at this price.
</div>
<div>
**Pixel 9** — 6.3-inch Actua display with 120Hz refresh rate. Aluminum frame, Gorilla Glass Victus 2, IP68. Available in obsidian, porcelain, wintergreen, and peony. Slightly heavier at 198g. The smoother 120Hz panel is a clear win.
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Performance and battery">
<Columns>
<div>
**iPhone 16** — The A18 chip is significantly faster than the Tensor G4 in both CPU and GPU benchmarks. Great for gaming and heavy apps. Battery life is good but not class-leading; 25W wired charging (sold separately) and 15W MagSafe.
</div>
<div>
**Pixel 9** — The Tensor G4 focuses on AI tasks rather than raw speed. It's fine for everyday use but lags behind in gaming. The larger battery gives better endurance, and 27W charging gets you to 50% in about 30 minutes.
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Camera comparison">
<Columns>
<div>
**iPhone 16** — 48MP main sensor with 2x optical-quality zoom. New Camera Control button. Excellent video recording (4K60 Dolby Vision). Photographic Styles let you customize look. Great point-and-shoot results, especially in video.
</div>
<div>
**Pixel 9** — 50MP main + 48MP ultrawide. Super Res Zoom for digital zoom. Magic Editor, Best Take, and other AI photo tools. Excellent still photography, especially in low light. Video is good but not as polished as iPhone.
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Software and AI">
<Columns>
<div>
**iPhone 16** — iOS 18 with Apple Intelligence rolling out. Siri improvements, Writing Tools, Genmoji, and Clean Up in Photos. Tight integration with Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Privacy-focused on-device processing.
</div>
<div>
**Pixel 9** — Android 14 with Gemini AI built in. Gemini Live for natural conversations, Call Notes, Screenshots search, and Pixel Studio. Best-in-class AI features on Android. Seven years of OS updates and security patches.
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Price and value">
<Columns>
<div>
**iPhone 16** — $799 for 128GB. $899 for 256GB. No charger included. Excellent resale value. AppleCare+ at $199 for 2 years.
</div>
<div>
**Pixel 9** — $799 for 128GB. $899 for 256GB. Charger not included. Frequent discounts and trade-in deals. Often found for $100 less than list price within months.
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Which one fits you?">
<Columns>
<div>
**Choose the iPhone 16 if:**
- You use an iPhone, Mac, or Apple Watch
- You want better performance and gaming
- Video quality is your priority
- You value long-term resale value
- You prefer iOS ecosystem lock-in
</div>
<div>
**Choose the Pixel 9 if:**
- You want the best still photography
- A 120Hz display matters to you
- You care about AI features today
- You want 7 years of updates
- You prefer Android's flexibility
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Bottom line">
<Callout tone="success" title="Our recommendation">
**For most people: iPhone 16.** It's the more polished, faster, and more reliable phone. The Pixel 9 is a better value if you prioritize camera and AI, but the iPhone's ecosystem and longevity make it the safer choice.
</Callout>
</Card>
