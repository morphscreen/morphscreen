### About the role

Lumina is looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design Systems Team. You'll design the foundational components, patterns, and guidelines that power our product suite. Working closely with engineering and product, you'll ensure every pixel we ship is intentional, accessible, and consistent. If you obsess over the details — from focus states to spacing scales — this role is for you.

<Callout tone="info" title="Why join Lumina?">Competitive salary, generous equity, health coverage, and a remote-first culture with quarterly team meetups.</Callout>

<Stat label="Salary range" value="$120,000 – $150,000" note="USD base, plus equity and benefits" />
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications" subtitle="What we're looking for">
- 5+ years of experience in product design
- A portfolio that shows strong visual and interaction design skills
- Deep experience with Figma, tokens, and component libraries
- Working knowledge of WCAG accessibility standards
- Ability to partner with engineers to ship high-quality UI
- Clear communication and documentation habits
</Card>
