### The role

We’re looking for a product designer to craft intuitive, accessible design-system components used by every product team at Acme. You’ll partner with engineers, content designers, and brand to turn messy problems into scalable patterns.

### What we’re looking for

- 5+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years on design systems
- A portfolio showing component thinking, documentation, and accessible UI
- Strong Figma and prototyping skills; comfort with tokens and variables
- Experience collaborating with engineers in code reviews — HTML/CSS is a plus
- Clear written communication and a habit of leaving things better than you found them

<Details title="Bonus qualifications">
- Motion design or micro-interaction experience
- Experience with Storybook or similar documentation tools
- Familiarity with WCAG 2.2 AA and inclusive design practices
</Details>
