# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

---

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our core design team and help shape the future of our collaboration platform. You'll own complex features from concept to launch, partnering closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers. This is a high-impact role where your work will directly affect how thousands of teams communicate and build together every day.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead end-to-end design for major product areas
- Conduct user research and translate insights into intuitive flows
- Build and maintain design system components
- Mentor junior designers and advocate for design quality
- Collaborate cross-functionally to define product strategy
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- Strong portfolio showing shipped products with measurable impact
- Expertise in Figma, prototyping, and design systems
- Excellent communication and storytelling skills
- Experience designing for complex B2B workflows a plus
</Card>
