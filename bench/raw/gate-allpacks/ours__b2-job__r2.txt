# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for new features, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for craft across the team. This is a high-impact role where your work directly influences how thousands of teams communicate and build together every day.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for complex, cross-functional product initiatives from discovery through launch
- Collaborate with PMs, engineers, and researchers to define problems and validate solutions
- Contribute to and evolve our design system, ensuring consistency and accessibility
- Mentor other designers and participate in design reviews and critiques
- Advocate for user needs while balancing business goals and technical constraints
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience at a software company (SaaS or consumer tech)
- Strong portfolio demonstrating shipped products with measurable impact
- Expertise in Figma, prototyping, and designing for responsive web applications
- Excellent communication skills — you can articulate design rationale to any audience
- Experience with design systems and component-based workflows
- Bonus: familiarity with developer handoff tools (Storybook, Zeroheight) and front-end basics (HTML/CSS/React)
</Card>
