# Senior Product Designer

<Card title="Senior Product Designer" subtitle="Design Systems · Platform Team · Remote (US/EU) · Full-time">
We're looking for a **Senior Product Designer** to own the end-to-end experience of our design system and the products built on it. You'll partner closely with engineering and product to turn complex workflows into interfaces that feel effortless — and you'll set the bar for craft across the team.

<Badge tone="success">Actively hiring</Badge> <Badge tone="info">Remote-friendly</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Posted 3 days ago</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="About the role">
You'll lead design for our component library and the core surfaces that use it, from first sketch to shipped release. Expect deep collaboration: pairing with engineers, running critique, and shaping how the whole org approaches interface quality.

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Own the design system end-to-end: tokens, components, patterns, and documentation
- Ship high-fidelity designs and prototypes for core product surfaces
- Run research and usability testing to validate direction
- Partner with engineers through implementation and QA
- Mentor mid-level designers and raise the craft bar
- Define accessibility standards and hold the team to them
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
<Columns>
<Card title="Must have">
- 5+ years product design experience
- Strong systems thinking and component-level craft
- Fluency in Figma and design tokens
- Portfolio showing shipped, complex work
- Comfortable in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment
</Card>
<Card title="Nice to have">
- Experience building or maintaining a design system
- Background in B2B / SaaS tooling
- Prototyping in code (React, HTML/CSS)
- Accessibility (WCAG) expertise
- Experience mentoring designers
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
