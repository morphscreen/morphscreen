<Details title="How thresholds are calculated">
Each item has its own threshold. The default is **10 units**, but high-turnover items like paper cups use a higher value (50) because they sell faster than they can be restocked.

An alert fires when `remaining ≤ threshold`. The item stays listed until a reorder is received and stock is updated.
</Details>
