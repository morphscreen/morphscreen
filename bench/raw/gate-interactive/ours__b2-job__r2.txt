# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design team and help shape the future of our core platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to define and deliver intuitive, accessible experiences for millions of users. This is a high-impact role where you'll own the design process from discovery through delivery, mentor junior designers, and contribute to our design system.
</Card>

<Card title="Key qualifications">
- 6+ years of product design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS or complex web applications  
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design thinking: research, ideation, prototyping, usability testing, and shipping  
- Expertise in Figma (or equivalent) and modern design systems  
- Comfortable collaborating with engineers in an agile environment  
- Excellent communication skills — you can articulate design rationale to stakeholders at all levels  
- Experience mentoring designers and advocating for user-centered practices
</Card>
