# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Remote (US/Canada) · Full-time**

<Card title="About this role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for new features, refine existing workflows, and help build a cohesive design system. This is a high-impact role where you'll partner closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to turn complex problems into intuitive, delightful experiences.
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS  
- Strong portfolio showing end-to-end feature work and design systems contributions  
- Fluency in Figma (prototyping, auto-layout, component libraries)  
- Comfortable conducting lightweight user research and usability testing  
- Excellent written and visual communication skills  
- Experience mentoring junior designers and collaborating cross-functionally
</Card>
