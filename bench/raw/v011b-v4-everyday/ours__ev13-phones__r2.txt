<Card title="Which one should you buy?">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="You're in the Apple ecosystem">
<Callout tone="success" title="Get the iPhone 16">
If you already own an iPhone, AirPods, Apple Watch, or a Mac, switching to Pixel means losing iMessage, seamless handoff, AirDrop, and your app purchases. The iPhone 16 is the obvious choice.
</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="You want the best AI & camera software">
<Callout tone="success" title="Get the Pixel 9">
Google's AI features are ahead of Apple's. Magic Editor, Best Take, Circle to Search, and Gemini make the Pixel feel smarter day-to-day. The camera software is more creative and forgiving.
</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="You value display smoothness">
<Callout tone="success" title="Get the Pixel 9">
The 120Hz screen makes scrolling, animations, and gaming noticeably smoother. The iPhone 16 is stuck at 60Hz until you go Pro.
</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="You want the best video camera">
<Callout tone="success" title="Get the iPhone 16">
For video recording – stabilization, audio, color grading, cinematic mode – nothing beats the iPhone at this price. If you shoot a lot of video, this is your phone.
</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="You keep phones for 4+ years">
<Callout tone="success" title="Either works, but…">
Both get long updates. iPhone has a stronger resale market. Pixel has 7 years of updates vs. Apple's 6+. Pick based on which OS you prefer for the long haul.
</Callout>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
