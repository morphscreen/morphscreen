<Card title="🧳 Packing checklist">
<Checklist title="Packing essentials" name="packing">
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (you'll do 10k–15k steps/day)
- [ ] Reusable water bottle (free water at convenience stores)
- [ ] Portable Wi-Fi or eSIM (pick up at airport)
- [ ] Small hand towel (many restrooms don't have paper towels)
- [ ] Coin purse (lots of ¥100–¥500 coins)
- [ ] Light jacket (October is 15–20°C)
- [ ] Umbrella (October can be rainy)
</Checklist>
