<Tab title="Camera & Photos">

**iPhone 16 wins for:** natural, true-to-life colors; excellent video recording (4K Dolby Vision); smooth stabilization.

**Pixel 9 wins for:** computational photography (Best Take, Magic Eraser); better low-light shots; more detailed ultra-wide.

<Tab title="Software & Ecosystem">

**iPhone 16:** iOS 18 with tight Apple ecosystem (iMessage, AirDrop, Apple Watch, Mac). Longest software support (6+ years). App Store quality control.

**Pixel 9:** Pure Android 15 with first access to new features. Google ecosystem (Google Photos, Workspace, Assistant). 7 years of updates.

<Button ask="Which has better software features?">Deep dive on software</Button>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Performance & Gaming">

**iPhone 16:** A18 Bionic is faster in benchmarks and real-world gaming. Better GPU for demanding titles. More consistent frame rates.

**Pixel 9:** Tensor G4 focuses on AI/ML tasks, not raw speed. Good for everyday use but lags in heavy gaming.

<Button ask="Show gaming benchmarks">See benchmark scores</Button>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Battery & Charging">

**Pixel 9:** Larger battery (4,700 mAh vs 3,561 mAh). Faster wired charging (27W vs 20W). Usually lasts 1–2 hours longer.

**iPhone 16:** More efficient processor helps. MagSafe ecosystem (magnetic accessories, faster wireless charging). Better battery optimization over time.
