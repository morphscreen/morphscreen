# The Solar System: Our Cosmic Neighborhood

<Card title="The Solar System at a Glance">
Our solar system is a vast collection of celestial bodies orbiting the Sun — a medium-sized star at its center. It includes **8 planets**, 5 dwarf planets (like Pluto), hundreds of moons, and countless asteroids and comets. The planets divide into two groups:

<Card title="The Inner Planets — Small & Rocky">
These are closest to the Sun, made mostly of rock and metal, with solid surfaces.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Mercury">
<KeyValue title="Mercury">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 57.9 million km |
| Diameter | 4,879 km |
| Day length | 59 Earth days |
| Year length | 88 Earth days |
| Moons | 0 |
| Temperature range | -180°C to 430°C |
</KeyValue>
- The smallest planet and closest to the Sun.
- No atmosphere to hold heat — scorching days and freezing nights.
- Covered in craters, like our Moon.

<Tab title="Venus">
<KeyValue title="Venus">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 108.2 million km |
| Diameter | 12,104 km |
| Day length | 243 Earth days |
| Year length | 225 Earth days |
| Moons | 0 |
| Surface temperature | ~465°C |
</KeyValue>
- The hottest planet, thanks to a thick CO₂ atmosphere that traps heat.
- Rotates **backwards** — the Sun rises in the west.
- Often called Earth's "sister planet" due to similar size.

<Tab title="Earth">
<KeyValue title="Earth">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 149.6 million km |
| Diameter | 12,756 km |
| Day length | 24 hours |
| Year length | 365.25 days |
| Moons | 1 (the Moon) |
| Surface temperature | -89°C to 57°C |
</KeyValue>
- The only known planet with life.
- 71% covered in liquid water — the "Blue Planet".
- A magnetic field protects us from solar wind.

<Tab title="Mars">
<KeyValue title="Mars">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 227.9 million km |
| Diameter | 6,792 km |
| Day length | 24.6 hours |
| Year length | 687 Earth days |
| Moons | 2 (Phobos, Deimos) |
| Surface temperature | -87°C to -5°C |

- The "Red Planet" — iron oxide (rust) gives it its color.
- Has the tallest mountain in the solar system: **Olympus Mons** (21.9 km high).
- Home to the largest canyon: **Valles Marineris**.

<Card title="The Outer Planets — Large & Gaseous">
Beyond the asteroid belt, these are huge, have rings, and many moons. They're mostly hydrogen and helium.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Jupiter">
<KeyValue title="Jupiter">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 778.5 million km |
| Diameter | 142,984 km |
| Day length | 9.9 hours |
| Year length | 11.86 Earth years |
| Moons | 95 known |
| Surface temperature | -110°C (cloud top) |
</KeyValue>
- The largest planet — over 1,300 Earths could fit inside.
- Famous for the **Great Red Spot**, a storm larger than Earth that's raged for centuries.
- Has a faint ring system.
<Button ask="Tell me more about Jupiter">More on Jupiter</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Saturn">
<KeyValue title="Saturn">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 1.43 billion km |
| Diameter | 120,536 km |
| Day length | 10.7 hours |
| Year length | 29.46 Earth years |
| Moons | 146 known |
| Surface temperature | -140°C (cloud top) |
</KeyValue>
- Known for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock.
- The least dense planet — it would float in water.
- Its moon **Titan** has a thick atmosphere and liquid methane lakes.
<Button ask="Tell me more about Saturn">More on Saturn</Button>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Uranus">
<KeyValue title="Uranus">
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from Sun | 2.87 billion km |
| Diameter | 51,118 km |
| Day length | 17.2 hours |
| Year length | 84.01 Earth years |
| Moons | 27 known |
| Surface temperature | -195°C |
</KeyValue>
- Rotates on its **side** — tilted 98° — likely from a massive ancient collision.
- An "ice giant" with a blue-green color from methane in its atmosphere.
- Has faint rings and 27 known moons.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Neptune">
<KeyValue title="Neptune">

| Year length | 164.8 Earth years |
| Moons | 16 known |
| Surface temperature | -200°C |
</KeyValue>
- The farthest and windiest planet — winds reach 2,100 km/h.
- Also an ice giant, deep blue in color.
- Its largest moon **Triton** orbits **backwards**.
