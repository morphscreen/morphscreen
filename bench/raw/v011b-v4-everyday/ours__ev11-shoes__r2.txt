<Card title="What to Look For">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check the fit" status="current" subtitle="Width and length">
Your toe should have a thumb's width of space from the end. Shoes should feel snug in the heel but not tight.
</Step>
<Step title="Match your gait" status="todo" subtitle="Neutral vs. stability">
All shoes above are for **neutral runners** (most common). If your ankles roll inward, you may need a stability shoe.
</Step>
<Step title="Consider your mileage" status="todo" subtitle="How often you run">
For 3+ runs a week, invest in the Brooks Launch 10 or New Balance Arishi v4. For occasional runs, the ASICS or Saucony work great.
</Step>
</Steps>
