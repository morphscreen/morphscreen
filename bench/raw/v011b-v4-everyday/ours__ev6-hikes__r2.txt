<Card title="Lynn Canyon Loop" subtitle="North Vancouver | 30 min drive">
<KeyValue title="Trail facts">
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 4.5 km loop |
| Elevation gain | 150 m |
| Time | 1.5–2 hours |
| Difficulty | Easy |
| Best for | Families, beginners, rainy days |
</KeyValue>

A suspended footbridge over a deep canyon, temperate rainforest, and a swimming hole at the bottom. The suspension bridge is free (unlike Capilano). Park at the Lynn Canyon Ecology Centre.

<Card title="Pacific Spirit Regional Park" subtitle="Vancouver | 20 min drive">
<KeyValue title="Trail facts">
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 5–12 km (choose your loop) |
| Elevation gain | Minimal |
| Time | 1–3 hours |
| Difficulty | Easy |
| Best for | Forest walks, trail running, dogs |
</KeyValue>

A huge coastal rainforest on the Point Grey peninsula with 73 km of trails. The Salish Sea Trail leads to a quiet beach with views of the Strait of Georgia. No mountain views, but pure tranquility.

<Tab title="Moderate & Scenic">

<Card title="Stawamus Chief (First Peak)" subtitle="Squamish | 1 hour drive">
<KeyValue title="Trail facts">
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 3 km one way |
| Elevation gain | 500 m |
| Time | 3–4 hours return |
| Difficulty | Moderate–Strenuous |
| Best for | Iconic views, chain sections, rock climbers |
</KeyValue>

The most photographed hike in BC. First Peak gives you the classic view of the Shannon Falls valley and Howe Sound without committing to the full traverse. The last section uses chains bolted to the granite — thrilling but safe.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Prepare well">Bring grippy shoes and at least 1 L of water per person. The chains can be slippery when wet.</Callout>

<Card title="Joffre Lakes (Lower & Middle)" subtitle="Pemberton | 2 hours drive">
<KeyValue title="Trail facts">
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 5 km to Middle Lake |
| Elevation gain | 250 m |
| Time | 2–3 hours return |
| Difficulty | Moderate |
| Best for | Turquoise lakes, glacier views, photos |
</KeyValue>

Three glacier-fed lakes in a row, each a more vivid shade of blue-green than the last. You can stop at Lower Lake (10 min) or Middle Lake (1–1.5 hr) for a shorter day. The full hike to Upper Lake is another 2 hours and much steeper.

<Badge tone="warning">Popular — arrive before 8 AM or after 3 PM to avoid crowds</Badge>

<Tab title="Challenging & Alpine">

<Card title="Garibaldi Lake" subtitle="Squamish | 1.5 hours drive">
<KeyValue title="Trail facts">
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 9 km one way |
| Elevation gain | 810 m |
| Time | 5–6 hours return |
| Difficulty | Strenuous |
| Best for | Alpine lake, Barrier views, wildflowers |
</KeyValue>

A steady climb through old-growth forest opens to one of the most stunning alpine lakes in the world — a deep turquoise bowl ringed by snow-capped peaks. Pack a picnic for the shore. The trail is well-graded but relentless.

<Callout tone="info" title="Summer 2026">The Garibaldi Lake day-use area is open. Parking at Rubble Creek lot fills by 7 AM on weekends. Shuttle available from Squamish.</Callout>

<Card title="Panorama Ridge" subtitle="Squamish | 1.5 hours drive">
<KeyValue title="Trail facts">
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance | 15 km one way |
| Elevation gain | 1,500 m |
| Time | 10–12 hours return |
| Difficulty | Very Strenuous |
| Best for | 360° alpine views, Garibaldi from above, experienced hikers |
</KeyValue>

The ultimate day hike near Vancouver. From the summit you see Garibaldi Lake, Black Tusk, Mount Garibaldi, and the Coast Range stretching to the horizon. Start before 5 AM. Snow lingers into July — check conditions.

<Card title="Essential hiking tips">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check trail conditions" status="current" subtitle="Before you go">
BC Parks website and the AllTrails app for recent trip reports. Many trails have snow well into June.
</Step>
<Step title="Pack the 10 essentials" status="todo" subtitle="Safety basics">
Navigation (map + phone), headlamp, sun protection, first aid, knife, fire starter, extra layers, extra food, extra water, emergency shelter.
</Step>
<Step title="Arrive early" status="todo" subtitle="Parking fills fast">
Popular trailheads (Joffre, Chief, Garibaldi) fill by 7–8 AM on weekends. Have a backup hike in mind.
</Step>
<Step title="Leave no trace" status="todo" subtitle="Pack it in, pack it out">
All these trails are in protected areas. Carry out all garbage, including food scraps and toilet paper.
</Step>
</Steps>
