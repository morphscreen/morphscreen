# How Photosynthesis Works

Photosynthesis is how plants, algae, and some bacteria convert sunlight into chemical energy — essentially turning light into food.

<Card title="The Big Picture">
<Chart type="area" x="Time of day" y="Rate of photosynthesis">
| Time | Light intensity | CO₂ uptake |
|---|---|---|
| Dawn | 10 | 5 |
| Mid-morning | 60 | 35 |
| Noon | 100 | 50 |
| Afternoon | 80 | 40 |
| Dusk | 20 | 10 |
| Night | 0 | -5 |
</Chart>

At its core, photosynthesis takes **carbon dioxide + water + sunlight** and produces **glucose + oxygen**.
</Card>

<Card title="The Chemical Equation">
<Math tex="6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{light energy} \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2" label="Photosynthesis equation" />

**In words:** 6 molecules of carbon dioxide + 6 molecules of water + light energy → 1 molecule of glucose + 6 molecules of oxygen
</Card>

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Where it happens">
<Card title="Inside the leaf">
Photosynthesis takes place inside **chloroplasts** — tiny organelles in plant cells.

<KeyValue title="Key parts">
| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Chloroplast | The factory where photosynthesis happens |
| Chlorophyll | Green pigment that absorbs sunlight |
| Thylakoid | Stacked discs where the light-dependent reactions occur |
| Stroma | Fluid around thylakoids where the Calvin cycle happens |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="info" title="Why leaves are green">
Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light well but **reflects green light** — that's why leaves look green to us.
</Callout>
</Card>
</Tab>

<Tab title="The two stages">
<Steps>
<Step title="Light-dependent reactions" status="current" subtitle="Thylakoid membranes">
1. Sunlight hits chlorophyll, exciting electrons
2. Water molecules split (photolysis), releasing oxygen
3. Energy is stored in **ATP** and **NADPH**
4. These energy carriers move to the next stage
</Step>

<Step title="Calvin cycle (light-independent)" status="todo" subtitle="Stroma">
1. CO₂ enters the leaf through stomata
2. ATP and NADPH from stage 1 power the conversion of CO₂ into glucose
3. The cycle regenerates to keep going
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Card title="Key terms to remember">
<Flashcards title="Photosynthesis flashcards">
| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Chlorophyll | Green pigment that absorbs light energy |
| Stomata | Tiny pores on leaves that let CO₂ in and O₂ out |
| ATP | Energy-carrying molecule used to power the Calvin cycle |
| Calvin cycle | Stage that uses ATP and NADPH to turn CO₂ into glucose |
| Photolysis | Splitting of water molecules using light energy |
</Flashcards>
