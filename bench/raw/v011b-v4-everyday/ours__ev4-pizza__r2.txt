# How to Make a Proper Neapolitan Pizza at Home

Authentic Neapolitan pizza (pizza napoletana) is defined by its soft, chewy, slightly charred crust, simple San Marzano tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella, and basil. The key challenges at home are achieving the high heat (800-900°F / 430-480°C) and the right dough hydration. Here's how to get as close as possible.

<Card title="Step-by-Step Process">
<Steps>
<Step title="Make the Dough" status="current" subtitle="Day before or morning of">
Mix 500g "00" flour, 325g water (60-65°F), 10g fine sea salt, 2g active dry yeast. Knead 10-15 min until smooth and elastic. Cover and rest 8-24 hours at room temp (68-72°F). Divide into 4 balls (about 210g each), rest another 2-4 hours.
</Step>
<Step title="Prepare the Sauce" status="todo" subtitle="Simple is best">
Crush a can of San Marzano tomatoes (DOP) by hand. Add a pinch of salt and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. **No cooking** – the oven does the work.
</Step>
<Step title="Shape the Pizza" status="todo" subtitle="By hand only">
Flour your hands and the dough ball. Press from the center outward, leaving a 1-inch rim. Stretch by draping over your knuckles, rotating. Aim for a 10-12 inch round, slightly thicker at the edge. **Never use a rolling pin** – it crushes the air bubbles.
</Step>
<Step title="Assemble & Bake" status="todo" subtitle="Work fast">
Spread 2-3 tbsp sauce in a spiral, leaving the rim bare. Add torn fresh mozzarella (fior di latte or buffalo). Bake at maximum oven temperature (see oven methods below) for 60-90 seconds. Finish with fresh basil and a drizzle of EVOO.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Oven Methods: What Works at Home">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Oven + Steel (Best)">
<Callout tone="success" title="Recommended">Preheat a 1/2-inch thick baking steel on the top rack at 550°F (290°C) for 45-60 min. Switch to broil for 5 min before launching. Bake 3-4 min, then broil 1-2 min more for leopard spotting.</Callout>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Oven + Stone (Good)">
Same as steel but preheat 60 min. Stone retains less heat, so the bake is slightly slower. Still works well with broiler finish.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Outdoor Pizza Oven (Best)">
<Badge tone="success">Authentic</Badge> A portable propane or wood-fired oven (Ooni, Roccbox, Gozney) reaches 800-950°F. Bake 60-90 seconds, rotating halfway. This is the only way to get true Neapolitan char at home.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Cast Iron Skillet (Good)">
Preheat a 12-inch cast iron skillet on the stovetop on high for 5 min. Stretch the dough, lay it in (it will sizzle), add sauce and cheese. Cook 2-3 min until bottom is golden, then transfer under the broiler for 2-3 min to finish the top.
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Common Mistakes & Fixes">
<Details title="Dough is too sticky" open=false>
Add flour 1 tbsp at a time while kneading, but the dough should be tacky – that's the high hydration. Use wet hands or a bench scraper. Next time, reduce water by 15-20g.
</Details>
<Details title="Crust is too dense / not airy" open=false>
The dough likely wasn't proofed long enough. Ensure 8-24 hours at room temp (not fridge). Also, handle the dough gently when shaping – don't deflate the bubbles.
</Details>
<Details title="Bottom is burnt, top is undercooked" open=false>
The stone/steel is too hot relative to the air. Lower the stone one rack position, or reduce preheat time. For steel, try baking at 500°F instead of 550°F.
</Details>
<Details title="No leopard spotting (char marks)" open=false>
You need higher heat or a broiler finish. The broiler mimics the intense top heat of a wood-fired oven. Switch it on for the last 60-90 seconds of baking.
</Details>
</Card>
