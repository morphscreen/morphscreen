<Card title="Essential Tips">
- Get a **Suica** or **Pasmo** IC card at the airport (¥500 deposit, refundable)
- Download **Google Maps** and **Japan Travel** app
- Many restaurants accept cash only — carry ¥20,000–30,000
- Book teamLab and Shibuya Sky tickets online in advance
- For kaiseki dinner, book 1–2 weeks ahead via hotel concierge
</Card>
