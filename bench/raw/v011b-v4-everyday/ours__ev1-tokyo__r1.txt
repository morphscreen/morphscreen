<Card title="Essential Tips">
<Details title="Getting around">
- **Suica/Pasmo card**: Prepaid IC card for trains, buses, and convenience stores. Buy at airport or any station.
- **JR Pass**: Only worth it if you take the Shinkansen. For 5 days in Tokyo, don't buy one.
- **Taxi**: ¥730 first 2 km. Use for late nights or heavy luggage.
- **Walking**: Tokyo is very walkable. Comfortable shoes are essential.
</Details>
<Details title="Food & dining">
- **Tipping**: Not customary in Japan. Service is included.
- **Reservations**: Book popular restaurants 1–2 weeks ahead. Use TableCheck or OpenTable.
- **Convenience stores**: 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson—great for breakfast, snacks, and ATMs.
- **Dietary needs**: Vegetarian/vegan options are limited. Look for shojin ryori (Buddhist temple cuisine).
</Details>
<Details title="Culture & etiquette">
- **Shoes off**: In temples, traditional restaurants, and some shops.
- **Onsen**: No tattoos allowed in many public baths. Look for "tattoo-friendly" onsens.
- **Quiet on trains**: No phone calls. Speak softly.
- **Bowing**: A slight bow is polite. No handshake unless offered.
</Details>
</Card>
