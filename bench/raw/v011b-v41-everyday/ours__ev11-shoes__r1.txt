<Card title="How to choose in 30 seconds">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check your arch and wear pattern" status="done">
Look at an old pair: worn on the outer heel and inner forefoot = neutral. Worn heavily on the inner edge = consider stability.
</Step>
<Step title="Match shoe to your main run type" status="current">
Mostly easy/slow miles → Cumulus 26 or Clifton 9. Mixed easy + steady → Ride 17. One shoe for everything → Ghost 16.
</Step>
<Step title="Buy the size you actually need" status="todo">
Running shoes run ~half a size small. Leave a thumb's width in front of your longest toe; feet swell on longer runs.
</Step>
<Step title="Rotate if you can afford it" status="todo">
Two cheaper shoes (e.g. Ghost 16 + a sale Clifton) outlast one expensive pair and reduce injury risk.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
