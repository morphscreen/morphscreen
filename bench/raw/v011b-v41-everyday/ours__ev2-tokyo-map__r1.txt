# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A walkable, transit-light loop that starts in the old city, ends in the neon, and keeps two people fed, rested and mostly off the rush-hour trains.

<Callout tone="info" title="How this plan is built">
Two bases, not five. Stay in **Asakusa** nights 1–2 (old Tokyo, cheap, quiet) and **Shinjuku** nights 3–5 (transport hub, late food, easy airport exit). Days are grouped by area so you walk between stops instead of riding the Yamanote line all day.
</Callout>

<Card title="At a glance">
<KeyValue title="Trip facts">
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Length | 5 days, 4 nights |
| Party | 2 adults |
| Best months | Late March–May, October–November |
| Getting around | Suica/PASMO card + two day-passes |
| Rough budget | ¥38,000–55,000 per person per day incl. hotel |
| Airport | Narita or Haneda, both direct to Asakusa/Shinjuku |
</KeyValue>
</Card>
