# 5 Days in Tokyo, for Two

<Callout tone="info" title="How this is built">
Five days grouped by area so you're not crossing the city twice a day. Mornings are the busy sights, afternoons are neighborhoods, evenings are food and views. Everything below sits on the map in walking order.
</Callout>

<KeyValue title="Trip at a glance">
| | |
|---|---|
| Travelers | 2 adults |
| Dates | 5 days, flexible |
| Base | Shinjuku or Shibuya (best rail access) |
| Getting around | Suica/PASMO IC card + your feet |
| Best value pass | 72-hour Tokyo Subway Ticket (~¥1,500) |
| Daily budget | ¥18,000–25,000 per person, food + entry |
</KeyValue>

<Checklist title="Before you go" name="tokyo-prep">
- [ ] Suica or PASMO loaded on phone (Apple Wallet / Google Wallet)
- [ ] Pocket wifi or eSIM ordered
- [ ] Cash: ¥20,000–30,000 for small shops and markets
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes — 15,000+ steps a day
- [ ] Portable battery, trash bag (bins are rare)
- [ ] Book Shibuya Sky, teamLab, one splurge dinner
- [ ] Download offline maps and a translation app
</Checklist>
