# 10 Spanish phrases for your trip

Ten phrases that cover most of what a traveller actually needs: greeting, asking, ordering, and getting unstuck. Each one comes with how to say it, what it means, and when to use it.

<Callout tone="info" title="How to use this">
Read the phrase aloud once, then say it in the situation. The pronunciation column is written the way it sounds to an English speaker, not the official phonetic alphabet — good enough to be understood.
</Callout>

<Card title="The 10 phrases">
<KeyValue title="Say it, mean it, use it">
| Phrase | Sounds like | Meaning | When to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hola | OH-lah | Hello | Walking into any shop, café or hotel |
| Buenos días | BWEH-nos DEE-as | Good morning | Before about 2pm |
| Por favor | por fah-VOR | Please | Tag it onto any request |
| Gracias | GRAH-see-as | Thank you | Always, everywhere |
| Perdón | per-DON | Sorry / Excuse me | Getting attention or squeezing past |
| ¿Habla inglés? | AH-blah een-GLÉS | Do you speak English? | When you're stuck |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah | How much does it cost? | Markets, taxis, anywhere without prices |
| La cuenta, por favor | lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR | The bill, please | End of every meal |
| ¿Dónde está…? | DON-deh es-TAH | Where is…? | Add the place after it |
| No entiendo | noh en-TYEN-doh | I don't understand | The honest rescue line |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="Two that do the most work">
<Callout tone="success" title="Por favor and gracias are your safety net">
In Spain and most of Latin America, a request without *por favor* can sound blunt. Adding it, plus a *gracias*, turns a bare demand into a polite one — and locals will meet you halfway.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="neutral" title="¿Dónde está…? is a plug-in phrase">
Stick any place name on the end: *¿Dónde está el baño?* (the toilet), *¿Dónde está la estación?* (the station), *¿Dónde está el hotel?* You only have to learn one sentence.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="The sounds that trip people up">
| Letter | Say it like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| h | silent | *hola* = OH-lah |
| j | a hard h | *ajo* = AH-hoh |
| ll | a y sound | *llave* = YAH-veh |
| ñ | ny as in canyon | *mañana* = mah-NYAH-nah |
| v | sounds like b | *vino* = BEE-noh |
| r (single) | a soft tap | *pero* = PEH-roh |
</Card>
