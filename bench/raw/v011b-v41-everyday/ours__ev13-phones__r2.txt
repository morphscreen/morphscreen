# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9

Short answer: they're both excellent, and the choice comes down to **ecosystem and software taste**, not raw specs. Here's how to decide.

<Card title="The 30-second version">
<Compare title="Head to head">
| Feature | iPhone 16 | Pixel 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | $799 | $799 |
| Chip | A18 | Tensor G4 |
| Display | 6.1" 60Hz | 6.3" 120Hz |
| Battery | ~3,600 mAh | ~4,700 mAh |
| Charging speed | 20W wired | 27W wired |
| Telephoto lens | No | No |
| AI assistant | Apple Intelligence | Gemini |
| OS updates | ~5–6 years | 7 years |
| Customization | Locked down | Very open |
| Ecosystem lock-in | Strong (if you own a Mac/Watch) | Google-centric |
</Compare>
</Card>

<Card title="Pick the iPhone 16 if…">
- You already own a **Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, or AirPods** — the handoff, iMessage, and Find My integration is the real product.
- You care about **resale value and long-term support** without thinking about it.
- You want the **most polished, consistent app experience** — developers ship to iOS first.
- You're fine with a **60Hz screen** (the one spec that genuinely feels dated in 2026).

<Button ask="Tell me more about the iPhone 16">More on the iPhone 16</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="Pick the Pixel 9 if…">
- You want a **120Hz display** and a **bigger battery** at the same price.
- You like **Android's flexibility** — sideloading, custom launchers, real file access.
- You want **Gemini's on-device AI features** (call screening, Magic Editor, Now Playing).
- You want **7 years of OS updates** — the longest commitment in the industry.

<Button ask="Tell me more about the Pixel 9">More on the Pixel 9</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="What actually decides it">
<KeyValue title="Decision shortcuts">
| If this is true | Get this |
|---|---|
| You own a Mac or Apple Watch | iPhone 16 |
| You own a Chromebook or Pixel Watch | Pixel 9 |
| You shoot a lot of photos in low light | Pixel 9 |
| You shoot a lot of video | iPhone 16 |
| You want the phone to feel fast (scrolling) | Pixel 9 |
| You want the phone to feel smooth (animations) | iPhone 16 |
| You keep phones 5+ years | Either — both are covered |
| You resell every 2 years | iPhone 16 |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="The one question to ask yourself">
<Callout tone="info" title="The real test">
Which phone do your **closest friends and family** use? If everyone you message daily is on iMessage, switching to Android means green bubbles and broken group chats. If they're on WhatsApp or Signal, that pressure disappears entirely — and then the Pixel 9 is the better hardware for the money.
</Callout>
</Card>
