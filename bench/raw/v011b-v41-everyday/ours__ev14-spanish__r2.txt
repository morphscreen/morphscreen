# 10 basic Spanish phrases for your trip

Start with these ten. They cover greetings, politeness, ordering, directions, and emergencies — enough to get through most travel moments.

<Card title="The ten phrases" subtitle="Say them out loud as you read">
| # | Spanish | Say it roughly | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hola | OH-lah | Hello |
| 2 | Por favor | por fah-VOR | Please |
| 3 | Gracias | GRAH-syahs | Thank you |
| 4 | Buenos días | BWEH-nos DEE-ahs | Good morning |
| 5 | ¿Habla inglés? | AH-blah een-GLAYS | Do you speak English? |
| 6 | La cuenta, por favor | lah KWEHN-tah por fah-VOR | The bill, please |
| 7 | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah | How much is it? |
| 8 | ¿Dónde está…? | DON-deh es-TAH | Where is…? |
| 9 | No entiendo | noh ehn-TYEHN-doh | I don't understand |
| 10 | Ayuda | ah-YOO-dah | Help |
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Two habits that make you sound better">
Say **por favor** and **gracias** constantly — they carry more weight in Spanish than in English. And greet before you ask: "Hola, ¿dónde está…?" lands far better than launching into the question.
</Callout>

<Tabs>
<Tab title="At a restaurant">
| Situation | What to say |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Buenos días |
| Ordering | Quisiera esto, por favor |
| Asking the price | ¿Cuánto cuesta? |
| Leaving | La cuenta, por favor |
</Tab>
<Tab title="On the street">
| Situation | What to say |
|---|---|
| Getting attention | Perdón |
| Asking for a place | ¿Dónde está el metro? |
| Not understanding | No entiendo |
| Asking for English | ¿Habla inglés? |
</Tab>
<Tab title="In trouble">
| Situation | What to say |
|---|---|
| You need help | Ayuda |
| You feel unwell | Necesito un médico |
| You're lost | Estoy perdido |
| Emergency number | 112 (most of Europe) |
</Tab>

<Callout tone="success" title="How to actually remember them">
Ten phrases is small enough for one sitting. Say each one aloud three times, then run the flashcards once before bed and once tomorrow morning — that spacing does more than an hour of cramming.
</Callout>
