# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A first-timer's loop: old Tokyo in the east, the big set pieces in the middle, one day out west, and a day trip if you want it. Two people, moderate budget, mostly trains.

<Callout tone="info" title="How this is built">
Each day is a compact area so you're not criss-crossing the city. Mornings start early (temples and markets are best before 9am), evenings end near food and a train line.
</Callout>

<KeyValue title="Trip at a glance">
| | |
|---|---|
| Dates | 5 days, 4 nights |
| Party | 2 adults |
| Base | Shinjuku or Ueno (both on the Yamanote loop) |
| Getting around | Suica/Pasmo card + metro day passes |
| Daily walking | 12,000–18,000 steps |
| Best money saver | Lunch sets (teishoku) at ¥1,000–1,500 |
</KeyValue>

<Card title="Day 5 — Day trip or last-minute Tokyo" subtitle="Pick one, then head to the airport">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Hakone (hot springs + views)">
Leave Shinjuku around 07:00 on the Odakyu line. Ropeway over Owakudani, Lake Ashi cruise, and a ryokan day-bath if you have time. Back by 19:00–20:00. Buy the Hakone Free Pass.
<Button ask="Plan the Hakone day trip hour by hour">Plan Hakone</Button>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Nikko (shrines in the forest)">
Tobu line from Asakusa, about 2 hours. Toshogu Shrine, the three wise monkeys carving, and the cedar avenue. Cooler and quieter than Tokyo. Back by evening.
<Button ask="Plan the Nikko day trip hour by hour">Plan Nikko</Button>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Stay in Tokyo (easy last day)">
TeamLab Planets or the Ghibli Museum if you booked ahead, then Akihabara for electronics and arcades, then a final depachika food haul at Tokyo Station before your train.
<Button ask="Plan a relaxed final day in Tokyo">Plan the final day</Button>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
