# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is ringed by mountains and coastline, so you can be on a summit by lunch and back for dinner. Here are the standouts, roughly ordered from easiest to hardest.

<Callout tone="info" title="Before you go">
Most of these need a car (or a shuttle) to reach the trailhead. Check trail conditions on AllTrails or the BC Parks site — snow lingers on higher trails into June, and the North Shore gets muddy fast after rain.
</Callout>

<Card title="Quick and close: Quarry Rock & Lighthouse Park">
<KeyValue title="At a glance">
| | |
|---|---|
| Quarry Rock | Deep Cove, North Vancouver — 3.8 km round trip, ~1.5 h |
| Lighthouse Park | West Vancouver — 6 km of easy trails, ~2 h |
| Transit | Both reachable by bus from downtown |
| Best time | Weekday mornings; Quarry Rock gets packed on sunny weekends |
</KeyValue>

Quarry Rock is the classic starter: a forest walk ending on a bluff over Deep Cove, with a doughnut stop in the village afterwards. Lighthouse Park is flatter and quieter, with old-growth fir and views across to Point Atkinson.
</Card>

<Card title="The big one: Garibaldi Lake">
<KeyValue title="At a glance">
| | |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Rubble Creek, near Squamish — 1 h 45 min drive |
| Distance | 18 km round trip, 820 m gain |
| Time | 6–7 hours |
| Permit | Day-use pass required in summer |
| Season | July–October (snow into June) |
</KeyValue>

A steady switchback climb through forest, then a sudden reveal of a glacial lake ringed by volcanoes. Worth every step, but it's a full day — start early, carry 2+ litres of water, and bring layers; the lake sits at 1,500 m and runs cold even in August.
</Card>

<Card title="Other strong options">
<Timeline title="Pick by what you want">
- 1.5 h, easy: Quarry Rock — forest bluff over Deep Cove
- 2 h, easy: Lighthouse Park — old-growth and ocean views
- 3 h, moderate: Norvan Falls — flat 14 km through Lynn Valley to a waterfall
- 4 h, moderate: St. Mark's Summit — best effort-to-view ratio on the North Shore
- 4 h, moderate: Joffre Lakes — three turquoise lakes, but a long drive and a day pass
- 5 h, hard: Diez Vistas — ridge loop above Buntzen Lake with ten viewpoints
- 6–7 h, hard: Garibaldi Lake — the alpine showpiece
</Timeline>
</Card>

<Card title="What to bring">
<Checklist title="Day hike packing" name="packing">
- [x] Water — 1.5–2 L for short hikes, 3 L for Garibaldi
- [x] Trail runners or hiking shoes
- [ ] Rain shell (North Shore weather turns fast)
- [ ] Layers — summit temperatures run 8–10 °C cooler
- [ ] Snacks and a lunch
- [ ] Bear spray for Garibaldi, Joffre, and Norvan Falls
- [ ] Headlamp if you're starting after noon on a long trail
- [ ] Park pass / day-use permit printed or on your phone
</Checklist>
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Bear country">
Black bears and the occasional grizzly live in these areas. Make noise on blind corners, keep food sealed, and never leave a pack unattended at a viewpoint.
</Callout>
