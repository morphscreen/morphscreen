The sweet spot for value road trainers sits between **$95 and $120** — you can get a daily trainer with a modern foam and 400+ km of life without paying flagship prices. Below are the four strongest picks right now, then a way to narrow it down to one.

<Callout tone="info" title="How to choose fast">
If you mostly run easy miles: pick the **daily trainer**. If you race or do speedwork: pick the **lightweight tempo shoe**. If you've had shin or knee niggles: pick the **max-cushion** option. If you're a heavier runner or want durability above all: pick the **firm, high-mileage** shoe.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Two things that matter more than the shoe">
1. **Fit**: leave a thumbnail's width at the toe, and make sure the heel locks down. A $110 shoe that fits beats a $180 shoe that doesn't.
2. **Rotation**: two cheaper pairs worn alternately last longer than one expensive pair, because the foam recovers between runs.
</Callout>

<Card title="Buying checklist">
<Checklist title="Before you order" name="shoe-checklist">
- [ ] Measure your foot in the evening, standing, in the socks you run in
- [ ] Check the return policy — most brands allow 30–90 days of actual running
- [ ] Confirm the size you're ordering in that brand (they run differently)
- [ ] Look for last season's colourway of your pick: often 30–40% off
- [ ] Add a second pair if you run more than 40 km a week
</Checklist>
</Card>
