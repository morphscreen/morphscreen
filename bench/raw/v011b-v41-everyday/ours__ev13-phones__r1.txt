# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9

Both are excellent — the right pick depends on what you value most. Here's the honest breakdown.

<Card title="The short answer">
<Callout tone="info" title="Quick verdict">
Pick the **iPhone 16** if you're already in Apple's world (Mac, Watch, AirPods) or want the longest software support. Pick the **Pixel 9** if you want the best camera tricks, the cleanest Android, and a lower price.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Where each one wins">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">

- **Longer track record** on updates and resale value
- **Best-in-class video** and Action button
- Familiar, polished, low-fuss
</Card>
<Card title="Pixel 9">
- **120Hz display** at the same price — noticeably smoother
- **Camera magic** — Magic Editor, Best Take, Add Me
- **Gemini built in** for on-device AI help
- **Faster charging** and slightly bigger battery
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="success" title="Bottom line">
If you own a Mac, Apple Watch, or AirPods, the **iPhone 16** is the easy choice — it just works together. If you're on Android or open to switching and love photography and AI, the **Pixel 9** gives you more screen and more camera for the same money.
</Callout>
