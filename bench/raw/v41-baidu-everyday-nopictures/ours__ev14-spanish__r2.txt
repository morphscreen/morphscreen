# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

Start with these ten. They cover greetings, ordering, getting help, and being polite — the situations you'll hit most.

<Card title="The 10 phrases" subtitle="Say them out loud as you read">
| # | Spanish | English | Say it like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hola | Hello | OH-lah |
| 2 | Buenos días | Good morning | BWEH-nos DEE-as |
| 3 | Por favor | Please | por fah-VOR |
| 4 | Gracias | Thank you | GRAH-see-as |
| 5 | De nada | You're welcome | deh NAH-dah |
| 6 | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | How much does it cost? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah |
| 7 | La cuenta, por favor | The bill, please | lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR |
| 8 | ¿Dónde está el baño? | Where is the bathroom? | DON-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyo |
| 9 | No entiendo | I don't understand | noh en-TYEN-doh |
| 10 | ¿Habla inglés? | Do you speak English? | AH-blah een-GLÉS |
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Two things that make you sound better fast">
The "h" in Spanish is silent (hola = OH-lah), and most words stress the second-to-last syllable unless written with an accent. Getting those two right is most of the way there.
</Callout>
