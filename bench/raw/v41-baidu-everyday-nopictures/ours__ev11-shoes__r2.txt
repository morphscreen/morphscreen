# Road running shoes under $120

At this price the sweet spot is **last year's top trainer** or **this year's daily trainer**. You don't need carbon plates for everyday miles — you need a shoe that fits and that you'll actually lace up.

<Card title="What matters most at this budget" subtitle="In rough order of impact">
<KeyValue title="Priorities">
| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Fit (width + length) | The single biggest cause of blisters, black toenails, and quitting |
| Midsole foam | Cheaper foams pack out in ~300 km; good EVA blends last 600+ |
| Outsole rubber | Road shoes wear fast; look for full-coverage rubber, not exposed foam |
| Drop | 6–10 mm suits most heel strikers; 0–4 mm only if you're already used to it |
| Weight | Under ~280 g (men's 9) feels light enough for tempo days |
| Carbon plate | Not needed here — skip it and spend the money on fit |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="Before you order" subtitle="Two minutes that save a return">
<Checklist title="Buying checklist" name="shoe-checklist">
- [ ] Measure your feet in the evening — feet swell during the day
- [ ] Add 0.5–1 cm of space at the toe (roughly a thumb's width)
- [ ] Check the width option — most models come in 2E/4E
- [ ] Confirm the return window (30 days worn is common, 14 is not)
- [ ] Order two sizes if you're between them, return the loser
- [ ] Buy two pairs if it's a discontinued model you love
</Checklist>
</Card>

<Card title="First 50 km" subtitle="Break them in without breaking down">
<Steps>
<Step title="Run 5 km easy" status="todo">
Keep it short and slow. Expect a slightly stiff ride.
</Step>
<Step title="Alternate with your old shoes" status="todo">
Two or three runs in the new pair, then back to the old ones. This avoids sudden load changes.
</Step>
<Step title="Long run by week two" status="todo">
If nothing hurts after ~30 km total, take them on your long run.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide by 100 km" status="done">
Returns need the shoes unworn-looking, so make the call before the tread scuffs badly.
</Step>
</Steps>
