# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

You'll get furthest with greetings, "please/thank you," and a few survival questions. Here are the ten worth memorizing first.

<Card title="The 10 phrases" subtitle="Say them out loud a few times each">
| English | Spanish | Say it like |
|---|---|---|
| Hello | Hola | OH-lah |
| Good morning | Buenos días | BWEH-nos DEE-as |
| Please | Por favor | por fah-VOR |
| Thank you | Gracias | GRAH-syas |
| You're welcome | De nada | deh NAH-dah |
| Excuse me / Sorry | Perdón | per-DON |
| Do you speak English? | ¿Habla inglés? | AH-blah een-GLAYS |
| I don't understand | No entiendo | no en-TYEN-doh |
| How much is it? | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah |
| The bill, please | La cuenta, por favor | lah KWEN-tah por fah-VOR |
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Two habits that carry you far">
1. **Add "por favor" to any request** — it turns a demand into a polite ask.
2. **Start with "Perdón"** before a question. It buys you a moment and signals you're trying.
</Callout>

<Card title="When to use which">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Greeting someone">
**Hola** works any time of day. **Buenos días** before noon, **Buenas tardes** in the afternoon, **Buenas noches** in the evening — the last one doubles as goodbye at night.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Ordering & paying">
Point at the menu and say **¿Cuánto cuesta?** for one item, or **La cuenta, por favor** when you're done. **Gracias** as they hand it over.
</Tab>
<Tab title="When you're stuck">
**¿Habla inglés?** is your lifeline. If the answer is no, **No entiendo** plus pointing usually gets you through.
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>
