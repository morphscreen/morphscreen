# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9

Both are 2024 flagships at similar prices, but they optimize for different things. Here's the honest breakdown.

<Card title="The short answer">
- **Pick the iPhone 16** if you're already in Apple's world (Mac, iPad, Watch, AirPods), want the longest software support, and value polished video.
- **Pick the Pixel 9** if you want the best still photos, the smartest assistant features, and more AI tricks baked in — and you're happy on Android.

Neither is a mistake. The deciding factor is usually your ecosystem, not the spec sheet.
</Card>

<Card title="Where each one wins">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="iPhone 16">
- **Video** — class-leading stabilization and color, the pick if you shoot a lot of clips.
- **Ecosystem** — seamless with Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods, AirDrop, iMessage.
- **Resale & support** — holds value well and gets updates for years.
- **Downside** — 60Hz screen feels dated next to the Pixel, and charging is slower.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Pixel 9">
- **Still photos** — Google's processing is the best point-and-shoot around, especially in low light.
- **AI features** — Gemini, Magic Editor, Call Screen, live transcription.
- **Screen** — 120Hz makes scrolling noticeably smoother.
- **Downside** — Tensor chip trails the A18 in raw performance and gaming.
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="If you're truly stuck">
The Pixel 9 is the better value on hardware (bigger battery, 120Hz screen, more RAM) at the same price. The iPhone 16 wins on ecosystem lock-in and long-term polish. If you have no strong ties either way, the Pixel 9 gives you more phone for the money.
</Callout>
