# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A first-timer's itinerary that balances the big sights with slow mornings, food streets, and one day out of the city. Base yourself in **Shinjuku** (transport hub, easy late nights) or **Asakusa** (quieter, cheaper, riverside).

<Callout tone="info" title="Before you go">
Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at the airport (or add Suica to Apple Wallet) — it covers trains, buses, and most convenience stores. Two people, five days, roughly **¥60,000–90,000** in spending money on top of lodging if you eat well but not lavishly.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day-by-day plan">
<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1 — Land, settle, Shinjuku at night" status="todo" subtitle="Ease in, no jet-lag heroics">
Arrive, drop bags, walk **Shinjuku Gyoen** if you have daylight left. Evening: Omoide Yokocho yakitori alley, then the free **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** observation deck (open late, no charge). Finish in Kabukicho for the neon.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 2 — Old Tokyo: Asakusa & Ueno" status="todo" subtitle="Temples, markets, museums">
Morning at **Senso-ji** before 9am (beat the crowds), snack down **Nakamise** street. Walk to the river for a Sumida cruise or just the view of **Skytree**. Afternoon in **Ueno Park** — pick one museum (Tokyo National Museum is the classic). Dinner: monjayaki in Ueno.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 3 — Tsukiji, Ginza, Imperial Palace" status="todo" subtitle="Food first, then polish">
Breakfast at **Tsukiji Outer Market** — tamagoyaki, uni, fresh tuna. Walk or metro to **Ginza** for the department store food halls (depachika) and architecture. Afternoon: **Imperial Palace East Gardens** (free, closed Mon/Fri). Evening: **Yurakucho** under-the-tracks izakaya.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 4 — Shibuya, Harajuku, and a view" status="todo" subtitle="The postcard day">
**Meiji Shrine** early, then **Takeshita Street** in Harajuku, then walk down to **Shibuya Crossing**. Book **Shibuya Sky** sunset slot in advance — it sells out. Dinner in Shibuya, then karaoke if you have energy.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 5 — Day trip: Nikko or Kamakura" status="todo" subtitle="Out of the city, back by night">
**Kamakura** is easier (1 hr): Great Buddha, bamboo temple, seaside. **Nikko** is grander (2 hrs): Tosho-gu shrine, mountain scenery. Either way, be back for a final Tokyo dinner — or swap this for a half-day in **Akihabara** or **Yanaka** if you'd rather stay put.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Book ahead">
**Shibuya Sky**, popular **teamLab** exhibits, and any **themed cafe** need reservations days or weeks out. Trains stop around midnight — plan your last ride or budget for a taxi.
</Callout>
