# iPhone 16 vs Pixel 9

Both are 2024 flagships, and both are excellent. The choice comes down to **ecosystem, camera style, and software philosophy** rather than raw power — they're close on specs.

<Card title="Head to head">

|---|---|---|
| Display | 6.1" OLED, 60Hz | 6.3" OLED, 120Hz |
| Chip | A18 | Tensor G4 |
| RAM | 8 GB | 12 GB |
| Main camera | 48 MP | 50 MP |
| Telephoto | No | No |
| Ultrawide | 12 MP | 48 MP |
| Battery | ~3,561 mAh | ~4,700 mAh |
| Charging | 20W wired | 27W wired |
| Starting price | $799 | $799 |
| OS updates | ~6–7 years | 7 years |

</Compare>

<Card title="Where each one wins" subtitle="Honest strengths and weaknesses">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">
<Badge tone="info">Best if you're in Apple's world</Badge>

- **Ecosystem**: AirDrop, iMessage, Handoff, Apple Watch, AirPods all just work together
- **Video**: clearly better stabilization and consistency
- **Performance**: A18 is faster in sustained heavy use
- **Resale**: holds value far better over 2–3 years
- **Weak spot**: 60Hz screen feels dated next to the Pixel
- **Weak spot**: no telephoto, so zoomed shots are digital
</Card>
<Card title="Pixel 9">
<Badge tone="success">Best if you want the better camera and screen</Badge>

- **Camera**: best-in-class stills, especially in low light and for skin tones
- **Screen**: 120Hz makes everything feel smoother
- **Battery**: larger cell, generally lasts longer
- **AI**: Gemini, Magic Editor, Call Screen, transcription are genuinely useful
- **Weak spot**: Tensor G4 trails A18 in heavy gaming
- **Weak spot**: video and app polish still behind iPhone
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="The one-line answer">
If you already own a Mac, AirPods, or an Apple Watch — get the **iPhone 16**. If you don't, and photos plus a smooth screen matter more than ecosystem lock-in — get the **Pixel 9**. They cost the same, so ecosystem is the tiebreaker.
</Callout>
