<Card title="What actually matters at this price">
<KeyValue title="Buying checklist">
| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Half a thumb's width at the toe | Feet swell on runs; a tight toe box causes black toenails |
| Heel locked, no slip | Blisters come from movement, not from the shoe being "too small" |
| Try on at the end of the day | Feet are largest then, so the fit is realistic |
| Replace at ~500 km / 300 mi | Midsole foam stops absorbing before the outsole looks worn |
| Rotate two pairs if you can | Foam recovers between runs, so both pairs last longer |
</KeyValue>
</Card>
