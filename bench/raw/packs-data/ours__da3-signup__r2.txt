<Card title="Biggest Drop-off">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Started signup → Completed signup: 42% drop-off">
<strong>16,100 visitors</strong> started but didn't finish signup. The 6-step form with mandatory phone verification and company details is the likely cause — session recordings show 68% abandon at step 3 (company info) and 22% at step 4 (phone OTP).
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Two Ideas to Fix It">
<Steps>
<Step title="Reduce form to 3 steps + progressive profiling" status="todo">
Merge steps 1–2 (account basics), make company details optional with "skip for now," and defer phone verification until after first login. Target: cut drop-off from 42% → 25%.
</Step>
<Step title="Add inline trust signals & live chat at step 3" status="todo">
Show security badges, "1,200+ teams joined this week" counter, and a "Questions?" chat widget exactly where abandonment spikes. Target: recover 15–20% of step-3 exits.
</Step>
</Steps>
