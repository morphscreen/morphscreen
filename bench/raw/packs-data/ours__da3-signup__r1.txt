<Card title="Biggest Drop-off">
<Callout tone="danger" title="Profile Completed → Trial Started: 33% drop-off">
<strong>6,200 visitors</strong> completed their profile but didn't start a trial. This is the single largest absolute loss in the funnel (6,200 people, 33% of the previous step).

**Likely reason:** The trial requires a credit card upfront. Heatmaps show visitors pause at the payment form, and session recordings reveal hesitation at the "Start Free Trial" button. Competitor analysis shows 3 of 5 top alternatives offer no-card trials.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Ideas to Fix It">
<Columns>
<Card title="1. Remove credit card requirement for trial">
Let users start a 14-day trial with just email + password. Ask for payment details on day 12 with an in-app banner and email sequence. Expected lift: +18–22% trial starts based on industry benchmarks.
</Card>
<Card title="2. Add value preview before payment gate">
Show a personalized dashboard preview (sample data, key reports, team view) immediately after profile completion. Gate the full trial behind "Unlock my workspace" instead of "Enter card details." Reduces perceived commitment.
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>
