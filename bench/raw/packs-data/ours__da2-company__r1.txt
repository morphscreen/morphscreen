# Company History

<Card title="Company Milestones" subtitle="Key events from founding to present">
<Timeline title="Milestones">
- 2015-03-12: Company founded by Sarah Chen and Michael Torres in a San Francisco garage with $50K seed funding
- 2016-06-20: Launched first product, TaskFlow, a project management tool for small teams
- 2017-01-15: Raised $2.5M Series A led by Vertex Ventures; team grows to 12
- 2018-09-03: Released TaskFlow Enterprise with SSO, audit logs, and advanced reporting
- 2019-11-12: Crossed 10,000 paying customers; annual recurring revenue hits $5M
- 2020-03-28: Launched remote collaboration suite (Docs, Chat, Video) in response to pandemic demand
- 2021-06-14: Raised $40M Series B at $350M valuation; expanded to London and Singapore offices
- 2022-02-07: Acquired competitor SyncPoint, adding 15 engineers and 3,000 customers
- 2023-08-21: Released AI-powered workflow automation; reaches 50,000 customers globally
- 2024-01-15: Launched developer platform with APIs, webhooks, and marketplace
- 2024-11-01: Announced profitability with $120M ARR and 420 employees across 6 offices
</Timeline>
</Card>
