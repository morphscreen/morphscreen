<Card title="What we're looking for">
<Steps>
<Step title="5+ years designing shipped product surfaces" status="current" subtitle="Web or native, at real scale">
You've taken features from problem statement to production and can talk about what happened after launch.
</Step>
<Step title="Strong systems thinking" status="todo" subtitle="Tokens, components, documentation">
You can design a component once and explain why it should never be forked.
</Step>
<Step title="Fluency in Figma and code-adjacent tools" status="todo" subtitle="Prototypes, not just mockups">
Comfortable reading HTML/CSS, working in a repo, and pairing with engineers.
</Step>
<Step title="Research literacy" status="todo" subtitle="You know when to test and when to ship">
Able to run a usability session and turn findings into a decision.
</Step>
<Step title="Clear written communication" status="todo" subtitle="Decisions documented, not implied">
Our design reviews run on written rationale.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Nice to have">
- Experience with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- Design ops or tooling work
- Motion and interaction prototyping
- Prior work on a design system used by 50+ engineers
</Card>
