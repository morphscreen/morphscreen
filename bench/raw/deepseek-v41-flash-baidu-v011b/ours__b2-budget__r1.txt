<Details title="How the numbers are worked out">
- **Spent so far** sums every logged expense dated in the selected month.
- **Budget used** compares that total against the monthly budget you set ($2,300).
- **Category split** groups the same expenses by category; percentages are of the month's total.
- **Largest expense** is the single highest-amount entry, not the highest category.
- Changing the month re-runs all of the above for that month's entries.
</Details>
