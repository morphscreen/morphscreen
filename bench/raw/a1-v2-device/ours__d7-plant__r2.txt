<Tab title="Details">
<Details title="Spider Mites (87%)" open="true">
Tiny arachnids that suck sap from leaves. Thrive in hot, dry conditions. Look for fine silk webbing on leaf undersides and between stems.
</Details>
<Details title="Powdery Mildew (72%)">
Fungal disease appearing as white powdery spots. Spreads via spores in humid, crowded conditions with poor airflow.
</Details>
<Details title="Root Rot (45%)">
Caused by overwatering and poor drainage. Roots turn brown/black and mushy. Plant wilts despite wet soil.
</Details>
<Details title="Nitrogen Deficiency (38%)">
Older leaves turn pale yellow while veins stay green. Growth slows. Common in depleted soil or heavy-feeding plants.
</Details>
</Tab>

<Card title="3. Care Steps" subtitle="Recommended treatment plan">
<Steps>
<Step title="Isolate the plant" status="current">
Move the affected plant away from others to prevent spread. Quarantine for at least 2 weeks.
</Step>
<Step title="Remove heavily damaged leaves" status="todo">
Prune leaves with >50% damage using sterilized scissors. Dispose in sealed bag — don't compost.
</Step>
<Step title="Treat for spider mites" status="todo">
Spray neem oil or insecticidal soap every 3–4 days for 2 weeks. Cover undersides thoroughly. Increase humidity.
</Step>
<Step title="Improve airflow & reduce humidity" status="todo">
Space plants apart. Run a fan on low. Avoid overhead watering. Water at soil level only.
</Step>
<Step title="Check roots & repot if needed" status="todo">
If root rot suspected, unpot, trim mushy roots, repot in fresh well-draining mix. Water only when top 2″ dry.
</Step>
<Step title="Fertilize lightly" status="todo">
After recovery signs appear, apply balanced liquid fertilizer at ½ strength. Repeat monthly in growing season.
</Step>
<Step title="Monitor & prevent recurrence" status="todo">
Inspect weekly. Wipe leaves monthly. Quarantine new plants 2 weeks. Maintain consistent care routine.
</Step>
</Steps>
