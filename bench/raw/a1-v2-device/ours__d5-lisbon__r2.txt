<Card title="Day-by-Day Schedule">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 – Baixa/Chiado/Bairro Alto">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:30 – Arrive & drop bags" status="todo">Check into hotel/hostel in Baixa or Chiado area</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Praça do Comércio" status="todo">Start at the grand riverside square, grab coffee at Café com Calma</Step>
<Step title="10:45 – Rua Augusta Arch" status="todo">Walk up Rua Augusta, climb the arch for city views (€3)</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Rossio Square & Ginjinha" status="todo">Try cherry liqueur at Ginjinha Sem Rival (€1.50)</Step>
<Step title="12:15 – Santa Justa Lift" status="todo">Ride the neo-Gothic elevator (€5.30 round-trip)</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch at Carmo Convent area" status="todo">Tapas at Topo Chiado or seafood at Mar do Inferno (€20-30)</Step>
<Step title="14:30 – Carmo Convent Ruins" status="todo">Visit the archaeological museum in the ruined church (€5)</Step>
<Step title="15:30 – Walk to Miradouro São Pedro de Alcântara" status="todo">Uphill walk through Chiado, panoramic city views (free)</Step>
<Step title="17:00 – Explore Bairro Alto" status="todo">Wander the narrow streets, browse shops, early petiscos</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Dinner in Bairro Alto" status="todo">Traditional Portuguese at Zé dos Cornos or Sea Me (€25-35)</Step>
<Step title="21:30 – Fado or bars" status="todo">Fado at Tasca do Chico (free entry, 1 drink min) or bar hop</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2 – Belém">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 – Tram 15 to Belém" status="todo">Take tram 15E from Praça da Figueira (€3 with 24h pass)</Step>
<Step title="09:45 – Belém Tower" status="todo">Visit the iconic fortress (€6, book ahead)</Step>
<Step title="11:00 – Jerónimos Monastery" status="todo">Cloisters & church (€10 combined with tower)</Step>
<Step title="12:30 – Pastéis de Belém" status="todo">Original pastéis de nata since 1837 (€1.30 each)</Step>
<Step title="13:30 – Lunch at MAAT vicinity" status="todo">Riverfront lunch at Pão de Canela or MAAT café (€20-25)</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – MAAT or Coach Museum" status="todo">Modern art (€9) or National Coach Museum (€8)</Step>
<Step title="17:00 – Riverside walk back toward center" status="todo">Stop at Belém Cultural Center gardens</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Dinner back in city center" status="todo">Seafood at Cervejaria Ramiro (queue early) or Taberna da Rua das Flores (€30-40)</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 3 – Alfama & Departure">
<Steps>
<Step title="08:30 – Walk/Tram 28 to Alfama" status="todo">Take tram 28 or walk up from Baixa (€3 with pass)</Step>
<Step title="09:30 – São Jorge Castle" status="todo">Explore castle walls & archaeological site (€10)</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Miradouro da Graça & Senhora do Monte" status="todo">Two stunning viewpoints, free</Step>
<Step title="12:30 – Feira da Ladra (if Tue/Sat)" status="todo">Flea market at Campo de Santa Clara</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch at Time Out Market" status="todo">Food hall with 30+ vendors (€15-25)</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – Last coffee & depart" status="todo">Café da Garagem or Nicolau Lisboa, head to airport</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
