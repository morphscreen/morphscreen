## Day-by-day schedule

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 — Baixa & Bairro Alto">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:30 — Arrive & drop bags" status="todo">Check into hotel/hostel in Baixa or Chiado. Leave luggage if early.</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — Praça do Comércio" status="todo">Riverside square, statue of King José I. Coffee at Café com Lisboa.</Step>
<Step title="10:45 — Rua Augusta Arch" status="todo">Walk through arch, take elevator to terrace (€3).</Step>
<Step title="11:30 — Rossio Square" status="todo">Wave-patterned cobblestones, D. Maria II Theatre.</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Santa Justa Lift" status="todo">Ride up (€5.30 return) or walk up via Carmo. Skip queue: buy ticket at Carmo Convent.</Step>
<Step title="12:30 — Carmo Convent & Archaeology Museum" status="todo">Gothic ruins, open sky. €5 entry.</Step>
<Step title="13:30 — Lunch in Chiado" status="todo">Seafood rice at Mar do Inferno or petiscos at Taberna da Rua das Flores.</Step>
<Step title="15:00 — Explore Chiado shops & cafés" status="todo">A Brasileira, Bertrand (oldest bookshop), Livraria Bertrand.</Step>
<Step title="17:30 — Bairro Alto for sunset" status="todo">Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara. Drink at Pavilhão Chinês or Foxtrot.</Step>
<Step title="19:30 — Dinner in Bairro Alto" status="todo">Traditional: Zé dos Cornos. Modern: Taberna das Almas. Reserve.</Step>
<Step title="21:30 — Fado or bars" status="todo">Fado: Tasca do Chico (no cover, arrive early). Bars: Park Bar (rooftop), Pensão Amor.</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2 — Belém">
<Steps>
<Step title="08:30 — Tram 15 to Belém" status="todo">Buy 24h public transport pass (€6.80) or use Viva Viagem. Tram 15E from Praça da Figueira.</Step>
<Step title="09:30 — Belém Tower" status="todo">Book online (€6). Walk the ramparts, Tagus views.</Step>
<Step title="10:30 — Jerónimos Monastery" status="todo">Book online (€10). Cloisters are the highlight. Allow 1.5h.</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Pastéis de Belém" status="todo">Original since 1837. Eat warm with cinnamon & powdered sugar. Queue moves fast.</Step>
<Step title="12:45 — MAAT or Coach Museum" status="todo">MAAT (€9) for contemporary art + architecture. Coach Museum (€8) for royal carriages.</Step>
<Step title="14:30 — Lunch by the river" status="todo">Darwin's Café (MAAT) or informal at Jardim da Praça do Império.</Step>
<Step title="16:00 — Walk back via waterfront" status="todo">Flat 5km walk or tram back. Stop at Padrão dos Descobrimentos.</Step>
<Step title="18:30 — Rest / aperitivo" status="todo">Back in Baixa/Chiado. Ginjinha at Ginjinha Sem Rival (€1.40).</Step>
<Step title="20:00 — Dinner in Cais do Sodré" status="todo">Seafood: Marisqueira Azul. Tapas: Taberna da Rua das Flores (second location).</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3 — Alfama & Castle">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 — Tram 28 to Alfama" status="todo">Board at Martim Moniz or Praça da Figueira. Get off at Largo das Portas do Sol.</Step>
<Step title="09:30 — Miradouro de Santa Luzia" status="todo">Azalea-covered pergola, Tagus panorama. Free.</Step>
<Step title="10:00 — São Jorge Castle" status="todo">Book online (€10). Walls, towers, peacocks, 360° views. Allow 2h.</Step>
<Step title="12:00 — Wander Alfama lanes" status="todo">Rua dos Remédios, Rua de São Miguel. Lisbon Cathedral (Sé) — free entry.</Step>
<Step title="13:00 — Lunch at Time Out Market" status="todo">Food hall with 30+ stalls. Try: croquetes, bifana, grilled fish, pastel de nata.</Step>
<Step title="14:30 — Last coffee & depart" status="todo">Café da Garagem or Hello Kristof. Grab bags, metro to airport (red line, 20 min).</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Day 1 walking route (Baixa → Bairro Alto)

<Map title="Day 1 walking route" route="true">
| Stop | Address | Order | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Praça do Comércio | Praça do Comércio, 1100-148 Lisboa | 1 | Start — riverside |
| Rua Augusta Arch | Rua Augusta, 1100-053 Lisboa | 2 | 300 m, 4 min walk |
| Rossio Square | Praça de D. Pedro IV, 1100-200 Lisboa | 3 | 400 m, 5 min walk |
| Santa Justa Lift | R. de Santa Justa, 1100-521 Lisboa | 4 | 350 m, 4 min walk (uphill) |
| Carmo Convent | Largo do Carmo, 1200-092 Lisboa | 5 | 100 m, 1 min walk |
| Bairro Alto (Miradouro São Pedro de Alcântara) | R. de São Pedro de Alcântara, 1250-096 Lisboa | 6 | 600 m, 8 min walk (steep) |
</Map>

<Callout tone="info" title="Walking notes">
Total: ~2.1 km, ~30 min walking (excluding stops). Steep climb from Carmo to Bairro Alto — take the Glória Funicular (€3.80 with 24h pass) if tired. Cobblestones everywhere: wear supportive shoes.
</Callout>

## Budget estimate (per person, mid-range)

<Card title="Budget breakdown (€)">
| Category | Cost | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Accommodation (2 nights)** | 120–200 | Central hostel private room or 3★ hotel double occupancy |
| **Food & drink** | 100–150 | Breakfast €5, lunch €15, dinner €25, drinks/snacks €15/day |
| **Transport** | 15–20 | 24h pass €6.80 × 2 + airport metro €2.50 × 2 + funiculars |
| **Attractions & tickets** | 45–60 | Arch €3, Santa Justa €5.30, Carmo €5, Belém Tower €6, Jerónimos €10, Castle €10, MAAT €9 |
| **Miscellaneous** | 20–30 | Souvenirs, extra coffee, tips, water |
| **Total (mid-range)** | **300–460** | Excludes flights & travel insurance |
</Card>

<Columns>
<Card title="Money-saving tips">
- Buy **Lisboa Card** (24h €21, 48h €35, 72h €44) if hitting many paid sites + unlimited transport
- **Free entry** Sundays until 14:00 at many museums (Jerónimos, MAAT, etc.)
- **Pastéis de Belém**: takeaway line is faster than sit-down
- **Tram 28**: board at **Martim Moniz** (first stop) to guarantee a seat
- **Water**: refill at public fountains (água potável)
</Card>
