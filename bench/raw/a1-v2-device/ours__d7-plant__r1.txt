<Card title="3. Care steps" subtitle="Follow in order; re-check after each step">
<Steps>
<Step title="Isolate the plant" status="current">Move it away from other plants to prevent spread.</Step>
<Step title="Rinse foliage" status="todo">Spray leaves (top and bottom) with lukewarm water to dislodge mites.</Step>
<Step title="Apply neem oil" status="todo">Mix per label and spray every 7 days for 3 weeks.</Step>
<Step title="Adjust watering" status="todo">Let top 2–3 cm of soil dry before watering again.</Step>
<Step title="Feed lightly" status="todo">Use a balanced liquid fertilizer at half strength once monthly.</Step>
<Step title="Monitor & repeat" status="todo">Check daily; if no improvement in 10 days, consult a local expert.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
