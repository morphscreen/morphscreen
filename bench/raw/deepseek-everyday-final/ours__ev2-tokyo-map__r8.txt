<Card title="Day 1 — Neon Lights & Youth Culture">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Start your trip with the city's beating heart">
Visit the observation deck of the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** (free!). Then stroll through **Shinjuku Gyoen** for a peaceful garden break.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Harajuku & Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="Tradition meets street style">
Walk through the torii gates of **Meiji Shrine**, then explore **Takeshita Street** for crepes and quirky fashion. End at **Omotesando** for upscale shopping.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Shibuya" status="done" subtitle="The world's busiest crossing">
Watch the famous **Shibuya Crossing** scramble from the Starbucks overlook. Dinner at a *izakaya* in **Nakameguro** for a local vibe.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 2 — Old Tokyo & Skyline Views">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Asakusa" status="done" subtitle="Senso-ji and the old town">
Visit **Senso-ji**, Tokyo's oldest temple, and browse **Nakamise-dori** for souvenirs. Try *ningyo-yaki* (stuffed cakes).
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ueno & Akihabara" status="done" subtitle="Museums and electronics">
Explore **Ueno Park** and its museums (the National Museum is a must). Then dive into **Akihabara** for anime, arcades, and electronics.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="Sunset from 634m">
Take the elevator to the **Tembo Deck** for a golden-hour view. Have dinner in **Solamachi** mall below.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 3 — Food, Art & Waterfront">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="Breakfast of fresh sushi">
Sample *tamagoyaki*, uni, and tuna at the market stalls. Try the famous **Shiro-hako** (whitebait) at a tiny counter.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Hamarikyu & teamLab" status="done" subtitle="Gardens and digital art">
Stroll the tidal garden of **Hamarikyu**, then take the water bus to **teamLab Planets** for immersive art (book tickets in advance).
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Odaiba" status="done" subtitle="Rainbow Bridge at night">
Walk the waterfront, see the **Unicorn Gundam** statue, and enjoy dinner with a view of Tokyo Bay.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 4 — Imperial Grounds & City Views">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Imperial Palace" status="done" subtitle="History in the heart of the city">
Join a free guided tour of the **East Gardens** and see the Nijubashi Bridge. Then walk the **Marunouchi** business district.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Ginza" status="done" subtitle="Luxury and department stores">
Browse **Ginza Six** and the flagship stores. Try a *kaiseki* lunch or simple sushi at the basement food halls.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Roppongi Hills & Tokyo Tower" status="done" subtitle="Night views from above">
From the **Mori Art Museum** observation deck, see Tokyo Tower glowing below. End with a cocktail at a sky bar.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 5 — Relaxed Farewell">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen" status="done" subtitle="A green pause">
This national garden is perfect for a slow morning — cherry blossoms in spring, maples in autumn.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Tiny bars and yakitori">
Wander the narrow alleys of **Golden Gai** (200+ tiny bars). For lunch, squeeze into **Omoide Yokocho** for grilled skewers.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Last-minute shopping" status="done" subtitle="Don Quijote and depachika">
Pick up souvenirs at **Don Quijote** or the basement food halls of **Isetan** — then head to the airport.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Practical Tips">
<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at any station — tap on/off trains, buses, and even convenience stores. The **Yamanote Line** loops through most key areas.
</Callout>
<Callout tone="warning" title="Book ahead">
**teamLab Planets** and **Tokyo Skytree** — book tickets online at least 2–3 days ahead. For **Senso-ji**, go early (before 9am) to avoid crowds.
</Callout>
<Callout tone="success" title="Cash vs card">
Many small shops and restaurants are **cash-only**. Carry ¥10,000–20,000 in cash for daily use. Cards work at major stores and hotels.
</Callout>
</Card>
