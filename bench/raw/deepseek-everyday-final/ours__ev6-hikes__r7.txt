# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is blessed with incredible access to nature. Within an hour's drive, you can be on a mountain peak, by a glacial lake, or deep in old-growth forest. Here are the best day hikes, from easy coastal strolls to challenging summit scrambles.

<Callout tone="info" title="Hike smart">
Always check trail conditions before you go, carry the 10 essentials, and be bear-aware. Many trails are snow-covered well into summer.
</Callout>

<Card title="The Top 6 Day Hikes">
<Columns>
<div>

### 1. Stawamus Chief (The Chief)

- **Difficulty:** Challenging
- **Distance:** 11 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 700 m
- **Time:** 5–7 hours
- **Best season:** Year-round (ice in winter)

The granite monolith overlooking Squamish offers three peaks with jaw-dropping views of Howe Sound. The chain-assisted sections on the Second Peak are a fun scramble. Go early to avoid crowds.

</div>
<div>

### 2. Joffre Lakes

- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Distance:** 10 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 400 m
- **Time:** 4–5 hours
- **Best season:** July–October

Three turquoise glacial lakes stacked in a valley north of Pemberton. The turquoise colour comes from suspended glacial silt. The trail ends at the foot of Matier Glacier. Very popular — arrive before 8 am.

</div>
</Columns>

<Columns>
<div>

### 3. Garibaldi Lake

- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Distance:** 18 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 900 m
- **Time:** 6–8 hours
- **Best season:** July–October

A steady climb through forest opens up to one of the most stunning alpine lakes in BC, backed by the Battleship Islands and Black Tusk. The water is an unreal blue-green. Extend to Panorama Ridge for a full-day epic.

</div>
<div>

### 4. Quarry Rock (Deep Cove)

- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Distance:** 3.8 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 100 m
- **Time:** 1.5–2 hours
- **Best season:** Year-round

A short forest walk above Deep Cove with a panoramic view of Indian Arm. Perfect for a quick escape, families, or a sunset stroll. It gets busy on weekends — go early or on a weekday.

</div>
</Columns>

<Columns>
<div>

### 5. Dog Mountain (Mount Seymour)

- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Distance:** 5.7 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 280 m
- **Time:** 2–3 hours
- **Best season:** June–October (snowshoe in winter)

A rooty, rocky trail through old-growth forest to a rocky knoll with sweeping views over Vancouver, the harbour, and the Gulf Islands. The trail can be muddy — proper shoes are a must.

</div>
<div>

### 6. Black Tusk

- **Difficulty:** Very challenging
- **Distance:** 22 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 1,700 m
- **Time:** 10–12 hours
- **Best season:** July–October

The iconic volcanic spire visible from everywhere in the Sea to Sky corridor. The final scramble to the summit is exposed and not for the faint-hearted. The views of Garibaldi Lake and the surrounding peaks are extraordinary.

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Pro Tips">
<Callout tone="success" title="Make it a great day">
- **Start early** — by 7 am for popular trails; parking fills by 9 am on weekends.
- **Check conditions** — use [BC Parks](https://bcparks.ca) or the [Vancouver Trails](https://www.vancouvertrails.com) for up-to-date trail reports.
- **Pack layers** — mountain weather changes fast; bring a windbreaker even in summer.
- **Bring bear spray** — black bears and cougars are present; make noise on the trail.
- **Carry water** — at least 2 litres per person; there's no reliable water source on most trails.
- **No dogs** — Joffre Lakes and Garibaldi Lake do not allow dogs. The Chief and Quarry Rock do, on-leash.
</Callout>

<Card title="Common Questions">
<Details title="Which hike is best for beginners?" open="false">
Quarry Rock is the gentlest with the biggest reward. It's short, well-trodden, and the view over Deep Cove is stunning. Dog Mountain is a good next step if you want a bit more challenge.
</Details>

<Details title="Do I need a reservation or pass?">
No reservation is needed for any of these trails. Joffre Lakes and Garibaldi Lake require a day-use pass from BC Parks during summer (free, available online 48 hours in advance). Parking lots fill early.
</Details>

<Details title="Can I hike with kids?">
Quarry Rock is great for older kids. Joffre Lakes and Garibaldi Lake are doable with experienced hiker kids over 10. The Chief and Black Tusk are not recommended for children.
</Details>

<Details title="What should I wear?">
Sturdy hiking shoes or boots with good grip are essential — trails are rocky and often muddy. Dress in layers: a base layer, insulating mid-layer, and waterproof shell. A hat and sunscreen are musts in summer.
</Details>

<Details title="Is there cell service on the trails?">
Spotty at best. You'll get signal at viewpoints on The Chief and Dog Mountain, but Joffre Lakes and Garibaldi have almost none. Download offline maps before you go.
</Details>
</Card>
