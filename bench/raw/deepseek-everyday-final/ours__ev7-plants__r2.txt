# Which houseplants are hard to kill?

If you're new to plant care, or you just have a busy life, these tough plants will forgive missed waterings, low light, and general neglect. Here are the best options, from the nearly indestructible to the slightly pickier but still resilient.

### 1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata)

Also called "Mother-in-Law's Tongue," this is the ultimate survivor. It can go weeks without water, thrives in dim corners, and even tolerates fluorescent office light. Its tall, sword-like leaves come in various patterns and colors.

- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 2–4 weeks, let soil dry completely
- **Bonus:** Purifies air (NASA study)
- **Watch out:** Toxic to pets if ingested

### 2. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

This trailing vine is almost impossible to kill. It grows quickly, tolerates low light, and tells you when it's thirsty by drooping—then perks right back up after a drink. Great in hanging baskets or on shelves.

- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 1–2 weeks, top inch of soil dry
- **Bonus:** Grows in water alone
- **Watch out:** Toxic to pets

### 3. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

The ZZ plant stores water in its thick, potato-like rhizomes, so it can survive months of neglect. Its glossy, dark green leaves look almost artificial. It's the go-to for offices and low-light apartments.

- **Light:** Very low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 2–3 weeks, let soil dry fully
- **Bonus:** Survives fluorescent light
- **Watch out:** Toxic to pets; don't overwater (yellow leaves)

### 4. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)

A classic for a reason. Spider plants produce baby "spiderettes" that you can propagate easily, making them fun for kids. They tolerate a range of conditions and are non-toxic to pets.

- **Light:** Bright indirect is best, but tolerates low
- **Water:** Every 1–2 weeks, keep slightly moist
- **Bonus:** Pet safe, air-purifying
- **Watch out:** Brown tips if tap water is hard—use filtered

### 5. Aloe Vera

A succulent that stores water in its fleshy leaves. It loves bright light but will forgive missed waterings. The gel inside the leaves soothes minor burns and cuts.

- **Light:** Bright, direct sun preferred
- **Water:** Every 3–4 weeks, soil bone dry between
- **Bonus:** Medicinal gel, pet safe (but the gel can upset stomachs)
- **Watch out:** Prone to rot if overwatered; needs more sun than others

## Care Tips for Neglectful Plant Parents

<Card title="Set yourself up for success">
<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the right pot" status="done">
Make sure the pot has drainage holes. Root rot from standing water is the #1 killer, even of "unkillable" plants.
</Step>
<Step title="Use well-draining soil" status="done">
A cactus or succulent mix works for most of these. Regular potting soil holds too much water.
</Step>
<Step title="Water less, not more" status="done">
When in doubt, wait. Underwatering is recoverable; overwatering is fatal.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the right spot" status="done">
Most of these tolerate low light but grow better with some indirect light. Avoid direct hot sun except for aloe and jade.
</Step>
<Step title="Ignore them sometimes" status="done">
These plants thrive on neglect. Check them weekly, but don't fuss daily.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="The #1 killer: overwatering">
More houseplants die from too much water than too little. Before watering, stick your finger an inch into the soil. If it's damp, wait. If it's dry, water thoroughly until it drains out the bottom.
</Callout>

## What to buy as a gift

If you're buying for someone who says they kill everything, go with a snake plant or ZZ plant. They're the most forgiving and look great in any room.

<Card title="Quick picker">
| Situation | Best choice |
|---|---|
| Dark apartment, no windowsill | Snake Plant or ZZ Plant |
| Bright sunny windowsill | Aloe Vera or Jade Plant |
| Hanging basket or shelf | Pothos or Spider Plant |
| Pet owner | Spider Plant or Cast Iron Plant |
| Frequent traveler | Snake Plant or ZZ Plant |
| Kids who might touch | Spider Plant (safe, fun to propagate) |
</Card>
