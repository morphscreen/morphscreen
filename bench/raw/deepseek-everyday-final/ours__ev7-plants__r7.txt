# Houseplants That Are Hard to Kill

If you're new to plants or tend to forget to water, these resilient houseplants will forgive your neglect and still look great.

<Card title="The Unkillable Top 5">
<Columns>
<div>

### 1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria)
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 2–3 weeks
- **Why it survives:** Tolerates drought, low light, and irregular care

</div>
<div>

### 2. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** When soil feels dry
- **Why it survives:** Grows in almost any condition, even fluorescent office light

</div>
<div>

### 3. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)
- **Light:** Low to bright indirect
- **Water:** Every 2–4 weeks
- **Why it survives:** Stores water in rhizomes; thrives on neglect

</div>
<div>

### 4. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)
- **Light:** Bright indirect
- **Water:** Weekly, but survives missed waterings
- **Why it survives:** Hardy, fast-growing, and forgiving

</div>
<div>

### 5. Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum)
- **Light:** Low to medium indirect
- **Water:** Weekly; droops dramatically when thirsty but perks back up
- **Why it survives:** Tells you when it needs water—no guesswork

</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Quick Tips for Success">
<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the right pot" status="done">Drainage holes are essential—soggy roots kill more plants than drought.</Step>
<Step title="Water less, not more" status="done">When in doubt, wait another week. Most of these plants prefer dry soil.</Step>
<Step title="Find your light" status="done">Even low-light plants need some indirect light; a north-facing window works.</Step>
<Step title="Ignore them sometimes" status="done">These plants genuinely thrive on occasional neglect.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Common Mistakes to Avoid">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Overwatering is the #1 killer">
Most "unkillable" plants die from too much water, not too little. Always check the soil is dry before watering.
</Callout>
</Card>
