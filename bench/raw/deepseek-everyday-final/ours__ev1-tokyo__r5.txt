# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A balanced itinerary mixing iconic sights, neighborhood wandering, great food, and a bit of romance. Designed for a couple who wants to experience the city's energy without feeling rushed.

<Card title="Day-by-Day Plan">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 – Arrival & Shinjuku">

**Theme: Settle in, neon lights, first izakaya**

- **Afternoon:** Check into your hotel in Shinjuku. Drop bags, grab a quick lunch at a depachika (department store food hall) in Takashimaya Times Square.
- **Late afternoon:** Walk around **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** (¥500 entry; closed Mondays) — a peaceful green escape with Japanese, French, and English garden styles.
- **Evening:** Explore **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane), a narrow alley of tiny yakitori stalls. Then head to the top of the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** — free observation deck with views of Mt. Fuji on clear days.
- **Nightcap:** Golden Gai for a drink in a tiny, atmospheric bar (some charge cover fees; look for ones without).

<Callout tone="info" title="Jet lag tip">If you arrive early, skip the garden and go straight to the observation deck — it's easier on tired legs. Save the garden for a fresher day.</Callout>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2 – Asakusa & Sumida River">

**Theme: Old Tokyo, temples, river views**

- **Morning:** Start at **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa, Tokyo's oldest temple. Walk through the Kaminarimon Gate and Nakamise-dori shopping street (try freshly baked *senbei* rice crackers).
- **Late morning:** Stroll down **Nakamise-dori** to the temple, then wander the quieter side streets for coffee and vintage shopping.
- **Lunch:** Try *monjayaki* (savory pancake) in Tsukishima, or *unagi* (eel) at a riverside restaurant.
- **Afternoon:** Take the **Tokyo Cruise** water bus (about 40 min) down the Sumida River to Hinode Pier — great views of the city skyline from the water.
- **Evening:** Walk across **Kachidoki Bridge** or take the train to **Tokyo Skytree** for sunset views. Book sunset time slots in advance.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 3 – Shibuya, Harajuku & Omotesando">

**Theme: Youth culture, fashion, and a bit of chaos**

- **Morning:** Start at **Meiji Shrine**, a serene Shinto shrine in a forest right in the city. Arrive early to avoid crowds.
- **Mid-morning:** Walk down **Takeshita Street** in Harajuku — colorful, crowded, and full of crepes and quirky shops.
- **Lunch:** Head to **Omotesando** for a calmer vibe. Try a sit-down restaurant or a fancy *kaiten* (conveyor belt) sushi spot.
- **Afternoon:** Explore the backstreets of **Ura-Harajuku** for independent designers and vintage stores.
- **Evening:** Experience the iconic **Shibuya Crossing** at dusk — go up to the Starbucks or Shibuya Sky for the best view. Then have dinner in a *izakaya* or *yakiniku* (grilled meat) place.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Crowd alert">Shibuya Crossing is busiest around 5–7 PM. If crowds stress you out, visit at 8 AM instead — it's still iconic but far more pleasant.</Callout>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 4 – Day Trip: Hakone or Kamakura">

**Theme: Nature, hot springs, or coastal temples**

Choose one — both are about 1–1.5 hours from central Tokyo.

#### Option A: Hakone (Mount Fuji & Onsen)
- **Morning:** Take the Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (about 85 min). Ride the **Hakone Ropeway** for views of Mt. Fuji and volcanic Owakudani valley.
- **Afternoon:** Cruise across **Lake Ashi** on a pirate ship. Visit the **Hakone Shrine** with its iconic red torii gate in the water.
- **Evening:** Soak in an **onsen** (hot spring) — book a day-use bath at a ryokan or public bath. Return to Tokyo by evening.

#### Option B: Kamakura (Temples & Coast)
- **Morning:** Take the JR Yokosuka Line from Tokyo Station (about 1 hour). Visit the **Great Buddha** at Kotoku-in.
- **Afternoon:** Walk the **Komachi-dori** shopping street, then visit **Hase-dera Temple** for ocean views. End at **Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine**.
- **Evening:** If time allows, take the Enoden train to **Enoshima Island** for sunset. Return to Tokyo.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 5 – Art, Markets & Departure">

**Theme: Slow morning, last-minute finds**

- **Morning:** Visit **teamLab Planets** (book tickets in advance) — an immersive digital art museum in Toyosu. It's a sensory, romantic experience.
- **Lunch:** Head to **Tsukiji Outer Market** for fresh sushi, tamago (egg) skewers, and street food. It's touristy but fun.
- **Afternoon:** Last-minute shopping in **Ginza** — high-end stores, or browse **Uniqlo** and **Muji** flagship stores. Pick up souvenirs at a department store basement.
- **Evening:** If your flight allows, have a farewell dinner at an *omakase* sushi bar or a *kaiseki* restaurant. Or grab a final bowl of ramen before heading to the airport.

</Tab>

<Card title="Practical Tips">
<Columns>
<div>
#### Getting Around
- Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** — tap on/off trains and buses, and pay at convenience stores.
- The **Tokyo Metro 24/48/72-hour ticket** is great value if you're staying central.
- Trains run from ~5 AM to midnight. After that, taxis are expensive — plan accordingly.

</div>
<div>
#### Etiquette & Logistics
- Carry cash — many small shops and restaurants don't take cards.
- Tipping is not customary — service is included.
- Learn a few phrases: *arigato* (thank you), *sumimasen* (excuse me/sorry).
- Keep noise down on trains; phone calls are discouraged.

</div>
