## Day-by-Day Plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Get oriented, neon lights, robot restaurant area">
- Land at Narita or Haneda, take the Narita Express or Limousine Bus to your hotel in Shinjuku.
- Check in, then walk around **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** (if time allows).
- Evening: **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane) for yakitori and beer in a nostalgic alley.
- Night: **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** observation deck — free views of the city lights.
</Step>

<Step title="Day 2: Asakusa & Sumida River" status="current" subtitle="Old Tokyo, temples, river cruise">
- Morning: **Senso-ji Temple** and **Nakamise-dori** shopping street. Try freshly baked *ningyo-yaki* (stuffed cakes).
- Walk to **Kappabashi** kitchenware street (great for souvenirs).
- Afternoon: **Sumida River cruise** to Hamarikyu Gardens — a lovely ride with skyline views.
- Evening: **Tokyo Skytree** at sunset for a 360° view.
</Step>

<Step title="Day 3: Meiji Shrine, Harajuku & Shibuya" status="todo" subtitle="Nature, fashion, crossing">
- Morning: **Meiji Jingu** shrine, serene forest in the city.
- Walk through **Takeshita Street** in Harajuku for quirky fashion and crepes.
- Lunch: **Uobei Sushi** — fun conveyor-belt sushi with tablets.
- Afternoon: **Shibuya Crossing** — the famous scramble. Visit **Shibuya Sky** for a rooftop view.
- Evening: **Nakameguro** — canal-side cafés and a romantic stroll.
</Step>

<Step title="Day 4: Day Trip to Hakone or Nikko" status="todo" subtitle="Onsen or UNESCO temples">
**Option A: Hakone (classic)**
- **Hakone Open-Air Museum** (sculpture garden with Fuji views), **Owakudani** volcanic valley, **Lake Ashi** cruise, and an onsen (hot spring) soak.
- Return to Tokyo by evening.

**Option B: Nikko (cultural)**
- **Toshogu Shrine** (lavish mausoleum), **Kegon Falls**, and **Lake Chuzenji**.
- A longer day but worth it for history lovers.
</Step>

<Step title="Day 5: Ginza, Tsukiji & Departure" status="todo" subtitle="Shopping, sushi, last tastes">
- Morning: **Tsukiji Outer Market** — fresh sushi breakfast, tamago (egg) skewers, and matcha.
- Stroll through **Ginza** — high-end shops, art galleries, and the **Kabuki-za** theater (exterior).
- Last-minute shopping at **Don Quijote** for souvenirs.
- Head to the airport with time to spare.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Money & Practical Tips

<Callout tone="info" title="Transport">
Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** (reloadable) for trains and convenience stores. The **Tokyo Metro 24/48/72-hour pass** is great for subway-heavy days.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Cash is still king">
Many smaller restaurants and shops only take cash. Carry ¥10,000–¥20,000 per person for the day.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="success" title="Etiquette">
- Bow slightly when greeting.
- Don't tip — it's not customary.
- Eat quietly on trains; phone on silent.
- Remove shoes when entering traditional restaurants or homes.
</Callout>

## Final Tips

<Card title="Make it romantic">
- **Night view**: Shibuya Sky at sunset, or the free Tokyo Metropolitan Building.
- **Quiet moment**: Hamarikyu Gardens tea house, or Shinjuku Gyoen in the morning.
- **Special dinner**: A teppanyaki or kaiseki meal at a high-end hotel.
</Card>

<Card title="If you have extra time">
- **TeamLab Planets** — immersive digital art (book in advance).
- **Ghibli Museum** — magical, but tickets sell out a month ahead.
- **Odaiba** — seaside island with shopping and a giant Gundam statue.
</Card>
