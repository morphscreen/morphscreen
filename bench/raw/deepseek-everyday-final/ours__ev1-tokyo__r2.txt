# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A balanced itinerary covering iconic sights, hidden gems, great food, and romantic moments — paced for a couple who want to experience the city without rushing.

<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Pick up a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at the airport — it works on all trains, subways, and buses, and even at convenience stores. Most days you'll spend ¥1,000–1,500 per person on transport.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1 — Arrival & Shinjuku Nights">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check in & lunch near your hotel" status="done">
Drop bags in Shinjuku. Grab an early lunch at a *ramen* shop like **Ichiran** or **Afuri** (both have English menus).
</Step>
<Step title="Meiji Shrine & Harajuku" status="done">
Take the JR Yamanote line to Harajuku. Walk through the forested **Meiji Shrine** (free, closes at sunset), then wander **Takeshita Street** for crepes and quirky shops.
</Step>
<Step title="Shibuya Crossing & dinner" status="done">
Walk down **Cat Street** to Shibuya. See the famous scramble crossing from the **Shibuya Sky** observation deck (book sunset slot in advance, ¥2,000 each). Dinner at **Uobei Shibuya** for fun conveyor-belt sushi.
</Step>
<Step title="Golden Gai bar hop" status="done">
End the night in **Golden Gai** — tiny alley bars with 6–10 seats each. Try **Bar Albatross** for its three-floor retro vibe. Two drinks and you'll be ready to sleep.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 2 — Asakusa & Sumida River">
<Steps>
<Step title="Senso-ji Temple early" status="done">
Arrive at **Senso-ji** by 8:30 AM to beat crowds. Walk through the **Kaminarimon Gate**, browse **Nakamise-dori** for souvenirs, and draw a fortune (*omikuji*) at the temple.
</Step>
<Step title="Kimono photos & river cruise" status="done">
Rent kimonos from **Tokyo Kimono Rental Asakusa** (~¥5,000/person for the day). Take the **Sumida River cruise** (¥800) from Asakusa to **Hamarikyu Gardens** — a beautiful 40-minute ride.
</Step>
<Step title="Tsukiji Outer Market lunch" status="done">
From Hamarikyu, walk 15 minutes to **Tsukiji Outer Market**. Share a fresh **tamago** (egg omelette on a stick), **uni** (sea urchin) and grilled scallops. Go before 2 PM when stalls close.
</Step>
<Step title="Ginza & Tokyo Tower at night" status="done">
Walk through **Ginza** to see the high-end shops and the **Kabukiza Theatre** facade. Take the subway to **Tokyo Tower** — the **Main Deck** (¥1,200) has great night views, less crowded than Skytree.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 3 — Day Trip to Hakone (Mt. Fuji Views)">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Check the weather">
Mt. Fuji is only visible on clear days. If the forecast is cloudy, swap this day with Day 4 — or stay in Tokyo and do a food tour instead.
</Callout>
<Steps>
<Step title="Romancecar to Hakone" status="done">
Take the **Odakyu Romancecar** from Shinjuku (85 min, ~¥2,400 one-way). Book the **Hakone Free Pass** (¥6,100) which covers the train, cable car, ropeway, and boat.
</Step>
<Step title="Hakone Open-Air Museum" status="done">
Get off at **Chokoku-no-Mori Station**. The museum has 120 sculptures outdoors with Mt. Fuji in the background. The **Picasso Pavilion** and the **Cloudscape** net installation are highlights. (¥1,600)
</Step>
<Step title="Ropeway & Lake Ashi" status="done">
Take the **Hakone Ropeway** over volcanic **Owakudani** valley — try the black eggs (¥500 for 5). Continue to **Togendai** and board the **pirate ship** across **Lake Ashi**, with Fuji views on clear days.
</Step>
<Step title="Onsen & return" status="done">
Before heading back, soak in **Hakone Yumoto Onsen** — try **Tenzan** (¥1,500), a beautiful outdoor bath in a bamboo grove. Return to Shinjuku by 9 PM.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 4 — TeamLab & Ueno Culture">
<Steps>
<Step title="TeamLab Planets (book 9:30 AM)" status="done">
The immersive digital art museum in Toyosu takes 60–90 minutes. You'll walk through shallow water and a garden of glowing spheres. Book tickets online 2–3 weeks ahead (¥3,200 each).
</Step>
<Step title="Ueno Park & museums" status="done">
Take the subway to Ueno. Picnic under cherry trees or in the shade, then visit either the **Tokyo National Museum** (¥1,000, best for samurai and ukiyo-e) or the **National Museum of Nature and Science** (¥630, with the famous Hachiko statue).
</Step>
<Step title="Ameyoko market street" status="done">
Walk to **Ameyoko** — a lively market street under the train tracks. Try **fruit daifuku** (mochi with fresh fruit) and **yakitori** skewers. It's also great for bargain sneakers and cosmetics.
</Step>
<Step title="Rooftop drinks in Akihabara" status="done">
End the day in Akihabara — even if you're not into anime, the neon streets are fun. Grab cocktails at the **MAAYA Sky Lounge** (14th floor of the Akihabara Washington Hotel) for views over the electric town.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Day 5 — Yanaka, Kagurazaka & Farewell Dinner">
<Steps>
<Step title="Yanaka Ginza morning stroll" status="done">
Take the train to Nippori and walk to **Yanaka Ginza** — a charming old shopping street with cats, small temples, and retro snacks. Try **menchi-katsu** (fried meat croquette) from the famous Satou shop.
</Step>
<Step title="Yanaka Cemetery & Nezu Shrine" status="done">
Walk through the peaceful **Yanaka Cemetery** (cherry blossoms in spring) to **Nezu Shrine** — a vermilion torii tunnel that rivals Fushimi Inari without the crowds. Free entry.
</Step>
<Step title="Kagurazaka lunch & canals" status="done">
Take the Chuo line to **Kagurazaka** — a former geisha district with cobblestone lanes and French bakeries. Lunch at a small *izakaya* or **Kado** for handmade soba noodles.
</Step>
<Step title="Farewell dinner in Shinjuku" status="done">
Back to Shinjuku for a final dinner. Splurge at **Nakajima** (Michelin-starred sardine cuisine, ~¥5,000/person) or keep it casual with *okonomiyaki* at **Kiji** in Lumine department store. End with a nightcap at the **Park Hyatt's New York Bar** — the *Lost in Translation* spot.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Budget for two (per person)">
| Category | Cost (¥) |
|---|---|
| Accommodation (4 nights, mid-range) | 40,000–60,000 |
| Transport (IC card + day trips) | 15,000–20,000 |
| Food (3 meals + snacks) | 20,000–30,000 |
| Attractions & experiences | 12,000–18,000 |
| **Total per person** | **87,000–128,000** |

<Callout tone="success" title="Money-saving tips">
- Book TeamLab Planets and Shibuya Sky at least 2 weeks ahead to skip queues and get better prices.
- Lunch sets are half the price of dinner at most restaurants — plan big meals for lunch.
- Convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart) have excellent onigiri, egg sandwiches, and coffee for a cheap breakfast.
</Callout>
</Card>
