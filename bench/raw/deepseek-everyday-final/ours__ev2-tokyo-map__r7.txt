# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two

A balanced itinerary covering modern sights, historic temples, great food, and romantic evenings — all mapped out for easy navigation.

<Card title="Your 5-Day Tokyo Itinerary">
<Steps>
<Step title="Day 1: Arrival & Shibuya/Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Neon lights, crossing, and skyline views">
Land, drop bags, and dive into the city's electric heart. Start at **Shibuya Crossing**, grab lunch in the backstreets, then head to **Shinjuku** for golden-hour views from the Metropolitan Government Building (free!) and dinner in Omoide Yokocho.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 2: Asakusa & Sumida River" status="done" subtitle="Senso-ji, river cruise, and Skytree">
Morning at **Senso-ji Temple** and Nakamise-dori street food. Take a **Sumida River cruise** to Hamarikyu Gardens, then end the day at **Tokyo Skytree** for sunset views.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 3: Ueno & Akihabara" status="done" subtitle="Museums, parks, and otaku culture">
Explore **Ueno Park** and its museums (the National Museum is unmissable). Afternoon in **Akihabara** for electronics, anime, and themed cafés. Evening: izakaya hopping in Ueno.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 4: Harajuku & Omotesando" status="done" subtitle="Meiji Shrine, Takeshita Street, and chic shopping">
Quiet morning at **Meiji Shrine**, then the whirlwind of **Takeshita Street**. Stroll down **Omotesando** for architecture and cafés. Sunset at **Shibuya Sky** for a 360° view.
</Step>
<Step title="Day 5: Day Trip or TeamLab & Departure" status="done" subtitle="Flexible finale">
Either a **day trip to Kamakura** (Great Buddha, bamboo forest) or stay in the city: **teamLab Planets** in the morning, souvenir shopping in Ginza, then depart.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Evening Ideas for Two">
- **Golden Gai** (Shinjuku): Tiny bars, one drink each, and the neon maze — romantic and quirky.
- **Yakatabune cruise** (Sumida River): A traditional boat dinner with lantern light.
- **Odaiba seaside**: Sunset walk on the beach with the Rainbow Bridge lit up.
- **Karaoke** in Shibuya: Private rooms, silly duets, and late-night ramen after.

<Callout tone="success" title="Pro tip">Book teamLab Planets and Shibuya Sky tickets online a few days ahead — they sell out, especially on weekends.</Callout>
</Card>
