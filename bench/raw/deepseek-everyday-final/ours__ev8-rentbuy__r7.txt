## Key differences beyond money

<Card title="Renting vs buying — what you give up and gain">
<Compare title="Rent vs buy">
| Factor | Renting | Buying |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cost | £1,800 fixed | ~£1,700 variable |
| Maintenance | None — landlord handles it | You pay for everything |
| Flexibility | Move with 1–2 months notice | 3–6 months to sell |
| Pets & decor | Often restricted | Free to do what you like |
| Equity | None — money gone | Builds wealth over time |
| Interest rates | No exposure | Rates can rise |

| Stamp duty | None | £0 first-time buyer under £425k (England) |

## What the experts say

<Card title="The 5% rule">
A common rule of thumb: **buying beats renting when your rent is more than 5% of the property price per year**.

| Scenario | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Your rent | £1,800 × 12 = £21,600/yr |
| 5% of £300k | £15,000/yr |
| Your rent as % of price | **7.2%** |

<Callout tone="success" title="Your rent is high relative to prices">At 7.2%, you're well above the 5% threshold — a strong signal that **buying makes financial sense** if you can manage the deposit and plan to stay put.</Callout>
</Card>

## Your decision checklist

<Card title="Should you buy? Answer these first">
<Checklist title="Before you commit" name="buy-checklist">
- [ ] Can I put down at least 10% deposit (£30k) without emptying emergency savings?
- [ ] Do I plan to stay in this city for 3+ years?
- [ ] Am I comfortable with interest rate rises (stress test at 7–8%)?
- [ ] Have I checked leasehold terms (service charge, ground rent, remaining lease)?
- [ ] Have I budgeted £3–5k for moving, solicitor, survey, and furnishing?
- [ ] Is my credit score good enough for the best mortgage rates?
- [ ] Am I emotionally ready for homeownership responsibility?
</Checklist>
</Card>
