<Card title="Practical Tips for Two">
<Columns>
<Card title="Getting Around">
- **Suica/Pasmo IC card** — tap on/off trains & buses; buy at any station.
- **Tokyo Metro & JR lines** — most sights are within 30 min rides.
- **Walk a lot** — comfortable shoes are essential.
</Card>
<Card title="Food & Reservations">
- **Sushi at Tsukiji** — go before 8am to avoid lines.
- **TeamLab Planets** — book 2–3 weeks ahead online.
- **Izakaya in Omoide Yokocho** — arrive by 6pm or wait.
</Card>
</Columns>
<Callout tone="info" title="Budget-Friendly Tip">Use the **Tokyo Subway Ticket** (24/48/72h) for unlimited rides — covers most tourist spots.</Callout>
</Card>
