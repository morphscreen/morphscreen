## Day 1: Classic Tokyo — Shinjuku, Shibuya & Harajuku

<Card title="Day 1 Overview">
<Columns>
<div>
**Theme:** Iconic Tokyo energy  
**Neighborhoods:** Shinjuku, Shibuya, Harajuku  
**Total walking:** ~10 km (easy pace)
</div>
<div>
**Highlight:** Sunset from the free Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku & Meiji Shrine" status="done">
Start at **Shinjuku Station** (West Exit). Walk to the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** (free observation deck, 45th floor) for a panoramic city view. Then take the JR Yamanote Line to **Harajuku Station** and walk to **Meiji Shrine** — a peaceful forested sanctuary right in the city. Arrive early to see the torii gates before crowds.
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Harajuku" status="done">
Explore **Takeshita Street** for quirky crepes and street food. For a proper meal, try **Ichiran Ramen** (solo booth dining — a fun experience for two) or a cozy udon spot nearby.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya" status="done">
Walk down **Omotesando** (tree-lined avenue with high-end shops). End at **Shibuya Crossing** — the world's busiest intersection. Watch the scramble from the **Starbucks** second floor or the new **Shibuya Sky** observation deck (book ahead).
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done">
Back in Shinjuku, dive into **Omoide Yokocho** ("Memory Lane") — tiny yakitori alleys glowing with lanterns. Then explore **Golden Gai**, a maze of 200+ micro-bars. Find a cozy two-seat bar for a nightcap.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 2: Old & New — Asakusa, Ueno & Akihabara

<Card title="Day 2 Overview">
<Columns>
<div>
**Theme:** Tradition meets tech  
**Neighborhoods:** Asakusa, Ueno, Akihabara  
**Total walking:** ~8 km
</div>
<div>
**Highlight:** Senso-ji at golden hour
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="done">
Take the **Tokyo Metro Ginza Line** to Asakusa. Enter through the **Kaminarimon Gate** (huge red lantern), stroll **Nakamise-dori** (shopping street with traditional snacks), and reach **Senso-ji**, Tokyo's oldest temple. Try a fortune ("omikuji") together.
</Step>

<Step title="Midday: Tokyo Skytree & Sumida River" status="done">
Walk along the **Sumida River** to **Tokyo Skytree** (634 m). Go up to the **Tembo Deck** (350 m) for a breathtaking view. Have lunch at **Solamachi** complex at its base (try Tokyo Banana or matcha soft serve).
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Ueno Park & Ameyoko" status="done">
Take the Tobu Skytree Line back to **Ueno**. Wander **Ueno Park** (cherry blossoms in spring, museums, and a zoo). Explore the nearby **Ameyoko** market street — a lively mix of food stalls, spices, and street vendors.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Akihabara Electric Town" status="done">
Take the JR Yamanote Line to **Akihabara**. Explore multi-story arcades, anime shops, and electronics stores. For dinner, try an **izakaya** with a themed twist (e.g., Gundam cafe or maid cafe if you're adventurous).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 3: Waterfront & Markets — Tsukiji, Ginza & Odaiba

<Card title="Day 3 Overview">
<Columns>
<div>
**Theme:** Markets, gardens, and bay views  
**Neighborhoods:** Tsukiji, Ginza, Odaiba  
**Total walking:** ~9 km (plus ferry)
</div>
<div>
**Highlight:** Fresh sushi breakfast and sunset at Odaiba
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done">
Start early (7–8 AM) at **Tsukiji Outer Market** — the inner market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market still has 400+ stalls. Share a **fresh sushi breakfast** at one of the tiny counters, try tamagoyaki (grilled egg), and sample grilled scallops.
</Step>

<Step title="Late Morning: Hamarikyu Gardens" status="done">
Walk 10 minutes to **Hamarikyu Gardens** — a beautiful tidal garden with a teahouse on an island in a pond. Enjoy matcha and a sweet while overlooking skyscrapers. Take a **water bus** from here to Odaiba (40 min, scenic).
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Odaiba" status="done">
Explore **Odaiba** — a man-made island with the **teamLab Borderless** digital art museum (book tickets weeks ahead!), the **Statue of Liberty replica**, and **DiverCity** mall with its giant Gundam statue. Walk along the beach promenade.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Ginza" status="done">
Take the Yurikamome Line back to **Ginza**. Window-shop along **Chuo-dori** (closed to traffic on weekends), see the **Kabukiza Theatre** facade, and have dinner at a high-end sushi or teppanyaki restaurant. End with a cocktail at a rooftop bar.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 4: Studio Ghibli & Kichijoji — A Whimsical Day

<Card title="Day 4 Overview">
<Columns>
<div>
**Theme:** Art, animation, and cozy vibes  
**Neighborhoods:** Mitaka, Kichijoji  
**Total walking:** ~6 km
</div>
<div>
**Highlight:** Ghibli Museum (book tickets 1 month ahead!)
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Book in advance">
Ghibli Museum tickets sell out quickly. Reserve them online **exactly 1 month before** your visit (10 AM Japan time on the 10th of each month). You'll need your passport for pickup at Lawson convenience stores.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Ghibli Museum" status="done">
Take the JR Chuo Line to **Mitaka Station**, then the shuttle bus (or a 15-min walk through **Inokashira Park**) to the **Ghibli Museum**. Explore the whimsical exhibits, the catbus room (kids only!), and the rooftop **Robot from Laputa**. Don't miss the short exclusive film in the Saturn Theater.
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Kichijoji" status="done">
Walk through **Inokashira Park** (rent a swan boat on the pond) to **Kichijoji**, consistently voted Tokyo's most desirable neighborhood. Try **Satou**'s famous melt-in-your-mouth beef croquettes, or a cozy curry shop.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Kichijoji Shopping & Harmonica Yokocho" status="done">
Explore **Sun Road** shopping arcade and the charming **Harmonica Yokocho** — a warren of tiny bars and eateries under the train tracks. Stop for a craft beer or a pour-over coffee.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Back to Shinjuku" status="done">
Return to Shinjuku for a farewell dinner. Try **Ishigamaya** for stone-grilled steak, or **Nakajima** for a legendary sardine set meal (Michelin-starred, under ¥1,000 at lunch). End with a night view from **Park Hyatt's Peak Bar** (from *Lost in Translation*).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 5: Nature Escape — Mt. Takao

<Card title="Day 5 Overview">
<Columns>
<div>
**Theme:** Fresh air and mountain views  
**Neighborhood:** Takaosan (1 hour from Shinjuku)  
**Total walking:** ~8 km (optional hike)
</div>
<div>
**Highlight:** Mount Fuji view from the summit
</div>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Cable Car & Hike" status="done">
Take the **Keio Line** from Shinjuku to **Takaosanguchi Station** (about 50 min). Ride the **cable car** or **chairlift** up the mountain. Then hike 40–60 minutes to the **summit** (599 m) through cedar forests and past the **Yakuo-in Temple** complex with its giant statues.
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Mountain Top" status="done">
On a clear day, enjoy a **panoramic view of Mount Fuji** from the summit. Try the local specialty — **tengu soba** (buckwheat noodles) or grilled dango (rice dumplings) at a mountain hut.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Descent & Onsen" status="done">
Descend via a different trail (the **Biwadaira** route is gentle and scenic). At the base, visit **Takao Onsen** for a relaxing soak in natural hot springs — the perfect way to end the trip.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Farewell Dinner" status="done">
Return to Shinjuku. For a memorable last meal, try **robot restaurant** (a wild, kitschy show — book ahead), or keep it classic with **shabu-shabu** at a cozy restaurant in **Omoide Yokocho**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Practical Tips for Two

<Card title="Getting Around">
<KeyValue title="Transport essentials">
| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Suica/Pasmo card** | Get at any JR station; tap for trains, buses, and convenience stores |
| **Tokyo Metro 24/48/72h pass** | Great value if staying central; sold at tourist offices |
| **JR Yamanote Line** | Circular loop connecting most major areas; easiest for beginners |
| **Taxis** | Expensive but useful late at night; use the **GO** app |
