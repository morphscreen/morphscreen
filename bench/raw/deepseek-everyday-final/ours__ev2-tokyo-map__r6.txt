# 5 Days in Tokyo for Two — A Map-Guided Itinerary

Tokyo is a city of contrasts — ancient temples beside neon skyscrapers, serene gardens around frantic crossings. This 5-day plan balances iconic sights, hidden gems, great food, and romantic moments, all arranged by area so you spend less time commuting and more time exploring.

<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at the airport — tap on/off trains and buses, and pay at convenience stores. The **Tokyo Metro 72-hour pass** (¥1,500) covers most central routes if you plan 3+ metro rides a day.
</Callout>

<Card title="Day 1 — Shinjuku, Shibuya & Harajuku" subtitle="Classic Tokyo energy">
Start in the west side of the city, where old meets new.

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="9:00 AM">
A peaceful forested shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji. Walk the gravel path under towering cedars — a 10-minute stroll from Harajuku Station. Free entry. Try a **wooden ema** (wish plaque).
</Step>

<Step title="Late morning: Harajuku & Takeshita Street" status="done" subtitle="11:00 AM">
The epicenter of youth culture. Browse quirky shops, crepe stands, and vintage stores. Don't miss **Kiddy Land** for cute souvenirs. Grab a **crêpe at Marion Crepes** (¥500–700).
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko" status="done" subtitle="1:30 PM">
The world's busiest pedestrian crossing. Watch the scramble from the **Starbucks on the 2nd floor of QFRONT** for the best view. Visit the **Hachiko statue** — the loyal dog's meeting spot.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Shinjuku at night" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM">
**Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane) — tiny yakitori alleys with smoky grills. For a view, head to the free **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** observation deck (open until 11 PM). Dinner at **Ishigama-yaki Pizza Napolista** for wood-fired pizza, or **Kouen** for upscale Japanese.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 2 — Asakusa, Ueno & Akihabara" subtitle="Old Tokyo & otaku culture">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="9:00 AM">
Tokyo's oldest temple. Enter through the **Kaminarimon** (Thunder Gate), walk **Nakamise-dori** shopping street, and see the main hall. Try **senbei** (rice crackers) and **ningyo-yaki** (sweet filled cakes). Free.
</Step>

<Step title="Late morning: Ueno Park & Museums" status="done" subtitle="11:30 AM">
A vast park with museums. Choose: **Tokyo National Museum** (¥1,000) for samurai armor and ukiyo-e, or **Ueno Zoo** (¥600) for pandas. Grab lunch at **Innsyoutei** — a charming Japanese restaurant in the park.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Akihabara Electric Town" status="done" subtitle="2:30 PM">
The anime, manga, and electronics district. Visit **Mandarake** for retro collectibles, **Yodobashi Camera** for gadgets. Try a **maid café** experience (from ¥1,500) or **Gundam Café** for themed fun.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Sumida River walk" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM">
Walk from Asakusa along the Sumida River to see **Tokyo Skytree** light up. Dinner at **Kanda Matsuya** — a soba shop since 1884, or **Asakusa Imahan** for sukiyaki (¥5,000+).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 3 — Tsukiji, Hamarikyu & Ginza" subtitle="Food, gardens & shopping">

<Steps>
<Step title="Early morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="7:00 AM">
The inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market thrives. Try **fresh uni** (sea urchin) at **Tsukiji Sushi Dai** (arrive by 6:30 AM to queue), **tamago** (egg) at **Marutake**, and **matcha soft-serve**.
</Step>

<Step title="Late morning: Hamarikyu Gardens" status="done" subtitle="10:00 AM">
A serene landscape garden with a tidal pond and teahouse. Take a **boat ride from here to Asakusa** (¥1,200) if you want a different view. Entry ¥300.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Ginza shopping" status="done" subtitle="1:00 PM">
Tokyo's luxury district. Visit **Ginza Six** for design, **Itoya** for beautiful stationery, and **Uniqlo Ginza** (12 floors). For lunch, try **Gonpachi Nishiazabu** (the "Kill Bill" restaurant) for soba and robata.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM">
The world's tallest tower (634 m). Go up to **Tembo Deck** (¥2,100) for sunset views over the city. Book tickets online to skip the line. Dinner at **Solomon** in the Skytree complex for Japanese curry.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 4 — Shinjuku Gyoen & Nightlife" subtitle="Gardens, food & bars">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden" status="done" subtitle="9:00 AM">
One of Tokyo's most beautiful parks — Japanese, French, and English gardens in one. Perfect for a picnic or a slow stroll. Entry ¥500. **Cherry blossoms in spring** are spectacular.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Nakano Broadway" status="done" subtitle="1:00 PM">
A retro shopping mall for anime, manga, and vintage goods — less crowded than Akihabara. Great for **finds and figurines**. Lunch at **Chuka Soba Nakano** for excellent ramen.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="7:00 PM">
**Golden Gai** — over 200 tiny bars in six alleys, many seating only 5–10 people. Go early or before 10 PM to avoid lines. Try **Bar Albatross** for a cozy vibe. For dinner, **Piss Alley** (Omoide Yokocho) for yakitori and beer.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Day 5 — Odaiba & TeamLab" subtitle="Waterfront, art & views">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Odaiba Seaside" status="done" subtitle="10:00 AM">
A man-made island with a beach, shopping, and views of Rainbow Bridge. Walk the **Odaiba Seaside Park** promenade, visit **DiverCity** for the life-size **Unicorn Gundam statue**. Ride the **ferris wheel** (¥900) for panoramic views.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: teamLab Planets" status="done" subtitle="1:30 PM">
An immersive digital art museum where you walk through water and mirror rooms. **Book tickets in advance** (¥3,200). Allow 2 hours. The **floating flower garden** is unforgettable.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Rainbow Bridge & farewell dinner" status="done" subtitle="6:00 PM">
Walk or bike across **Rainbow Bridge** at sunset (free, until 9 PM). For dinner, book **Ninja Akasaka** for a theatrical ninja-themed experience (¥8,000+), or **Monsoon Café** in Odaiba for a budget-friendly curry.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Practical tips">
<Checklist title="Before you go" name="tokyo-checklist">
- [ ] Book teamLab Planets tickets online (at least 1 week ahead)
- [ ] Reserve a hotel near Shinjuku or Ueno Station (easy access to Narita/Haneda)
- [ ] Get a pocket Wi-Fi or eSIM (essential for maps & translation)
- [ ] Download Google Maps offline for Tokyo
- [ ] Bring cash — many small shops and temple stalls don't take cards
- [ ] Pack comfortable walking shoes — you'll walk 15,000+ steps daily
- [ ] Learn a few Japanese phrases: "arigato" (thank you), "sumimasen" (excuse me)
- [ ] Check the weather and pack layers — Tokyo can be humid in summer, chilly in winter
</Checklist>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Cherry blossom season">
If visiting late March–early April, book everything **2–3 months ahead**. The **Chidorigafuchi walk** (near the Imperial Palace) and **Meguro River** are spectacular but crowded. Consider adding a morning at **Ueno Park** for blossom viewing.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="Romantic touches for couples">
- **Sunset at Shibuya Sky** (¥2,000) — rooftop observation deck with 360° views
- **Night boat cruise** on the Sumida River (¥1,500–3,000) — see the city lights from the water
- **Tea ceremony** at Happo-en garden (¥3,000/person) — intimate and traditional
- **Dessert at Bills** in Omotesando (¥1,800) — famous ricotta hotcakes with a view
- **Couples photoshoot** in a rented kimono (from ¥5,000/person) — ask your hotel to arrange
