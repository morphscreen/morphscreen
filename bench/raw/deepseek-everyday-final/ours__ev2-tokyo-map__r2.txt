<Card title="Day-by-Day Plan">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1 — West Tokyo Icons">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Neon, gardens, and the busiest station on Earth">
Start at **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** — a peaceful mix of Japanese, French, and English gardens. Then wander through the neon chaos of **Kabukicho** and the **Omoide Yokocho** alley (Memory Lane) for a retro snack.
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Omoide Yokocho" status="done" subtitle="Tiny yakitori bars">
Grab skewers and beer at one of the tiny smoky stalls. Try the *tsukune* (chicken meatballs) and *negima* (leek and chicken).
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Shibuya" status="done" subtitle="The crossing, the statue, the shopping">
Cross the famous **Shibuya Scramble**, see the **Hachiko statue**, and explore **Shibuya Sky** for panoramic views. Shop at **Shibuya 109** or browse **Loft** for quirky gifts.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Harajuku & Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="From kawaii to calm">
Walk down **Takeshita Street** for crepes and crazy fashion, then enter the serene **Meiji Shrine** forest. End with dinner in **Harajuku** — try *omurice* or a trendy vegan spot.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="info" title="Tip">Buy a **Suica/Pasmo** card at the airport — it works on all trains, buses, and most convenience stores.</Callout>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 2 — Old Tokyo & Otaku Culture">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Asakusa" status="current" subtitle="Senso-ji and Nakamise">
Begin at **Senso-ji**, Tokyo’s oldest temple. Walk through the **Kaminarimon Gate** and the **Nakamise** shopping street for *senbei* (rice crackers) and *ningyo-yaki* (sweet filled cakes).
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Asakusa" status="todo" subtitle="Street food or a riverside café">
Try *monjayaki* (savory pancake) or grab *tempura* at a local spot. For a view, head to the **Asakusa Culture Tourist Information Center** rooftop — free!
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Ueno Park & Akihabara" status="todo" subtitle="Museums, pandas, and electronics">
Stroll **Ueno Park**, visit the **Tokyo National Museum** or the **Ueno Zoo** (see the pandas). Then dive into **Akihabara** — arcades, anime shops, and retro game stores.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Akihabara dinner" status="todo" subtitle="Izakaya or maid café?">
Choose a themed café or a classic *izakaya*. For a quieter end, explore **Kanda Shrine** nearby, lit up at night.
</Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 3 — Ginza, Tsukiji & Tokyo Tower">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="todo" subtitle="The freshest sushi breakfast">
Arrive early (7–8 am) for **tamagoyaki** (egg omelet), fresh *uni* (sea urchin), and a sushi breakfast. Watch tuna cutting if you're lucky.
</Step>

<Step title="Late Morning: Ginza" status="todo" subtitle="Chic shopping and art">
Wander **Ginza**’s flagship stores — **Uniqlo**, **Muji**, and **Dover Street Market**. Check out the **Ginza Six** rooftop garden.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Tokyo Tower & Roppongi" status="todo" subtitle="Retro views and nightlife">
Visit **Tokyo Tower** (the iconic red tower) for views, then head to **Roppongi Hills** for the **Mori Art Museum** and the **Tokyo City View** observation deck at sunset.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Roppongi dinner" status="todo" subtitle="International eats">
Roppongi has everything from sushi to Italian. Try a **robatayaki** (open-fire grill) for a theatrical dinner.
</Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 4 — Futuristic Tokyo & Odaiba">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: TeamLab Planets" status="todo" subtitle="Immersive digital art">
Book tickets in advance for **teamLab Planets** in Toyosu — walk through water and light installations. Mind-blowing and romantic.
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Odaiba" status="todo" subtitle="Bay views and food courts">
Cross the **Rainbow Bridge** to **Odaiba**. Eat at **Aqua City** or **DiverCity** — try *okonomiyaki* or ramen.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Odaiba sights" status="todo" subtitle="Gundam, shopping, and a giant wheel">
See the life-size **Gundam** statue, shop at **VenusFort** (a mall styled like 18th-century Europe), and ride the **Palette Town Ferris Wheel**.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Skytree" status="todo" subtitle="Sunset from the tallest tower">
Take the train to **Tokyo Skytree** for sunset views. Dine at **Solamachi** mall below — try *unagi* (eel) or *soba*.
</Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Day 5 — Laid-back West Tokyo">

<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shimokitazawa" status="todo" subtitle="Vintage shopping and coffee">
Explore **Shimokitazawa** — vintage clothing stores, indie bookshops, and excellent coffee. Have a late breakfast at a hip café.
</Step>

<Step title="Lunch: Kichijoji" status="todo" subtitle="Harmonica Yokocho">
Take the train to **Kichijoji**. Eat at **Harmonica Yokocho** — a warren of tiny food stalls. Try *korokke* (croquettes) and *ramen*.
</Step>

<Step title="Afternoon: Inokashira Park" status="todo" subtitle="Paddle boats and a shrine">
Rent a swan boat on **Inokashira Pond**, visit the small **Benzaiten Shrine**, and stroll through the forest. Perfect for a relaxed afternoon.
</Step>

<Step title="Evening: Last dinner" status="todo" subtitle="One more Tokyo feast">
Head back to **Shinjuku** or **Shibuya** for a final dinner — maybe *shabu-shabu* or a high-end sushi omakase. End with a night view from **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** (free!).
</Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>

<Card title="Practical Tips">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Book in advance">
- **teamLab Planets** — tickets sell out days ahead
- **Shibuya Sky** — book sunset slots 2–3 weeks out
- Popular restaurants (especially sushi omakase) need reservations
</Callout>

<Callout tone="info" title="Transport">
- **Suica/Pasmo** card: ¥500 deposit, reload at any station
- **Tokyo Metro 24h/48h/72h Ticket**: unlimited rides on Tokyo Metro & Toei lines — great value for Days 1–3
- **JR Pass** only worth it if you take day trips (e.g., Nikko, Kamakura)
</Callout>

<Callout tone="success" title="Couple-friendly extras">
- **Gondola at Inokashira Park** — romantic sunset ride
- **Night view from Tokyo Metropolitan Building** — free and quiet
- **Rooftop bars** in Shibuya or Roppongi for a special night
</Callout>
