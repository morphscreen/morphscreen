# Houseplants That Are Hard to Kill

Looking for greenery that survives neglect, low light, and erratic watering? These plants are practically indestructible — perfect for beginners or busy people.

<Card title="The Top 7 Unkillable Houseplants">
<Columns>
<Card title="Snake Plant">
- Thrives in low light and bright light alike
- Water every 2–3 weeks; tolerates drought
- Purifies air (NASA study)
- Height: 1–4 ft
</Card>
<Card title="Pothos (Devil's Ivy)">
- Grows in almost any light, even fluorescent
- Water when leaves droop slightly
- Vining; easy to propagate in water
- Height: trailing up to 10 ft
</Card>
<Card title="ZZ Plant">
- Survives weeks without water
- Tolerates very low light
- Glossy, waxy leaves
- Height: 2–3 ft
</Card>
</Columns>
<Columns>
<Card title="Spider Plant">
- Adaptable to most conditions
- Water when soil dries out
- Produces baby "spiderettes" to share
- Height: 1–2 ft
</Card>
<Card title="Aloe Vera">
- Needs bright light, but forgiving
- Water deeply, then let dry completely
- Medicinal gel in leaves
- Height: 1–2 ft
</Card>
<Card title="Jade Plant">
- Succulent; stores water in leaves
- Bright light preferred, tolerates medium
- Water every 2–3 weeks
- Height: up to 3 ft
</Card>
<Card title="Peace Lily">
- Wilt visibly when thirsty — easy to read
- Low to medium light
- White blooms in spring
- Height: 1–4 ft
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="Common Mistakes (Even With Hardy Plants)">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Overwatering kills more than neglect">
The #1 cause of death for these plants is too much water. Always check soil moisture before watering — when in doubt, wait.
</Callout>
- **No drainage holes** — roots rot in standing water
- **Cold drafts** — keep away from AC vents and icy windows
- **Sudden light changes** — move plants gradually
- **Ignoring pests** — check undersides of leaves monthly
</Card>

<Card title="First-Time Buyer Checklist">
<Checklist title="Before you buy" name="first-plant">
- [ ] Check for drainage holes in the pot
- [ ] Choose a pot 1–2 inches larger than the root ball
- [ ] Buy well-draining potting mix (cactus mix for succulents)
- [ ] Place near a window but out of direct afternoon sun
- [ ] Water only when the top 2 inches of soil are dry
- [ ] Learn the plant's specific light needs
</Checklist>
</Card>

<Card title="Pro Tip: Propagation">
<Callout tone="info" title="Free plants forever">
Pothos and spider plants root easily in water. Cut a stem with 2–3 leaves, place in a jar of water, and in 2 weeks you'll have roots — then pot it up. One plant becomes many, and you can share with friends.
</Callout>
</Card>
