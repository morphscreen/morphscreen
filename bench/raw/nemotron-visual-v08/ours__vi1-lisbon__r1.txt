<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1: Belém & Riverside">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 – Pastéis de Belém" status="done" subtitle="Try the original custard tarts">
Warm pastéis de nata with cinnamon and powdered sugar. Arrive early to avoid the queue.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Jerónimos Monastery" status="done" subtitle="UNESCO Manueline masterpiece">
Allow 1.5 h for the church and cloisters. Buy a combined ticket with Belém Tower.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Belém Tower" status="done" subtitle="Iconic riverside fortress">
Climb the narrow stairs for Tagus views. Combined ticket €13.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch at Ponto Final (Almada)" status="done" subtitle="Ferry across the river">
Take the ferry from Belém to Cacilhas (€1.50), short walk to this riverside restaurant with Lisbon skyline views.
</Step>
<Step title="15:00 – MAAT or Coach Museum" status="todo" subtitle="Modern art or royal carriages">
MAAT for contemporary art & architecture, or Museu dos Coches for gilded carriages.
</Step>
<Step title="18:00 – Sunset at Padrão dos Descobrimentos" status="todo" subtitle="Monument to the Discoveries">
Elevator to the top for panoramic river views. €6.
</Step>
<Step title="20:00 – Dinner in Belém" status="todo" subtitle="Taberna dos Mercadores or local tasca">
Book ahead for Taberna dos Mercadores (small, excellent petiscos).
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2: Alfama, Castle & Mouraria">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:00 – Tram 28 to Martim Moniz" status="todo" subtitle="Classic yellow tram ride">
Board early at Praça Martim Moniz; pickpocket alert. Ride to Graça or walk up.
</Step>
<Step title="10:00 – Miradouro da Senhora do Monte" status="todo" subtitle="Best panoramic viewpoint">
Quieter than Santa Luzia, sweeping city views.
</Step>
<Step title="11:00 – São Jorge Castle" status="todo" subtitle="Moorish castle & ramparts">
€10. Explore towers, archaeological site, and peacocks in the gardens.
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch in Mouraria" status="todo" subtitle="Zé dos Cornos or Tasca do Chico">
Mouraria is the birthplace of fado; try bifana (pork sandwich) or grilled sardines.
</Step>
<Step title="14:30 – Fado Museum & Alfama wander" status="todo" subtitle="Lose yourself in the alleys">
Visit Museu do Fado (€5) or simply explore Rua dos Remédios, São Miguel, and hidden miradouros.
</Step>
<Step title="17:00 – Miradouro de Santa Luzia" status="todo" subtitle="Azalea garden & river view">
Perfect for late-afternoon photos.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Fado dinner" status="todo" subtitle="Clube de Fado or Mesa de Frades">
Book a table with live fado; expect €40–60 pp for dinner + show.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3: Baixa, Chiado & Modern Lisbon">
<Steps>
<Step title="09:30 – Praça do Comércio & Rua Augusta Arch" status="todo" subtitle="Grand riverside square">
Elevator to the top of the arch (€3) for Baixa grid views.
</Step>
<Step title="10:30 – Elevador de Santa Justa" status="todo" subtitle="Neo-Gothic lift to Carmo">
€5.30 round-trip; or walk up via Rua do Carmo to avoid queues.
</Step>
<Step title="11:30 – Carmo Convent & Chiado" status="todo" subtitle="Ruined church & literary cafés">
Visit the archaeological museum in the roofless nave. Coffee at A Brasileira (Fernando Pessoa statue).
</Step>
<Step title="13:00 – Lunch at Time Out Market" status="todo" subtitle="Curated food hall, 30+ stalls">
Try Marisqueira Azul (seafood), Croquetaria (croquettes), or Nuno Mendes’ petiscos. Busy at peak.
</Step>
<Step title="14:30 – LX Factory & Ler Devagar" status="todo" subtitle="Creative hub under the bridge">
Tram 15 or bus 728. Bookshop Ler Devagar in an old printing press; street art, shops, brunch spots.
</Step>
<Step title="17:00 – Pink Street (Rua Nova do Carvalho)" status="todo" subtitle="Sunset drinks by the river">
Former red-light district, now bars & clubs. Pensão Amor for quirky cocktails.
</Step>
<Step title="19:30 – Farewell dinner in Cais do Sodré" status="todo" subtitle="Seafood at Ramiro or modern at Tabik">
Ramiro (no reservations, queue early) for percebes, lobster, garlic shrimp. Tabik for tasting menu.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Tab>

<Card title="Practical tips" icon="lightbulb">
- **Transport:** Buy a **Viva Viagem** card (€0.50) and load **zapping** credit (€1.50/ride) or a **24 h pass** (€6.80) for unlimited metro/tram/bus.  
- **Airport → city:** Metro Red Line (€1.50 + card) or Aerobus (€4).  
- **Hills & cobbles:** Wear grippy shoes; Alfama and Mouraria are steep.  
- **Pickpockets:** Tram 28, elevators, and busy viewpoints — keep bags front-zipped.  
- **Reservations:** Essential for Taberna dos Mercadores, Ramiro (queue instead), fado houses, and popular dinner spots.  
- **Cash:** Many small tasca and Pastéis de Belém takeaway are cash-only.  
- **Free entry:** Most museums free **Sunday mornings until 14:00** (check each site).  
- **Luggage storage:** Lockers at Santa Apolónia station, Rossio, or apps like Bounce/LuggageHero.
</Card>
