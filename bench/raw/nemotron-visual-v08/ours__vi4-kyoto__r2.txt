# Kyoto's Best Temples — One-Day Itinerary

<Callout tone="info" title="Planning tip">Start early (8 AM) to beat crowds at Fushimi Inari. Allow 6–7 hours total including travel between sites.</Callout>

## Best Order for a Single Day

<Steps>
<Step title="7:30 AM — Fushimi Inari Taisha" status="todo" subtitle="Arrive before crowds; hike to Yotsutsuji (45 min round trip)">
Start here at dawn. The gates are most photogenic in early light. Wear comfortable shoes — the full summit loop takes 2–3 hours, but the lower sections give the iconic experience.
</Step>

<Step title="10:00 AM — Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)" status="todo" subtitle="Bus 101 or 205 from Inari Station → Kinkaku-ji (≈45 min)">
Peak brilliance around mid-morning when sun hits the gold leaf. Walk the garden circuit (20 min). Grab matcha at the tea house near the exit.
</Step>

<Step title="12:30 PM — Lunch near Kinkaku-ji" status="todo" subtitle="Try yudofu (simmered tofu) at a temple-area restaurant">
Several traditional spots along the approach road serve vegetarian Buddhist cuisine (shojin ryori).
</Step>

<Step title="2:00 PM — Kiyomizu-dera" status="todo" subtitle="Bus 100 or 206 from Kinkaku-ji → Gojo-zaka (≈35 min), then 15-min uphill walk">
Explore the main hall, Jishu Shrine (love deity), and Otowa Waterfall. The stage views are best in afternoon light. Allow 90 minutes.
</Step>

<Step title="4:30 PM — Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion)" status="todo" subtitle="Bus 100 from Gojo-zaka → Ginkaku-ji (≈25 min)">
End with the contemplative sand garden and moss garden. The path circles a pond with mountain views — perfect for a quiet close to the day.
</Step>

<Step title="6:00 PM — Dinner in Gion" status="todo" subtitle="Walk 20 min or short bus ride from Ginkaku-ji">
Stroll historic streets, spot geisha heading to appointments, and dine on kaiseki or ramen in Kyoto's famous geisha district.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="success" title="Transport tip">Buy a **Kyoto City Bus & Subway One-Day Pass (¥900)** — covers all buses above. IC cards (ICOCA/Suica) also work.</Callout>

<Callout tone="neutral" title="Seasonal notes">
• **Cherry blossoms** (late Mar–early Apr): Maruyama Park near Kiyomizu-dera is spectacular  
• **Autumn foliage** (mid–late Nov): Kinkaku-ji and Ginkaku-ji gardens peak  
• **Summer festivals**: Gion Matsuri (July) — book hotels months ahead
</Callout>
