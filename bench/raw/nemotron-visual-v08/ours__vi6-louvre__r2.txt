# Louvre Quick Guide

Three icons you can't miss, where to find them, and how to beat the queues.

<Card title="Mona Lisa" subtitle="Leonardo da Vinci • c. 1503–1519" icon="image">
![Mona Lisa painting in the Louvre](find)
**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 711 (Salle des États)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → follow signs for "Peintures italiennes" → Salle des États
</Card>

<Card title="Venus de Milo" subtitle="Alexandros of Antioch • c. 130–100 BC" icon="image">
![Venus de Milo statue in the Louvre](find)
**Location:** Sully Wing, Ground Floor, Room 346 (Galerie des Antiques)  
**Nearest entrance:** Porte des Lions → Sully Wing → follow "Antiquités grecques"
</Card>

<Card title="Winged Victory of Samothrace" subtitle="Hellenistic • c. 200–190 BC" icon="image">
![Winged Victory of Samothrace statue at the Louvre](find)
**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 703 (top of the Daru Staircase)  
**Nearest entrance:** Pyramid → Denon Wing → Escalier Daru
</Card>

<Card title="Crowd‑avoidance tips" icon="users">
<Callout tone="info" title="Golden rule">Arrive at opening (9 am) or late afternoon (after 3 pm). The museum is quietest 9–10 am and 4–6 pm.</Callout>

- **Buy a timed‑entry ticket online** – skip the Pyramid security line entirely.
- **Enter via Porte des Lions** (when open) or Carrousel du Louvre – both are faster than the Pyramid.
- **Do the "Big Three" first** – hit Mona Lisa, Venus, and Winged Victory in the first 90 minutes before tour groups arrive.
- **Use the lesser‑known wings** – Richelieu and Sully are far calmer than Denon; save them for later.
- **Wednesday & Friday evenings** (open until 9:45 pm) are significantly quieter than weekends.
- **Download the official Louvre app** – it has real‑time crowd density maps for each room.
</Card>

</Callout>
