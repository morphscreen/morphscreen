# Spaghetti Carbonara

![spaghetti carbonara](find)

<Card title="Ingredients (serves 4)">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Spaghetti | 400 g (14 oz) |
| Guanciale (or pancetta) | 150 g (5 oz), diced |
| Egg yolks | 4 large |
| Whole egg | 1 large |
| Pecorino Romano cheese | 80 g (3 oz), finely grated |
| Parmigiano Reggiano | 40 g (1.5 oz), finely grated |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp, freshly cracked |
| Salt | For pasta water |
</Card>

<Card title="Steps">
<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare the guanciale" status="done">
Place a large cold skillet over medium heat. Add the diced guanciale and cook until the fat renders and the pieces are crisp and golden, about 5–7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside, leaving the rendered fat in the pan.
</Step>
<Step title="Cook the pasta" status="done">
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti until al dente, about 1 minute less than the package directs. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
</Step>
<Step title="Make the carbonara sauce" status="done">
In a bowl, whisk together egg yolks, whole egg, most of the Pecorino and Parmigiano (save a little for serving), and a generous amount of black pepper.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine" status="done">
Add the hot drained spaghetti to the skillet with the guanciale fat. Toss over low heat for 30 seconds. Remove from heat completely. Let cool for 1 minute, then pour the egg–cheese mixture over the pasta, tossing vigorously. Add reserved pasta water a splash at a time until a creamy sauce coats the strands.
</Step>
<Step title="Finish and serve" status="done">
Divide among warm bowls. Top with the crisp guanciale, remaining cheese, and an extra crack of black pepper. Serve immediately.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Key tips">
• Work quickly off‑heat so the eggs emulsify instead of scrambling.  
• Use the pasta water—its starch binds the sauce.  
• Guanciale is traditional; pancetta works if that’s what you have.
</Callout>
