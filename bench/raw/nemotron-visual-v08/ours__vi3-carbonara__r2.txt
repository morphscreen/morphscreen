# Spaghetti Carbonara

Classic Roman pasta with eggs, cheese, guanciale, and black pepper.

![Spaghetti carbonara in a white bowl, creamy sauce coating the pasta, topped with crispy guanciale and grated Pecorino Romano](find)

<Card title="Ingredients" icon="list">
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Spaghetti | 400 g (14 oz) |
| Guanciale (or pancetta) | 150 g (5 oz), cut into 1 cm strips |
| Egg yolks | 4 large |
| Whole eggs | 2 large |
| Pecorino Romano | 80 g (3 oz), finely grated |
| Parmigiano Reggiano | 40 g (1.5 oz), finely grated |
| Black pepper | 2 tsp, freshly cracked |
| Salt | For pasta water (lightly) |
</Card>

<Card title="Method" icon="list-numbers">
<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare the guanciale" status="done">
Place guanciale in a large cold pan. Cook over medium-low heat until fat renders and pieces are golden and crisp, 8–10 minutes. Remove with slotted spoon; keep fat in pan.
</Step>
<Step title="Cook the pasta" status="done">
Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Salt lightly (guanciale and cheese are salty). Cook spaghetti 1 minute less than package directions. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
</Step>
<Step title="Make the carbonara sauce" status="done">
In a bowl, whisk egg yolks, whole eggs, most of the Pecorino and Parmigiano, and a generous amount of black pepper. The mixture should be thick like a paste.
</Step>
<Step title="Combine" status="current">
Add drained spaghetti to the pan with rendered fat (pan off heat). Toss to coat. Let cool 30 seconds. Pour egg mixture over pasta, tossing vigorously. Add reserved pasta water a splash at a time until sauce is creamy and coats every strand.
</Step>
<Step title="Finish and serve" status="todo">
Fold in crispy guanciale. Plate immediately. Top with remaining cheese and more black pepper.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Key tips">
• **Temperature control is everything** — the pan must be off heat when you add the eggs, or they'll scramble.  
• **Use guanciale if you can** — its fat renders cleaner and tastes sweeter than pancetta.  
• **Grate cheese finely** — microplane texture melts into the sauce without clumping.  
• **Work fast** — carbonara waits for no one. Serve the second it's ready.
</Callout>
