# Low-Light Houseplants for a Dark Flat

Four reliable choices that tolerate shade, with watering guidance and cat safety.

<Card title="Snake Plant (Sansevieria)" subtitle="Very low light tolerant" icon="leaf">
![Snake plant with tall upright leaves](find)

**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (let soil dry completely)  
**Cat safety:** <Badge tone="danger">Toxic</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)" subtitle="Thrives in neglect" icon="leaf">
![ZZ plant with glossy dark green leaves](find)

**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (allow soil to dry out)  
**Cat safety:** <Badge tone="danger">Toxic</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)" subtitle="Trailing, adaptable" icon="leaf">
![Pothos vine with heart-shaped variegated leaves](find)

**Water:** Every 1–2 weeks (top inch of soil dry)  
**Cat safety:** <Badge tone="danger">Toxic</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Cast Iron Plant (Aspidistra elatior)" subtitle="Nearly indestructible" icon="leaf">
![Cast iron plant with broad dark green leaves](find)

**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (keep evenly moist, not soggy)  
**Cat safety:** <Badge tone="success">Safe</Badge>
</Card>

<Callout tone="info" title="Quick tips">
- All four prefer indirect light but survive in dim corners.
- Water less in winter; always check soil before watering.
- If you have cats, place toxic plants out of reach or choose the Cast Iron Plant.
</Callout>
