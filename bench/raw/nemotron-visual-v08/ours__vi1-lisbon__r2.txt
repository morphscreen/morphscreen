<Tabs>
<Tab title="Day 1: Belém & Riverside">
**Morning**  
- 09:00 – Pastéis de Belém for iconic custard tarts  
- 10:00 – Jerónimos Monastery (UNESCO)  
- 11:30 – Belém Tower & Monument to the Discoveries  

**Afternoon**  
- 13:30 – Lunch at **Darwin's Café** (Champalimaud Centre)  
- 15:00 – MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture & Technology)  
- 17:00 – Riverside walk to **Praça do Comércio**  

**Evening**  
- 19:30 – Dinner at **Taberna da Rua das Flores** (book ahead)  
- 21:30 – Drinks at **Pavilhão Chinês** (eclectic bar)
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 2: Alfama, Castle & Views">
**Morning**  
- 09:00 – Coffee at **Pois Café**  
- 10:00 – São Jorge Castle (best early)  
- 12:00 – Wander Alfama's alleys to **Miradouro das Portas do Sol**  

**Afternoon**  
- 13:00 – Lunch at **Casa de Linhares** (Fado at night)  
- 15:00 – National Tile Museum (Azulejos)  
- 16:30 – Tram 28 ride through Graça & Baixa  

**Evening**  
- 19:30 – Dinner at **Bairro do Avillez** (José Avillez food hall)  
- 21:30 – Fado show at **Clube de Fado** or **Mesa de Frades**
</Tab>

<Tab title="Day 3: Baixa, Chiado & LX Factory">
**Morning**  
- 09:00 – Breakfast at **Nicolau Lisboa**  
- 10:00 – Elevador de Santa Justa & Carmo Convent ruins  
- 11:30 – Explore Chiado: Bertrand Bookstore, churches, shops  

**Afternoon**  
- 13:00 – Lunch at **Time Out Market** (many stalls)  
- 15:00 – LX Factory: Ler Devagar bookshop, street art, shops  
- 17:00 – Sunset at **Miradouro da Senhora do Monte**  

**Evening**  
- 19:30 – Farewell dinner at **Taberna das Flores** or **Sea Me** (sushi)  
- 21:30 – Nightcap at **Foxtrot** (speakeasy) or **Park Bar** (rooftop)
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Card title="Where to Eat" icon="utensils">
**Breakfast & Coffee**  
- **Nicolau Lisboa** – healthy bowls, great coffee (Chiado)  
- **Pois Café** – cozy spot near castle (Alfama)  
- **Fauna & Flora** – light bites, good vibe (Príncipe Real)  

**Lunch & Casual**  
- **Time Out Market** – 30+ stalls, something for everyone (Cais do Sodré)  
- **Darwin's Café** – riverside, modern Portuguese (Belém)  
- **O Trigueirinho** – traditional petiscos, locals' favorite (Alfama)  

**Dinner & Special Occasions**  
- **Taberna da Rua das Flores** – small plates, no reservations (Baixa)  
- **Bairro do Avillez** – multiple concepts under one roof (Chiado)  
- **Sea Me** – Portuguese-Japanese fusion, peixinhos da horta (Chiado)  
- **Clube de Fado** – dinner + authentic Fado (Alfama)  

**Sweet Treats**  
- **Pastéis de Belém** – the original since 1837 (Belém)  
- **Manteigaria** – arguably better custard tarts (multiple locations)  
- **Nannarella** – gelato near Time Out Market
</Card>
