# Louvre Quick Guide

<Card title="Three Unmissable Masterpieces" subtitle="Photos, locations, and insider tips">

## Mona Lisa
![Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci](find)
**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, Room 711 (Salle des États)  
**Tip:** Visit at opening (9 am) or during the last hour before closing for the shortest queue.

## Venus de Milo
![Venus de Milo ancient Greek statue](find)
**Location:** Sully Wing, Level 0, Room 345 (Gallery of Antiquities)  
**Tip:** The room is spacious—arrive early and you’ll often have a clear view without waiting.

## Winged Victory of Samothrace
![Winged Victory of Samothrace Hellenistic sculpture](find)
**Location:** Denon Wing, Level 1, at the top of the Daru staircase (Room 703)  
**Tip:** The staircase itself is a landmark; come right at 9 am to photograph it without crowds.

</Card>

<Card title="Beat the Crowds" icon="users">
<Callout tone="info" title="Best times">
Weekday mornings (Tue–Wed) right at 9 am, or late afternoon after 4 pm. The museum is closed Tuesdays.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="success" title="Buy tickets online">
Pre‑book a timed‑entry ticket on the official Louvre website to skip the ticket line entirely.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="neutral" title="Use the side entrances">
The Porte des Lions (south side) and the Carrousel du Louvre (underground mall) often have shorter security queues than the Pyramid.
</Callout>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Plan a route">
Grab a free map at the entrance or download the Louvre app. Group the three works above—they’re all in the Denon and Sully wings, reachable in a 30‑minute walk.
</Callout>
</Card>
