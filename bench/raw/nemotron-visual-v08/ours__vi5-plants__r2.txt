# Houseplants for a Dark Flat

Four low-light champions. Water sparingly—only when the top few centimetres of soil are dry.

<Card title="Snake Plant" subtitle="Sansevieria trifasciata" icon="leaf">
![Snake plant with tall, sword-like leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (allow soil to dry completely)  
<Badge tone="danger">Toxic to cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="ZZ Plant" subtitle="Zamioculcas zamiifolia" icon="leaf">
![ZZ plant with glossy, dark green leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (let soil dry out fully)  
<Badge tone="danger">Toxic to cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Pothos" subtitle="Epipremnum aureum" icon="leaf">
![Pothos trailing vine with heart-shaped leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 1–2 weeks (when top 2–3 cm of soil is dry)  
<Badge tone="danger">Toxic to cats</Badge>
</Card>

<Card title="Cast Iron Plant" subtitle="Aspidistra elatior" icon="leaf">
![Cast iron plant with broad, dark green leaves](find)
**Water:** Every 2–3 weeks (keep slightly moist, not soggy)  
<Badge tone="success">Safe for cats</Badge>
</Card>
