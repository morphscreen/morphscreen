# Senior Product Designer

<Badge tone="info">Design Team</Badge>

---

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Design team and help shape the future of our core product. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to define and deliver intuitive, delightful experiences for millions of users. This is a high-impact role where you'll own the design process end-to-end—from discovery and ideation through to polished, production-ready interfaces.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for key product areas, collaborating with cross-functional teams
- Conduct user research and usability testing to validate concepts
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs in Figma
- Contribute to and evolve our design system
- Mentor junior designers and champion design quality across the org
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS or consumer tech
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design process and shipped products
- Expertise in Figma and modern design tools
- Deep understanding of user-centered design, accessibility, and design systems
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills
- Experience mentoring designers and influencing product strategy
</Card>
