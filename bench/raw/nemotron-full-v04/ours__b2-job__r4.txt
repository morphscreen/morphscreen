# Senior Product Designer

<Badge tone="info">Design Team</Badge>

<Card title="About the Role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our growing Design team. You'll own the end-to-end design process for core product features, from research and ideation through to high-fidelity prototypes and design systems. You'll collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to create intuitive, accessible experiences that delight our users.
</Card>

<Card title="Key Qualifications">
- 5+ years of product design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS or consumer tech
- Strong portfolio demonstrating user-centered design process and shipped products
- Expertise in Figma, prototyping tools, and design systems
- Experience conducting user research and usability testing
- Excellent communication skills and ability to present design rationale to stakeholders
- Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Bonus: motion design, design tokens, or frontend development experience (HTML/CSS/React)
</Card>

<Card title="Compensation">
<Callout tone="success" title="Salary Range">
$145,000 – $185,000 USD per year (base salary)
</Callout>
<Callout tone="neutral" title="Additional Benefits">
Equity package • Health, dental, vision • 401(k) match • Flexible PTO • Learning stipend • Home office budget
</Callout>
</Card>
