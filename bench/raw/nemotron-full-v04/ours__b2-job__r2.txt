# Senior Product Designer — Growth Team

<Card title="About the Role" subtitle="Design • San Francisco (Hybrid) • Full-time">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Growth team and help shape the user journey for millions of customers. You'll own end-to-end design for acquisition, activation, and retention flows, collaborating closely with product, engineering, data, and marketing. This is a high-impact role where your work directly moves key business metrics.
</Card>

<Card title="What You'll Do">
- Lead design for core growth surfaces: onboarding, paywalls, referral loops, and re-engagement
- Run rapid experimentation cycles — concept, prototype, test, iterate, ship
- Partner with data to identify opportunities and measure impact
- Build and maintain design system components for growth features
- Mentor junior designers and advocate for user-centered practices across the org
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
- 5+ years product design experience, with 2+ years on growth/consumer-facing products
- Strong portfolio showing measurable impact on activation, retention, or revenue metrics
- Expert in Figma; comfortable prototyping in code (React/HTML/CSS) a plus
- Fluency in A/B testing methodology and statistical significance
- Excellent communication — you can articulate design rationale to any audience
- Experience designing for mobile and web responsive layouts
</Card>
