# Senior Product Designer

**Design Team · Full-time · Remote (US/Canada)**

---

<Card title="About this role">
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to shape the core experience of our collaboration platform. You'll own end-to-end design for high-impact features, partner closely with product and engineering, and help raise the bar for design quality across the team. This is a high-autonomy role with broad scope—ideal for someone who thrives in ambiguity and loves turning complex problems into simple, elegant solutions.
</Card>

<Card title="What you'll do">
- Lead design for major product initiatives from discovery through launch
- Collaborate with PMs, engineers, and researchers to define problems and validate solutions
- Contribute to and evolve our design system
- Mentor other designers and run design critiques
- Advocate for the user in every conversation
</Card>

<Card title="Qualifications">
<Columns>
- **5+ years** product design experience at a SaaS or consumer tech company
- **Strong portfolio** showing shipped work with measurable impact
- **Expert-level** Figma skills; comfort with prototyping and motion
- **Systems thinking**—experience building or scaling design systems
- **Communication**—you can articulate rationale to any audience
- **Bonus:** experience with developer tooling, B2B collaboration, or PLG products
</Columns>
</Card>
