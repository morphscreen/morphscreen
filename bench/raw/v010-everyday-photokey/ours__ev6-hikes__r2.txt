# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is a hiker's paradise — within 30–90 minutes of the city you'll find everything from easy coastal strolls to alpine scrambles with jaw-dropping views. Here are the top picks, organized by experience level.

<Callout tone="info" title="Before you go">
Check trail conditions and weather before heading out. Many trails are snow-covered until July at higher elevations. Always carry the 10 essentials, even on "easy" hikes.
</Callout>

## Essential tips

<Card title="Make it a great day">
<Steps>
<Step title="Check conditions" status="done">Trail reports and weather at <a href="https://www.vancouvertrails.com">VancouverTrails.com</a> or Parks BC</Step>
<Step title="Start early" status="done">Popular trails fill parking lots by 8–9 AM on weekends. Aim for a 6–7 AM start.</Step>
<Step title="Pack the 10 essentials" status="done">Map, headlamp, sun protection, first aid, knife, fire starter, shelter, extra food, extra water, extra layers</Step>
<Step title="Leave no trace" status="done">Pack out everything, stay on trails, and respect wildlife.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="What to bring">
<Checklist title="Day hike packing list" name="packing">
- [x] Water (at least 2L)
- [x] Snacks and lunch
- [x] Layers (weather changes fast)
- [x] Rain jacket
- [x] Sturdy hiking shoes
- [x] Map or GPS
- [x] Headlamp
- [x] Sunscreen and hat
- [ ] Trekking poles (optional)
- [ ] Bear spray (for remote trails)
</Checklist>
</Card>
