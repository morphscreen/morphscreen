# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

¡Hola! Here are the essential phrases you'll actually use, with pronunciation tips and when to say them.

<Card title="The 10 Phrases" subtitle="Tap a phrase to practice it out loud">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Greetings">
<KeyValue title="Essential greetings">
| Phrase | Meaning | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hola** (OH-lah) | Hello | Any time, casual |
| **Buenos días** (BWEH-nos DEE-as) | Good morning | Before noon |
| **Buenas tardes** (BWEH-nas TAR-des) | Good afternoon | Noon to evening |
| **Buenas noches** (BWEH-nas NO-ches) | Good night | Evening and night |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="info" title="Pronunciation tip">
The **h** in Spanish is always silent. **Hola** sounds like "OH-lah," not "HOH-lah."
</Callout>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Politeness">
<KeyValue title="Manners that open doors">
| Phrase | Meaning | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Por favor** (por fah-VOR) | Please | Add to any request |
| **Gracias** (GRAH-syas) | Thank you | After anything |
| **De nada** (deh NAH-dah) | You're welcome | When someone thanks you |
| **Perdón** (per-DON) | Excuse me / Sorry | Getting attention, bumping into someone |
</KeyValue>

<Callout tone="success" title="Pro tip">
Saying **"Hola, buenos días"** together is friendly and natural — it's like "Hello, good morning!"
</Callout>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Essential Questions">
<KeyValue title="The questions that save you">
| Phrase | Meaning | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **¿Dónde está...?** (DON-deh es-TAH) | Where is...? | "Where is the bathroom?" |
| **¿Cuánto cuesta?** (KWAN-toh KWES-tah) | How much does it cost? | Markets, taxis, anything with a price |
| **¿Hablas inglés?** (AH-blas een-GLES) | Do you speak English? | When you're stuck |
| **No entiendo** (no en-TYEN-do) | I don't understand | When you're lost |
</KeyValue>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>
