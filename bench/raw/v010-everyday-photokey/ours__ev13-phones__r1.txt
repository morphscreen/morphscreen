## Where each one wins

<Tabs>
<Tab title="iPhone 16">

- **Video recording** is still the best in any phone — 4K/60 with Dolby Vision, plus new spatial audio capture
- **Build quality** feels premium; titanium frame and Ceramic Shield front
- **Ecosystem** — AirDrop, iMessage, Apple Watch, Mac integration just works
- **App quality** — iOS apps are typically more polished, especially games and creative tools
- **Resale value** — iPhones hold value better over time
- **Audio** — louder, cleaner speakers for media

</Tab>
<Tab title="Pixel 9">

- **Display** is smoother — 120Hz vs 60Hz makes scrolling feel much more fluid
- **AI features** are genuinely useful: Magic Eraser, Best Take, Call Screen, Live Translate
- **Camera zoom** — 8x Super Res zoom beats iPhone's 2x optical
- **Battery** lasts a bit longer in mixed use
- **Updates** — 7 years of OS updates vs iPhone's 5
- **Price drops** — Google often discounts heavily during sales

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Which one should you pick?

<Card title="Decision guide">
<Steps>
<Step title="Do you live in the Apple ecosystem?" status="current" subtitle="iPhone, Mac, Apple Watch, AirPods">
If yes, the iPhone 16 is the obvious choice — everything just syncs. If no, the Pixel is more open and flexible.
</Step>
<Step title="How much do you care about video?" status="todo" subtitle="Shooting vlogs, family moments, or events">
iPhone wins here. If video is a priority, get the iPhone 16.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you want the best screen experience?" status="todo" subtitle="Scrolling, gaming, watching content">
The Pixel's 120Hz display is noticeably smoother. If you notice refresh rates, go Pixel.
</Step>
<Step title="Do you want AI features that actually help daily?" status="todo" subtitle="Photo editing, call screening, translation">
Pixel's AI is more integrated and useful day-to-day. iPhone's is more limited.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>
