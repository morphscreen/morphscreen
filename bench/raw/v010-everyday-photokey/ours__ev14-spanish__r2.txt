# 10 Basic Spanish Phrases for Your Trip

<Badge tone="info">Beginner friendly</Badge>

Here are the 10 most useful Spanish phrases for traveling. Each one comes with pronunciation tips and when to use it.

<Card title="The 10 Phrases">
<Flashcards title="Practice the phrases">
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| Hola | Hello (OH-lah) |
| Por favor | Please (por fah-VOR) |
| Gracias | Thank you (GRAH-see-as) |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | How much does it cost? (KWAN-to KWEH-sta) |
| ¿Dónde está...? | Where is...? (DON-day es-TAH) |
| La cuenta, por favor | The check, please (la KWEHN-ta por fah-VOR) |
| No entiendo | I don't understand (no en-tee-EN-do) |
| ¿Habla inglés? | Do you speak English? (AH-blah een-GLES) |
| Ayuda | Help (ah-YOO-dah) |
| Adiós | Goodbye (ah-dee-OS) |
</Flashcards>
</Card>

<Card title="When to Use Each Phrase">
<Tabs>
<Tab title="Greetings & Basics">
<KeyValue title="Essential Greetings">
| Phrase | When to use |
|---|---|
| Hola | Any time of day, with anyone |
| Adiós | When leaving a shop, restaurant, or conversation |
| Gracias | After any service, purchase, or help |
| Por favor | When ordering, asking, or requesting anything |
</KeyValue>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Getting Around">
<KeyValue title="Navigation & Shopping">
| Phrase | When to use |
|---|---|
| ¿Dónde está...? | Follow with a place: "¿Dónde está el baño?" (Where is the bathroom?) |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | At markets, shops, or asking for prices |
| La cuenta, por favor | At restaurants when you want to pay |
</KeyValue>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Emergencies & Help">
<KeyValue title="When You Need Help">
| Phrase | When to use |
|---|---|
| No entiendo | If someone speaks too fast or you're confused |
| ¿Habla inglés? | To switch to English if needed |
| Ayuda | In any emergency or if you need assistance |
</KeyValue>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Card>

<Card title="Tips for Success">
<Callout tone="success" title="Pronunciation tip">
Spanish is phonetic—say every letter. The "ll" sounds like "y" in "yes," and "j" sounds like "h" in "hello."
</Callout>

<Callout tone="info" title="Practice tip">
Try saying each phrase out loud 3 times. Repetition is key for remembering them.
</Callout>
