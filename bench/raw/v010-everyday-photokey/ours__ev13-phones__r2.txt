## Which one fits you? Choose your priority

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Camera & photos">

<Card title="Camera comparison">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">
- **Main:** 48 MP, great for everyday shots
- **Ultrawide:** 12 MP, solid but not class-leading
- **Video:** Best-in-class, 4K Dolby Vision
- **Photographic Styles:** Tune color in real time
- **No telephoto** — 2x zoom is digital crop
</Card>
<Card title="Pixel 9">
- **Main:** 50 MP, excellent detail and dynamic range
- **Ultrawide:** 48 MP, also serves as macro
- **Video:** Good but not iPhone-level
- **Magic Editor:** Remove objects, reframe shots
- **No telephoto** — but 2x "Super Res Zoom" is surprisingly good
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="info" title="Verdict">
If you shoot **video** or want the most reliable point-and-shoot, get the **iPhone 16**. If you take **photos** and love AI editing tools, the **Pixel 9** wins.
</Callout>

</Card>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Performance & gaming">

<Card title="Performance">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">
- **A18 chip** — blazing fast, great for gaming
- 60Hz display (smooth but not 120Hz)
- Excellent thermal management
- iOS optimization means apps run flawlessly
</Card>
<Card title="Pixel 9">
- **Tensor G4** — efficient but not a gaming powerhouse
- 120Hz display — noticeably smoother scrolling
- Can lag on heavy games
- 12 GB RAM helps multitasking
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Note">
If you play demanding games or want the fastest possible experience, the **iPhone 16** is the clear winner. The Pixel's 120Hz screen is nicer for daily use, but the chip is less powerful.
</Callout>

</Card>
</Tab>

<Tab title="AI & smart features">

<Card title="AI capabilities">
<Columns>
<Card title="iPhone 16">
- **Apple Intelligence** (coming in updates)
- Siri improvements, notification summaries
- Cleanup tool in Photos
- On-device processing, privacy-focused
</Card>
<Card title="Pixel 9">
- **Gemini AI** built in — very capable
- Call screening, live translate
- Magic Editor, Best Take, Audio Magic Eraser
- AI-powered photo editing is unmatched
</Card>
</Columns>

<Callout tone="success" title="Verdict">
If **AI features today** matter most, the **Pixel 9** is ahead. If you're happy to wait for Apple Intelligence to mature, the iPhone will catch up.
</Callout>

</Card>
</Tab>

<Columns>

## Your decision guide

- You're already in the Apple ecosystem (iPhone, Mac, Watch, AirPods)
- You record lots of video or vlog
- You want the smoothest, most reliable experience
- You play mobile games
- You prefer iOS over Android

## Final verdict

<Callout tone="success" title="Bottom line">
Both phones are excellent at $799. The **iPhone 16** is the safer, all-round choice — great performance, superb video, and a mature ecosystem. The **Pixel 9** is the more exciting pick for photography lovers and AI enthusiasts, with a smoother screen and longer support.

**Still undecided?** Think about your daily habits: do you live in iMessage and FaceTime? Get the iPhone. Do you live in Google Photos and Assistant? Get the Pixel.
</Callout>
