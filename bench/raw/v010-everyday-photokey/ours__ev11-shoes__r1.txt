<Card title="Sizing tips">
- **Run shoes run small**: buy a half-size up from your dress shoe size.
- **Toe room**: you should have a thumb's width between your longest toe and the shoe end.
- **Try in the evening**: feet swell during the day.
- **Wear your running socks** when trying on.
</Card>
