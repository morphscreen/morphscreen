# What to Eat in Lisbon (and Where)

Lisbon's food scene is a love letter to the sea, the sun, and centuries of trade. Here’s your edible map—from 2-euro street snacks to white-tablecloth seafood.

<Card title="The Essentials: 5 Dishes You Can’t Skip">
<Columns>
<Card title="1. Pastel de Nata">
The iconic custard tart with a blistered, caramelized top and shattering puff pastry. Eat it warm, dusted with cinnamon.
**Where:** *Manteigaria* (multiple branches) or *Pastéis de Belém* (the original, since 1837).
</Card>
<Card title="2. Bacalhau à Brás">
Shredded salt cod, matchstick potatoes, onions, and scrambled eggs—comfort in a pan. Every restaurant has a version.
**Where:** *Casa Portuguesa do Pastel de Bacalhau* (Bairro Alto) or *Cervejaria Ramiro*.
</Card>
<Card title="3. Bifana">
A humble, glorious sandwich: marinated pork in a crusty roll, often with a spicy mustard kick. Street food royalty.
**Where:** *Casa Bifanas* (Rossio) or *O Trevo* (Santos).
</Card>
<Card title="4. Cataplana de Marisco">
A steaming copper pot of clams, shrimp, and fish in a tomato-garlic-coriander broth. Made for sharing.
**Where:** *Cervejaria Ramiro* (classic) or *Uma Marisqueira* (Chiado).
</Card>
<Card title="5. Ginjinha">
A sour cherry liqueur, served in a tiny plastic cup or a chocolate cup. The perfect digestif after a long meal.
**Where:** *A Ginjinha* (Rossio) or *Ginjinha Sem Rival* (Bairro Alto).
</Card>
</Columns>
</Card>

<Card title="A Day of Eating: My Suggested Route">
<Steps>
<Step title="9:00 — Breakfast" status="done">A *pastel de nata* and a *galão* (milky coffee) at *Manteigaria* in Chiado.</Step>
<Step title="10:30 — Morning Snack" status="done">A *bifana* at *Casa Bifanas* near Rossio. Yes, it’s early. You’re on vacation.</Step>
<Step title="13:00 — Lunch" status="done">*Bacalhau à Brás* at *Cervejaria Ramiro*. Order a *fino* (small beer) to cut the salt.</Step>
<Step title="16:00 — Afternoon Pick-Me-Up" status="done">A *ginjinha* in a chocolate cup at *A Ginjinha*. Walk it off through the Alfama hills.</Step>
<Step title="20:00 — Dinner" status="done">*Cataplana de marisco* at *Uma Marisqueira*. Share it. Sip white wine. Repeat.</Step>
<Step title="22:00 — Nightcap" status="done">Another *ginjinha*, or a *medronho* (firewater) if you’re brave, at *Ginjinha Sem Rival*.</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

<Card title="Quick Picks: If You Only Have 24 Hours">
| Spot | What to Order | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Manteigaria* | Pastel de nata | The gold standard, fresh all day |
| *Casa Bifanas* | Bifana | Fast, cheap, legendary |
| *Cervejaria Ramiro* | Garlic shrimp, *bacalhau à Brás* | A Lisbon institution since 1856 |
| *Time Out Market* | Anything from the *Marisqueira* stall | Variety in one roof |
| *Pastéis de Belém* | The original tart, with cinnamon | History on a plate |

<Callout tone="warning" title="Watch Out">
- **Tourist traps near Praça do Comércio**: menus in 10 languages = often overpriced. Walk one street back.
- **Portions are generous**: share mains unless you’re very hungry.
- **Cash is king** for tiny tascas; cards work at most sit-down places.
</Callout>
</Card>
