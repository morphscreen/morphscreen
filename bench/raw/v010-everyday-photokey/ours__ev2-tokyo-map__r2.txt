<Card title="How to use this plan">
This route keeps you moving east to west across the city, with each day focused on one area so you spend less time on trains and more time exploring. The map above shows the order of visits — start each morning where the previous day ended, or use the JR Yamanote Line loop to jump between hubs quickly.

<Callout tone="info" title="Getting around">
Buy a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at any station (¥500 deposit, refundable). Tap on and off trains, buses, and even convenience stores. The Yamanote Line covers most of your route.
</Callout>
</Card>

## Day 1 — Shinjuku, Shibuya & Harajuku

<Card title="Day 1: Neon, shrines & crossing the busiest street on Earth">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Start at the station's west exit">
Walk through the skyscraper district, visit the free **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** observatory (45th floor) for a panoramic view of Mt. Fuji on clear days.
</Step>
<Step title="Late morning: Meiji Shrine" status="done" subtitle="A forest in the city">
Take the JR Yamanote Line to Harajuku Station, then walk 10 minutes through the torii gates into this serene Shinto shrine surrounded by 100,000 trees.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Harajuku & Shibuya" status="done" subtitle="Takeshita Street, then the scramble">
Explore **Takeshita Street** for quirky fashion and crepes, then walk 15 minutes south to **Shibuya Crossing** — watch the famous scramble from the Starbucks second floor, then visit the **Hachiko statue**.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Back to Shinjuku" status="done" subtitle="Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho">
Return to Shinjuku for dinner in **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane), a narrow alley of tiny yakitori stalls. Later, explore **Golden Gai** — 200+ micro-bars in six alleys.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="success" title="Budget lunch tip">
Grab a **¥500–800 ramen bowl** at Ichiran in Shibuya — solo booths, perfect for a quick refuel before more walking.
</Callout>
</Card>

## Day 2 — Asakusa, Ueno & Akihabara

<Card title="Day 2: Old Tokyo & otaku culture">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Senso-ji Temple" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's oldest temple">
Take the Ginza Line to Asakusa. Walk through the **Kaminarimon Gate**, down **Nakamise-dori** shopping street, and into the temple grounds. Try a **ningyo-yaki** (stuffed cake) from a street vendor.
</Step>
<Step title="Late morning: Ueno Park" status="done" subtitle="Museums & a pond">
A 15-minute walk or one stop on the Ginza Line to Ueno. Visit the **Tokyo National Museum** (¥1,000) for samurai armor and ukiyo-e prints, or just stroll around Shinobazu Pond.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Akihabara" status="done" subtitle="Electric Town">
Take the JR Yamanote Line two stops to Akihabara. Explore **Yodobashi Camera** (8 floors of electronics), browse anime stores like **Animate**, and try a **maid café** if you're curious (¥1,000–2,000 cover).
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Asakusa at night" status="done" subtitle="Sumida River views">
Return to Asakusa for dinner at a **monjayaki** (savory pancake) restaurant, then walk along the Sumida River for a nighttime view of **Tokyo Skytree**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Day 3 — Ginza, Tsukiji & Odaiba

<Card title="Day 3: Markets, shopping & a futuristic island">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market" status="done" subtitle="Fresh sushi for breakfast">
Take the Hibiya Line to Tsukiji. The inner market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market still has 400+ stalls. Try **tamago (egg) on a stick**, **uni (sea urchin)**, and **fresh sushi** from a standing bar.
</Step>
<Step title="Late morning: Ginza" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's luxury district">
Walk 10 minutes east to Ginza. Browse **Mitsukoshi department store** (basement food hall is a must), see the **Kabuki-za Theatre** exterior, and check out **Uniqlo's flagship** (12 floors).
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Odaiba" status="done" subtitle="A man-made island with a beach">
Take the Yurikamome driverless train from Shimbashi. Visit **teamLab Planets** (immersive digital art, book tickets in advance ¥3,200), walk on **Odaiba Beach**, and see the **life-size Gundam statue** at DiverCity.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Rainbow Bridge at night" status="done" subtitle="The best view of Tokyo Bay">
Stay for sunset and watch the **Rainbow Bridge** light up. Have dinner at **Aqua City Odaiba** with a bay view.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Book ahead">
**teamLab Planets** sells out days in advance. Reserve tickets online at least 3 days before your visit.
</Callout>
</Card>

## Day 4 — Shinjuku Gyoen, Roppongi & a local neighborhood

<Card title="Day 4: Gardens, art & night views">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen" status="done" subtitle="A peaceful garden in the middle of the city">
A 10-minute walk from Shinjuku Station. This 144-acre garden has Japanese, French, and English sections. **¥500 entry**. Perfect for a picnic — grab a bento from the station's department store basement.
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Roppongi Hills" status="done" subtitle="Art & architecture">
Take the Oedo Line to Roppongi. Visit the **Mori Art Museum** (¥1,800, includes the 52nd-floor observation deck with glass-walled views), and walk through the **Roppongi Hills complex** — look for the giant spider sculpture by Louise Bourgeois.
</Step>
<Step title="Late afternoon: Daikanyama" status="done" subtitle="A quieter, stylish neighborhood">
One stop on the Tokyu Toyoko Line from Shibuya. Walk down **Kyū-Yamate-dōri** street, browse **Tsutaya Books** (a gorgeous glass-and-wood bookstore), and grab coffee at one of the many specialty cafés.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Tokyo Tower" status="done" subtitle="The iconic red tower">
Take the Hibiya Line to Kamiyacho, then walk 5 minutes. The **Main Deck** (¥1,200) is cheaper than Skytree and less crowded. Go at dusk to see the city light up.
</Step>
</Steps>
</Card>

## Day 5 — Skytree, Sumida River & last-minute souvenirs

<Card title="Day 5: High views, river walks & shopping">
<Steps>
<Step title="Morning: Tokyo Skytree" status="done" subtitle="Japan's tallest structure">
Take the Tobu Skytree Line to Tokyo Skytree Station. Go to the **Tembo Deck** (¥2,100) for a 360° view up to 75km on clear days. The **Solamachi** shopping complex below has 300+ shops.
</Step>
<Step title="Late morning: Sumida River walk" status="done" subtitle="A scenic stroll">
Walk south along the river toward **Ryogoku**. Cross at the **Sakurabashi Bridge** for a classic photo of the Skytree over the water. Stop at **Edo-Tokyo Museum** if you want history (¥600).
</Step>
<Step title="Afternoon: Kappabashi Kitchen Street" status="done" subtitle="Tokyo's restaurant-supply district">
Take the Ginza Line to Tawaramachi. This street sells **plastic food samples** (perfect souvenirs), chef knives, and ceramics. Try **Kotobuki** for mini food models — they make great gifts.
</Step>
<Step title="Evening: Farewell dinner" status="done" subtitle="One last memorable meal">
Choose a **kaiseki** (multi-course) dinner in Asakusa or a **sushi omakase** in Ginza. Book ahead — places like **Sushi Iwa** (¥10,000–15,000 per person) fill up fast.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="success" title="Packing tip">
Bring a **small daypack** — you'll be walking 15,000–20,000 steps daily. Comfortable shoes are non-negotiable.
</Callout>
</Card>
