# Best Day Hikes Near Vancouver

Vancouver is a hiker's paradise — you can be on a mountain trail within 30 minutes of downtown. Here are the top day hikes, ranked by how much they deliver per effort.

<Card title="1. Stawamus Chief — The classic for panoramic views">
<Columns>
<div>
The Chief, in Squamish, is a giant granite monolith with three peaks. The trail is steep and often involves ladders and chains, but the payoff is a sweeping view of Howe Sound and the surrounding peaks. Peak 2 is the most popular; Peak 1 is slightly easier.

- **Distance:** 11 km round trip (to Peak 2)
- **Elevation gain:** 700 m
- **Time:** 5–7 hours
- **Best season:** May–October (chains can be icy in spring)
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="warning">Very popular</Badge> — go early (before 8 AM) on weekends.
</div>
</Columns>

<Card title="2. Garibaldi Lake — Turquoise water and mountain drama">
<Columns>
<div>
A steady climb through forest opens onto a vast, impossibly blue lake ringed by snowy peaks. The trail is long but well-graded, and the view at the end is worth every step. If you have energy, continue to the Panorama Ridge viewpoint for an even bigger payoff.

- **Distance:** 18 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 900 m
- **Time:** 6–8 hours
- **Best season:** July–October (snow lingers into June)
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">Day-use pass required</Badge> on weekends and holidays (May–October). Book on BC Parks.
</div>
</Columns>

</Card>

<Card title="3. Joffre Lakes — Three lakes, all impossibly turquoise">
<Columns>
<div>
This is the most Instagrammable hike near Vancouver. Three glacier-fed lakes in a row, each more stunning than the last. The trail is well-maintained and not too steep, but it's long. The third lake is the showstopper.

- **Distance:** 10 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 500 m
- **Time:** 4–6 hours
- **Best season:** July–October
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="warning">Very high crowds</Badge> — arrive by 7 AM or go on a weekday. Day-use pass required in summer.
</div>
</Columns>
<Button ask="What's the best time to visit Joffre Lakes?" >Best time to go</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="4. Quarry Rock — Quick and accessible">
<Columns>
<div>
A short, family-friendly hike in Deep Cove that ends on a granite outcrop overlooking Indian Arm. Perfect for a half-day trip or a first hike with kids. The trail is mostly forest with some stairs.

- **Distance:** 3.8 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 100 m
- **Time:** 1.5–2.5 hours
- **Best season:** Year-round (pack microspikes in winter)
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="neutral">Best for beginners</Badge>
</div>
</Columns>
<Button ask="What should I bring for a short hike like Quarry Rock?">Packing list</Button>
</Card>

<Card title="5. Dog Mountain — City views from a mountain top">
<Columns>
<div>
A short but steep climb near Mount Seymour that rewards you with a panoramic view of Vancouver, the harbour, and the Gulf Islands. The final stretch is a rocky summit. It's a great sunset hike.

- **Distance:** 5.5 km round trip
- **Elevation gain:** 300 m
- **Time:** 2–3 hours
- **Best season:** June–October (snow possible until July; microspikes in shoulder seasons)
</div>
<div>
<Badge tone="info">Best for sunset</Badge>
</div>
</Columns>

<Card title="Getting there from downtown Vancouver">
<KeyValue title="Transport options">
| Option | Details |
|---|---|
| Car | All hikes have parking lots. Expect to pay $3–$10. Arrive early to get a spot. |
| Public transit | Quarry Rock (bus 212 from Phibbs Exchange) and Dog Mountain (bus 228 to Mount Seymour) are doable. Others need a car. |
| Ride-share | Uber/Lyft works for Quarry Rock and Dog Mountain, but expensive for Garibaldi or Joffre. |
| Shuttle | Some operators (e.g., Parkbus) run seasonal shuttles to Garibaldi and Joffre. |
</KeyValue>
</Card>

<Card title="Safety & etiquette">
<Callout tone="warning" title="Mountain weather">Always check the forecast before heading up. It can be sunny in Vancouver and snowing on the trail. Turn around if conditions worsen.</Callout>
- **Leave no trace:** Pack out all trash, including orange peels and dog waste.
- **Wildlife:** Black bears are common on all these trails. Carry bear spray (especially Garibaldi and Joffre) and make noise.
- **Trail conditions:** Check [Vancouver Trails](https://www.vancouvertrails.com) or the BC Parks website for closures and snow reports.
- **Cell service:** Spotty on most trails. Download offline maps in advance.
</Card>
