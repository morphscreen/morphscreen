# Running Shoes Under $120 for Road Running

Finding a solid road shoe under $120 is very doable—you just need to focus on last season's models and well-established value lines. Here's what to look for and three strong options.

<Card title="What to check before you buy" subtitle="Fit and feel matter more than specs">
- **Heel-to-toe drop**: 8–12 mm is standard for road shoes; lower drops (4–6 mm) suit midfoot strikers
- **Cushioning**: Look for a firm-to-moderate foam—too soft can feel unstable on long runs
- **Weight**: Under 10 oz (283 g) per shoe is a good target for daily training
- **Size**: Road shoes should have a thumb's width of space ahead of your longest toe
- **Try them in the evening**: Feet swell during the day, so shop later if possible
</Card>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Don't buy for the brand">
The best shoe is the one that fits *your* foot shape and gait. If you overpronate (knees roll inward), you may need a stability shoe—but many neutral shoes work fine for mild pronation. If you're unsure, ask a running store to watch you jog.
</Callout>
