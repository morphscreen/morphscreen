# Senior Product Designer

**Design Systems Team** <Badge tone="info">Full-time</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Remote · US & EU</Badge>

<Card title="About the role">
We're looking for a product designer who loves building systems, not just screens. You'll own our design language end-to-end — from tokens and components to documentation and adoption — working alongside engineers, PMs, and other designers to ship consistent, accessible experiences across all our products.
</Card>

<Card title="What we're looking for">
<Checklist title="Core qualifications" name="quals">
- 5+ years in product design, with at least 2 focused on design systems
- A portfolio showing shipped component libraries or design tokens in production
- Strong Figma skills — variables, components, and auto-layout mastery
- Experience with accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and cross-platform constraints
- Clear written and visual communication for documenting guidelines
</Checklist>

<Callout tone="info" title="Nice to have">
- Experience with code (HTML/CSS or React) to bridge design and engineering
- Prior work on theming or white-label products
- Familiarity with token naming conventions like W3C Design Tokens
</Callout>
</Card>
