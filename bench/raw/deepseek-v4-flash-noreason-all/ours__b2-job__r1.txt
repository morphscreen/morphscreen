<Card title="What you'll do">

- Partner with engineers to ship accessible, reusable components
- Document usage guidelines and run office hours for product teams
- Advocate for accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) across all surfaces
- Mentor one associate designer and contribute to design critiques
</Card>
