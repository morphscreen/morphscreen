## Keynote Details

<Event title="Opening Keynote: The Future of AI in Product Design" start="2025-03-18T09:00" end="2025-03-18T10:00" where="Main Auditorium" tone="info">
**Speaker:** Dr. Maya Chen — Chief Design Officer, Nexus Labs

**Abstract:**  
AI is reshaping every layer of the product development lifecycle. In this keynote, Dr. Chen shares insights from Nexus Labs' latest research on generative design systems, predictive user modeling, and the evolving role of designers in an AI-augmented workflow. Expect live demos, hard-won lessons, and a framework for integrating AI without losing the human center of design.

**Bio:**  
Dr. Maya Chen leads design at Nexus Labs, where she directs a 200+ person organization spanning product design, research, and design infrastructure. Previously, she founded the Design Intelligence group at Google and authored *Designing with Machines* (O'Reilly, 2023). She holds a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction from Stanford.
</Event>
