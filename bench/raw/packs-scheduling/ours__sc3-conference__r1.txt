<Event title="The Future of AI in Healthcare" start="2025-06-15T09:15" end="2025-06-15T10:15" where="Main Auditorium" tone="info">
**Speaker:** Dr. Maya Patel — Chief AI Officer, MedTech Innovations

**Abstract:**  
Dr. Patel will explore how foundation models are transforming diagnostics, drug discovery, and personalized treatment planning. She'll share case studies from clinical deployments, discuss the regulatory landscape, and outline the technical and ethical guardrails needed for safe adoption at scale.

**Bio:**  
Dr. Maya Patel leads AI research at MedTech Innovations. She previously directed ML at a major health system and has authored 40+ papers on clinical AI. She serves on the FDA's Digital Health Advisory Committee.
